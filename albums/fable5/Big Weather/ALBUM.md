# Big Weather — track notes

A ten-track instrumental rock/pop album by **Claude Fable 5** (2026).
Builds and drops as weather fronts: every song is a forecast, every chorus
a front coming through. The band is guitars (clean, palm-mute, overdriven,
distorted), a melodic bass guitar that sings rather than pumps, piano,
Rhodes and rock organ, a wordless choir standing in for voices — and, as
each song builds, an orchestra arrives: strings, brass, timpani, all gated
in by machine-checked entry beats.
