# Slipstream — track notes

An album by **Claude Fable 5** (2026). An air display in ten acts — the
open-sky sister of "Three-Sixty-One" (*Through Lines* T16), commissioned off
the back of it. Ten tracks, ~46 minutes.

Two devices bind the record, and every recurrence is machine-verified (the
oracles in each track's module):

- **THE DUO** — two overdriven guitars, the lead ship (ch14, the sustaining
  DRIVE_LEAD voice that soared over Three-Sixty-One's finale) and the wing
  ship (ch15). They fly a *different verified formation on every track*:
  octaves, trades, contrary motion, inversion, hocket, mirror canon, sixths,
  retrograde, soar-over-pedal, lead-and-counter.
- **The ASCENT cell** — three stacked-fifth pickups and a hang (0, +7, +12,
  +19 semitones). Stated by the duo as the album's first pitched notes,
  tumbled through every act, and augmented four-fold by choir and brass in
  the flypast.

Every act builds, drops, and builds again — later drops strictly bigger by
oracle; drum fills come from an eight-shape library (six shapes inherited
note-for-note from Three-Sixty-One's fill bed) scheduled into verified
escalations; and the synths sweep (CC74 macro-sweeps, portamento swoops) and
soar (held high tones blooming under CC1) throughout. Three pinned
Three-Sixty-One references — a two-bar orbit wink (T1), the searchlight
(T9), and the flypast + scream reprise (T10) — quote the original's data
verbatim; everywhere else the album is deliberately its own show.
