# Through Lines — track notes

A double album by **Claude Fable 5** (2026). Every piece is a line — a lineage,
a high wire, a fault line, a bass line, a timeline. Fifteen tracks and a bonus
reprise, ~91 minutes.

Two devices bind the record, and every recurrence is verified by machine oracle
(the "through-line oracles" in each track's module):

- **The FABLE cell** — F, A, B♭, then a half-beat rest where the L would sing
  (the silent letter), then E. A major third, a semitone, a tritone. Stated in
  the opener, it becomes the ground bass of the suite's anthem and, augmented
  four-fold, the music-box melody that closes the album.
- **Morse lanes** — real Morse rhythm, decodable from the MIDI: CLAUDE (T2),
  HOLD THE LINE (T4), GERMAN BIGHT (T7), GOODNIGHT (T15).

Cross-track recurrences, all pinned note-for-note against `material.py`: the
bridge chorale is cut off mid-phrase throughout *Fault Lines* and returns
unbroken at the dawn of *German Bight*; the three Fine Line motifs (the dive
cascade, the walker theme, the orbit riff) stack in counterpoint in
*Three-Sixty*'s finale; the hidden epilogue of *The Estuary Suite* quotes *The
Ledger* and is cut off mid-phrase; *Landing Lights* ends on the album's first
pitch.

Oracle-first, per the house method: every headline musical claim above exists
as a falsifiable check in the track's module (`movements/tNN_*.py`) and in the
album verifier — roughly three hundred structural oracles plus per-track audio oracles measured
on the rendered waveforms. The pieces were composed *to pass them*.

The record is also the début of ferrosintesis v0.11, whose voices it
commissioned: taiko, steel drums, tinkle bell, agogo, woodblock, melodic tom,
synth drum, the reverse-cymbal riser, a true hammered dulcimer, the
