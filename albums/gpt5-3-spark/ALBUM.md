# The Spark

Original cinematic-minimalist MIDI album for piano, strings, celesta, and sparse low percussion.
The album is an emotional journey through rapid motion, slow grief, kinetic pressure, introspection, and tragedy.

This is original material using broad film-music and contemporary-classical vocabulary; it is not a direct imitation of any living composer or existing score.

## Tracklist

01. The Quiet Before Dawn - 300.7s - quiet, still, expectant
    - File: `midi/01 - The Quiet Before Dawn.mid`
    - Role: A room-temperature hush with soft piano harmonics and distant bowed strings.
02. Windowlit Water - 291.0s - quiet, contemplative, slow-building
    - File: `midi/02 - Windowlit Water.mid`
    - Role: Paper reflections over soft piano, then a patient pulse of strings beneath.
03. Letters in Winter Ink - 368.4s - tender, wounded, and restrained
    - File: `midi/03 - Letters in Winter Ink.mid`
    - Role: A three-note lament repeated through glassy chords and long cello sighs.
04. First Alarm - 231.1s - urgent, tense, investigative
    - File: `midi/04 - First Alarm.mid`
    - Role: A nervous pulse rises under staccato bass and fractured piano runs.
05. A City of Small Fires - 268.3s - restless, urban, intimate
    - File: `midi/05 - A City of Small Fires.mid`
    - Role: Piano and strings circle in an anxious dance across muted harmonies.
06. Mercy of the Rail Line - 239.3s - propulsive, searching, relentless
    - File: `midi/06 - Mercy of the Rail Line.mid`
    - Role: Low percussion and repeated harmonic cells carry the album into a kinetic state.
07. Midnight Engine - 239.9s - luminous, patient, turning inward
    - File: `midi/07 - Midnight Engine.mid`
    - Role: A bright center where the pulse stays calm and the harmony opens.
08. A Door You Didn't Hear Close - 392.5s - quiet but dramatic, haunted
    - File: `midi/08 - A Door You Didn't Hear Close.mid`
    - Role: The momentum slips backward, then gathers grief through deep cello echoes.
09. The Big Arc Begins - 226.8s - urgent, sweeping, resilient
    - File: `midi/09 - The Big Arc Begins.mid`
    - Role: The album re-accelerates with a wide pulse and more open harmony.
10. Lightning Over Stone - 222.2s - fast, brilliant, triumphant
    - File: `midi/10 - Lightning Over Stone.mid`
    - Role: A large emotional crest: percussion, cello, and celesta ignite the mid-album peak.
11. Chamber of Witness - 293.9s - massive, cathartic, expansive
    - File: `midi/11 - Chamber of Witness.mid`
    - Role: A final expansion before quiet; the emotional weight lands with layered strings and tolling piano.
12. After the Spark, You Return - 374.3s - quiet, reflective, unresolved
    - File: `midi/12 - After the Spark, You Return.mid`
    - Role: A calm close with gentle fragments returning, now carrying the scar of the journey.
