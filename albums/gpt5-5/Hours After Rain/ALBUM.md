# Hours After Rain

A cinematic-minimalist MIDI album for piano, strings, celesta, and sparse low percussion.
The album is an emotional journey through rapid motion, slow grief, kinetic pressure, introspection, and tragedy.

The brief uses broad film-music and contemporary-classical vocabulary and asks for a new composition rather than a direct imitation. That is a statement of intent, not a guarantee against incidental similarity.

## Tracklist

01. The City Holds Its Breath - 251.8s - slow, apprehensive, introspective
    - File: `midi/01 - The City Holds Its Breath.mid`
    - Role: A restrained opening with distant low strings and a hesitant piano pulse.
02. Glass on Wet Pavement - 181.4s - rapid, nervous, urban
    - File: `midi/02 - Glass on Wet Pavement.mid`
    - Role: Sixteenth-note piano motion under thin strings, built for forward pressure.
03. Mercy in Three Notes - 380.2s - slow, tender, wounded
    - File: `midi/03 - Mercy in Three Notes.mid`
    - Role: A long-breathed lament around a three-note cell.
04. A Door Left Open - 204.1s - energetic, tense, investigative
    - File: `midi/04 - A Door Left Open.mid`
    - Role: Short cells and accented bass notes move like a discovery scene.
05. Rooms That Remember Us - 299.5s - introspective, warm, haunted
    - File: `midi/05 - Rooms That Remember Us.mid`
    - Role: A slower interior track with piano harmonics and answering cello phrases.
06. Red Line Fugue - 205.3s - rapid, energetic, dangerous
    - File: `midi/06 - Red Line Fugue.mid`
    - Role: A hard-running ostinato track with staggered string entries.
07. A Quiet Arithmetic of Light - 299.5s - luminous, emotional midpoint
    - File: `midi/07 - A Quiet Arithmetic of Light.mid`
    - Role: A five-minute center of gravity for piano, strings, and celesta.
08. Newsprint Ashes - 385.5s - tragic, low, fatalistic
    - File: `midi/08 - Newsprint Ashes.mid`
    - Role: A minor-key elegy with heavy basses and bleak high-string suspensions.
09. Borrowed Pulse - 232.7s - energetic, resilient, searching
    - File: `midi/09 - Borrowed Pulse.mid`
    - Role: The album reaccelerates with an insistent pulse and more open harmony.
10. The Hour Before Dawn - 338.1s - slow, suspended, fragile
    - File: `midi/10 - The Hour Before Dawn.mid`
    - Role: Near-still piano figures and long string lines before the final descent.
11. No Witness Choir - 348.3s - tragic, climactic, expansive
    - File: `midi/11 - No Witness Choir.mid`
    - Role: The broad tragic crest: thicker strings, tolling piano, and low percussion.
12. Last Train, Empty Platform - 349.5s - quiet, final, unresolved
    - File: `midi/12 - Last Train, Empty Platform.mid`
    - Role: A spare closing track that releases the album without fully resolving it.

Regenerate the album from this directory with:

```powershell
python3 build.py
```

Verify the generated MIDI files with:

```powershell
python3 build.py --verify
```
