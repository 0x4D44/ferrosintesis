Why this piece
The album opens by making apprehension feel physical: a hesitant piano pulse tests an empty city while distant low strings seem to wait for permission to move. The falling melody and late slowdown turn held breath into a restrained, unresolved exhale.

Listening guide
0:00 — Opening: sparse piano establishes D minor at 66 bpm; ensemble and low strings enter at 0:15, then cello at 0:29.
0:58 — The pulse edges up to 70 bpm as the texture begins to gather.
1:25 — Development: celesta enters at 1:26, violin at 1:39, and viola at 2:11; intensity rises without becoming a full chase.
2:20 — At 74 bpm the piece reaches its most alert state.
2:46 — Turn: piano, upper strings, low strings, and celesta hold the city in brighter suspension.
3:25 — Tempo drops to 62 bpm.
3:40 — Coda: piano, cello, ensemble, and low strings withdraw to the opening scale.

MIDI techniques
GM programs are acoustic piano, cello, viola, violin, string ensemble, contrabass, and celesta. Static CC7 volume, CC10 pan, and CC91 reverb place the ensemble. Every active lane receives per-bar CC11 expression shaped by the written intensity curve; piano CC64 sustain is re-authored each bar, longer in the sparse passages. Tempo changes are MIDI tempo-map events. There is no percussion, mod wheel, wah/filter automation, chorus/echo send, pitch bend, aftertouch, portamento, or mid-piece program change.
