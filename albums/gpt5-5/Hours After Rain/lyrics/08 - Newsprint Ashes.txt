Why this piece
Heavy basses and suspended high strings turn public tragedy into an elegy whose details are already becoming ash. The fatalism comes from weight below and unresolved light above, not from a dramatic percussion climax.

Listening guide
0:00 — Opening: piano establishes B-flat minor at 60 bpm; ensemble and heavy low strings enter at 0:16, cello at 0:32, and viola at 2:03.
1:36 — The elegy moves to 64 bpm.
2:09 — Development: celesta enters at 2:10 and violin at 2:21, sharpening the bleak suspension above piano and the string floor.
3:21 — At 68 bpm the texture reaches its broad tragic weight.
4:10 — Turn: piano, cello, viola, ensemble, basses, and celesta begin letting the page burn away.
4:59 — Tempo drops to 56 bpm.
5:51 — Coda: piano, cello, ensemble, low strings, and sparse celesta leave only the afterimage.

MIDI techniques
GM piano and string programs are joined by celesta; the contrabass lane is kept centred and in its playable register. Static CC7 volume, CC10 pan, and CC91 reverb establish depth. Per-bar CC11 shapes the elegy's climb and collapse, while piano CC64 sustains the slow harmonic smears. There is deliberately no percussion. No CC1, CC74, chorus/echo automation, pitch bend, aftertouch, portamento, or mid-piece program change is used.
