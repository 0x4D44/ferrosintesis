Why this piece
After the fatalism of Newsprint Ashes, the album borrows enough momentum to move again. An insistent pulse and more open Dorian harmony make resilience feel provisional but real: energy returned on loan rather than magically restored.

Listening guide
0:00 — Opening: piano restarts the album at 104 bpm; ensemble and low strings enter at 0:09, cello and drums at 0:18, then viola at 1:07.
1:04 — Momentum increases to 112 bpm.
1:21 — Development: celesta enters at 1:22 and violin at 1:30, joining the piano/string/percussion pulse.
2:13 — At 120 bpm the borrowed energy reaches its strongest, most open form.
2:37 — Turn: the full ensemble keeps searching as expression begins to release.
3:17 — Tempo eases to 108 bpm.
3:34 — Coda: piano, cello, ensemble, basses, and percussion return the borrowed motion.

MIDI techniques
GM piano, strings, contrabass, and celesta combine with written channel-10 percussion. Static CC7 volume, CC10 pan, and CC91 reverb place the ensemble. Per-bar CC11 automation follows the resilient intensity arc; piano CC64 sustain is shortened beneath the 12-note rising pulse. The propulsion is composed MIDI notes and tempo changes, not an arpeggiator. There is no CC1, CC74, chorus/echo automation, pitch bend, aftertouch, portamento, or mid-piece program change.
