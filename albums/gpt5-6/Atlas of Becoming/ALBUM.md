# Atlas of Becoming — album notes

This album treats the request as one connected atlas rather than fourteen unrelated
exercises. A compact rising cell, an open-fifth answer, and a short-long rhythmic
breath recur in altered forms. They are never literal quotations from the named
references.

## Track notes

1. **Tidal Geometry** — glass percussion, piano, and flute trace 7/8 arcs over a
   centred underwater bed. A suspended drop clears space for the final high dive.
2. **Wire and Wake** — high-wire tension becomes a fast guitar-and-percussion wake.
   Its 5/4 cable pattern fractures into eleven steps before the line releases.
3. **Blue Horizon Machine** — the AquaTheater triptych closes as a night-time
   pageant: precise spectacle, a sudden hush, then brass, choir, water, and light.
4. **Broadcasts from a Fractured Planet** — incompatible metres and instrumental
   groups interrupt one another. A common pulse emerges without pretending that
   conflict has vanished.
5. **One Cell, Many Skies** — one small musical organism mutates through monody,
   imitation, counterpoint, rhythmic specialization, orchestral ecology, and memory.
6. **Late for the Ordinary** — a bright piano-and-bass change of scene about the
   comic velocity of an ordinary morning. Meter detours keep the routine off balance.
7. **Carry the Lantern** — a continuous suite of short songs. Motifs survive abrupt
   seams, cross-pollinate, pass through a drum feature, and meet in a contrapuntal
   finale before a brief human-scale coda.
8. **Parameters in Bloom** — a symbolic history from GPT-1 to GPT-5.5. Context,
   polyphony, multimodality, reasoning space, and tool-like call-and-response enter
   as musical capabilities. The o-series appears as a separate reasoning counterline
   that joins the GPT-5 material. The endpoint follows OpenAI's current public model
   guide, which identifies GPT-5.5 as the flagship; GPT-5.6 remains a preview rather
   than a claimed released milestone.
9. **Black Glass Pursuit** — chromatic thirds, clipped guitar, vibraphone glints,
   brass stabs, string engines, and large percussion drive a spy-film chase.
10. **The Library at the End of Weather** — chamber counterpoint inside an impossible
    library widens until pages become birds and the storm enters the reading room.
11. **Clockwork Orchard** — imitative lines are colonized by clocks and mechanical
    percussion. Living rubato gradually breaks the machine's grid.
12. **Letters to a Future Ocean** — luminous messages pass among harp, piano, winds,
    strings, and choir. One storm tests whether the musical letter survives.
13. **Night Market in Thirteen** — percussion hockets and vendor-call counterpoint
    turn 13/8 into a dance, with a square 4/4 interruption that cannot stay square.
14. **Every Door Opens at Once** — the finale recombines the album's materials without
    simply replaying them. Glass, cell, clocks, odd metre, brass, and layered calls
    converge, then leave one quiet door open.

## Reference boundaries

The first three pieces respond to *The Fine Line* as Royal Caribbean describes it:
an immersive, 360-degree AquaTheater production built around high-wire, aerial, and
extreme-sport motion. The named Beatles and spy-film references contribute only
large-scale dramatic ideas: a jaunty change of scene, linked miniature-song flow,
motif recall, and fast chromatic pursuit. No source recording was transcribed.
