Why this piece
A bright piano-and-bass change of scene captures the comic velocity of an ordinary morning. Crooked metre turns stairs, street corners, queues, and traffic lights into playful interruptions without losing the tune.

Listening guide
0:00 — Shoelaces and Sunlight: upright piano and walking bass begin; busy clarinet, flute, strings, drums, and celesta clock ticks join.
0:29 — Three Flights Down: 3/4 piano and bass climb beneath clarinet, flute, oboe, drums, and corner trumpet.
0:49 — The Street Has Seven Corners: 7/8 adds a four-way woodwind/celesta hocket, strings, tom-led kit, trumpet, and trombone.
1:15 — Everybody Else Is Early: brisk 4/4 fills out the ensemble with trumpet, trombone, and shop-door horn.
1:41 — Queue in Five: quieter 5/4 leaves piano and bass beneath clarinet, oboe, celesta, and strings before drums return.
2:01 — Green Light Fugato: piano, bass, strings, and drums launch staggered woodwinds and answering brass.
2:26 — Tea Before the Clock Notices: 3/4 settles piano, bass, strings, woodwinds, celesta, drums, trumpet, and trombone.

MIDI techniques
Every channel has authored GM program and CC7/10/91/93/94 mix placement. Clarinet CC1 modulation/vibrato grows through the seven-corner detour, peaks in the fugato, then relaxes for tea. CC11 expression shapes piano, clarinet, flute, trumpet, and trombone from 1:15–2:48. There is no pitch bend, aftertouch, portamento, sustain automation, or mid-piece program change.
