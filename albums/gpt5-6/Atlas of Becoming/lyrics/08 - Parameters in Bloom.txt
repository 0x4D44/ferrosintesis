Why this piece
A symbolic GPT history lets one five-token seed accumulate context, instruction following, multimodality, reasoning gaps, tool calls, and finally joined orchestral voices. The written timeline ends at GPT-5.5; it does not claim GPT-5.6 as a released milestone.

MIDI techniques
Every channel has authored GM program and CC7/10/91/93/94 mix placement. Reasoning-cello CC11 rises from 3:33 through the joined finale. Eight principal channels share a final CC11 swell from 6:52, peaking near 7:29. Piano CC64 sustain runs from 7:13–7:46. There is no dynamic wah, autopan, pitch bend, mod-wheel lane, aftertouch, portamento, or mid-piece program change; most capabilities are demonstrated through added voices, denser notes, tempo, and metre.
