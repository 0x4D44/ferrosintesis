Why this piece
Chamber counterpoint inside an impossible library gradually admits rain, airborne pages, and a full storm. The opening subject survives and returns after the weather.

Listening guide
0:00 — The Catalogue Breathes: cello presents the subject; piano, viola/second violin, first violin, bassoon, and sparse cymbal enter.
0:45 — Rain Writes on the Windows: harp, cello, two violins, centred strings, and pointillist hi-hat/click rain.
1:25 — The Missing Folio: quiet choir and piano surround cello while bassoon, clarinet, and flute trade fragments.
2:19 — Pages Learn the Air: flute, clarinet, and harp hocket over cello and strings; drums join around 2:27.
2:54 — Storm in Every Aisle: driving strings, harp, cello, pads, full drums, horns, flute, and clarinet fill the stacks.
3:25 — Readers Hold the Roof: cello, strings, choir, drums, winds, and answering horns broaden the subject.
4:06 — Birds Carry the Index: flute/clarinet calls, harp, cello, quiet strings, and sparse ride cymbal.
4:37 — One Lamp After Weather: piano, cello, centred strings, viola, violin, and flute fade through incomplete statements.

MIDI techniques
Every channel has authored GM program and CC7/10/91/93/94 mix placement. Harp CC10 autopan moves from 2:19–2:54. Choir CC11 swells during The Missing Folio. Piano, strings, cello, flute, and choir receive a final expression fade from 4:37–5:01. No dynamic filter, echo, mod wheel, pitch bend, aftertouch, portamento, sustain automation, or mid-piece program change is authored; the storm is orchestration and percussion.
