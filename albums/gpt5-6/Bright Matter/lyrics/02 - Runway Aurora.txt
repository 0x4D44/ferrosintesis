BRIGHT MATTER — INSTRUMENTAL LISTENING GUIDE

0:00  I. COLD LAMPS
Marimba and celesta alternate left and right like runway edge lights.

0:15  II. EDGE LIGHTS
Muted guitar, bass, pad, and drums gather under the hocket.

0:45  III. IGNITION
Kicks arrive, brass flares answer across the field, and the runway hook climbs through the lead.

1:16  IV. FIRST LAUNCH
The first full drop: lamp hocket, distorted bends, high strings, harp, brass, and orchestra hits accelerate together.

2:09  V. CLOUD DECK
A half-time 6/8 suspension removes the engine. Guitar becomes a distant bent signal above choir and harp.

2:39  VI. RE-ENTRY BUILD
The runway reforms with denser lamps, earlier kicks, choir, rising fills, and three increasingly urgent guitar phrases.

3:25  VII. AURORA DROP
Tempo jumps to 138 bpm. The second launch is brighter, denser, and wider in transient detail without turning the sustained bed into stereo haze.

4:17  VIII. WHEELS DOWN
The engine releases into strings, celesta, one slow guitar statement, and a final orchestral landing light.
