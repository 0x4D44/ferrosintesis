BRIGHT MATTER — INSTRUMENTAL LISTENING GUIDE

0:00  I. WEIGHTLESS COUNT
Glass, pad, and a long gravity call float in 7/8 before the low field becomes audible.

0:24  II. FIRST VECTOR
A 7/8 arpeggiator, asymmetrical hats, bass, toms, and brass acquire momentum.

0:48  III. GRAVITY DROP
The meter snaps to 4/4. Choir enters behind the hook as the whole arrangement falls onto a four-square floor.

1:42  IV. ZERO-G CHOIR
Tempo halves and the pulse disappears. Two choirs open their vowels at different rates over strings and harp in 5/4.

2:17  V. MASSING
The 7/8 vector returns with twice the subdivision, upper harmony, brass, choir, and a long reverse-cymbal pull.

2:53  VI. GRAVITY RETURNS
A larger 4/4 drop adds full choir, faster harp, denser drums, and stronger brass/hit punctuation.

3:48  VII. CHORUS OF MASS
The cathedral organ finally enters. Its expression and tremulant rise continuously while every acoustic and electronic layer converges.

4:18  VIII. QUIET FIELD
Organ pedal, strings, and closing choir remain after the rhythmic mass has gone.
