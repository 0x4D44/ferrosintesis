Why this piece
The finale treats the first four tracks as reusable musical material, not just references in the notes. Their orbit, runway, gravity, and delayed-light hooks accumulate until all four sound together, then the complete stack rises from E to F-sharp major for the album's summit.

Listening guide

Every eight-beat window contains four simultaneous, identifiable hooks: number lead, runway guitar, gravity choir, and late-light harp. The steel-pan source continues circling around a centred full-band bed.

5:15  VIII. BRIGHT MATTER
Everything lifts from E to F-sharp major. Organ expression reaches full, guitar bends upward, fills occur every bar, and the lead crosses the album's highest summit.

5:29  IX. AFTERGLOW
Only strings, harp, a stretched number hook, organ pedal, and one final orchestral point remain.
