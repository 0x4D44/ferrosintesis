# VIGIL — a neo-classical song-cycle in twelve parts

*Music for the hours between. In the world of Max Richter, Philip Glass ("The Hours"),
and Howard Shore ("The Departed") — strings and piano, slow time, a repeating ground
that remembers.*

An emotional journey: from a held breath of grief, through fragile hope, restless
motion, descent, tragedy, anxiety, stillness, catharsis, bittersweet momentum, the
deepest elegy, healing, and finally a light that turns at last to major.

---

## The album's DNA (shared across all twelve tracks)

These threads make twelve separate pieces feel like one work:

- **The memory motif** — a falling three-note figure, scale-degrees **5–4–3** in the
  minor (e.g. A–G–F in D minor). It surfaces, transposed and re-coloured, in most
  tracks; tracks 1, 10 and 12 share it most directly.
- **The lament bass** — a stepwise *descending* bass line (the passacaglia / falling
  tetrachord 8–7–6–5… or 1–♭7–♭6–5). The oldest sign of sorrow in Western music; it
  underpins the slow tracks.
- **Suspensions that resolve downward** — the "sigh": hold a note through a chord
  change so it becomes a dissonance, then resolve it down by step. This, not
  chromaticism, is where the feeling lives.
- **D is home.** The cycle opens in D minor and closes in D (major, at last). Other
  tracks move through closely-related keys and return.
- **One palette.** Acoustic chamber forces only — Piano, Violin, Viola, Cello,
  Contrabass, and two string-ensemble pads (warm under-bed + main bed). General-MIDI
  programs: piano 0, violin 40, viola 41, cello 42, contrabass 43, strings 48 & 49.
- **One long breath per track.** Each piece is a single dynamic arc (an `Arc`):
  enter from near-silence, swell to one climax, recede. Strings *breathe* via a
  CC11 expression envelope; nothing sits at a dead level.

## The engine

All twelve are generated by `engine.py` (see `tracks/01_of_the_light_that_stays.py`
for a fully worked example). It provides voice-leading, a humanised event `Score`, the
`Arc`, and idiomatic blocks: `pad`, `bass`, `tied_line`, `arpeggiate`, `ostinato`,
`melody`, `piano_chords`, `expression`, `fade_out`, plus `write_midi` and `analyze`.

## The twelve tracks

| 01 | Of the Light That Stays | D aeolian | 60 | 5:00 | opening elegy — introspective, slow |

**Total ≈ 52 minutes.**

The arc of moods, in order: introspective → hope → rapid → dark → tragic → anxious →
still → cathartic → bittersweet → tragic → healing → resolution. Rapid (3, 6, 9),
slow (1, 4, 5, 8, 10, 12), energetic (3, 6), introspective (1, 2, 4, 7, 11),
tragic (5, 8, 10) — every requested colour, shaped into a single journey.

## Output

Each `tracks/NN_title.py` renders `midi/NN - Title.mid`. Regenerate the whole album
with `python3 build.py`. Play any `.mid` through an orchestral-strings library for the
intended sound; the GM defaults are a serviceable preview.
