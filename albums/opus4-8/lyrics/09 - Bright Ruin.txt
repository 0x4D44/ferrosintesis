Why this piece

Bright Ruin lets the cycle move forward without pretending that grief has vanished. D mixolydian supplies major-key momentum, but C natural removes the leading-tone certainty and the bass still carries a lament-shaped descent. The result is sunlight with an ache inside it: the memory motif can run now, though it cannot quite close.

Listening guide

MIDI techniques

This is type-1 MIDI at 480 ticks per beat and a constant 104 BPM. Seven fixed GM programs supply the album's full chamber palette. Piano chords, high eighth-note arpeggios, sustained tied strings, and a stepwise bass are separately sequenced, allowing texture and register to drive the arc. The Asus4-to-A-minor motion writes the downward suspension directly into the notes. CC11 shapes bowed channels; CC64 pedals the piano. The peak is an octave transposition, not pitch bend. No pan, effects sends, aftertouch, drums, tempo map, or mid-piece program change is authored.
