# ferrosintesis-samples-ccby

Embedded **CC-BY 4.0** instrument-onset samples for
[ferrosintesis](https://github.com/0x4D44/ferrosintesis): GM 4 electric piano (Rhodes tine)
and GM 15 hammered dulcimer, recorded by tim.kahn and iternetcone respectively and shared on
Freesound under CC BY 4.0.

Each sample is a mono 16-bit 44.1 kHz attack transient stored as FLAC; `ferrosintesis` crossfades it into the
modeled voice's sustain. Consumers normally access this crate through `ferrosintesis`, not
directly. **Attribution is required** — see `NOTICE`; full provenance (pack IDs) in
`PROVENANCE.md`. The GM 10 music box (moodyfingers, CC0) ships separately in `-orchestral2`.
