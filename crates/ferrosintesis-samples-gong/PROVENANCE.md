| `gong_*` | 2 |

Pitched gong-ageng bank for the GM14 CC0=2 alt-bank voice: 2 mono 16-bit
44.1 kHz WAVs (2.83 MiB aggregate), a soft + loud velocity pair. Generated by
the **local-file intake** of `tools/ferrosintesis-samples/prepare.py`
(`python3 tools/ferrosintesis-samples/prepare.py --local-only`) from the two
committed source WAVs under `tools/ferrosintesis-samples/gong-src/`. The WAVs
under `samples/` are **source, not build output** — they are committed and
embedded into the synth binary via `include_bytes!`.

Attribution obligations ship in `NOTICE` (this bank is **CC BY 3.0**, not CC0 —
see `../ferrosintesis` for how the credit flows to downstream users).

## Inventory

Gong ageng chosen for the lowest/clearest fundamental (matches *Bronze Water*'s
C2 colotomy anchor → least repitch stretch). Two velocity layers only — no
clean (non-NonCommercial) source offers deeper layering; the `GongOneShot`
voice selects exactly one recording: soft through velocity 83, loud at velocity
84 and above. This is a hard switch at velocity 84 because summing the two
different recordings would comb-filter.

| Output file | Source ID | Duration | Bytes |
|---|---|---|---|
| `gong_ageng_soft.wav` | 261890 | 13.640 s | 1,203,058 |
| `gong_ageng_loud.wav` | 261893 | 20.049 s | 1,768,340 |

Aggregate: 2,971,398 bytes (2 files). Pinned by the `EXPECTED_BYTES` constant
in `src/lib.rs` and the `inventory_matches_packaged_wavs` parity test.

## Processing (deterministic, `prepare.py` local intake)

Per file: `read_wav` decode; stereo→mono downmix (0.5/0.5); linear resample to
44.1 kHz (a no-op — the sources are already 44.1 kHz); leading pre-onset silence
trimmed (onset at 3% of peak, with an 8 ms lead-in pad); a lead-bounded 2 ms
fade-in de-clicks the start; a 0.30 s squared fade-out removes the
end-truncation click; peak-normalize to 0.9. **The full ring is kept** — unlike
the attack-transient bank there is NO short attack trim, because a gong's
multi-second bloom is the whole instrument. Output: 16-bit mono WAV. One-shots
like the drum sources — `measure_f0` is skipped.

## Regenerating

```powershell
python3 tools/ferrosintesis-samples/prepare.py --local-only
```

Pure stdlib. `--local-only` regenerates just this bank from the committed
`gong-src/` WAVs (it skips the fetched VSCO/FreePats full bank, so it needs no
network and does not touch the core/orchestral samples). A bare
`python3 tools/ferrosintesis-samples/prepare.py` regenerates everything,
including this bank.
