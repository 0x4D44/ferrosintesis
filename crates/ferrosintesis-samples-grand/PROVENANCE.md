# Provenance — ferrosintesis-samples-grand

Grand-piano attack/body bank for the **GM 0 Acoustic Grand** voice: 54 mono
16-bit 44.1 kHz WAVs, 9 pitch zones (C2–C6) × 3 dynamics (pp/mf/f) × 2 round
robins. Generated by the fetched-archive path of
`tools/ferrosintesis-samples/prepare.py` from the pinned Salamander Grand Piano
V3 tarball. The WAVs under `samples/` are **source, not build output** — they
are committed and embedded into the synth binary via `include_bytes!`.

Attribution obligations ship in `NOTICE` (this bank is **CC BY 3.0**, not CC0 —
see `../ferrosintesis` for how the credit flows to downstream users). It is kept
in its own crate so that `ferrosintesis-samples-core` stays under the crates.io
10 MiB publish cap.

## Selection

The source samples every minor third across 16 velocity layers (`<Note><Oct>v<NN>.wav`),
with no round robins. We take:

- **Zones** C2, F#2, C3, F#3, C4, F#4, C5, F#5, C6 — F# is the nearest sampled
  pitch to each G, mirroring the upright bank's ~C/G spacing (max repitch ±3 st).
- **Dynamics** pp → layer v2/v3, mf → v9/v10, f → v15/v16 (the first of each
  pair is round robin 1, the second round robin 2).
- **Round robins:** the source has none, so RR2 is the adjacent-higher velocity
  layer. Because every file is peak-normalized to 0.9, RR1 and RR2 land at the
  same level and differ only in the natural harder-strike timbre — real
  round-robin variation, defeating the machine-gun tell on repeated notes.
- The `rel*` (hammer-release) and `harm*` (string-resonance) layers are skipped.

## Inventory

Aggregate: 7,184,592 bytes (54 files). Pinned by the `EXPECTED_BYTES` constant
in `src/lib.rs` and the `inventory_matches_packaged_wavs` parity test.

## Processing (deterministic, `prepare.py` fetched-archive path)

Per file: `read_wav` decode; stereo→mono downmix (0.5/0.5); linear resample to
44.1 kHz (a no-op — already 44.1 kHz); `trim_to_onset` cuts to the onset (3% of
peak, 8 ms lead-in pad) and keeps **1.5 s** of body (KEEP_FAM `grand`) with a
lead-bounded 2 ms fade-in and a 0.6 s squared fade-out, peak-normalized to 0.9;
`measure_f0` records the autocorrelation root (the value pinned in the
`grand_*` zone tables in `crates/ferrosintesis/src/sampler.rs`). Output:
16-bit mono WAV.

## Regenerating

```powershell
python tools/ferrosintesis-samples/prepare.py
```

Pure stdlib (no 7z — the archive is `.tar.bz2`). A bare run regenerates the
whole bank including this one; the Salamander tarball is fetched once and cached
under the system temp dir, then SHA-256-verified before extraction.
