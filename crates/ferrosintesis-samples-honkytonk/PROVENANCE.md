# Provenance — ferrosintesis-samples-honkytonk

## Packaged family inventory

| Family | Files |
|--------|------:|
| `honkytonk_*` | 9 |

Attack/body bank for a **GM 0 Acoustic Grand alternate** (bank select CC0=8): 9 pitch
zones (C2–C6) × single velocity, mono 16-bit 44.1 kHz WAVs. Generated by
`tools/ferrosintesis-samples/prepare.py` (`_bake_honkytonk`) from the FreePats
honky-tonk player piano. The WAVs under `samples/` are **source, not build output**
— committed and embedded via `include_bytes!`.

**This bank is the DISTINCTIVE one** — a detuned, jangly, saloon/tack player-piano
attack that none of the properly-tuned grand/upright banks can produce. Its value
is character, not fidelity.

## Source

**FreePats Honky-tonk Piano** — a Francis Bacon player piano recorded by Piotr
Barcz (handheld Zoom H1n), sound bank by roberto@zenvoid.org.

- URL: <https://github.com/freepats/old-piano-FB/releases/download/2020-04-01/PianoFB-SFZ+FLAC-20200401.7z>
  (35,745,634-byte `.7z`; SHA-256 pinned in `prepare.py`; extracted with `7z`, as the
  Spanish-guitar / bagpipe archives).
- Format: per-note FLAC (`samples/<Note>.flac`), single velocity, no round robin.
- License: **CC0 1.0** (public domain) —
  <https://freepats.zenvoid.org/Piano/honky-tonk-piano.html>. No attribution required;
  `NOTICE` is a courtesy credit only.

## Selection

Single-velocity multisample, 9 zones C2–C6. The source omits F#2 and F#4 (the SFZ
pitch-shifts them), so **F2 and F4 stand in** at those positions: C2, F2, C3, F#3,
C4, F4, C5, F#5, C6. Roots are MEASURED (the detuned unisons read ~15 cents off);
the LA layer repitches by the measured root.

## Processing (deterministic)

Per note: `7z`-extract the FLAC; ffmpeg → 16-bit PCM; `read_wav` (→ mono); resample
to 44.1 kHz if needed; `trim_to_onset` keeps the onset + **1.5 s** of body (0.6 s
fade-out), peak-normalized to 0.9; `measure_f0` (tight window around the nominal)
records the root pinned in `honkytonk_*` in `crates/ferrosintesis/src/sampler.rs`.
Output: 16-bit mono WAV.

## Regenerating

```
python tools/ferrosintesis-samples/prepare.py --only=honkytonk
```

Needs `7z` (archive) and `ffmpeg` (FLAC decode) on PATH.
