# Provenance — ferrosintesis-samples-ydp-grand

## Packaged family inventory

| `ydpgrand_*` | 9 |

Grand-piano attack/body bank for a **GM 0 Acoustic Grand alternate** (bank select CC0=7):
9 pitch zones (C2–C6, C/F# minor thirds) × single velocity, mono 16-bit 44.1 kHz
WAVs. Generated by `tools/ferrosintesis-samples/prepare.py` (`_bake_ydp_grand`)
from the FreePats YDP Grand SoundFont. The WAVs under `samples/` are **source, not
build output** — committed and embedded via `include_bytes!`.

**This bank is the BRIGHTER grand** in the audition — a Yamaha Disklavier Pro with
a harder, more present hammer strike than the distant Salamander C5 capture.

## Source

**FreePats YDP Grand Piano** (roberto@zenvoid.org, from the Zenph Studios / OLPC
Yamaha Disklavier Pro reperformance). A modern Yamaha grand, close-mic'd via a
robotic reperformance — clean and present.

- URL: <https://freepats.zenvoid.org/Piano/YDP-GrandPiano/YDP-GrandPiano-SF2-20160804.tar.bz2>
  (36,737,684-byte `.tar.bz2` holding one SF2; SHA-256 pinned in `prepare.py`).
- Format: **SF2 with raw 16-bit PCM** samples (not SF3/Ogg). One preset "Grand
  Piano" (program 0) with 5 velocity-layer instruments; we extract the **middle
  layer** ("piano layer 3") as a single-velocity multisample.
- License: **CC BY 3.0** —
  <https://freepats.zenvoid.org/Piano/acoustic-grand-piano.html>. Attribution
  (Zenph/OLPC/FreePats) ships in `NOTICE`.

## Selection & the single-velocity simplification

The preset is velocity-layered (5 layers); to keep the extraction tractable this
bank takes the **middle layer only** (a mf capture), one sample per chosen root.
Dynamics in the render come from the LA blend gain + the modeled voice, not from
the sample layer. Zones: MIDI 36/42/48/54/60/66/72/78/84 (C2, F#2 … C6), each
present in layer 3's minor-third sampling.

## Processing (deterministic)

Per zone: slice the SF2 `smpl` chunk by the `shdr` FRAME offsets (× 2 bytes, raw
16-bit PCM — no ffmpeg); `trim_to_onset` keeps the onset + **1.5 s** of body (0.6 s
fade-out), peak-normalized to 0.9; `measure_f0` (tight window around the MIDI root)
records the root pinned in `ydpgrand_*` in `crates/ferrosintesis/src/sampler.rs`.
Output: 16-bit mono WAV.

## Regenerating

```
python tools/ferrosintesis-samples/prepare.py --only=ydpgrand
```

Pure stdlib (raw-PCM SF2 — no ffmpeg).
