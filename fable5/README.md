## The Ninth Bell

An original single-track orchestral piece (5:46) in the **Gabriel Knight:
Sins of the Fathers** title-music idiom (Robert Holmes' dark-cathedral
gothic drama). It grows out of the string-chord gesture from
`demos/orchestral_demo.py` ch0: the piece opens with those voice-led
Am–F–C–G chords **verbatim**, then compresses one dramatic spring twice —
a build into a hit on the wrong chord (a fortissimo E-major slam where
A minor was promised), a drop into an eerie music-box void 35 dB below,
and a larger rebuild into the true climax on a hardened harmonic-minor
ground, closing with a second betrayal (a Neapolitan B-flat that steals
the theme's last note). One 13-note theme carries everything — cello
lament, music-box ghost, fortissimo peal — and its collapsing-staircase
countersubject is machine-verified consonant over both grounds. Eight
tubular-bell tolls frame the sections; the **ninth**, a lone tonic A, is
the resolution the theme withholds for six minutes and the piece's final
note.

- File: `The Ninth Bell/midi/01 - The Ninth Bell.mid` (5:46)
- Eight movements with MIDI markers; timed map in
  `The Ninth Bell/album_manifest.json`
- Docs: `The Ninth Bell/README.md` and `The Ninth Bell/ALBUM.md`;
  design in `wrk_docs/2026.07.07 - HLD - The Ninth Bell.md`
- Regenerate/verify: `python build.py` / `python build.py --verify`
  (thirteen oracles, written before the music — including the intro
  fidelity check against the demo gesture and the build/drop dynamic-arc
  contour)
- Listen via [hollowsynth](hollowsynth/README.md) **v0.8** or the
  committed `The Ninth Bell/audio/01 - The Ninth Bell.opus`

This is original material using Gabriel-Knight-vocabulary gestures; it
quotes no existing piece.
