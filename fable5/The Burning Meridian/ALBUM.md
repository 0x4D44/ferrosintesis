# The Burning Meridian — track notes

Three tracks, 9:37 total.

## 01 — The Muster (3:14, D aeolian, 132, 12/8)

Embers (0:00): pedal D, tolling low piano, closed-mouth choir, a
timpani crescendo.  The Ostinato (0:14): the 12/8 low-string engine,
taiko drums layering in three stages, horn fragments answering, a
cello line climbing every eight bars.  The Call (1:20): the horn theme
three times — organ + saw section, then + choir at full vowel, then +
the snapped descant on fiddle, flute, choir II and glockenspiel —
over full percussion; apotheosis and cut.  Over the Hill (2:41): harp
and solo fiddle take the theme apart; one last tutti hit rings out.

## 02 — Lanterns on the Water (3:19, A aeolian, 88, 3/4)

Lanterns (0:00): harp arpeggios, strings gathering, the fiddle lights
the first phrase.  Duet (0:24): the verified fiddle/flute elegy —
twice, then with the voices swapped, then with the cello beneath —
over harp and pedalled piano pools.  Swell (1:30): the orchestra takes
both voices; bell, timpani rolls, the peak with the fiddle an octave
above.  Ashfall (2:27): the lanterns go out one by one; rit.

## 03 — Meridian (3:03, D aeolian → D major, 138, 5/4)

War Footing (0:00): the 5/4 ostinato (3+2), taiko, unison stabs.
Cavalry (0:26): the theme from track 1 stretched onto the odd meter,
three statements, descending cello runs.  The Break (1:09): 4/4
half-time — the elegy remembered on fiddle and flute over harp, cello
portamento sighs.  Charge (1:26): four stacked waves (theme, + choir,
+ descant + glockenspiel, + strings and bell), a near-silent
held-breath bar, and a two-semitone horn fall into the grand pause.
Daybreak (2:27): the same theme in **D major** over pealing bells and
a tonic pedal; the last chord rung out by everyone.
