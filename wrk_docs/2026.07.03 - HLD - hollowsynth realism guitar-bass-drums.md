## 1. Scope & non-goals

### 1.1 Goal
Make **guitars, bass and drums** read as recorded instruments — close the
high-impact findings coherently, **evolving** hollowsynth, not rewriting it.

### 1.2 Success
The acceptance oracles in §5 pass (each fails on current `main`, passes after),
**all pre-existing tests still pass**, the deterministic seeded render stays
bit-reproducible, and per-family mix balance holds within ±2.5 dB of a committed
golden baseline. "Sounds better" is never the definition of done — a measured
centroid / band-energy / decay / pitch / choke / correlation delta is.

### 1.3 In scope
Rebuild the guitar/bass/drum **voice** models and the drum engine; extend shared
DSP (`dsp.rs`) and the engine signal path **only where a finding demands it** — a
guitar cabinet insert, a drum-room bus, a guitar sympathetic bus, per-piece drum
panning, and one new synth-bass voice.

### 1.4 Non-goals (deliberately NOT built — with the reason, per simplest-viable)
- **No rewrite** of the render loop, bus/FX chain, `reverb.rs`, `midi.rs`,
  `wav.rs`, or the `sampler.rs` LA mechanism — all *reused*. **One narrow,
  acknowledged exception** (V2, per CORR-2/OVER-4): the ch-9 drum render path is
  restructured so drum voices pan per-piece into the stereo mix (§3.4) — the
  melodic-channel path is untouched. `reverb.rs` gains **one** contained parameter
  (predelay, per CORR-3); its topology is otherwise unchanged.
- **No external crates** (std only). **No new env vars, no feature flags** — the
  existing `samples: bool` and the v0.6 `solo: u16` channel mask (V4: §1.4 predated
  v0.6; `--solo` now exists and the stem-level oracles use it) are the only knobs.
- **No migration / rollback / back-compat** — sandbox build.
- **+0 MB by default.** No `include_bytes!` additions in the main phases. Guitar/
  bass/drum onsets are impulsive transients a *seeded synth models better* than a
  fixed PCM one-shot (velocity-continuous, deterministic, no repitch). A bounded
  **≤ 2 MB** drum one-shot escalation is pre-designed (§6) but gated behind the
  existing `samples` flag and fired **only if** oracle **22** (kick beater — V4:
  §1.4/§6 previously miscited oracle 25, which is the hat tick) cannot be met
  synthetically.
- **Deferred as fragile / low-payoff** (residual risks §6, not in the main
  phases): high-gain *feedback bloom* (GTR-7#2, impact 2), unconditional *4×
  oversampling* (GTR-3#2 — the steep cabinet LP already kills the fizz), and
  *micro-timing humanisation* (see §3.4 — a real realism lever but arguably a
  composition-side concern; flagged for the review loop to rule in or out).

The design is **six abstraction *families***; two of them (D the KS core, E ambience
& imaging) each bundle a few concrete mechanisms, so the honest count of distinct
new pieces is **~11** (V3, per OVER-1). Name the six families and you hold its
*shape*; §3/§4 enumerate every mechanism.

### 1.5 Compatibility waiver & versioning (V4 — resolves the 2026.07.07 opt-in doctrine)

The repo lesson of 2026.07.07 (postdating this design's sign-off) says new synth
features must stay **opt-in** ("authored channel" pattern), proven by rendering all
prior albums byte-identical. This design *deliberately* changes the default sound of
existing GM programs — guitars, basses, all of ch 9 — that is its entire purpose, so
the byte-identity proof cannot and should not hold. Resolution, explicit rather than
silent:

- **This is a sanctioned, versioned RE-VOICING, not an opt-in feature.** The opt-in
  lesson governs *controller features* (CC-driven behaviours a file authors); it does
  not freeze voicing quality. This task is the one deliberate exception, made under
  Arthur's 2026-07-07 build instruction.
- **The byte-identity proof is replaced by oracle 34** (golden per-channel RMS +
  spectral-balance guard, ±2.5 dB against a pre-work v0.7 baseline binary) plus the
  full 29-test v0.7 regression suite (oracle 37, §5.3). A compatibility flag is
  rejected — §1.4 forbids new flags and a per-channel "old velocity law" cannot be
  expressed in the authored-channel pattern anyway.
- **Version target: one bump, 0.7.0 → 0.8.0** (Cargo.toml + Cargo.lock in lockstep,
  in the finalize commit). §7's "blocks the merge" means *blocks advancing to the
  next phase on the one task branch* — phases do NOT integrate separately; the task
  integrates once.
- **Historical sound stays reproducible** via the committed tagged renders and the
  per-album version pins in `fable5/README.md` ("Listen via hollowsynth v0.6/v0.7"):
  an old album at bit-fidelity is re-rendered from its pinned version's commit. At
  integration, Arthur decides whether the committed `.opus` renders are refreshed to
  v0.8 (re-run `render_opus.py`) or kept at their pins; the README pins update in the
  same integration either way.

## 2. Design overview

Six abstraction families carry ~90% of the work; the rest is table/preset edits.
(E = three signal-path additions — drum room + guitar sympathetic + per-piece pan;
D = five KS upgrades — K1-K4 + G6 — so ~11 distinct new mechanisms total.)

**A. The velocity→timbre law** — one formula applied at every plucked- and
drum-voice construction. Today velocity moves only amplitude — the single biggest
"sampled-at-one-dynamic" tell. `vn = vel/127` opens the excitation lowpass and
loop-damping cutoff for `Pluck`, and scales membrane start-pitch, glide, click
energy and decay for `Drum`. Closes **KS-2, GTR-1#2, BASS-2, DRM-2**. Highest
impact ÷ risk in the design — ~a dozen lines, no new state, tuning-safe.

**B. `Burst` — a parallel one-shot transient** (new small `dsp.rs` primitive):
a filtered, exponentially-decaying noise burst summed **in parallel** to a voice's
output, **never into a feedback loop**. It is **one struct with per-use config**
(filter, gain, t60, and an insertion point — pre-body for the pick click so it
knocks the body, post-out for the finger-noise/slap/thump so the out-LP doesn't
swallow it), not a zero-config primitive (V2 honesty, per OVER-2/FIDE-7): its four
Pluck uses (pick attack GTR-2, finger/fret BASS-7, slap/pop BASS-1, release thump
BASS-6) and the palm chuff (GTR-5) each pass a different filter and insertion point.
The `stop`-thump lowpass is a `Biquad::lowpass(250)` inside the same `Burst`
(consistent filter type). Its drum cousin is the extended `NoiseBand` (delayed
`onset` + `swell`) for snare wire-engage (DRM-5) and crash bloom (CYM-2) — a
separate, already-present type, not the same struct.

**C. The Cabinet** — replaces the lone 3.4 kHz lowpass in `Drive` with a 5-biquad
IIR speaker cab (low resonance + presence peak + ~24 dB/oct cliff), preceded by a
program-keyed pre-voicing peak and a DC-bias asymmetric clip. The clean-guitar
preset gets its own light cab. Closes **GTR-1, GTR-4, GTR-2#2, GTR-6** and, for
free, the fizz of GTR-3#2 (the steep cab LP *is* the decimation filter).

**D. The KS physics core** — shared `KsLoop`/`Pluck` upgrades: cubic-Lagrange
fractional tap (KS-5), triangle-displacement initial condition (KS-1),
energy-conserving polarization coupling (KS-4), release darkening (GTR-7), and
register-split wound-ness / optional dispersion allpass (KS-3, GTR-5#2). These are
the *risky* ones (loop stability at E1 = 41 Hz, retune clicks, re-levelling) so
they land **last**, each behind its own oracle.

**E. Ambience & imaging** — two reverb-style buses reusing tested `Reverb`/comb
code: a **drum room** (short `Reverb`, ch 9 only, pre-hall; DRM-7) and a **guitar
sympathetic** comb bank (open-string tuning, acoustic guitars only; GTR-3/KS-6) —
plus **per-piece drum panning** so the kit images across the stereo field instead
of as a mono point source (§3.4, the shared blind spot).

**F. `SynthBass`** — one genuinely new voice (saw + envelope-swept resonant LP)
for GM 38/39, which are wrongly modelled as acoustic plucked strings today
(BASS-4).

### 2.1 Signal path (edits marked ►)
```
NoteOn ─┬─ ch9  → drums::make(key,vel)          ► choke() live hats first (CYM-4)
        │                                        ► per-piece pan offset (§3.4)
        └─ mel  → voices::make(prog,key,vel,samples)
                     ► velocity→timbre law (A)
                     ► Burst onsets summed parallel (B)

per voice ─► ch_buf[ch]                    (V4: ch-9 voices render to held buffers
                                            instead — see the dedicated pass below)
  ch_buf ─► [Drive insert (prog 29|30): pre-voice → bias-tanh → CABINET (C)]
         ─► strip gain · expr · pan · Haas          (melodic channels, unchanged)
            ├─► mix_l / mix_r
            ├─► send_rev (CC91, hp'd)                → hall Reverb
            ├─► send_cho / send_del                  → Chorus / PingPong
            ├─► send_sym  (piano)                    → Sympathetic
            └─► send_sym_gtr (prog 24|25)   ►NEW     → GuitarSymp (GTR-3/KS-6)

  ►NEW dedicated ch-9 pass (V4, after the mix/send clear — replaces the strip for
  drums; full spec in §3.4 D9): per-piece equal-power pan (LEVEL-ONLY, **no Haas**)
  × strip volume·expr·at_gain × wah (CC74/71, stereo pair)
            ├─► mix_l / mix_r
            ├─► send_rev (CC91, hp'd, default 0.3)   → hall Reverb (kept!)
            └─► send_room (no hp)                    → drum_room Reverb (DRM-7, pre-hall)
  mix_l/mix_r ─► BusGlue ─► out
```
Nothing in the reverb/chorus/echo/glue math changes; new buses sum into `mix_*`
exactly like the existing ones. `Reverb` is already stereo (per-side early taps,
`combs_l`/`combs_r`), so the drum room returns a stereo room, not a mono blob.

### 2.2 Determinism invariant (V4 — rewritten; the old text misdescribed the RNG model)
There is **no shared cross-note RNG stream** in hollowsynth: each voice owns an
independent `Rng` seeded from the spawn counter (engine.rs `0x9E37 ^
voices_spawned·2654435761`), so determinism is **per-binary, not per-draw-count**.
Two same-seed renders of the same binary are bit-identical regardless of how many
draws a voice makes (oracle 35). Adding `Burst`/`edge` draws shifts only that
voice's *own* jitter values; old-binary vs new-binary jitter equality is neither
promised nor needed — the timbre deliberately changes (§1.5). The two real rules:
(1) **no phase may change how many voices spawn for a given MIDI stream** (D6's
choke mutates live voices, never spawns; drums::make's `_` fallback already spawns
for every ch-9 key, so D7's new china/splash mappings don't shift the counter);
(2) all new noise comes from the voice's own `rng` — never a new global stream.
(The lessons_learnt "one seeded RNG stream" entry is about the Python composition
engines, not this synth.) The old "exactly one `rng.white()` in the bright line"
constraint is dropped — it bought nothing.

## 3. Per-family design

Presets gain fields, all defaulted to 0/none on families that don't use them:
`click, click_hp` (onset hardness), `attack_noise` (finger/fret), `stop_thump`
(release), `cab_lp` (clean-cab rolloff, 0 = none).

### 3.1 GUITAR

**G1 — Cabinet + amp voicing** (GTR-1, GTR-4, GTR-2#2; closes GTR-3#2).
`engine.rs::Drive` gains `voice: Biquad`, `cab: [Biquad;5]`, `bias: f32`,
`dc: f32` — all built at `sr*2.0` (existing 2× path):
- **Pre-voicing** (before gain/tanh): prog 30 `peak(650,0.9,-5)` (scooped chug);
  prog 29 `peak(800,0.8,+4)` (mid-push lead).
- **Asymmetry** (even harmonics): `bias = if prog==30 {0.55} else {0.4}`; shape
  `(pre·voice·gain + bias).tanh()`. **The `- dc` constant subtract is removed in V3**
  (it only zeroed silence, not the signal-dependent DC) — the DC blocker below does
  the real work. See the next bullet.
- **DC blocker** (V2, per CORR-1/TEST-8/DEVI-3): the constant `- dc` subtract only
  zeroes *silence*; an asymmetric tanh under a driven note produces large
  signal-dependent DC (worked example prog 29 ≈ −0.21) that the cabinet's
  unity-at-DC biquads cannot remove and that ramps with the amp envelope (a thump).
  Insert a real DC blocker **after the shaper, before the cabinet**:
  `Biquad::highpass(20.0, 0.7, sr*2.0)` per sub-sample. `bias` stays (for the
  even-harmonic asymmetry); the `- dc` subtract is dropped. This makes oracle 4
  (DC < 1e-3 **and** measurable 2nd-harmonic) satisfiable — they were mutually
  exclusive in V1.
- **Cabinet** (post-tanh, per sub-sample): `peak(100,1.2,+4)`, `peak(500,1.0,-3)`,
  `peak(2600,1.5,+5)`, `lowpass(4800,1.2)` → `lowpass(5200,0.7)`. The two cascaded
  LPs give the ~24 dB/oct cliff and **replace** the box-average decimator (take
  the cab-filtered sub-samples at base rate → tanh fizz killed, GTR-3#2 needs no
  4×). Trim `post` ≈ −2.5 dB to offset the boosts (prog 30 → ~0.22, prog 29 →
  ~0.32); pinned by oracle 34.
- **Routing:** `Drive` insert stays on **29|30 only**. Prog 28 (muted) and 31
  (harmonics) do **not** get channel distortion (fixes the double-distortion /
  clean-funk hazard) — their grit is contained in the preset (G4, G7).

**G2 — Clean-amp cab** (GTR-6). `CLEAN` preset: `body = [(200,1.0,+2),(2500,1.0,+3)]`
(feeds the existing per-voice body chain). Add `cab_lp: Option<OnePole>`—no, a
`Biquad::lowpass(4500,0.9,sr)` for CLEAN only (do **not** touch the shared
`out_lp` that BASS/MUTED depend on), processed right after the body chain. Static,
no clicks.

**G3 — Velocity→timbre** (KS-2, GTR-1#2 — the law, guitar half). In `Pluck::new`:
`vn = vel/127`; `bright = (p.bright*(0.65+0.55*vn)*(1.0+0.08*rng.white())).min(sr*0.45)`
(exactly one `rng.white()` preserves the round-robin sequence);
`pick_lp = (p.pick_lp*(0.5+0.9*vn)).max(400.0)` feeds the excitation `OnePole`.
`bright` flows unchanged into both `KsLoop::new` calls, so damper and `delay_for`
stay tuning-consistent.

**G4 — Pick attack & palm chuff** (GTR-2, GTR-5). `Pluck` gains `onset: Option<Burst>`.
Preset `click`/`click_hp`: STEEL/CLEAN ≈1.0 @1500 Hz, DRIVE 1.2, NYLON 0.5 @1000 Hz,
BASS/FRETLESS use the finger-noise variant, others 0. Triggered at level
`click·vel_amp(vel)` in `new`, summed into `y` **before** the body EQ so the click
knocks the body. MUTED (GTR-5): per-voice soft-clip `y=(y*2).tanh()/2` gated to the
preset; enrich `sub` with 2f/3f partials; shorten the sub ramp 220→~90 samples;
add a short chuff `Burst`. No channel distortion.

**G5 — Guitar sympathetic bus** (GTR-3, KS-6; V2 per OVER-6: reuse, don't copy).
**Parameterize the existing `Sympathetic`** struct (comb-frequency list, damp
cutoff, feedback, return gain, hp corner) rather than copy-pasting a second near-
identical struct. Instantiate a guitar variant: 6 combs at E2 A2 D3 G3 B3 E4
(82.41…329.63 Hz), in-loop `OnePole::lowpass(3400)`, feedback 0.85,
`hp=highpass(120,0.7)`, return 0.30, denormal flush `<1e-20`. Fed **only** by prog
24|25 at send 0.03 (`send_sym_gtr`). Restricted to true acoustics so driven
electrics don't smear. (The piano `Sympathetic` becomes one instantiation of the
generalized type.)

**G6 — Release darkening** (GTR-7; V2 per DEVI-5: no existing control-rate gate).
`Pluck::render` is per-sample and has **no** control-rate tick today, so add one:
`if self.t % CTRL == 0 { … }` (the `CTRL=16` cadence the SawStack/Wind/Bowed voices
already use). `KsLoop::set_bright(bright,f)` sets the damper cutoff + `retune(f)`
(retune recomputes the damper-phase-compensated delay, pitch holds). **(V4, INT-3)**
The control tick must pass the **composed current frequency** —
`set_bright(new_bright, self.base_f * self.bend)` (vert loop at ×1.0013) — because
the v0.6/v0.7 engine keeps calling `set_pitch` on *released* voices (CC1 vibrato,
portamento glide, RPN recompose); a nominal-`base_f` retune would snap a bent or
gliding release back to the unmodulated pitch and fight the engine writer every
block. `set_bright` stores the new cutoff into `KsLoop::bright` so those subsequent
engine-driven `retune` calls recompute the delay with the darkened damper's phase
compensation. `Pluck` keeps
`rel_bright_h/v`; while released, on each control tick glide each toward a floor
`min(current, ~800/920)` — already-dark presets unaffected. The existing per-glide
smoothing keeps it click-free.

**G7 — Prog 31 harmonics** (GTR-6#2; V3 mechanism corrected per CORR-5). **In scope,
in the main phases.** *V1/V2 were wrong:* a feedforward pick-position comb at
`pos=1/2` nulls the **even** harmonics and *boosts* the fundamental (|1+0.9|≈1.9) —
it cannot null the fundamental, and the KS loop tuned to `sr/f` regenerates it
regardless. **V3 mechanism:** synthesize the flageolet by **retuning the KS delay
loop to the touched-harmonic pitch** — resonate at `2f` (octave) or `3f`
(octave+fifth) so the loop period is `sr/(2f)` or `sr/(3f)` — with a thin,
high-passed excitation and a shorter glassy ~2 s t60, light grit, no heavy amp. The
pick comb may still shape upper-partial balance but is **not** described as removing
the fundamental. **(V4, INT-2)** The harmonic multiple is **persistent voice state
composed into every retune**: prog 31 sits in the engine's vibrato family, so CC1
vibrato, RPN recompose, aftertouch and portamento glide all call `set_pitch` per
block — `apply_pitch` must compute `f = base_f * harm * bend` (and `legato_to` must
preserve `harm`), otherwise the first modulator collapses the flageolet back to the
fundamental mid-note. Pin which harmonic per register (V4: **2f below E4, 3f from E4
up** — natural-harmonic playability) so oracle 40 knows its target. New oracle (see
§5.2/§5.3): fundamental suppression + a positive 2f/3f peak clause + a
modulator-active clause. Not deferred; kept in oracle 36's routing set.

### 3.2 BASS

**B1 — Velocity→timbre** (BASS-2): same G3 formula; verify `pluck_pitch_a4`
tolerance at vel 30 and 120 (fundamental-only measurement → safe).

**B2 — Articulation split** (BASS-1, BASS-3, BASS-7). New presets in `make()`:
- `SLAP` (36/37): `pick_lp≈4500, bright≈3500, out_lp≈4000, pos≈0.12`, `click` high
  → thumb slap + near-bridge pop via `Burst` (highpass 1500, ~3 ms, linear-to-zero
  → no tail click), **parallel** to the string.
- `PICK` (34): `pick_lp≈2600, bright≈2200, pos≈0.15, out_lp≈2400` so the plectrum
  click survives.
- `UPRIGHT` (32): `t60≈2.4`, second body peak `(110,1.0,+3)`, woody attack noise.
- Fingered 33 stays on `BASS`. **Finger/fret noise** (BASS-7): preset
  `attack_noise` (FRETLESS 0.12, BASS 0.08, else 0) drives the `onset` `Burst` as a
  `bandpass(2000,0.8)` layer, gain `(attack_noise·(0.45+0.55·v)).min(0.5·v)` so soft
  fretless still exposes finger noise without it dominating.

**B3 — Stop thump** (BASS-6). Preset `stop_thump` (BASS/FRETLESS 0.15, else 0).
`Pluck` gets `stop: Option<Burst>` = `Biquad::lowpass(250, 0.7, sr)` low thud (V3
per FIDE-7 — matches the `Burst` struct's `Biquad`, not a `OnePole`), ~60 ms t60,
armed by `note_off`, summed into `y` (rides amp/release, keeps the voice alive to
play out, then dies). Fires only on explicit `note_off`, not natural end. **(V4,
INT-7)** "note_off" means the **`Voice::note_off` call** — under v0.6 CC64 sustain /
v0.7 CC66 sostenuto that call is deferred to the (block-quantised) pedal lift, so
all pedal-held bass notes thump together at the lift. That is *intended* (physically
the damp lands when the string is stopped), and oracle 10 measures from the call,
not the MIDI NoteOff event.

**B4 — Synth bass** (BASS-4). New `voices::SynthBass` (NOT SawStack — no
amp-env-driven filter path): one `BlepSaw` (38); two detuned ±6–10 ¢ (39); optional
sub `Sine`; amp `Adsr` (fast A, mid D/S, short R); resonant `Biquad::lowpass(base,q)`
q≈3–6 whose cutoff is driven at control rate by a **separate filter `Adsr`**:
`cut = 300 + fenv·depth` (depth scales with velocity), `cut.clamp(80, sr*0.4)` via
`retune_lowpass` (state-preserving). No body EQ, no pickup comb, no tanh; own gain
calibration. **(V4, INT-5)** Programs 38/39 sit in the engine's vibrato/aftertouch
families and today route to `Pluck`, which bends — so `SynthBass` **must implement
`set_pitch(mult)`** (retune both saws with their detune spread and the sub, at
control rate; `BlepSaw::set_freq`/`Sine::set_freq` are click-free) or bend, CC1
vibrato, portamento and RPN fine-tune silently die on synth bass. `legato_to`
returns false (a fresh voice re-attacks — synth basses retrigger; stated, not
accidental). Oracle 13 gains a pitch clause (§5.3). **(V4, INT-6)** The v0.7 strip
wah (CC71 Q ≤ 8) can stack ~+30 dB with this voice's own resonance on an authored
channel; accepted as authored-opt-in — oracle 34 is the trip-wire (§6 row updated).

**B5 — Sub enrichment** (BASS-5). In `Pluck::new` randomise sub start phase
(`rng.white()*π`). In render, replace the pure add with a DC-free even-harmonic
shaper: `y += (s + 0.3*(s*s - 0.5)) * gain * sub_env` (`s²−0.5 = −½cos2x`, a clean
2nd harmonic, no DC). Keep the `t60*0.8` decay (no feedback coupling).

### 3.3 DRUMS

**D1 — Velocity→timbre** (DRM-2). In `make()`'s `d` closure, transform tables before
`Drum::new`: membrane start-freq `×(1+0.03·vn)`; glide `×(0.6+0.8·vn)`; decays

### 3.4 Kit imaging — the shared blind spot (all three drafts missed)

Even with every voice upgraded, the kit still images as a **mono point source**:
ch 9 sums into a single per-channel pan (default centre), and today drums have no
per-piece placement. A real recorded kit is spread — hats/ride to one side, toms
sweeping across, kick/snare centre, overheads/room wide. Mono placement is itself a
strong "programmed, not recorded" tell.

**D9 — Per-piece drum pan** (new; V2 restructured per CORR-2/OVER-4/CORR-4). A
static `drum_pan(key) -> f32` table in `[0,1]` pan-space (0 = hard L, 0.5 = centre,
1 = hard R, drummer's perspective): kick/snare 0.5; hats 42/44/46 ≈ 0.33; toms
sweep 41→0.55, 43/45→0.62, 47/48→0.42, 50→0.32; ride 51/59 ≈ 0.70; crash 49 ≈ 0.25,
57 ≈ 0.75.
*Why V1's spec was unimplementable:* ch-9 voices sum into one mono `ch_buf[9]`
**before** the single strip pan, so no per-voice datum survives to the mix step.
**V3 fix (correct loop location, per the external review's one blocking item):** the
ch-9 special-case must run in a **dedicated pass placed AFTER `mix_l/mix_r` and the
send buffers are cleared**, not in the render pass (whose output would be wiped by
that clear). **(V4 line anchors, v0.7:** the `retain_mut` render pass is
engine.rs:920-928, the six-buffer clear — `mix_l/mix_r/send_rev/send_cho/send_del/
send_sym` — is 931-936, the strip loop 937-985; the CC1 (801-866) and v0.7
controller (871-913) per-block loops both already skip ch 9 and are unaffected.
Anchor structurally — "the `retain_mut` voice pass", "after the `fill(0.0)` block" —
not by line number.**)** Concretely: in the `retain_mut` render pass, drum voices
(ch 9) render into `scratch` and are held (not summed into `ch_buf[9]`); then, after
the mix/send buffers are zeroed, a ch-9 pass equal-power pans each held drum buffer
by `drum_pan(a.key)` into `mix_l/mix_r` and adds a mono tap to `send_room`. Simplest
implementation: give `Active` a small per-voice output buffer for ch 9, or run the
ch-9 render inside the post-clear pass. Melodic channels keep the existing strip
path verbatim. This is the one acknowledged render-loop exception (§1.4).

**D9 strip parity (V4 — the review's one converged CRITICAL; DRIFT-1/INT-1/ORC-2/
DOC-5 + the Codex review).** As worded above, the dedicated pass silently drops
everything the ch-9 strip does today — and every committed fable5 album authors
CC7/CC10/CC91 on ch 9 (verified by SMF parse), with drums defaulting to
`reverb_send 0.3`, so built literally it changes every album's drum level and kills
their hall send (oracle 34 trips, Phase 5 fails). The ch-9 pass therefore
**reproduces the strip semantics** on the panned stereo pair:
- **Gain:** multiply by `strip.volume · strip.expr · at_gain` exactly as the strip
  does (CC7/CC11 keep working on drums).
- **Hall send (CC91):** keep the hp'd `send_rev` tap at `strip.reverb_send * 0.9`
  from the summed post-gain drum mono, **alongside** the new `send_room` tap —
  D10's room is *pre-hall*, not *instead of* the hall. (CC93/94 chorus/echo sends
  are zeroed for ch 9 by the existing `Prog` handler — unchanged.)
- **Authored CC10:** the channel pan **offsets the per-piece table**: effective pan
  `= (drum_pan(key) + (strip.pan − 0.5)).clamp(0.0, 1.0)`; centre (default) leaves
  the table verbatim.
- **Haas: NONE — level-only equal-power pan** (V4/DOC-4/DSP-6; the 2026.07.07
  mono-collapse lesson: the strip's 0-5 ms far-side Haas tap comb-filters sustained
  sources in mono, and crash/ride washes live 2.6-4.6 s at pans 0.25/0.70/0.75).
  Width comes from different pieces at different equal-power positions, never from
  inter-channel delay. Oracle 32b gains a mono-sum clause (§5.3).
- **CC74/CC71 wah:** applied to the drum stereo pair as **two mirrored `Biquad`
  instances** (L/R), retuned per block from the same slewed cutoff/Q the strip
  computes — an authored ch-9 wah keeps filtering the kit as it does today. (Two
  extra biquads per block; trivial cost.)
- **New oracle 42 (§5.3)** pins the parity: ch-9 CC7=0 silences drums; CC74=20
  measurably darkens the kit; the drum hall send still reaches the reverb; authored
  CC10 shifts the kit image.

**D10 — Drum room bus** (DRM-7; V2 predelay fix per CORR-3). `drum_room =
Reverb::new(sr, 0.42, 0.55, wet*0.9)` (V4/DSP-2: comb feedback passes straight into
the unchanged 1116-1617-sample combs, so t60 = 3·delay/(−log₁₀ fb); the V1 fb 0.55
actually rang ~0.42 s — for the intended tight <0.3 s room the feedback must be
≈0.42, giving t60 ≈ 0.29 s on the longest comb). *Problem:*
`Reverb`'s predelay is a fixed 24 ms module constant, which pushes a "tight room"'s
first reflections out past a real close-mic room and fights oracle 32a's 8-35 ms
window. *V2 fix:* make predelay a `Reverb::new` parameter (`PREDELAY_S`, the one
contained `reverb.rs` change) and pass ~2-4 ms for the drum room; the hall keeps
24 ms. `send_room` accumulates **only** ch 9, **un-highpassed** (kick keeps body) at
fixed `ROOM_SEND≈0.35`, processed into `mix_*` **before** the hall. `Reverb` is
already stereo (per-side early taps), so the room returns a decorrelated L/R image;
per-piece dry width comes from D9, not the room (oracle 32b measures the **dry**
path).

### 3.5 Shared KS core (guitar + bass)

**K1 — Cubic-Lagrange tap** (KS-5). Add `DelayLine::tap_cubic` (4-point, weights
sum to 1). Use it **only in `KsLoop::tick`**; leave the linear `tap` for
chorus/echo/reverb/pickup/haas so no bus output shifts. **(V4/DSP-1 — corrected
stability rationale:** the V1 "FIR → no feedback instability" was false reasoning —
an FIR interpolator inside a feedback loop destabilises it wherever |H| > 1, and
4-point Lagrange exceeds 1 near Nyquist *outside its central interval*, while DRIVE's
loop gain reaches ~0.988 (t60 clamps to 14 s at E1). Stability actually holds
because (a) the tap is evaluated **strictly in its central interval** — integer part
chosen so the fractional offset lies between the two middle stencil points (|H| ≤ 1);
(b) `loop_gain < 1`; (c) the in-loop damper attenuates HF where Lagrange ripple is
worst. The stability oracle (15) extends to a 10 s bounded-peak check on a **low
DRIVE note**, the worst case.**)** **(V4/INT-4 — capacity clamp:** v0.7's RPN bend
range (≤24 semitones ⇒ 4× nominal delay) and unbounded portamento origins can exceed
the delay line's `target·2.2 + 8` power-of-two headroom (e.g. target 130 → 512 =
3.94×), and `DelayLine::tap` wraps silently — garbage audio, not a crash. `KsLoop::
retune` therefore clamps the delay target to the line's capacity minus a 4-sample
tap margin; an out-of-range bend/glide pitch-limits instead of corrupting. §6 gains
a capacity row.**)**

**K2 — Triangle initial condition** (KS-1). Build `exc` as a normalised triangle
(ramp 0→1 over `[0,comb]`, 1→0 over `[comb,len]`), subtract mean (DC block), add
~0.12·noise through `pick_lp` for grit, keep `v/peak` scaling, then **RMS-match**
(target peak ~0.75×) so the higher-RMS triangle doesn't raise per-note loudness.
**(V4/DSP-4)** The existing feedforward pick-position comb (`raw[i] − 0.9·raw[(i+
comb)%len]`) is **dropped when the triangle IC is used** — the triangle's breakpoint
at `comb` already encodes pick position (harmonic amplitudes ∝ sin(nπp)/n²);
applying the comb on top would double-filter the pick nulls. The grit-noise term is
added comb-free. Oracle 17's rolloff clause is correspondingly sin(nπp)/n², measured
on the excitation buffer directly (§5.3), not pure 1/n² at the voice output.

**K3 — Polarization coupling** (KS-4). Add `h_prev/v_prev` to `Pluck`;
skew-symmetric coupling: `hc = horiz.tick(inject + k·v_prev)`,
`vc = vert.tick(0.7·inject − k·h_prev)` (note the minus). **(V4/DSP-5 — corrected
stability basis:** the V1 "pure-imaginary eigenvalues → unconditionally stable" was
a delay-free statement about the coupling matrix alone; the realized coupling feeds
one-sample-delayed state into two lossy filtered loops. The actual guarantee: each
loop is a contraction (`loop_gain < 1`) and the skew-symmetric cross-injection adds
no energy, so the composition stays bounded — pinned **empirically** by oracle 15,
which V4 extends to the high-loop-gain DRIVE case. And `k` is a **tuned parameter,
starting estimate 0.01-0.03** (not the V1 0.003, likely inaudible): large enough for
oracle 15's measurable secondary rise, small enough to keep the 10 s peak < 1.5.**)**

**K4 — Dispersion / inharmonicity** (KS-3, GTR-5#2) — two stages, cheap first:
- *Stage 1 (primary, near-zero risk):* register-split **wound-ness** — derive a
  wound factor from key (crossover ~G3 for guitar; whole range for BASS/FRETLESS),
  scale `bright` and skew `t60` so wound bass strings differ from plain treble.
  Pure arithmetic, no allpass.
- *Stage 2 (optional polish):* one 1st-order allpass `A(z)=(a+z⁻¹)/(1+a·z⁻¹)` per
  `KsLoop`, **negative** `a∈[−0.25,−0.05]` (partials stretch sharp, correcting the
  existing flat skew), `|a|` larger for low/wound strings, fixed for the note's
  life; `retune` recomputes loop delay after subtracting the allpass phase delay at
  `f`. Denormal-flushed.

## 5. Acceptance criteria (definition of done)

Each is a `cargo test` that **fails on `main`, passes after** the cited change,
numerical/structural (no ears).

**Shared std-only oracle helpers** (a `#[cfg(test)]` module — from
testability-first; **self-calibrated first**, oracle 0):
- `band_rms(seg, sr, f, q)` — `seg` through `Biquad::bandpass`/`highpass`, return RMS.
- `centroid(seg, sr)` — Goertzel magnitude at ~20 log-spaced bins 100 Hz–12 kHz;
  `Σf·|X| / Σ|X|`.
- `mag_at(seg, sr, f)` — single-bin Goertzel (cabinet response).
- `pitch_hz(seg, sr)` — existing zero-crossing counter.
- `rms(a,b)`, `t60_of(env)` — windowed RMS and decay-time from the RMS envelope.
- `inter_corr(l, r)` — normalised L/R cross-correlation (stereo-width oracle).

0. **Helper calibration** (guards the guards): on synthetic golden signals —
   `mag_at` of a pure 1 kHz sine peaks at 1 kHz and is ≥20 dB down at 2 kHz;
   `centroid` of white noise > `centroid` of a 200 Hz sine; `t60_of` of a known
   exponential recovers its decay within ±10%. Prevents a wrong DFT helper from
   silently passing/failing the 36 feature oracles.
1. **Velocity brightness** (KS-2/GTR-1#2/BASS-2): STEEL & BASS `centroid(ff) > 1.4·centroid(pp)` (bass 1.3×) at the same key.
2. **Velocity tuning-invariance**: `pluck_pitch_a4`-style check at vel 30 **and** 120 (±6 Hz).
3. **Cabinet response** (GTR-1): impulse through `Drive` (prog 30) — `mag_at(2600)−mag_at(1000) ≥ +3 dB`; `mag_at(6000)−mag_at(3000) ≤ −18 dB`; `mag_at(100)−mag_at(300) ≥ +2 dB`.
