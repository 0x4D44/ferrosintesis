# 2026.07.06 - HLD - The Signal Fire: composition roadmap

A single continuous ~17-minute instrumental in the Mike Oldfield idiom — a cross
between *Incantations Part IV* (the long guitar build), *TSDOE "Ascension"*
(ambient pools igniting into sequenced pulse, the slow filter-opening, the
major-mode lift), *Tubular Bells III* (moody minor electronica, four-on-the-
floor under pealing bells) and *"Mastermind"* (the funk engine: driving
syncopated bass riff, wah guitar, Hammond with a Leslie that spins up).

All material is original. The unifying device: **one bass riff recast three
ways** — 4/4 sixteenth funk, a 10/8 additive ostinato (3+3+2+2), and an
augmented half-time version under the climax — plus **three themes composed
over the same i–♭VII ground so they stack in counterpoint at the finale**.

Album bundle: `fable5/The Signal Fire/` per the repo layout HLD. One track:
`midi/01 - The Signal Fire.mid`. Renderer: hollowsynth **v0.6** (adds CC1
mod-wheel vibrato/Leslie ramp, CC74 filter cutoff, CC64 sustain, `--solo`).

## 3. Material (all in `material.py`, written as degrees)

### RIFF — shared skeleton `[1, 8, 7, 5, 6, 4, 5, 0, 1]` (0 = ♭7 below tonic)

- `RIFF_FUNK` (4/4, one bar, 16th syncopation + ghost notes): main hits
  `(1,0,.75)(1,.75,.25)(8,1,.5)(7,1.75,.25)(5,2,.5)(6,2.5,.25)(5,2.75,.25)(4,3,.5)(0,3.5,.25)(1,3.75,.25)`
  plus ghost tonic 16ths (vel −30) filling selected gaps.
- `RIFF_10` (10/8 = 5 beats, quaver=0.5, grouped 3+3+2+2):
  `(1,0,.5)(1,.5,.5)(8,1,.5) (7,1.5,.5)(5,2,.5)(6,2.5,.5) (4,3,.5)(5,3.5,.5) (0,4,.5)(1,4.5,.5)`
  accents on quavers 0,3,6,8.
- `RIFF_AUG` (16 beats, half-time under M5): the skeleton in minims/semibreves.

### Themes over the ground Am | G | Am | G (i–♭VII; chord degrees 1/3/5 and 7/2/4)

- `THEME_A` "The Signal" — 32 beats, singing arch 5→11→1, long tones.
  Peak degree 11 in bar 6. (Whistle M1, lead M4, bells augmented M5.)
- `THEME_B` "The Flare" — 16 beats, syncopated quaver call-and-response cells,
  register 1–9. (Organ/guitar M2, fiddle M3, figuration M5.)
- `THEME_C` "The Watch" — 32 beats, minim chorale, stepwise 1–7 register,
  designed to sit under A and B. (Organ flutes M3, strings/choir M5.)

The **counterpoint oracle** in `build.py --verify` checks strong-beat
intervals for every pair (A+C, B+C, A+B) over the ground: on integer beats
that are chord changes, allowed intervals mod 12 = {0,3,4,7,8,9,5}; no
2/♭2/♯4 clashes on strong beats; no parallel unison. Themes must be *written*
to pass — adjust the material, never the oracle.

### Lattice: the riff dissolved into three guitars (M3)

Composite quaver line per 10/8 cycle = RIFF melody at quaver rate:
`[1,5,8,7,5,6,4,5,0,1]` (D dorian). Split by quaver index: L1={0,3,6},
L2={1,4,8}, L3={2,5,7,9}. Each line sustains through its gaps (let ring,
gate ≈ 2.5 quavers). Even cycles accent 3+3+2+2, odd cycles cross-accent
2+2+3+3. Every 4th cycle each line adds one diatonic neighbor decoration.

## 4. Movement briefs

### M1 "Signal" (0–176, A aeolian→dorian, pp→mp)

- ch1 sweep pad: Am9 voicings, `pad_block` 16-beat spans; **CC74 ramps 30→100
  across the whole movement** (the Ascension filter-opening, starting the
  piece); CC11 breathing swells per chord.
- ch0 piano: pooled arpeggios (Am–Em–G–Am cycles), **CC64 down per 2-bar
  harmony, up at changes**; sparse, vel 30–50.
- ch2 crystal: single notes on degrees {1,5,9}, CC94 echo throws (spike to 90
  on the last note of each phrase, back to 20), slow autopan drift.
- ch3 fretless: long tones tracing the RIFF skeleton in augmentation
  (1…8…7…5…), **bend_ramp slides into each note** (from −1.5 semis over
  ~0.4 beats), recentre after.
- ch14 whistle: THEME_A complete at beat 64 (after the pad established),
  CC91=80 CC11 40→70 (far away), delayed-vibrato on notes ≥ 2 beats.
- Woodblock (ch9, note 76) taps **Morse "CQ CQ CQ"** quietly at ~beat 120
  (vel ~35) — a beacon calling anyone listening.
- Beat 96: F# (dorian 6) allowed in for the first time — pad voicings and
  piano turn brighter.
- 160–176 ignition spin-up: kick heartbeat (beats 160,164,168, then 172,174),
  tempo 72→76→80→88 (at 152/160/168/172), bass shifts to short pickup notes,
  pad CC11 dips to 50 to make room. Seam: pad holds an Am chord over the bar
  into M2; nothing else sustains across 176.

### M2 "Ignition" (176–480, A dorian, 100 bpm, the funk engine)

- Drums assemble one element per 8 bars: kick pattern (176), hats 16ths w/
  accents (208), snare backbeat + ghosts (240), fills each 16 bars.
- ch3 fingered bass: RIFF_FUNK loops from 208, vel 85–105, ghosts vel ~55.
  Every 4th bar: fill variation (walk-up 5-6-7-8 or drop to 0-1).
- ch10 palm-mute chug (prog 28 from 240): 16ths on 1 and 5, opening to prog
  27 power-5ths at section peaks; pan 30.
- ch11 **wah guitar**: RIFF_FUNK top voice + 16th scratches; **CC74 sine LFO
  quaver-rate between 40 and 100** synced to the beat (write a CC74 event
  every 16th); pan 98. This is the Mastermind sound.
- ch4 Hammond (prog 18): stabs on the &-of-2 and 4 (triads i and ♭VII);
  **CC1 Leslie choreography**: 0 (slow) at entry, ramp to 127 over the 8 bars
  into each section peak, drop back to 20 after.
- ch8 nylon: offbeat upstroke comps, vel 45 (reggae-light, mixes in).
- THEME_B: organ states it at 240 (call), clean guitar answers (272);
  antiphonal L/R 2-bar cells from 304 (ch10 L asks, ch11 R answers).
- ch14 whistle: THEME_A fragments (first 8 beats) floating over peaks.
- Break at 400–432: drums drop to kick+hats, bass solo féature w/ bends
  (slide 0→1), organ swell (CC1 ramp) rebuilds; 432–464 full groove tutti,
  ch12 lead's **first violining entries** (CC11 0→90 over 1.5 beats on held
  bend-up wails) — a teaser of M4.
- 464–480 dissolve: drums thin to hats, bass pivots to RIFF_10 in D
  (cross-metric over the 4/4), pad swells; tempo → 108 at 480.

### M3 "The Lattice" (480–800, D dorian, 10/8, 108 bpm)

- 64 cycles of 5 beats. Lattice L1/L2/L3 on ch7/8/10 (see §3): enter one per
  8 cycles (L1 @480, L2 @520, L3 @560), vel 55→75 terraced.
- ch3 bass: RIFF_10 from 520, vel 70→95; every 8th cycle substitutes the
  cross-accent variant.
- ch15 "mandolin" (prog 25, tremolo = 32nd-note repeated notes, jt=2):
  doubles the accent quavers only, from cycle 24.
- ch14 fiddle (prog 40 @480): THEME_B (adapted to the 10/8 grid: the 16-beat
  theme spans 3.2 cycles; write it phrase-by-phrase to breathe with the
  cycles) from cycle 16, CC68 legato slurs on the quaver runs, delayed
  vibrato on long notes.
- ch4 organ flutes (prog 16 @480): THEME_C chorale under the texture from
  cycle 32, vel 50.
- ch2 crystal: **3-beat loop** (degrees 1-5-9 arpeggio, one note per beat)
  running over the 5-beat cycle (realigns every 15 beats); **CC10 autopan
  L→R→L over each 15-beat realignment**; CC94=35.
- ch5 strings: pad the cycle roots from cycle 40 (i–i–♭VII–i harmonic rhythm
  per 4 cycles), CC11 swells.
- Drums: shaker 16ths + tambourine accents on group starts from cycle 32;
  no kit — the lattice IS the rhythm.
- Build to tutti at cycles 56–64 (everything in, vel +15, strings CC11 90);
  final cycle: all channels land the downbeat of 800 together, then CUT —
  only ch5 strings hold a D-minor add9 chord (CC11 fading 80→40) across the
  seam, and ch3 bass walks D→C→B→A (beats 796–800) into the new ground.

### M4 "The Long Climb" (800–1312, A dorian, 92→112, THE solo)

The ground (all movement): Am | G | Am | G. ch7 steel strums the harmony
(down-down-up per bar, vel 60); ch10 palm-mute chug (prog 28 @800) roots
8ths vel 70; ch3 bass: RIFF_FUNK adapted at the slower tempo, growing
countermelody fills in bars 4/8 of each 8-bar cycle (approach runs 3-4-5-6,
octave drops, 0–1 pivots — increasingly melodic each wave).

The solo (ch12, prog 30, CC94=35 delayed-lead echo): **five 16-bar waves**
(64 beats each: 800, 928?, no — waves at 800/864/928/... wait: 16 bars = 64
beats; waves at 800, 864, 928, 992, 1056; then 1056–1312 is wave 5 + peak
plateau — see below). Register ceiling and density rise per wave:

- **W1 (800–864)** lyrical: THEME_A paraphrase, ≤ E5, long tones, violining
  entries (CC11 0→95 over 1–2 beats), delayed vibrato (CC1 30), quarter-tone
  curls (bend +0.4 → 0) on phrase peaks.
- **W2 (864–928)** ≤ A5: **unison bends** — ch13 sustains target pitch, ch12
  bends up a whole tone into it (bend_ramp 0.25 beats, hold, release);
  pre-bend-and-release figures; blue thirds (bend +0.5 on degree 3).
- **W3 (928–992)** ≤ C6: **ch13 becomes +6¢ detune double** (constant bend
  0.06 semis, hard L/R 20/108, mirrors ch12 exactly, vel −8); triplet cells;
  first `run(legato=True)` bursts (1.5-octave scalar sweeps); strings terrace
  in (ch5, pad roots, CC11 40→70); accelerando underway.
- **W4 (992–1056)** ≤ E6: machine-gun runs alternate with held wails (bend
  vibrato deepens, CC1 70); choir terraces (ch6, "mm-ah" swells); organ back
  (prog 18, CC1 ramp to 127 — Leslie fast); ch15 mandolin tremolo doubles
  the strums; ch11 clean arps 16ths; drums add toms build.
- **W5 (1056–1312)** ≤ B6: full machine-gun with `octave_double=12`,
  hammer-run chains up and down A dorian, wails with +2 pre-bend releases;
  ch14 flute doubles select runs an octave up; ch2 glockenspiel (prog 9 @
  ~1230) sparkles the peaks; every 16 bars still terraces (+CC11 everywhere,
  vel +5 per cycle). 1296–1312: THE ASCENT — a 4-octave climbing run
  (16ths, all guitars + flute + glock in octaves), drums roll, and on
  1310–1312 **ch12+ch13 bend the final held chord +2 semitones** so the whole
  texture slides up into…

Terrace order (roll-call): strings 928 → choir 992 → mandolin 1056 → flute
1120 → glockenspiel 1230. CC91 eases 55→40 across the movement (getting
closer); master dynamic via velocities + CC11, never CC7 after setup.

### M5 "Ascension" (1312–1592, A ionian, 112, ff)

- Downbeat 1312: A major tutti. Drums four-on-the-floor + open hats; bass
  RIFF_AUG (16-beat) twice, then active RIFF_FUNK (major-adapted: degree 3
  natural) from 1376.
- ch15 tubular bells (prog 14 @1290): **THEME_A augmented ×2** (64 beats per
  statement) from 1312 — the peal. vel 100–115.
- ch5 strings + ch6 choir: THEME_C in octaves, CC11 85.
- ch12 lead: THEME_B phrases as answering wails between bell phrases;
  ch13 harmonizes in diatonic 3rds/6ths (not detune — real harmony now).
- ch10 (prog 27 @1312) + ch11: interlocking 16th figuration (major-key
  lattice condensed to 4/4: composite [1,5,8,7,5,6,4,5] 8ths split odd/even).
- ch0 piano: high glitter arps (degrees 1-3-5-8 across two octaves).
- ch4 organ full (CC1=127 throughout — Leslie fast), ch1 pad CC74=110.
- Second peal statement (1440): everything +CC11 5, glock doubles bells.
- 1560–1584: closing cadence IV–V–I suspensions (D–E(sus4)–A), whole band;
  1584: full stop — every channel note-off EXCEPT ch1 pad chord and ch15's
  final bell strike on A (beat 1583, let ring 16 beats). CC91 90 on both.

### M6 "Afterglow" (1592–1678, A ionian, rit. 112→66)

- Tempo steps down: 112@1592, 96@1608, 80@1628, 66@1648.
- ch1 sweep pad (prog 95 @1592): A major add9, **CC74 closes 100→25 over the
  movement** (bookend of M1's opening).
- ch8 nylon: THEME_A complete, intimate (vel 45–60, gate 0.9), rubato feel
  via jt=8; ch7 steel fingerpicks broken chords beneath (PICK-style).
- ch3 fretless (prog 35 @1592): slide tones on 1 and 5.
- ch14 whistle (prog 78 @1592): echoes the last A phrase, CC94 throw on the
  final note.
- ch9 woodblock: Morse **"K"** ("go ahead / over") at ~1650, vel 30 — the
  beacon's call from M1 finally gets its answer.
- Final: ch15 one bell on A3 (beat 1664, vel 70), ch2 crystal high A pings,
  pad settles to bare A–E open fifth, CC11 fade to 20 by 1678. Silence +
  reverb tail.

## 6. Verification oracles (`build.py --verify`)

1. MIDI parses; total duration 16:30–17:30; 17 tracks (conductor + 16); ≥6
   markers; tempo events ≥ 14; timesig events ≥ 3.
2. Counterpoint check (§3) passes for A+B, A+C, B+C over the ground.
3. Riff-skeleton identity: FUNK/10/AUG variants reduce to the same degree
   sequence.
4. CC inventory per channel: ch11 has ≥200 CC74 events in M2; ch1 CC74 ramps
   in M1 and M6; ch4 has CC1 ramps; ch0 CC64 downs == ups; ch12/13 bend
   events > 100; CC68 on/off balanced around every legato run.
5. Bend hygiene: |bend| ≤ 2 semis; every channel's bend recentred (|s| <
   0.01) before each movement boundary, EXCEPT ch13's intentional +0.06
   detune segments (whitelisted by beat range) and the M4→M5 chord bend
   (recentred at 1312.0).
6. Note ranges per program legal; no notes on ch9 outside GM percussion map.
7. Dynamics arc: mean velocity per movement follows M1 < M6 < M3 < M2 < M4 <
   M5; note-density (notes/beat) peaks in M5.
8. No all-channel silent gap > 1 beat anywhere (the M5 cutoff keeps pad+bell
   ringing).
9. Every movement module stays inside its beat range (no note may start
   outside [t0, t1) of its movement; sustains may cross only where §4 names
   a seam carry-over).

## 7. Audio verification (post-render, measured — this machine has no ears)

RMS-per-second arc matches the dynamics plan; spectral centroid rises across
M1 (the CC74 opening is measurable) and falls across M6; centroid oscillation

(sample-delta scan vs transient whitelist); stereo correlation per movement
(lattice and lead-double sections wider than M1); true-silence scan finds
nothing but the final fade.
