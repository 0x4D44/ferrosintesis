# 2026.07.06 - HLD - Winter Guests: composition roadmap

A two-part instrumental (~9 min per part, ~18:40 total) in the Mike Oldfield
idiom with two guest sorties: **ABBA** (*The Visitors*' cold sequenced
arpeggios and paranoia; *Super Trouper*'s stacked-thirds choruses,
off-beat piano octave comping and the truck-driver gear change) and the
**Crash Test Dummies** (*Mmm Mmm Mmm Mmm*'s low wordless baritone hum over
harmonium). The guests are *movements*, not pastiche: everything shares one
DNA.

**The frame**: Part One is the cold half (E minor — the guests arrive);
Part Two is the warm half (D major lifting to E MAJOR — the house warms
up). The piece opens in E minor and closes in E major: a parallel-major
arc.

**The DNA**: ONE theme (`THEME`, "the Guest theme") designed to live three
lives, all machine-verified in `material.py`:
1. **Hum guise** (CTD) — sung low and wordless by the formant choir
   (vowel "mm"), range ≤ a 10th (baritone-hummable, oracle-checked);
2. **Chorus guise** (ABBA) — re-rhythmed to a pop chorus, harmonized in
   parallel diatonic 3rds above and 6ths below (stack oracle: no minor-2nd
   or tritone against the ground roots on strong beats);
3. **Guitar guise** (Oldfield) — the same skeleton ornamented with runs.
All three reduce to the same strong-beat degree skeleton `[1 5 2 1 3 7 4 1]`
(oracle-checked), and the chorus survives the +2-semitone gear change
inside every channel's range (oracle-checked).

Plus a **cold ostinato** (Visitors): a 2-bar i–VI sixteenth-note arpeggio
cell, and a **7/8 footsteps cell** (3+2+2) for the dark third movement.

Renderer: **hollowsynth v0.7** (new: CC70 choir vowel morph, RPN 0
bend-range / RPN 1 fine-tune, CC5+CC65 portamento, CC71 resonance, channel
aftertouch, CC66 sostenuto / CC67 una corda, lyric + key-signature metas).

Album bundle: `fable5/Winter Guests/` per the repo layout HLD. TWO tracks:
`midi/01 - Winter Guests, Part One.mid`, `midi/02 - Winter Guests, Part
Two.mid`. Infrastructure cloned from The Signal Fire and adapted to two
Scores (Hollow Hill's two-track build.py is the exemplar).

## 3. Material (all in `material.py`, as degrees)

### THEME — "the Guest theme", 32 beats, strong-beat skeleton [1 5 2 1 3 7 4 1]

```
(1,0,2)(2,2,1)(3,3,1) (5,4,2)(4,6,1)(3,7,1) (2,8,1.5)(3,9.5,0.5)(4,10,1)(2,11,1) (1,12,3)(0,15,1)
(3,16,2)(4,18,1)(5,19,1) (7,20,2)(8,22,1)(6,23,1) (4,24,1.5)(3,25.5,0.5)(2,26,1)(3,27,1) (1,28,4)
```
Range degrees 0..8 = 14 semitones (≤ 16, hummable — oracle). Downbeats at
0/4/8/12/16/20/24/28 give the skeleton above.

### Grounds

- `HUM_GROUND` (M2, aeolian): 4-bar i | III | VII | iv (Em G D Am), chord
  degree sets {1,3,5} {3,5,7} {7,2,4} {4,6,8}. Theme downbeats are chord
  tones of it (oracle).
- `CHORUS_GROUND` (M4/M5, ionian): 8-bar I V ii IV | vi V IV I
  (D A Em G | Bm A G D; in E: E B F#m A | C#m B A E). Theme downbeats are
  chord tones (oracle). This is the ABBA progression.
- `COLD_CELL` (M1/M3, aeolian): 2-bar i | VI (Em | C). Arp realization:
  16ths over chord degrees [1,5,8,10,8,5] per bar with octave lifts —
  `arp_cell()` generator in material.py.
- `FOOTSTEPS` (M3, 7/8, quaver=0.5): degrees [1,1,5, 4,5, 0,1] grouped
  3+2+2, accents on group starts.

### Stacks (ABBA)

`stack_thirds(theme)` returns (top, mid, low) = theme +2 diatonic steps,
theme, theme −2 diatonic steps (a 3rd above and a 6th-below-equivalent).
Oracle: on every strong beat, every stack voice vs the CHORUS_GROUND root
avoids intervals {1, 6} mod 12 (no minor 2nd, no tritone); stack-internal
intervals are 3rds by construction.

### Gear change

Chorus material recurs at base+2 semitones (D→E) from beat 320 of Part
Two. Oracle: the +2 realization of (stack top voice, bells, bass) stays
inside verify.NOTE_RANGES for their channels.

## 4. Movement briefs

### M1 "Frost" (P1 0–256, E aeolian, 92) — The Visitors, arriving

- ch2 ice arp: COLD_CELL 16ths from beat 16, one register at first; CC74
  starts 45 and opens to 95 across the movement, **CC71 resonance rides
  50→85→60** (the analog sweep); `autopan` slow. Every 32 beats the cell
  adds an octave lift or a passing 9th (variation, not wallpaper).
- ch1 pad: Em(add9) bed, **aftertouch blooms** (pressure curves 0→90→0
  per 16-beat phrase — brightness/vibrato swell inside held chords).
- ch3 fretless: pedal E's, **portamento slides** (CC65 on, CC5 ~60) between
  1 and 5; sparse.
- ch4 (tacet until 128) low reed-organ cluster swells, CC11.
- ch15 music box: THEME phrase 1 fragments (bars 1–2 only), high and icy,
  echo throws; enters 64.
- ch6 choir I: at 176, the FIRST HUM — theme phrase 1 (16 beats), vowel
  **CC70=0 (mm)**, low octave (E2 base? E3 — keep inside choir range),
  vel ~50, aftertouch swell mid-phrase. The guests knock.
- ch9: electro pulse — kick on 1&3 from 128, rim ticks; sparse.
- Seam: arp thins to single notes 248–256; pad holds across into M2.

### M2 "The Humming" (P1 256–544, E aeolian→dorian, 84) — CTD

- Ground loops (HUM_GROUND, 8 beats/bar-pair? one chord per bar, 16-beat
  cycles): ch7 steel fingerpicks it (PICK-style 4-voice pattern), ch4
  harmonium holds chords with **CC66 sostenuto**: pedal the cycle's root
  at each cycle start, sustain it under the changing chords, release at
  cycle end (downs == ups, oracle).
- ch0 piano: **CC67 una corda ON** for the whole movement (off at 544);
  sparse pooled arpeggios.
- **Verse 1 (272–304)**: ch6 choir I sings THEME complete, vowel mm,
  low register, vel 55–65; `lyric` metas carry "Mm" / "hm" syllables on
  its notes (players display the humming).
- **Answer (304–336)**: ch14 flute answers with theme bars 5–6; nylon
  counter-arps.
- **Verse 2 (336–400)**: hum again; ch11 choir II joins a diatonic 3rd
  above at **CC70≈45 ("oo")**, vel −8; RPN fine-tune −6 cents on ch11 at
  256 (ensemble width, NOT the bend controller).
- **Bridge (400–464)**: dorian F# admitted (the light warms a shade);
  strings enter; harmonium walks III–IV(dorian)–VII; hum fragments trade
  between choirs; aftertouch crescendo.
- **Verse 3 (464–528)**: fullest — both choirs (3rd + melody), strings,
  flute descant (theme +2 steps, high), piano still una corda.
- 528–544 thins to harmonium + one choir "mm" holding degree 2 — half
  cadence, unresolved; seam into the dark.

### M3 "Footsteps in the Hall" (P1 544–864, E aeolian, 7/8, 92→104) — The Visitors, inside

- Timesig 7/8 at 544 (quaver 0.5: 3.5-beat bars, 3+2+2). ~82 bars to 832.
- ch3 bass (prog 33 @544): FOOTSTEPS cell, relentless, vel 70→90.
- ch10 palm-mute (28 @544): chug on the 3+2+2 accents.
- ch2 ice arp returns: COLD_CELL compressed to 7/8 (drop two 16ths),
  **CC71 high (80–100)** — colder, narrower; CC74 sweeps per 8 bars.
- ch9: 7/8 kit — kick on group starts, hats quavers, snare on group 2.
- ch12 **portamento synth lead** (prog 81): at 576, `bend_range(12)` +
  CC65 on, CC5 ~70. The solo: long gliding lines (portamento does the
  glide, bends do the expression), building phrases every 16 bars; at
  ~768 a **whammy dive: bend −12 semitones** over 2 beats on a held note,
  recover, THE scream at ~800: dive −12, rip back to 0, then `bend_range`
  reset to 2 and bend recentred by 832 (oracle checks vs the RPN state).
- ch5 strings: tension pads, semitone neighbor oscillations (aeolian ♭6–5).
- ch6 choir: fragmented "mm" stabs on group starts from 704 (the hum made
  anxious), aftertouch spikes.
- 832: 4/4 coda — everything stops except ch4 organ swell + ch6 choir I:
  hum theme bars 1–2 ending ON DEGREE 2 (half cadence), vowel mm, dying
  to nothing by 860. Part One ends unresolved: the guests are in the house.

### M4 "Searchlight" (P2 0–448, D ionian → E ionian @320, 118) — Super Trouper

- **Intro (0–64)**: ch0 piano OFF-BEAT OCTAVE COMP (the ABBA engine:
  octaves on the &s, roots on 1/3 in LH), four-on-floor assembles, ch7
  steel backbeat strums, bass pop 8ths on CHORUS_GROUND roots.
- **Chorus 1 (64–128)**: choir I sings the CHORUS GUISE (theme re-rhythmed
  — anticipations on the &-of-4 into each bar, one bar rest每 4? writer's
  call within skeleton), **CC70=100 (ah)**, with `stack_thirds` top+mid on
  ch6 (two notes) and low voice on ch11; glock doubles the hook.
- **Verse interlude (128–224)**: guitars take the guitar guise fragments
  (clean, delayed); piano comp continues; ch13 harmonizes ch12 in 3rds.
- **Chorus 2 (224–288)**: bigger — flute descant, strings pads, bass
  walks. **Breakdown (288–320)**: drums to kick, pad + both choirs hold
  "oo" (CC70 45) with a LONG aftertouch crescendo — then
- **GEAR CHANGE @320**: keysig meta E major; everything restates the
  chorus at +2 semis (oracle-checked ranges), full band, "ah", tambourine,
  glock 8va. **Chorus 3 (320–384)**, tail (384–448) rides the E ground
  vamp I–IV–I–V as the disco pulse hands over to…

### M5 "The Glass Ballroom" (P2 448–896, E ionian, 118) — the apotheosis

- Disco pulse continues but the guitars arrive: ch12 (30) violining
  entries then the GUITAR GUISE ornamented, machine-gun `run()` bursts;
  ch13 = ABBA DOUBLE: RPN fine-tune +8 cents @448, hard-split 20/108,
  mirrors ch12 (vel −8) — honest double-tracking, bends stay free.
- ch15 tubular bells (14 @448): THEME augmented ×2 pealing from 512;
  ch11 choir II sings the HUM GUISE low (CC70=0, mm) as a counterline —
  **all three guises sound together** (bells=theme, ch6 stack="ah"
  chorus phrases answering, ch11 low hum, guitars figurating between).
- ch4 full organ (18 @448) CC1 Leslie ramps into each 32-beat peak;
  ch14 fiddle countermelody (reel-flavoured 8ths, the Oldfield wink).
- Structure: 448–512 build (guitars assemble over the vamp), 512–640
  first peal + stacks, 640–704 breakdown (choirs + bells only,
  aftertouch swells — the ballroom breathes), 704–832 second peal, full
  (glock doubles, piano glitter, drums fills every 8), 832–880 the big
  IV–V–I(sus) cadence, 880–896: full stop at 884 except pad chord +
  final bell ring (CC91 to 90).
- Density: THE densest movement (oracle: density peaks here per part 2).

### M6 "Last Light" (P2 896–1024, E ionian, rit 118→60) — the guests depart

- ch1 sweep pad (95 @896): E(add9), CC74 closing 95→30.
- ch0 piano: **CC67 una corda** back on; theme phrase 2, slow, pooled
  (CC64 pedal pairs balanced).
- ch8 nylon: theme bars 7–8 farewell; ch3 fretless (35 @896) slides.
- ch6 choir I: the FINAL HUM — theme bars 1–4 vowel mm… and this time it
  ENDS ON 1 (the resolution Part One refused). `lyric` metas: "Mm… hm…
  mm… (goodnight)". Aftertouch bloom then decay.
- ch14 flute (73 @896) echo; ch15 ONE bell on E @~1000; crystal-less —
  keep it warm. Fade by 1020; silence + tail.

## 6. Verification oracles (build.py --verify, both parts)

1. Structure per part: parses; durations P1 ∈ [8:45, 9:59], P2 ∈ [8:45,
   9:59]; 17 tracks each; ≥3 markers each; tempo/timesig/keysig events
   per grid.
2. Material: tri-guise skeleton identity; hummable range ≤ 16 semis;
   theme downbeats chord tones of HUM_GROUND and CHORUS_GROUND;
   stack-vs-root clash check ({1,6} forbidden) in ionian AND at +2;
   FOOTSTEPS/COLD_CELL well-formed.
3. CC inventory: CC70 on ch6/ch11 (mm ≤ 10 in hum sections, ≥ 80 in
   choruses); CC71+CC74 sweeps on ch2; CC66 downs==ups; CC67 on/off
   paired; CC5/65 present around every portamento passage, 65 off after;
   aftertouch events ≥ N on ch1/ch6; RPN sequences WELL-FORMED (101→100→6,
   values sane, ch12 range 12 only within [576, 832], reset before 832).
4. Bend hygiene RPN-AWARE: |bend semis| ≤ current range; recentred at
   movement boundaries; ch13/ch11 fine-tune is RPN (not bend) so bend
   stays clean.
5. Ranges per channel; GM percussion only on ch9.
6. Dynamics arc per part: P1 mean velocity M1 < M2 < M3; P2 M6 < M4 < M5;
   density peaks in M5 (P2) and M3 (P1).
7. No all-channel gap > 1 beat inside a part (M5's stop keeps pad+bell).
8. Movement bounds per module (as Signal Fire).
9. Lyric metas present in M2 and M6; keysig metas at the grid points.
