# 2026.07.07 - HLD - The Ninth Bell

A single-track orchestral piece (~5:50) in the *Gabriel Knight: Sins of the
Fathers* title-music idiom (dark-cathedral gothic drama). **Original material
only** — GK-vocabulary gestures, no quotation. Grows out of the string-chord
gesture Arthur loves from `demos/orchestral_demo.py` ch0.

Synthesis of a three-design panel (film-dramaturgy winner "The Sin-Eater's
Vigil") plus all eight judge grafts. Album bundle: `fable5/The Ninth Bell/`.

## 0. Dramaturgical thesis

One spring, compressed twice: **trained expectation, violently betrayed, then
the betrayal made beautiful.** Stately dread → first ascent → HIT on the wrong
chord (E major) → the void → uncanny lullaby → long rebuild with one feint →
full toll → second betrayal (Neapolitan Bb slam + cut) → embers, and the only
resolution the piece grants: the ninth bell, a lone A.

- **Silence is scored, not left over.** After each hit: ~3 beats of true
  silence (reverb tail only). CC91 is pushed up one beat before each hit so
  the tail blooms into the silence.
- **The theme's final resolution (2→1) is withheld** at the climax fracture
  and granted only by the last bell note A.
- **Nine bells frame the piece** (see toll ledger) — the title is structural.

## 1. Fixed facts

- Key **A aeolian** (harmonic-minor V and Neapolitan bII as dramatic colour).
  TONIC = n("A3"). 4/4 throughout. 101 bars = 404 beats.
- Ground (home): **Am | F | C | G** = degrees 1, 6, 3, 7 (i bVI bIII bVII).
- Climax ground (reharm): **Am | Dm | Em | E7** (i iv v V7); E7's G# via
  raised degree (harmonic minor); theme fits both grounds (verified).
- Betrayal chords: hit #1 = **E major** (V with G#), hit #2 = **Bb major**
  (bII). Both contain the melody note they interrupt (E-hit under the held
  theme entry; Bb holds the theme's downbeat D as its third).
- Seed intro (MUST be reproduced exactly, ch0 untouched by anything else,
  beats 0–32): `channel(0, "strings", program=48, volume=88, pan=64,
  reverb=74)`; `pad_block(sc, 0, 0.0, chords+chords, span=4.0, size=4,
  lo=52, hi=79, vel=44, vel_end=70)` with chords = triads on degrees
  [1,6,3,7]; `cc_curve(sc, 0, 11, [(0.0,20),(8.0,90),(16.0,105)], step=0.5)`.
  (32 beats — the code, not the brief's "16 beats" prose, is ground truth.)
- Renderer: hollowsynth v0.8.0. **No GM programs 55–71 anywhere.**

## 2. The material (material.py — oracle-verified)

**THEME — "the lament"** (13 notes; (degree, start-in-bar, dur); base A3
register for cello, +12 for violin):

```
bar 1 (Am): (5,0.0,1.0) (10,1.0,1.5) (9,2.5,0.5) (8,3.0,1.0)
bar 2 (F):  (8,0.0,2.0) (7,2.0,1.0)  (6,3.0,1.0)
bar 3 (C):  (7,0.0,1.5) (8,1.5,0.5)  (7,2.0,1.0) (5,3.0,1.0)
bar 4 (G):  (4,0.0,2.0) (2,2.0,2.0)
```

Rising minor 6th E→C (the anguish leap), stepwise sigh down; ends hanging on
degree 2 (B) — the withheld 1. Downbeats 5/8/7/4 are chord tones of BOTH
grounds (Am:5, F:1≡8, C:7, G:4 | Am:5, Dm:1≡8, Em:7, E7:4).

**LEAP CELL** = the theme's opening (5,10) — sequenced in builds; music-box
fragment; stretto subject.

**COUNTERSUBJECT — "the collapsing staircase"** (quarters):
`bar1: 8 7 6 5 | bar2: 6 5 4 3 | bar3: 5 4 3 2 | bar4: 4 3 2 1`.
Verifier-clean over BOTH grounds (8≡1∈Am, 6∈F/Dm, 5∈C/Em, 4∈G/E7).

**TOLL MOTIF** (graft: bell derives from theme) = the leap cell inverted:
falling **10→5**; where harmony demands, simplified **8→5**; the cadence
(ninth) toll is **8, 5, 1** ending on the lone A.

**Bass lament tetrachord** (§5–6 bass line): 1 7 6 5 descending (A G F E) —
the phrygian gate F→E (bVI→V) is its last step, used verbatim at bars 72–73.

Per-section spec:

- **§1 The Veil (0–32).** The seed gesture VERBATIM and NOTHING ELSE on any
  channel — the intro stays pure (judge's fix: no bar-5 bell). Marker
  "I. The Veil".
- **§2 Processional (32–96).** Ground loops on ch0 pads (vel ~52–58, size 4,
  lo 52 hi 79, CC11 ~90). Theme on solo cello (bars 9–16, register A3,
  DEAD-straight tone — no vibrato). Bars 17–24: theme on violin +12 (still
  restrained, vibrato blooms only on the two longest notes), cello takes the
  countersubject. Timpani heartbeat A (beats 1 & 3, vel ~30–36) from bar 17.
  Contrabass pedal A whole-notes from bar 17 (vel ~40). Frame tolls #1 (beat
  32) and #2 (beat 64), pp.
- **§3 First Ascent (96–128).** The build. Frame toll #3 at beat 96 (mf).
  Organ enters (first CC1 event = 20, slow rotor) with low pedal A + fifth;
  choir "mm" bed (CC70=15) enters; ch0 strings switch from pads to pulsing
  quavers on the ground chords (vel ramp 60→92); leap cell (5,10) sequenced
  upward on violin bars 27–31 (5-10, then 6-11, then 7-12 — rising); cello
  countersubject continues; contrabass walks the tetrachord down each 2 bars;
  timpani roll crescendo bars 31–32 (rapid 16ths on A, vel ramp 50→110);
  CC11 on all sustained beds 70→115; CC91 push on bells/choir/timpani
  110→127 across bar 32 (the pre-hit bloom). The ear expects triumphant Am
  at bar 33. Marker "III. First Ascent".
- **§4 The Hit & the Hollow (128–132).** Marker "THE HIT (E major)" at 128.
  Downbeat beat 128: fortissimo E-MAJOR chord, one beat: full strings
  (E-G#-B voiced 52–79), organ (E2+B2+E3), choir "ah" stab (CC70 snapped to
  105 just for the stab), tubular bell E4 (frame toll #4), timpani E2 roll-
  flam, bass drum 36 + both crashes 49+57, piano low E1+E2 octave, vel ~118.
  All notes ≤ 1.2 beats except the solo cello: cello strikes E3 at 128 and
  holds ~3.5 beats, `bend_ramp` 0 → −0.5 st over beats 129.5–131.5 (the life
  leaving). NO other note-on in beats 129–132 (scored silence, tail only).
- **§5 Sotto Voce (132–196).** Tempo 63. Music box ALONE from beat 132:
  theme skeletal (quarter=quarter, one octave up, vel ~34, CC91=110, CC64
  down throughout the phrase). Bar 38 (beat 148): dark pad (89) ghost chord
  Am, pan 64, vel ~26, CC11 25–40. Bar 42 (beat 164): organ 16' pedal A
  creeps in pp + frame toll #5 (single low A, ppp, deep in reverb); choir
  "mm" CC70=15 long tones; harp lone harmonics (single notes A/E/C, one per
  2 bars, vel ~40). Bars 46–47 (beats 180–188): **Neapolitan Bb triad** in
  pad+choir. Bars 44–49: cello phrygian sighs Bb3→A3 — CC5/65 portamento ON
  for these sighs ONLY, off after (graft: portamento discipline); no
  vibrato anywhere in §5. Marker "V. Sotto Voce".
- **§6 Rising Tide (196–292).** Tempo staircase in conductor: 66@196,
  70@212, 74@228, 80@260. Frame toll #6 at beat 196 (pp 10→5). Bar 50
  ground returns pp on ch0 pads; bar 54 (212) theme on cello; bar 58 (228)
  theme on violin +12 with countersubject (cello) + harp 16th arps.
  **Bar 62 (beat 244) FEINT DROP**: everything cut for one bar to a pp ch0
  string sustain (Am) + frame toll #7 (single E); CC11 notch to 45.
  Bars 63–73 (248–292): full stack — timpani ostinato (beats 1 & 3 +
  quaver pickup into 1), organ ground chords (leslie ramp CC1 20→90 across
  248–292), choir CC70 morph 15→105 (the mouth opens), piano low tolling
  octaves on chord roots, contrabass tetrachord, **stretto** on the leap
  cell: cello at t, violin at t+2 beats, organ top voice at t+4 beats (bars
  67–70); string `run()` 16th sweeps with CC68 legato bars 70.5–72;
  **phrygian gate: bars 72–73 (284–292) harmony F → E major** (bVI → V, the
  knife) with CC91 push 60→127 on bells/choir/timpani across bar 73; CC74
  strings/organ opens 40→105 across the section; CC11 staircase up 50→115.
  Marker "VI. Rising Tide".
- **§7 Full Toll (292–356).** Tempo 80. Marker "VII. Full Toll". Climax
  ground **Am|Dm|Em|E7 ×3** (bars 74–85): theme ff on violin+strings 8va
  (violin +12, ch0 doubling in octaves), countersubject in cello+contrabass,
  organ power chords, piano hammered theme octaves, bells peal on every bar
  downbeat (10→5 figure on bars 74/78/82, single tones on other downbeats),
  full percussion (bass drum on 1, toms build, crash on ground starts),
  choir open "ah" (CC70=105). CC91 tutti pulled DOWN to ~60 (the room
  shrinks — terror is close and dry); CC74 full. Frame toll #8 = the peal
  entry at beat 292. Bars 86–88 (340–352): hypermetric compression, loop
  shrinks to 3 bars **Am|Dm|E7**, leap cell sequenced 5-10 (340) / 6-11
  (344) / **jammed 5-10 again** (348) — the promised third step up never
  comes, b6 scream (violin holds C6 = degree 10+7). Bar 89 (beat 352),
  marker "THE FRACTURE (Bb)": theme's last bar starts — downbeat D sounds
  (legal: third of Bb) — and the orchestra slams **Bb major** ff (one beat,
  vel ~120, bass drum + crashes + timpani Bb + bell Bb), then EVERYTHING
  cut. NO note-on in beats 353–358 (scored silence #2; CC91 was pushed
  during bar 88). The B-natural (degree 2) never sounds.
- **§8 Embers (356–404).** Tempo 60, rit to 52 from bar 98. Beat 358 (after
  ~1.5 beats of tail): music box, wrong-footed, plays the leap cell ONCE
  (5,10) vel ~30. Bars 92–95 (364–380): **the intro string gesture returns**
  — same pad_block params but vel 40, single pass Am F C G, CC11 REVERSED
  (364,90)→(380,15) — the piece exhaling. Bells: NONE until the end (the
  frame is spent — 8 tolls rung). Choir "mm" (CC70 snapped back to 15)
  long A dyad fading; timpani heartbeat (beats 1 & 3, vel 26→14) bars 92–98
  that simply STOPS at bar 99 (no note after beat 392). Bars 96–101: ch0 +
  contrabass open fifth A–E dying (CC11 → 10). Beat ~394: cadence toll
  begins — **THE NINTH BELL**: ch4 plays 8 (2 beats), 5 (2 beats), then at
  beat 398–400 the lone **A (degree 1)** with `echo_throw`, ~6 beats, the
  LAST note-on of the piece. Marker "VIII. Embers / The Ninth Bell".
