# 2026.07.07 - PLN - Design brief: a Gabriel Knight-idiom orchestral piece

## The task (Arthur)

> We've got a demo part - "orch_stem_ch0" - I love the string chords. Can you
> take that as the intro to a new piece of music - and then build on it? It
> feels like it goes somewhere dramatic and builds and drops - something like
> the intro to Gabriel Knight?

Target idiom: the *Gabriel Knight: Sins of the Fathers* title/intro feel
(Robert Holmes) — dark gothic orchestral drama, minor key, ominous tolling,
a memorable haunted theme, a big build, a sudden drop into eerie quiet, and a
larger rebuild. **Original material only** — GK-*vocabulary* gestures, no
quotation of any existing piece (repo-wide rule).

## The seed material (MUST open the piece, recognizably)

`orch_stem_ch0` is channel 0 of `demos/orchestral_demo.py` (untracked demo,
reproduced here). A natural minor (aeolian), **74 BPM**, 4/4. String ensemble:

```python
sc.channel(0, "strings", program=48, volume=88, pan=64, reverb=74)
TONIC = n("A3"); MODE = "aeolian"
prog_degs = [1, 6, 3, 7]                    # Am  F  C  G   (i bVI bIII bVII)
chords = [triad(TONIC, MODE, d, size=3) for d in prog_degs]
bed = chords + chords                        # two 4-chord passes
pad_block(sc, 0, 0.0, bed, span=4.0, size=4, lo=52, hi=79, vel=44, vel_end=70)
cc_curve(sc, 0, 11, [(0.0, 20), (8.0, 90), (16.0, 105)], step=0.5)   # swell
```

That is: 16 beats (4 bars, ~13 s) of voice-led 4-note string chords,
Am F C G twice, swelling from near-silence via CC11. The new piece's intro
must reproduce this gesture (same program/params/progression/voicing/swell)
before diverging. The i–bVI–bIII–bVII loop is the piece's harmonic home.

## Renderer constraints (hollowsynth v0.8.0 — hard limits)

MODELED (usable): piano 0–7 (incl. harpsichord 6), bells 8–15 (celesta 8,
glockenspiel 9, music box 10, tubular bells 14), organ 16–23, guitars 24–31,
basses 32–39, solo bowed strings 40–45 (all share violin body; pizz 45 sustains
— avoid; tremolo 44 has no trem), harp 46, timpani 47, string ensemble 48–51,
choir 52–54 (formant "mm-ah", CC70 vowel morph), flutes/winds 72–79,
pads 80–95, crystal 96–103, banjo 104–107, GM drums on ch 9.

**UNMODELED — NEVER use: GM 55–71** (orchestra hit, ALL brass, ALL reeds/sax
— they render as a plucked steel guitar). No brass fanfares; gothic drama must
come from strings/choir/organ/bells/timpani/percussion instead (which suits
the GK cathedral idiom).

Honoured controllers: pitch bend ±2 st, CC1 vibrato (Leslie speed on organs),
CC5/65 portamento, CC64 sustain, CC68 legato slur, CC70 vowel morph (choir),
CC74 brightness/wah, CC91/93/94 reverb/chorus/echo sends, CC11 expression,
RPN 0 bend range, channel aftertouch.

## Non-negotiable repo lessons (from lessons_learnt.md)

- **Write the musical oracle before the music** — themes are composed to pass
  a downbeat/chord-tone verifier; the verifier is written first.
- **Centre sustained beds (pan 64)**; get stereo width from transient sources
  (harp, bells, piano, percussion) — off-centre sustained pads mono-collapse
  via the pan Haas delay.
- Same-pitch overlapping notes are resolved by the engine's
  `_resolve_overlaps()` (already in this engine.py) — keep it.
- Re-measure the assembled build, not just movements (one seeded RNG stream).
- Verify headline effects on rendered stems (hollowsynth `--solo`), not just
  in the event data.

## Shape requirements (from Arthur's ask)

1. Intro = the loved string chords (verbatim gesture), ~4–8 bars.
2. It "goes somewhere dramatic": a strong original theme must emerge.
3. "Builds and drops": at least one full crescendo into a **hit + sudden
   drop to eerie near-silence**, then a larger rebuild to the real climax.
4. GK-idiom colours: tolling bells, gothic choir, organ, timpani, dark
   harmonic colour (harmonic-minor V, Neapolitan bII, phrygian bII–i moves).
5. Single track, roughly 5–7 minutes, single continuous dramatic arc.

## What the design panel must deliver (each proposal, plain text)

1. A movement-by-movement form map (name, bar/beat span, tempo, what
   happens, which channels active, dynamic level 0–10) with an explicit
   **dynamic-arc contour** (the build/drop/build shape as numbers).
2. An original main THEME as scale degrees over the 4-bar ground
   Am | F | C | G (chord tone on EVERY bar downbeat), range ≤ a 12th,
   8–16 notes, with a stated contour/character rationale; plus (optional)
   a reharmonized climax ground it also fits.
3. Channel/instrument plan (only modeled programs!) with pan/reverb/volume,
   respecting the pan-64-for-sustained-beds rule.
4. The controller-gesture plan (which CC effects carry the drama where).
5. A title suggestion for the piece.
