# 2026.07.09 — HLD — "Through Lines" (Claude Fable 5 double album)

## 1. Brief

Arthur's commission, 2026.07.09: a set of advanced MIDI pieces —

1. Something that represents Claude Fable 5.
2. Three tracks inspired by "The Fine Line" (AquaTheater show, Harmony of the Seas).
3. Something that represents the state of the world today.
4. Something that represents evolution.
5. Something inspired by the middle section of "A Day in the Life" (the McCartney part).
6. Something inspired by the Abbey Road side-2 medley flow ("You Never Give Me Your
   Money" → "The End").
7. Something representing the progression Claude 1 → today.
8. An epic, fast-paced film-score piece inspired by the James Bond Theme.
9. Five pieces of the composer's choosing.

Wide MIDI feature range required; new ferrosintesis instruments/features are in
scope. **All "inspired by" tracks are original music in the named idiom — no
copyrighted melodies, chord sequences, or hooks are quoted.**

## 2. Album concept

**"Through Lines"** — a 15-track double album by Claude Fable 5. Every piece is a
line: a lineage, a high wire, a fault line, a bass line, a timeline, a barometer
trace, a Morse line, a through-line. Two devices bind the album and are
machine-verified wherever they recur ("through-line oracles"):

- **The FABLE cell** — pitch motif F–A–B♭–E (the L is silent: scored as a rest).
  Major third up, semitone up, tritone down. The embedded tritone gives it bite
  (action cues) and its resolution E→F gives it tenderness (the closer).
- **Morse lanes** — the engine's `morse()` helper taps meaningful text as rhythm
  (each occurrence documented per track, decodable by oracle).

Disc 1, *Lines of Descent* — the ideas (self, lineage, evolution, world, memory,
process, weather). Disc 2, *Lines of Flight* — the spectacles (the Fine Line
trilogy, the action cue, night jazz, bronze, the suite, the lamp going out).

Recurring material across tracks (all oracle-pinned):
- FABLE cell: stated in T1, becomes the ground bass of T14(g), augmented 4× as the
  music-box melody of T15.
- The "bridge chorale" (a fragile 4-part hymn): interrupted repeatedly in T4,
  returns healed at the dawn of T7.
- Fine Line trilogy motifs (T8 dive-cascade, T9 walker theme, T10 orbit riff)
  stack in T10's finale — counterpoint oracle across tracks.
- CLAUDE Morse rhythm (-.-. .-.. .- ..- -.. .) is the rhythmic signature of T2.

## 3. Track designs

Times/keys are targets; oracles pin the structural claims, not the stopwatch.
Every track: sustained beds at pan 64 (mono-collapse rule); width only from
transient sources; GM43-style low beds floored at C2; bends recentred at section
boundaries; program whitelist per track.

### Disc 1 — Lines of Descent

**T1 · "Five Fables" (self-portrait of Fable 5) — ~5:00, F major/modal, varies.**
Five instrumental characters each state the FABLE cell in a different idiom, then
interleave into one fabric — many voices, one model. (i) Baroque: solo flute canon
at the fifth, 3/4. (ii) Romantic: strings, rubato tempo map, wide dynamics.
(iii) Jazz: guitar over swung brushes (2:1 swing oracle). (iv) Electronic: quantized
saw lead, filter (CC74) sweeps, four-on-floor. (v) Voice: choir on CC70 vowel
morphs. Finale: all five in 5-voice counterpoint (consonance oracle on downbeats),
collapsing to a unison F.
Oracles: FABLE cell fidelity per section (transposition-aware); swing ratio in
(iii); quantization-grid tightness in (iv) vs. loosened jitter elsewhere; 5-voice
downbeat consonance in the finale; unison ending.

**T2 · "Scaling Laws" (Claude 1 → Fable 5) — ~7:00, D dorian → D major.**
One theme (rhythm = CLAUDE in Morse) played through the model generations; the
arrangement's *capability set* grows monotonically, era by era, and the growth
itself is the oracle. Era markers name each release. Claude 1: monophonic,
velocity fixed at 80, hard-quantized, one octave range, zero CCs, metronomic 100
bpm. Claude 2: second voice (parallel organum), a "hallucinated" wrong note that
the next phrase gracefully corrects. Claude 3 era: a trio — Haiku (quick celesta
figures), Sonnet (piano, balanced), Opus (deep strings) in conversation; first
dynamics. 3.5/3.7: CC11 expression arcs, rubato, a long fugato ("extended
thinking"). 4/4.1: full orchestra, dense counterpoint. 4.5: subtlety — fewer notes,
more space, first pitch bends. Fable 5: the theme free — vowels, Leslie, bends,
aftertouch, tempo elasticity — resolving D dorian → D major.
Oracles: **monotonic capability** (era N's channel count, CC-number vocabulary,
velocity variance, jitter magnitude all ⊆/≤ era N+1's); era-1 fixed-velocity and
grid checks; CLAUDE Morse rhythm decodes from the theme; marker grid.

**T3 · "Descent with Modification" (evolution) — ~8:00, A minor pentatonic → full chromatic.**
The engine literally runs a seeded genetic algorithm over 4-bar motifs; fitness =
the verify oracle (ground consonance + contour smoothness + rhythmic viability);
generations become sections. (i) Primordial: drone + one repeating note. (ii) First
replicators: the motif copies with mutation errors. (iii) Cambrian explosion:
8-voice polyphony of variants. (iv) Selection: low-fitness variants' channels
starve and fade; fit ones thrive. (v) Extinction: orchestral crash; texture cut to
a tenth. (vi) Re-radiation: survivors diversify into instrument niches.
(vii) Emergence: the fittest lineage harmonized as a chorale — ends on a half
cadence (the story isn't over).
Oracles: mean population fitness non-decreasing generation-to-generation (evolution
demonstrably ran); per-bar voice-count curve matches the speciation/extinction
contour; extinction dynamics (post-crash bar energy ≤ 0.1× peak); the chorale's
soprano is a documented descendant (mutation-path ledger) of the primordial motif.

**T4 · "Fault Lines" (the state of the world, 2026) — ~6:00, C minor vs F♯ minor.**
Two tectonic blocks a tritone apart, each with its own meter (4/4 vs 7/8), grind
against each other. A woodblock news-ticker taps Morse ("HOLD THE LINE"). A
doomscroll ostinato accelerates and fragments. Between the blocks: the bridge
chorale — thin, 4-part, repeatedly cut off mid-phrase by orchestra-hit shocks. Coda:
both blocks play the chorale *simultaneously in their own keys*, rhythmically
united — coexistence, not resolution; ends on a quiet rising minor sixth.
Oracles: bitonal key-membership per block; chorale interruption pattern (every
statement before the coda is truncated; the coda statement is complete in both
keys at once); ostinato accelerando measured on inter-onset times; Morse decode.

**T5 · "The 8:32" (the McCartney middle-section idiom) — ~3:30, E♭ major → whole-tone lift.**
Original commuter vignette: brisk dry piano eighths (~126 bpm), walking bass, tight
hats; breathless 2-bar wind-lead phrases separated by gasping rests; sound-painting
(alarm bell, brushed-snare footsteps accelerating, string-gliss departure whistle).
Then the dream pivot: cymbal swell, tempo halves, meter dissolves, wordless choir
(CC70 "ah") climbs a whole-tone slope with harp arpeggi into floating light — and
hangs there, unresolved, handing straight into silence. No Beatles material quoted.
Oracles: groove density + phrase-gap rule (lead rests ≥ 1 beat between cells);
dream section pitch collection ⊆ whole-tone scale; tempo-map halving; choir-band
RMS lift in the render.

**T6 · "Two Rooms, One Clock" (process music) — ~5:00, C lydian.**
Reich-inspired continuous phasing, exact where tape loops drifted: pattern A
(12-note mallet figure, hard left, transient source) repeats with period P;
pattern B (hard right) repeats with period P×1.01 — float-beat onsets let the
phase sweep continuously through echo, canon, hocket, unison. A third centre
channel enters halfway playing only the emergent "resultant" accents; slow
dynamic arc; ends the cycle after B laps A exactly once.
Oracles: A and B note-sequences identical; phase offset grows linearly (per-cycle
onset delta constant); B laps A exactly once (total drift == P); resultant
channel's onsets ⊆ union of A∪B onsets (±ε); pan discipline (A/B hard-panned
transients, bed centred).

**T7 · "German Bight" (storm at sea) — ~6:30, D minor → D major dawn.**
A gale crosses a sea area, told as lines: the *barometer* — a chromatic lane
descending for the whole first half, one semitone per phrase (a literal falling
trace); the *swell* — 9/8 in three-wave groups; the *signal* — a coastal-station
bell tapping "GERMAN BIGHT" in Morse during the eye. Calm glass (harp + string
harmonics) → freshening (wind-voice breath rises) → Gale One (timpani rolls, brass
surges, wave-crash cymbals) → the eye (near-silence, the Morse bell, one flute) →
Gale Two, bigger → clearing: the bridge chorale from T4 returns *unbroken* at dawn
(through-line oracle), open fifths at sunrise.
Oracles: barometer lane strictly monotonic descending until the eye; storm-arc RMS
contour (calm < gale1 < gale2 == max; eye ≤ 0.25× gale1; dawn decrescendo); Morse
decodes to "GERMAN BIGHT"; T4-chorale note-fidelity, this time with no truncation.

### Disc 2 — Lines of Flight

**T8 · "Ten Metres of Air" (Fine Line I: the high dive) — ~5:00, B♭ minor → B♭ major.**
The climb: an ascending sequence that modulates up a minor third per platform,
heartbeat kick quickening. The board: time stretches — texture thins to one high
flute over the heartbeat. The jump: **one full bar of scored silence.** The plunge:
a four-octave descending cascade (strings, harp glissando), cymbal choke into the
splash (gong + low tom + sub swell). Underwater: dark, slow, CC74 closed, bends
like refracted light. Surfacing: the orchestra hit of the crowd, celebration groove.
Oracles: per-section mean-register ascent; modulation ladder (key centres rise by
m3); the silence bar has zero events and near-noise-floor RMS in the render;
underwater section authors CC74 low + audio spectral-centroid drop; splash peak =
global max.

**T9 · "Wirewalker" (Fine Line II: the slackline) — ~4:30, A minor, 5/8+6/8.**
The literal fine line: a solo walker melody confined to an ambitus of a perfect
fifth (the wire), scored over a huge registral void — bass far below, high
harmonics far above, **nothing in the middle two octaves** except the walker.
Alternating 5/8+6/8 (each phrase re-balances); wobbles are pitch bends that always
recover to centre within a beat; midway, the stumble — a dropped-beat 4/8 bar and
a dissonant lurch — recovered with grace. The far platform: the void finally fills
with a warm chord — safe.
Oracles: walker ambitus ≤ P5 until the final section; the register-gap (no
non-walker notes between C3 and C5 until the platform chord); bend excursions
recentre ≤ 1 beat; meter pattern (5/8 6/8 alternation, exactly one 4/8 bar).

**T10 · "Three-Sixty" (Fine Line III: the 360° finale) — ~5:30, E minor, 128 bpm.**
The panorama: a transient arp ostinato whose CC10 pan *orbits* the theatre — one
full rotation per 8 bars (transient source only; beds stay centred per the
mono-collapse rule). Antiphonal brass calls left/right; four-on-floor with
orchestral hits; two build-drop cycles (the second with full choir); a half-time
aerial bridge (suspended high strings, harp); finale sprint stacks the dive
cascade (T8), the walker theme (T9) and the orbit riff in counterpoint.
Oracles: pan lane completes ≥ N full cycles (cyclic CC10 sequence, monotonic
within each sweep); build-drop RMS contour ×2 with drop2 > drop1; trilogy recall —
note-fidelity against T8/T9 source material + downbeat-consonance of the stack;
mono-sum loss ≤ 2 dB despite the orbit (transients only).

**T11 · "Night Train to Tirana" (Bond-idiom action cue) — ~5:00, C minor, 168 bpm 12/8→7/8.**
Original spy-action material (no Bond quotes; Tuxedo Noir already owns spy-lounge —
this is its opposite: a symphonic sprint). Relentless driving bass ostinato with a
chromatically creeping inner line; close-voiced stabbing brass; twang guitar lead
with fall-off bends and an RPN-12 whammy dive; a suave reed secondary theme (the
bar car, briefly suave, swung); the roof fight — metric modulation 12/8→7/8,
timpani, orchestra hits; train whistle as brass cluster gliss, brakes as string
gliss + cymbal choke; ends on a brutal unison sting (not a chord — see Tuxedo
Noir differentiation).
Oracles: ostinato relentlessness (bass onset-grid coverage ≥ 90% of eighths across
the action sections); stab voicing (≥3 notes, span ≤ octave, duration ≤ 0.5 beat);
meter map (12/8 then 7/8 then 12/8); bend hygiene incl. the whammy dive authored
via RPN bend-range 12; unison final event (all melodic channels on C, one octave
apart allowed).

**T12 · "Three Flights Up" (night jazz) — ~4:30, F major, 92 bpm swing.**
Rubato solo-piano intro (tempo-map sculpted), then brushes at a 2:1 swing (oracle),
walking bass with chromatic approach notes, a darkened-trumpet head (CC74-muted),
trading fours piano→guitar→drums (markers), one shout-chorus key lift, tag ending
×3. The album's only straight-ahead swing.
Oracles: swing ratio window on the ride/hat lane; walking-bass approach rule
(beat-4 note within ±2 semitones of the next downbeat root); trading structure
(strict 4-bar alternation of solo channels); head-out = head-in (transposed)
fidelity; tag ×3.

**T13 · "Bronze Water" (gamelan-inspired) — ~5:30, slendro-ish 5-tone via bends.**
Colotomic bronze: gong cycle every 16 beats; nested layers each at 2× the density
of the layer below (density-hierarchy oracle); a slendro-approximating tuning table
authored as per-channel static pitch-bend offsets (RPN bend range + fixed bends,
documented in material.py); midway an irama shift — tempo halves while surface
density doubles (the gamelan gear-change); a bowed rebab-like line floats free;
final section: the bronze cycle and a Western string chorale coexist.
Oracles: gongs land exactly on cycle boundaries; layer-density ratios == 2:1 chain;
tuning table authored exactly (bend values per channel); irama transition (tempo
map halves while note density doubles across the seam); bend recentre exemption
documented for the static detune channels.

**T14 · "The Estuary Suite" (Abbey Road side-2 medley dramaturgy) — ~13:00, multi-key.**
Seven original songs + hidden epilogue, seamlessly segued; the *flow* is the homage
(ballad → dream → two grotesques → soul → lullaby-anthem → finale with trading
solos → silence → tiny acoustic afterthought); zero Beatles material.
(a) *The Ledger* — wistful piano ballad, A minor→C, that double-times into
honky-tonk and collapses into (b) *Sun on the Estuary* — one chord breathing,
choir + Leslie organ, birdsong flute; a held bass note segues into (c) *Mr.
Mudlark* — 60-second fuzz-riff grotesque stomp; hard cut to (d) *Polly on the
Towpath* — 75-second cheeky slide-guitar rocker; crash into (e) *Out of My
Window* — mid-tempo soul with a syncopated hook; winds down into (f) *Slumber
Line* — tender lullaby swelling into (g) *Carry the Current* — anthemic chorale
whose descending ground bass **is the FABLE cell** (through-line oracle), building
into (h) *The End of the Line* — the album's only drum break, then three leads
(guitar/organ/fiddle) trade 2-bar solos ×3 rounds, final chorale resolves the
FABLE cell against its own inversion to the major tonic. ~20 seconds of silence,
then (i) the hidden epilogue: 15 seconds of solo pluck quoting *The Ledger*,
cut off mid-phrase.
Oracles: segue continuity (no inter-song gap > 0.5 s except the pre-epilogue gap
= 18–22 s); per-song key/tempo map matches spec; (g) ground == FABLE cell;
(i) quotes (a) (transposed note-fidelity, truncated mid-phrase); trading-solo
alternation structure; total duration window.

**T15 · "Landing Lights" (closer) — ~3:30, F major.**
After the epic, a small direct goodnight: music box states the FABLE cell in 4×
augmentation over centred strings and low choir; a bell whispers "GOODNIGHT" in
Morse; the last note is a single high F — the album's first pitch — fading.
Oracles: melody == 4× augmentation of the material.py cell; Morse decode; final
note pitch == F5/F6 and is the piece's last event; last-minute RMS monotonic
decrescendo.

## 4. Engine & conventions (from repo survey)

- Shape: **movements-shape multi-track album** (Winter Guests `Part` pattern): one
  `Part` per track, per-part SEED/MOVEMENTS/TEMPO_MAP/CHANNELS/whitelists, one
  Score → one MIDI per track, `movements/tNN_*.py` modules.
- `engine.py` copied **verbatim** from The Ninth Bell (the most-evolved 53-def
  engine). New album-specific helpers (genetic-algorithm operators for T3, phase
  scheduler for T6, colotomy for T13, capability-era gates for T2) live in
  `material.py`/per-track modules, NOT in engine.py — engine stays shared.
- Oracle-first: `material.verify_material()` + per-track oracles in `verify.py`
  before composition; `analyze.py` mirrors headline claims in render RMS/pitch;
  `build.py`/`--verify`/`--check` contract identical to The Ninth Bell.
- Disciplines enforced: pan-64 beds, GM43/C2 floor, `_resolve_overlaps`, bend
  recentre (with documented static-detune exemptions for T13), gap whitelists for
  the scored silences (T8 jump bar, T14 pre-epilogue), program whitelist per track.
- `render_opus.py::ALBUMS` gains one entry:
  `"albums/fable5/Through Lines": ("Through Lines", "Claude Fable 5", <genre>)`.

## 5. Synth extensions (ferrosintesis)

Coverage survey result: almost everything the album needs **already exists** —
mallets/bells GM 8-14 (celesta, glock, music box, vibes w/ tremolo, marimba,
xylophone, tubular bells), harp 46 (Pluck + soundboard), timpani 47 (Modal),
brass 56-63 (incl. muted trumpet 59), reeds 64-71, winds, choir w/ CC70 vowels,
pizzicato via CC0 alt-bank on 40-45, drum-kit v2 via non-zero ch-10 program.
NB: The Ninth Bell's "GM 55-71 unmodeled" whitelist check is **stale** (pre-v0.9)
— do not copy it into this album's verify.py.

The genuine gaps this album hits, and the reduced extension set (current crate
version 0.10.7; one minor bump → 0.11.0):

1. **GM 112-119 proper voices** — tinkle bell, agogo, steel drums, woodblock,
   taiko, melodic tom, synth drum, reverse cymbal. Today ALL eight fall through
   to `Pluck(STEEL)` (undocumented). Serves: taiko (T7 storm, T8 splash, T11
   action), reverse cymbal (T5 dream pivot, T8, T10 builds), tinkle bell + agogo
   (T13 gamelan pots/kemanak), melodic tom + synth drum (T10 EDM fills), steel
   drums + woodblock (completes the block honestly).
2. **CC0 alt-bank "tam-tam/gong ageng" on GM 14 (tubular bells)** — deep pitched
   gong with slow bloom, ~60-120 Hz fundamentals. Serves T13 colotomy + T8
   splash. Alt-bank is opt-in by construction.
3. **Brush kit: channel-10 program 40** (GM2 brush-kit number) — brushed snare
   tap, brush sweep, softer hats, rim click. Serves T12, T5, T1(iii). The
   Kit::V1/V2 program-change mechanism already exists; add Kit::Brush.
4. *(Optional, cheap while in the dispatch)* GM 15 dulcimer as a hammered-zither
   Pluck instead of the current silent fold into tubular bells — used by T14(i)
   epilogue if available; NOT load-bearing.

Invariant discipline: items 1 and 4 change the rendering of any committed file
that already authors those programs — scan every `render_opus.py::ALBUMS` MIDI
(the listening blast radius) for programs 112-119/15, ch-10 program changes, and
CC0-with-GM14 BEFORE implementation; make the waiver set explicit (expected:
empty). Items 2 and 3 are opt-in by mechanism. Each new voice gets numeric
oracles (Goertzel peak for pitch claims, matched-pitch spread oracles per the
lessons file) + the golden-fixture canaries stay bit-exact + baseline-worktree
`cmp` byte-identity proof on all committed albums.

## 7. Risks

- Scope: ~87 minutes of music. Mitigation: per-track independence (own Part/seed),
  workflow fan-out, and the suite (T14) treated as its own mini-album internally.
- Synth work gates audio verification for tracks using new voices. Mitigation:
  implement synth extensions first; compose MIDI in parallel (MIDI structure
  doesn't depend on the renderer).
- Cross-track through-line oracles create coupling (T4↔T7, T8/T9→T10, T1→T14/T15).
  Mitigation: the shared material lives in material.py as data; oracles compare
  against material.py, never against another track's rendered file.
- Tuxedo Noir adjacency (T11): differentiated by tempo world, orchestration, and
  ending (unison sting vs m(maj9)); no swing in the A material.
