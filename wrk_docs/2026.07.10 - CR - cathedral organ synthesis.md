## Separate simplicity pass

  and exits immediately for fixed `--wet 0`, avoiding cathedral DSP cost on
  unrelated MIDI while preserving every active tail.
- Its five fixed early-reflection descriptors now use an array rather than a
  heap-allocated vector.
- No new abstraction or dependency was introduced; the three pre-sum source
  classes remain necessary for held-bank containment.

## Residual gate

can prove spectrum, level, decay, stability, and routing, but not whether the
result feels like a cathedral in the room.
