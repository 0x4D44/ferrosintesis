# Default solo bowed strings

**Status:** SIGNED-OFF

**Date:** 2026-07-10

**Mode:** Design-only until the hard sign-off gate

## Intent

Give the default-bank solo bowed family register-correct, program-specific identities without reopening the deferred string-section waveguide project or duplicating the active GM48–54 strings/choir work.

Success means violin, viola, cello, contrabass and fiddle no longer collapse to one violin body and one onset timing; the physically wrong violin transient is removed from viola/cello/bass; natural vibrato runs at its named musical rate without becoming hiss tremolo; GM44/45 become articulation-correct solo proxies; existing bend, legato and controller composition remain bounded; and the corpus changes only where a default-bank GM40–45 or GM110 note is actually rendered.

## Current evidence

- `voices.rs` routes GM40–45 and GM110 through the same `Bowed::new(key, ...)`, the same 280/610/1350 Hz violin body and, with samples enabled, the same violin attack bank.
- The nominal ~5.3 Hz natural vibrato oscillator advances once per 16-sample control tick while configured for the full sample rate. Its audible rate is therefore about 0.33 Hz. The repository already records the same defect for `Wind` and `Bowed` in `scratchpad.md`.
- The frozen alternate bank already proves a compact implementation for distinct violin/viola/cello/contrabass bodies, velocity-sensitive bow tremolo, and a violin-body pizzicato preset. It also proves useful test oracles. It must remain frozen and independent.
- A scan of the 87 MIDI files registered by `render_opus.py` found default-bank usage in 61 files: GM40 in 47, GM41 in 37, GM42 in 47, GM43 in 43, GM44 in 2, GM45 in 2, and GM110 in 6. No affected note currently selects the alternate bank.
- The active GM48–54/Iowa-steel branch has already grown the release binary from about 16.34 MiB to 17.68 MiB before its Iowa-steel sample work. A separate sample-crate split is also moving the current WAV layout. New solo-string WAV payloads would collide with both efforts and the 19 MiB target.

## Scope

### In scope

- Default-bank GM40 Violin, GM41 Viola, GM42 Cello, GM43 Contrabass and GM110 Fiddle.
- Correct default-bank GM44 Tremolo Strings and GM45 Pizzicato Strings at the same routing seam as articulation-correct solo proxies. They are included because current code does not keep them separate: both are incorrectly rendered as sampled arco violin. The alternate bank supplies a tested, small precedent.
- Correct the default `Bowed` natural-vibrato clock. Preserve engine-authored CC1 and aftertouch composition.
- Tests, corpus-diff evidence, exposed listening renders, documentation and the normal single shipped-code version bump.

### Out of scope

- GM48–54 section strings and choir, including their attack layers and choir formants. That work is already underway elsewhere.
- Any change to frozen `altbank.rs`, including its historical control-rate vibrato behavior.
- New WAVs, sample licensing work, a binary-size-cap increase, or a new sample-loading architecture.
- A waveguide/bowed-string physical-model rewrite, multi-player/ensemble identity for GM44/45, mic perspectives or new articulation-selection controls.
- The analogous `Wind` vibrato timing defect. It remains a separate scratchpad item.
- MIDI arrangement changes or compatibility machinery. This is the development sandbox.

## Approach

### 1. A small default-bank bowed preset map

Make `Bowed::new` accept the program and select a compact private preset. Keep one `Bowed` implementation and vary only stable instrument properties:

| Program | Identity | Body / brightness | Initial modeled onset |
|---|---|---|---|
| 40 | Violin | existing violin body; current pressure range | 70 ms attack base, 0.10 bite; existing violin WAV at the current handover |
| 41 | Viola | 220/475/1200 Hz body; lower pressure ceiling; ~5.1 Hz natural vibrato | 78 ms attack base, 0.095 bite; no violin WAV |
| 42 | Cello | 105/220/650 Hz body; lower pressure ceiling; ~4.8 Hz vibrato | 88 ms attack base, 0.09 bite; no violin WAV |
| 43 | Contrabass | 62/115/380 Hz body; lowest pressure ceiling; ~4.2 Hz vibrato | 102 ms attack base, 0.085 bite; no violin WAV |
| 44 | Tremolo strings proxy | violin body and pressure range | 70 ms base plus model-only 6–9 Hz bow reversals and short re-bites |
| 110 | Fiddle | violin-sized body, but quicker/brighter bow pressure and slightly quicker vibrato | 52 ms attack base, 0.12 bite; same violin WAV source at an initial 0.08–0.28 s handover |

The body and pressure starting values come from the already-shipped frozen alternate bank. Default-bank code owns its own literal values; it must not call into or share mutable constants with `altbank.rs`.

Do not add a general articulation framework. A private `BowedPreset` or equivalently small `match` is enough. Include only fields that differ in the table: body, pressure cutoff range, vibrato rate, attack base, onset bite level and optional tremolo values. Shared decay/sustain/release, scoop, pitch and steady bow noise stay in the voice. The onset numbers are starting values, not new public policy: tune them only to pass the stated tests and listening gate.

At practical rates all proposed body peaks are far below Nyquist. Preserve the alternate bank's simple Nyquist guard if the implementation uses a variable-length body collection; do not introduce allocation solely for that guard.

### 2. Fix the default natural-vibrato clock at its source

Configure the `Sine` oscillator for the effective control rate (`sr / CTRL`), matching the newer reed implementation. Keep frequency updates on the existing control cadence. Do not compensate by multiplying the named rates, and do not touch the engine-level CC1 LFO.

This deliberately changes GM40 as well as the new presets: the existing ~0.33 Hz movement is a defect, not a compatibility baseline.

Remove the current `noise_amp *= 1 + 0.4 * vib_val` coupling. Once the clock is correct, that expression would amplitude-modulate bow hiss by ±40% at 4–6 Hz—an unintended tremolo much stronger than the gentle pitch motion. Steady bow noise remains constant; GM44's explicit stroke/re-bite path owns audible periodic amplitude motion.

### 3. Make GM45 a pluck, not a bow

Route GM45 to a private default-bank violin-pizzicato `PluckPreset`: short decay, immediate excitation, violin body, no autonomous vibrato and no LA sample wrapper. Start from the alternate bank's proven PIZZ values, then change only level if the default-bank ±3 dB oracle requires it.

### 4. Make GM44 actual bow tremolo

Use the alternate bank's proven raised-cosine stroke envelope as the small starting design: 6–9 Hz across velocity, modest stroke jitter, and an 18 ms bow-noise re-bite at reversals. GM44 remains a `Bowed` voice so pitch bend and CC68 legato keep working. It never takes the sustained violin attack sample.

### 5. Keep sample use intentionally narrow

- GM40 keeps the existing violin attack configuration.
- GM110 may reuse the same physical violin source because fiddle is a playing style of that instrument, but initially uses a 0.08–0.28 s fade and a separately tuned gain so its quicker modeled bow character becomes audible. The fade may move only if the local continuity oracle requires it.
- GM41–45 do not receive the violin sample. Re-pitching a violin transient into cello and bass registers is the identity error this change removes.

No new assets are added. This accepts a known ceiling: cello and contrabass attacks remain modeled rather than recorded. If the sign-off listening gate finds that regression worse than the current mismatched transient, stop and design a separate sample-budget decision; do not silently expand this task.

### 6. Preserve expression and bank boundaries

Keep `set_pitch`, `legato_to`, release, engine CC1, channel/poly aftertouch, CC11 and CC68 behavior at their existing layers. Pizzicato follows the existing bendable `Pluck` contract. Bank selection remains spawn-time: non-zero CC0 continues to use the byte-frozen alternate bank.

Do not change `fx_profile` in this task. Program-specific bus tuning is subjective, affects full mixes broadly, and is not needed to establish instrument identity.

## Acceptance criteria

Tests are written first where practical. Prefer direct behavioral oracles over snapshots.

1. **Distinct arco identity without a loudness cheat.** Samples-off, same-seed renders for GM40–43 are not identical. At common keys 50/57/64, adjacent presets' normalized high-band fractions (`hp_rms / rms`) differ by at least 5% relative in either direction; do not use absolute high-band level as the identity oracle. At a representative natural-range key, each viola/cello/bass render lifts at least one predeclared body-band fraction (`band_rms / rms`) by 10% relative to same-pitch violin, while a separate whole-note level check stays within ±3 dB. Overall “bigger means strictly darker” is not a hard requirement; the listening gate rejects muffling.
2. **Distinct minimal onsets.** On samples-off renders, use 5 ms RMS windows and define attack-rise as the first window reaching 80% of the median steady RMS in 0.25–0.45 s. Require `GM110 < GM40 < GM41 < GM42 < GM43`, with at least 5 ms between adjacent measured rises. Do not require measured rise to equal the literal ADSR attack constant; scoop and pressure-filter opening also shape it. Only GM110 must show at least 10% more normalized high-frequency energy than GM40 in the first 80 ms; do not add more onset parameters merely to force spectral ordering across body sizes.
3. **Fiddle handover and identity.** With samples disabled, GM110 differs from GM40 in attack time and has at least 10% more normalized high-frequency energy in the first 80 ms while matching steady pitch. With samples enabled, 50 ms RMS windows around the proposed 0.08–0.28 s handover have a worst adjacent ratio no greater than 1.6; `peak_locate` stays within 45 cents through that interval. After the sample has retired (0.35–0.75 s), GM110 and GM40 normalized high-band fractions still differ by at least 5% relative; a whole-render inequality is insufficient.
4. **Correct vibrato rate without false AM.** Each sustained arco preset's output pitch trace measures within ±15% of its configured natural-vibrato rate after delay/ramp; no preset may exhibit the old ~0.3 Hz clock error. A direct control-LFO oracle may supplement, but not replace, output pitch liveness. In the 4–9 Hz envelope-autocorrelation band, each non-tremolo arco voice's periodic AM peak must be no more than 0.05 above its rebased pre-change GM40 reference and must remain below 0.20 absolute. Do not compare it only with deliberately deep GM44 tremolo.
5. **Bounded controller composition.** On GM40, GM43 and GM110 after the natural-vibrato ramp, autonomous pitch excursion remains 4–12 cents; full CC1 excursion is at least twice autonomous; and simultaneous full CC1 plus channel aftertouch stays below 75 cents total excursion. Across that composed measurement window, no 10 ms RMS window may fall below 25% of the median steady RMS. Exercise samples off for all three and samples on for GM40/110 after handover. Existing bend and CC68 legato tests remain green; add a focused legato retune test if current coverage does not exercise the new program-aware path.
6. **Correct articulation proxy.** GM44 exposes a stable 6–9 Hz amplitude stroke whose velocity trend is upward and whose reversals re-bite. GM45 has a late/early RMS decay ratio below 0.10 while GM40 remains above 0.70. Documentation calls both single-player articulation proxies, not completed ensembles.
7. **Pitch.** Low/mid/high keys stay within 45 cents using `peak_locate`: GM40/110 at 55/69/88, GM41 at 48/60/76, GM42 at 36/48/69, GM43 at 34/45/64, GM44 at 45/60/78, and GM45 at 52/64/76. Include the LA handover for GM40/110. Do not use zero-crossing counts where resonances can mislead.
8. **Sample and bank routing.** Factory tests beside `voices::make` prove samples-on/off bit identity for GM41–45 and a material difference for GM40/110. No default-bank program outside GM40–45/110 changes because of the factory edit. Non-zero CC0 continues to invoke the frozen alternate bank unchanged. Do not add program-specific assertions to generic `sampler.rs` tests.
9. **Level and numerical safety.** Samples-off, same-key whole-note RMS for GM41–43 and GM110 stays within ±3 dB of GM40. GM45 early RMS is no more than +3 dB above GM40 and stays within 10 dB of adjacent GM46 harp, matching the proven alternate-bank guard. At the current corpus-low GM43 key 34, 30–80 Hz RMS may rise by no more than +3 dB versus the rebased pre-change violin-body render. Representative two-second raw model renders are finite, have peak magnitude below 1.5, and keep settled true-DC (four-pole 8 Hz low-pass RMS divided by signal RMS) below `1e-3`.
10. **Corpus containment.** Against the rebased pre-change binary, scan every MIDI returned by `render_opus.py::all_midis`. Every file without a default-bank GM40–45/110 note is byte-identical. Every expected difference is explained by at least one such note; alternate-bank notes are excluded. Record the fresh inventory because the corpus may move before implementation.
11. **Listening gate.** Render exposed samples-on and `--no-samples` stems for violin, viola, cello, contrabass, tremolo, pizzicato and fiddle, plus full mixes from `demos/orchestral_demo`, `The Iron Tide`, `Through Lines`, `Riverwake`, and the affected synth-feature showcase. Arthur checks identity, attack handover, natural-plus-authored vibrato, bass boom, level and mix fit before integration.
12. **Committed listening freshness.** Every corpus file that differs under criterion 10 and has a committed `.opus` target from `render_opus.py` is re-rendered and committed in this task. The five named full mixes are the focused human gate, not the extent of the refresh obligation. Record the exact refreshed set and prove unaffected committed listening files remain untouched.
13. **Repository gate.** The repository's scoped tests, format, clippy with warnings denied, full tests and release build pass after the active branches are integrated. The shipped change carries exactly one patch version bump.

## Adversarial review and refutation

Two independent helper reviews completed: DSP/acoustics and simplicity/product risk. Their cross-refutation turns then failed before running because the helper harness could not refresh its access token. Per the review rule, none of those unchecked findings counted as survivors. The lead reran the refutation checks sequentially against the source and design; the following findings survived those checks and are folded above:

1. Correcting the LFO clock would turn the existing `1 + 0.4*vib_val` noise multiplier into ±40% hiss AM at 4–6 Hz. Direct data-flow inspection confirmed it; the coupling is removed and an arco-vs-tremolo AM guard is added.
2. GM41–43 attacks were underspecified. Inspection confirmed one shared `vel_attack(0.07)`, bite level and bite duration. The design now adds only attack base and bite level to the preset, with envelope checks.
3. Absolute high-band RMS can reward a quieter voice, while a strict “larger is always darker” ladder can reward muffling. The frozen test demonstrated only 5.3% absolute violin/viola separation. The replacement uses normalized high-band/body-band identity plus an independent ±3 dB level guard.
4. Correct natural vibrato composes with independent 35-cent CC1 and 25-cent aftertouch modulators. Existing tests prove only a lower-bound spread. The design now bounds the composed excursion and checks samples-on/off paths.
5. A different GM110 fade trivially makes whole renders unequal and the generic handover test permits a 2.4× adjacent-window jump. The design now fixes an initial interval and adds local continuity, pitch, early and post-handover identity checks.
6. GM44/45 remain single-voice mechanisms. They are now explicitly called articulation-correct solo proxies; ensemble identity remains out of scope.
7. Program routing is owned by `voices::make`, while `LaVoice` is program-agnostic. Program-specific sample/bank tests move beside the factory; `sampler.rs` is not expected to change.

No reviewer finding was rejected after a successful refutation check. The two failed helper refutation attempts are recorded as failed process evidence only, not as verification.

## External fresh-eyes review

A single read-only Claude Opus pass returned **approve with changes**. It used no subagents and made no edits. Triage:

- **Accepted:** the corpus-diff gate did not explicitly require all affected committed `.opus` files to be refreshed. Acceptance criterion 12 now makes the refresh set checkable while keeping five mixes as the focused human listen.
- **Accepted with corrected evidence:** the reviewer warned that the 62/115 Hz contrabass body could amplify pathological GM43 sub-bass. Its cited lesson describes old generated MIDI 12–23, but the fresh registered-corpus scan for this design found a current GM43 floor of key 34. The stale-key premise is rejected; a 30–80 Hz regression cap at actual key 34 is nevertheless useful and is now in criterion 9.
- **Accepted:** “less than half GM44” was weak because GM44 intentionally has −6 to −9 dB strokes. Criterion 4 now uses both a rebased pre-change delta and an absolute autocorrelation cap.
- **Accepted:** measured RMS rise is not the ADSR constant because scoop and pressure filtering also shape it, and the former ±25% bands overlapped. Criterion 2 now defines a measured time-to-80% feature and relative separation.

The reviewer found the central architecture sound: the solo family is separate from active GM48–54 work; `sr / CTRL` is the right clock repair; removing noise/vibrato coupling is justified; the GM44/45 split follows proven bendable mechanisms; default/alternate-bank independence is correct; `sampler.rs` need not change; and the existing pitch/legato controller seam remains appropriate.

## Decision log

- **Include GM44/45.** Rejected leaving them alone: they are not separate today, and correcting their routing uses the same factory edit plus already-proven small mechanisms. This avoids knowingly shipping “deepened strings” with tremolo and pizzicato still rendered as arco violin.
- **No new sample banks.** Rejected solo viola/cello/contrabass WAV expansion for this unit: the active branch is already consuming the size budget and another branch is relocating assets. Model-only attacks are a bounded compromise, not a claim of sample-library realism.
- **Reuse concepts, not frozen code.** Rejected sharing constants or implementation with `altbank.rs`: the alternate bank is a byte-frozen compatibility surface while the default bank must evolve.
- **Preset extension, not a new physical model.** Rejected a waveguide rewrite: it duplicates the deferred section-string expedition, raises tuning risk, and is unnecessary to fix the current program-collapse defect.
- **Fix only Bowed's clock.** Rejected bundling Wind's identical defect: it has a different corpus and listening surface and does not belong to this string-family acceptance contract.
- **Keep existing buses.** Rejected per-program chorus/echo changes until instrument identity is established and heard; otherwise bus deltas obscure whether the voice itself improved.
- **Proceed before the sample split lands.** The original conservative sequencing gate was amended with Arthur's approval after the split reached a clean commit and its exact diff proved the source overlap mechanical and isolated. This task does not copy or anticipate the new sample-package plumbing; the later split rebase preserves these voice changes and resolves the version line by taking the next patch if it lands second.

## Residual risks

- Model-only viola/cello/contrabass attacks may expose the saw-plus-noise source more than the current, physically wrong violin recording. The listening gate decides; a failure becomes a separate asset/budget design.
- This affects most of the registered corpus. Numeric tests cannot guarantee that a correct 4–6 Hz natural vibrato is tasteful in dense arrangements that already author CC1 or pitch-bend vibrato.
- The inherited body tables are compact EQ approximations, not instrument physics. They should improve register identity but will not approach a specialist solo-string library.
- GM110 “fiddle” is a style distinction on violin hardware. Its quicker/brighter/noisier preset is intentionally modest; over-separating it would be caricature.
- The active strings/choir and sample-split branches touch nearby factory and package seams. Rebasing after they land is part of the implementation, not optional cleanup.
