# HLD — organ showcase composition

Status: IMPLEMENTED

## Scope

Create one original GPT-5.6 piece that makes the new default GM19 cathedral
organ's physical strengths unmistakable on capable playback systems. Keep the
old secondary-bank organ out of the score. Do not change synthesizer code.

## Approach

- Ship a self-contained, deterministic one-track album named *The Architecture
  of Air* under `albums/gpt5-6/`.
- Use one centered GM19 channel for every headline organ event. This preserves a
  real shared wind chest and makes a meaningful organ-only render possible.
- Compose isolated windows for the 32-foot pedal, principal body, fixed
  tremulant, ten-note chest load/recovery, high mixtures, twelve-note climax,
  and room-only tail.
- Use choir, bells, and timpani only as sparse framing. They do not overlap the
  pedal, first mixture, or final decay measurements.
- Verify score structure and actual serialized MIDI in `verify.py`; verify the
  rendered signal in `analyze.py`.

## File impact

- New album bundle under `albums/gpt5-6/The Architecture of Air/`.
- One new `render_opus.py` registration and listening copy.
- GPT-5.6 index update plus task journal.
- No Rust source or crate-version change.

## Acceptance criteria

- Bank zero serializes before GM19 and the first organ note; no nonzero CC0 is
  authored.
- The composition covers the low pedal, middle principals, and MIDI 84+ mixture
  registers, with at least one ten-note same-channel wind load.
- CC1 provides measured 5.5 Hz tremulant contrast; CC10 stays centered and
  CC93/94 stay zero while CC91 strongly drives the cathedral room.
- The final twelve-note cadence releases into at least 30 beats without notes.
- Deterministic rebuild, structural oracles, audio oracles, repository tests,
  formatting, and listening render all pass.

## Decision log

- A new one-track album preserves the completed fourteen-track *Atlas of
  Becoming* rather than reopening its suite contract.
- The proven showcase engine was copied unchanged because album engines are
  deliberately self-contained; its CC0 priority guarantees correct bank order.
- One organ channel is preferable to simulated manuals on separate channels:
  the model's wind load is channel-global, and the audio stem remains exact.
- The score avoids generic chorus, delay, pan Haas, and filter sweeps so those
  effects cannot claim credit for the organ's width, brightness, or movement.
