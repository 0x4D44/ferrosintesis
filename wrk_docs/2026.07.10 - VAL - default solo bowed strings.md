## Corpus-low contrabass guard

A key-34 GM43 probe was rendered from both release binaries with samples and wet effects disabled. A Hann-windowed FFT measured 30–80 Hz energy over the settled 0.60–1.80 second interval.

- Baseline band magnitude: `4929.0544`
- Final band magnitude: `6700.6297`
- Final change: **+2.667 dB**
- Signed limit: **no more than +3.0 dB**

The first candidate was +4.843 dB and correctly failed. The final preset preserves the 62 Hz identity guard and family-level guard by moving support into the existing 115 Hz mode, rather than hiding the regression with whole-voice attenuation.
