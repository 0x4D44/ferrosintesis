# HLD — Bright Matter five-track build/drop album

**Date:** 2026-07-11  
**Status:** implemented  
**Owner:** GPT-5.6

## Context

`Through Lines` track 10, “Three-Sixty,” demonstrates a particularly effective large
form for this repository: a memorable transient loop, staged builds, two drops, a
literal half-time clearing, a larger second return, and a finale that recombines
established material. `Bright Matter` applies that grammar to five new compositions
without copying its melody, harmony, instrumentation map, or arrangement.

The album-wide harmonic request is diatonic **vi–V–ii–I** (6–5–2–1). That sequence is
stated literally in the bass of every track and is verified at score level rather than
left as prose.

## Goals

1. Add five upbeat, catchy pieces with genuine build/drop contrast and dramatic final
   recombination.
2. Keep every artifact deterministic and standard-library-only, matching repository
   composition conventions.
3. Use ferrosintesis-specific expressive features: CC11 organ drive, choir vowel CC70,
   pitch-bend hygiene, modeled guitar, alt-bank melodic percussion, transient pan, and
   reverse-cymbal rises.
4. Make “bigger” measurable: second drops must exceed first drops, hush sections must
   remove the rhythmic floor, and the finale must prove four simultaneous themes.
5. Commit source, finished MIDI, manifest, notes, and exact-stem listening guides; wire
   the album into `render_opus.py` for reproducible listening copies.

## Non-goals

- No transcription or close imitation of “Three-Sixty.” Its influence is limited to
  pacing and production grammar.
- No new Rust synth feature is required; the current modeled instrument surface is
  already broad enough.
- No checked-in WAV scratch files. Canonical audio remains reproducible from MIDI and
  the repository synth.

## Package architecture

```
albums/gpt5-6/Bright Matter/
├── engine.py                 deterministic SMF writer/parser
├── material.py               6–5–2–1 ground + four original hooks
├── tracks/
│   ├── common.py             voice leading, grooves, builds, fills, introspection
│   └── t01_...py .. t05_...py
├── build.py                  build, manifest, deterministic replay, verify entrypoint
├── verify.py                 generic and track-specific score oracles
├── analyze.py                optional rendered-WAV checks
├── midi/                     five committed outputs
├── lyrics/                   exact-stem instrumental listening guides
├── album_manifest.json
├── README.md
└── ALBUM.md
```

`build.py` builds each score twice and requires byte identity before writing. During
verification it also reparses the committed SMF and checks that it is fresh.

## Musical design

| # | Track | Tonal centre | Headline mechanism |
|---|-------|--------------|--------------------|
| 1 | Six-Five-Two-One | C → D major | full-circle steel-pan orbit, escalating fills, lifted finale |
| 2 | Runway Aurora | E major | alternating runway-light hocket, distorted guitar bends, tempo acceleration |
| 3 | Gravity Has a Chorus | D major | 7/8 ↔ 4/4 gravity changes, two choir vowels, full cathedral-organ arrival |
| 4 | All the Lights Arrive Late | A major | sixteen-beat false drop with every element except the kick, then delayed impact |
| 5 | Bright Matter (Everything at Once) | E → F-sharp major | four earlier hooks in simultaneous counterpoint, orbit, organ, and maximal key lift |

Every track follows a recognisable energy grammar but has different surface rhythm,
meter, timbral identity, hook contour, bridge behaviour, and climax mechanism.

## Verification strategy

Generic checks cover note/channel density, duration windows, modeled-program
whitelists, controller reset, centre-safe sustained beds, antiphonal/transient stereo,
well-formed markers, and repeated downbeat 6–5–2–1 transitions.

Track checks cover:

- exact bass roots at drop downbeats;
- second-drop velocity mass relative to the first;
- near-drumless negative-space sections;
- full-circle pan extrema and cycle count;
- bend range and centre reset at boundaries;
- meter changes, choir vowel range, and organ CC11 span;
- false-drop kick absence and delayed arrival;
- finale hook simultaneity and pitch-set identity;
- final key-lift summit.

`analyze.py` optionally checks rendered 16-bit stereo WAVs for peak range, mono-sum
loss, section dynamic spread, post-hush impact, and accidental interior silence.

## Delivery and integration

The root album count/list is updated, `render_opus.py` claims the new MIDI directory,
and the album provides exact-stem UTF-8 listening guides for Opus metadata. This change
ships canonical source and MIDI. Tagged Opus files may be generated later with the
normal repository synth/encoder toolchain; no generic-synth audition render is kept.

## Risks and mitigations

- **Maximal sections become undifferentiated.** Mitigated by distinct register and
  rhythm assignments plus a real vacuum before the finale.
- **Wide motion harms mono compatibility.** Only transient orbit/hocket voices move;
  sustained bass, pad, choir, strings, organ, and lead remain centred or antiphonal.
- **Pitch bends leak into later notes.** Every bend phrase returns to centre and the
  album-wide hygiene oracle verifies final state.
- **Claims drift from artifacts.** Manifest generation, exact-stem sidecar validation,
  deterministic replay, and structural oracles are part of the build path.
