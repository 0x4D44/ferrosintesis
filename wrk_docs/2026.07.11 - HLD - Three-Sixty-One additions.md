# HLD — Three-Sixty-One (the bonus reprise of T10)

**Author of the composition design: Fable 5** (the original composer of "Three-Sixty"),
engaged as a read-only consult. **Owner / implementer / verifier: Claude (lead).**
This document captures Fable's reasoning so it survives past the session; the code is in
`albums/fable5/Through Lines/movements/t16_three_sixty_one.py`.

## 1. What and why

Arthur asked for a variant of T10 "Three-Sixty" — **"Three-Sixty-One"** — that adds, on top
of the existing 360° AquaTheater finale:

1. an **overdriven/distorted guitar solo** that builds in through the second climb and soars
   on top of the finale stack ("layered on top of everything else");
2. a **choir counterpoint** — a genuine independent countermelody — arriving later on;
3. **escalating-density drum fills** through the builds (Arthur liked the big tom fill at ~1:14
   and wanted "an almost constant set of varied drum fills as we build").

Placement (Arthur's call): **track 16 on *Through Lines*** — a bonus track that reuses the
album's engine. The original T10 is untouched, note-for-note; the whole 360° architecture of
T16 is identical to T10 plus the three new layers. No synth change was needed — ferrosintesis
already renders GM programs 29/30 through a real `tanh` waveshaper + cabinet filter.

## 2. Voices and channels

T10 uses MIDI channels 0–13; 14 and 15 were free.

- **ch14 — distorted guitar (GM30).** Program 30 over 29: the heavier harmonic mass through the
  tanh stage keeps a single lead note audible on top of the five-lane fortissimo stack.
  Expression: **CC1** mod-wheel vibrato swells (the synth's 5.3 Hz LFO) carry the sustained
  wail, so the **pitch-bend lane (`0xE0`) carries only the marked bend gestures** — every one
  released to 0 before its section boundary, keeping `check_bend_hygiene` clean.
- **ch15 — choir counterpoint (GM53 Voice Oohs).** GM53 against the existing ch8 GM52 (Aahs)
  bed gives program contrast; CC70 held ≤ 40 ("oo" vs the bed's "ah") gives vowel contrast.
  **No pitch bends** on ch15 (so it never enters the hygiene check).
- Both new voices are **centred (CC10 64)** — they are sustained tonal sources, and off-centre
  sustained voices comb-filter in mono via the pan Haas delay (the repo's mono-collapse lesson).
- Fills reuse **ch10 (melodic tom, GM117)** and **ch11 (synth drum, GM118)**.

## 3. The two harmonic constraints that shaped every pitch

1. **The A-beat trap.** In the finale, the recalled **walker** (`WALKER_THEME`, root E5) onsets
   its degree-4 (= **A5**) on every beat ≡ 4 (mod 8): 548, 556, 564 … 636. Any note *sounding
   across* one of those beats must be consonant with **both** the tonic pedal E **and** that A.
   Intersecting the two consonant sets leaves only pitch-classes **{E, A, C}**. G and B are
   whole-tone traps against the A. Every long guitar/choir note is placed against this grid.
2. **E over D-bars.** In the builds/drops the chord loop is Em/C/G/D (8 beats each). A sustained
   E crossing a structural downbeat of a D-chord bar is a whole-tone dissonance, so the guitar
   never holds E across those beats (440/472/504/536 in drop two; 344/376/408 in build two).

Consonance test throughout = the module's `_CONSONANT = {0,3,4,5,7,8,9}` on `(a−b) mod 12`,
checked **only on structural downbeats** (every 4 beats) — passing dissonance between beats is
expected in a lead line.

## 4. The three additions (design summary; exact note tables are in the module)

- **Guitar solo.** Silent until beat 346. **Build two [320,416):** three fragments, strictly
  escalating (4/10/13 notes), mid-register, answering the climb. **Drop two [416,544):** four
  hook rotations, register rising 83→91, the "scream" bends, a descending hand-off in rotation 4
  (contrary to the rising choir). **Finale [544,640):** sparse, high, everything ≥ B5(83),
  peaking on a bent **A6(93)** scream at 602, then a farewell tone that exits at 632 — one full
  stack statement before the splashdown, so the outro hit lands on a cleared stage. The finale
  is *sparser but louder/higher* by design — a soaring line, not a shred.
- **Choir counterpoint.** Enters at 512 (final drop-two rotation) rising while the guitar
  descends (contrary motion is the entry's point), then a true countermelody through the finale,
  range E4–D5 (an octave under the solo). Predominantly contrary/oblique motion, rhythmically
  independent, resolving to the tonic at 628 and holding to 638.
- **Drum fills.** A six-shape library (A "comma" / B "descend" / C "lift" / D "gallop" /
  E "roll-drop" / F "sputter" + the existing 16-note BIG). Per-8-bar-window note counts rise
  **22 < 39 < 66** (build one) and **28 < 47 < 71** (build two), peaking in a ~6-beat unbroken
  fill into each drop (E→BIG at 153/156, F→BIG at 410/412); drops stay ≤ 8 fill notes/window.

## 5. Oracles (composed-to-pass; all green)

Three new falsifiable checks in the module's `oracles()`:

- **`guitar_solo_arc`** — silent before build two; window counts rise; mean register B2<D2<FIN;
  density rises into drop two; finale louder than drop two; min finale pitch ≥ 83; ≥ 5 phrases
  and a ≥ 4-beat rest in each of drop two / finale; and on every structural downbeat in
  [320,640) the bend-effective guitar pitch is consonant with the chord root **and**, in the
  finale, pairwise consonant with every stack onset (ch0/5/6) and the sounding counterpoint.
- **`choir_counterpoint`** — enters only at/after 512; ≥ 6 drop-two + ≥ 20 finale onsets; none in
  the outro; max choir pitch < min guitar finale pitch; consonant on downbeats; ≥ 12 finale
  onsets non-coincident with any guitar onset; ≥ 6 strictly contrary motions and ≥ 60%
  contrary+oblique; ≤ 20% pitch-class doubling of the concurrent guitar; CC70 vowel ≤ 45.
- **`drum_fill_escalation`** — per-window counts strictly rise within each build and each
  build-two window ≥ its build-one counterpart; ≥ 20 fill notes in the last 2 bars before each
  drop; ≥ 5 distinct fill signatures per build; drop windows ≤ 24 and < half the build peak.

The bend-effective consonance check was **negative-controlled**: injecting a B5 held across the
604 A-beat (consonant with the tonic E, a whole-tone against the walker's A5) fails
`guitar_solo_arc` with "guitar 83 clashes with stack 81" — proving the A-beat check is live, not
vacuous. The root-only check would have missed it.

**Audio side:** the 4 inherited full-mix audio oracles pass (mono loss 0.31 dB, no clicks).
Fable's speculative *stem* audio checks were **calibrated against the real render and one was
dropped**: the finale guitar stem is quieter in average RMS than drop two (it is deliberately
sparse), so a "finale ≥ build+6 dB" RMS threshold is a bad metric. Stem verification was done
manually instead: guitar stem silent before beat 320 (−99 dB), rising build2 (−35) → drop2 (−30);
choir stem silent before 512 (−99 dB), present in the finale (−18.5 dB).

## 6. Tripwires (Fable's; the re-escalation signals)

- **T1 — the d1/build1 budget (tightest).** Added velocity mass in build one must stay below
  ~15,500 or `drop1 > 1.15 × build1_mean` breaks; the design adds ~9,700 (~7% margin). Re-run the
  contour probe after any fill/velocity change. (Currently green.)
- **T2 — window flattening.** Escalation must be monotone in *added* mass; don't add guitar
  material to build-two window 0 before beat 344.
- **T3 — the A-beat trap** (§3.1): any finale note whose *span* crosses a beat ≡ 4 mod 8 must be
  E/A/C. Re-check spans (not just onsets) after any finale edit.
- **T4 — E over D-bars** (§3.2).
- **T5 — bends vs boundaries:** every bend plateau is integer-semitone and zeroed before
  416/544/640. Keep ch14 out of `BEND_EXEMPT`; ch15 authors no bends.
- **T6 — the `width_only_transients` set literal:** adding 14/15 to `CENTERED_CHANNELS` requires
  adding them to the hard-coded `want_centred` set inside `oracles()` too (done).
- **T7 — same-pitch knife edges:** `check_overlaps` forbids same-pitch overlap; durations at
  405.5 and 516 are shaved. Keep jt=0 on ch14/15 and all fills.

## 7. Implementation notes (owner)

- A non-obvious bug surfaced and was fixed: the ~405 new `note()` calls **shift the shared seeded
  RNG stream**, re-rolling the *outro* harp arp's onset jitter so its first note (beat 640,
  `en.arp` uses jt=4) landed at ~639.99 — inside the dive lane's `[544,640)` count, failing
  `finale_stack_fidelity` (385 vs 384). Fixed by emitting the outro arp deterministically (jt=0)
  in T16 so it cannot bleed back across the finale boundary.
- Reproducibility preserved: the other 15 MIDIs rebuild byte-identical; the 15 existing
  `listening/*.opus` were **not** re-encoded (track 16's opus was rendered alone via
  `render_opus.render_one` with `TRACKTOTAL=16`). See §8 for the one open metadata question.
