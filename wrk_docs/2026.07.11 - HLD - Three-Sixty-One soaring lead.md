# HLD — Three-Sixty-One: the sustaining distorted-lead voice + the soaring solo

Follow-up to `2026.07.11 - HLD - Three-Sixty-One additions.md`. On listening, the
guitar solo (a) was inaudible, then (b) after an audibility pass sounded like a
"wooden glockenspiel" (marimba) — "not a soaring lead guitar solo with held notes,
bends, hammer-ons and offs." Diagnosis: ferrosintesis's distorted guitar is a
**Karplus-Strong pluck that decays a beat after the strike**, so a "held" note dies;
re-picking it produced a stream of mallet strikes. This HLD covers the two-part fix.

**Owner / implementer / verifier: Claude (lead).** Composition designed by **Fable 5**
(consult; it composed the original T10). Fable is finite — its full report is captured here.

## Part 1 — the synth: an opt-in sustaining lead voice (my work)

A soaring lead needs the note to *ring*, which the decaying pluck cannot do. Fix, in
`crates/ferrosintesis/`:

- **`DRIVE_LEAD` PluckPreset** (`voices.rs`): a sustaining voicing of the driven guitar.
  - `t60 = 40` → the loop gain `10^(-3/(t60·f))` ≈ 0.999, so the *fundamental* barely
    decays (the `t60` scaling clamps to 14 s at pitch, already long).
  - `bright = 11000` → the in-loop damper is gentle, so the *harmonics* ring too. This was
    the real lever: at `bright = 4800` (default DRIVE) the harmonics — which carry the RMS —
    die in the loop even though the fundamental persists, so the note darkens and quietens.
  - `amp = 1.5` → drives the string HOT into the overdrive tanh; the saturation then holds
    the (decaying) note at its compressed level far longer (amp-like sustain), crucial for
    the fast-decaying HIGH finale notes.
  - `click = 1.3` → softer pick attack: a lead sings, it does not chug/strike.
- **Opt-in only.** `altbank.rs::make` routes program 29/30 to `DRIVE_LEAD` **only when the
  channel sets CC0 bank-select** (`Strip.alt_bank`). The default-bank DRIVE voice is
  untouched, so the four other guitar albums (Hollow Hill, Seven Kinds of Sunlight, The
  Signal Fire, the Estuary Suite) render **byte-identical** — verified by render-diff.
  The program-keyed overdrive/cabinet Drive insert (`engine.rs`) still applies.

**Measured:** a held E5 decays ~17 dB / 1.5 s (was ~40 dB → dead); a held finale E6/D6 rings
at roughly −20…−30 dB across its whole 6–9.5-beat length instead of blipping. High notes
still fade faster than mid notes — a physically real limit of a plucked string without
feedback; the phrasing (below) uses highs for bent wails and holds mid tones.

**Known limitation (deferred, Arthur's call):** the guitar timbre is only "sort-of OK." A
dedicated **guitar-synthesis review** is a separate pass — see `scratchpad.md`. This HLD
ships the sustain enabler, not a final guitar-tone verdict.

## Part 2 — the soaring lead (Fable 5's composition design)

Channel 14 (GM29 → DRIVE_LEAD), polyphonic: a **soaring lead** (pitch ≥ 76) over a **plucky
under-layer** (pitch ≤ 74) on the same voice — short low stabs read as chug and drive the
distortion harder, honouring Arthur's "keep the plucky layer" within the 16-channel limit.

**The harmonic spine (the load-bearing invariant).** E aeolian; consonance ic ∈ {0,3,4,5,7,8,9}.
A held tone that SPANS an 8-beat chord change must be a bridge tone:
Em→C {C,E,G,A} · C→G {C,E,G} · G→D **{G,B,D}** · D→Em {G,A,B}. The finale is a tonic-E pedal
with the recalled walker onsetting **A5 on every A-beat (548+8k)** → a lead tone spanning an
A-beat must be pitch-class **E, A or C only** (at register: C6/E6/A6); B5 and G6 must end at
or before an A-beat. **Every held tone is consonant on every downbeat it spans, and no
correctness rides on a bend** (all bends recenter to 0 at every 4-beat downbeat, so the
written pitch governs). The single biggest robustness change: the pre-drop wail is written
**B5** (consonant over both G and D) instead of the original's bent A5 — zero bend-dependence.

**The design.** Long held/ringing tones that build in through BUILD2, wail over DROP2 (two
9-beat soars — D6@432, G6@464 — sailing across chord changes on bridge tones), and soar in the
FINALE to a bent A6 peak; connected by **CC68 legato** hammer-on/pull-off runs (the specific
cure for the "marimba" attack stream); **12 pitch-bend** wail/scoop gestures; **CC1 vibrato**
blooms on the long tones. Exact tables are in `movements/t16_three_sixty_one.py`
(`GUITAR_LEAD`, `GUITAR_BED`, `GUITAR_BENDS`, `GUITAR_LEGATO`).

**Oracles (all green).** `guitar_solo_arc` (silent-before, lead windows rising, lead register
`mb<md<mf`, density, finale louder, soar ≥ 83, ≥5 phrases, duty ≤ 0.88, a ≥4-beat breath, and
consonance on EVERY spanned downbeat incl. the finale {E,A,C} trap) + `guitar_lead_detail`
(register split at 75, ≥12 held ≥2-beat tones and ≥55 beats of ring and a ≥6-beat soar, ≥6
full bends, ≥8 CC68 legato pairs, bed present-but-sparse). The bend-effective consonance loop
was negative-controlled in the prior HLD.

**Tripwires (Fable's).** The {E,A,C} A-beat trap (highest risk — G6/B5 must never span an
A-beat); bed straddling a 4-beat downbeat (keep dur ≤0.3, off-beat); register-mean inversion
(the shared-channel bed drags the section pitch mean — margins ~3 semitones); the two breaths
(474–480, 595.5–600) must stay empty of lead AND bed; every bend recentred before 416/544/640;
CC68 runs must be gapless so the retune fires (the one thing only a stem listen confirms).
