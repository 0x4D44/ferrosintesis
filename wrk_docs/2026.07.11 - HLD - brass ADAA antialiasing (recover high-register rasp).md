# HLD — First-order ADAA for the ferrosintesis brass rasp (composite bias-tanh cascade)

> **As-built addendum (2026.07.11, lead).** The core design (stage-wise ln-cosh ADAA,
> f64 divided-difference with h=1e-4 midpoint fallback, keep 2×, recompute F each
> sub-step) shipped as specified and is validated. Three parts of §4–§5 changed once
> real measurements came in — the HLD's own oracle-set discipline is what caught them:
>
> 1. **Worst case is A5 (key 81), not F♯6.** The HLD assumed higher f0 aliases worse;
>    measured, A5 is the binding note. Two reasons: (a) a *flat* floor of 0.3 clamps
>    A5's rasp *up* from its natural quartic 0.0625 — over-driving a note that never
>    needed boosting; (b) A5's 2200 Hz guard bin sits in the BR6 breath band, so
>    BR-O11 partly measures breath noise. F♯6 has ample margin (0.0122 ≤ 0.015).
> 2. **Flat floor → frequency-gated lift.** Replaced `(r2·r2).clamp(FLOOR,1)` with
>    `(r2·r2).max(FLOOR·smoothstep(900,1100,f0))`: the floor lifts ONLY the top octave
>    (C6+), leaving A5–B5 at their natural rasp so BR-O11 holds (0.0286 ≤ 0.03) while
>    F♯6 gets the full 0.3 lift (5× the old top-octave rasp). Committed FLOOR = 0.30.
> 3. **O-B is a math oracle, not a spectral one.** A single tone barely aliases through
>    the isolated shaper (harmonics decay before Nyquist), so a spectral unit test read
>    ~noise for both naive and ADAA — it could not reproduce the *voice's* aliasing.
>    Replaced with a robust property test: the ADAA divided-difference must equal the
>    shaper's boxcar mean (fine numerical integration of `brass_valve`), which directly
>    pins the antiderivative AND the fallback. ADAA's real-world efficacy is evidenced
>    at the voice level (F♯6 runs 5× more rasp at clean alias levels).
>
> Fable's tripwires held up: none of the seven fired, and the fallback/cascade math was
> exactly right — the deviations were all in the *floor-shaping and oracle design* (§4/§5),
> the empirical parts the HLD explicitly flagged as measurement-set.

## Problem

The brass rasp/cuivré (BR12 two-stage tanh cascade) is deliberately derated at high f0
(`BR_CASCADE_F0_FLOOR = 0.06` — as little as 6% of full rasp) purely to hold the aliasing
floor at the existing 2× oversampling. The committed albums contain ~1,344 loud (v≥90)
natural-brass notes above A4 (up to F♯6 ≈ 1480 Hz — Bright Matter 839, Through Lines 342,
Atlas 127, Big Weather 23, Iron Tide 13), so the top octave of loud brass is timbrally
neutered. Recover it with first-order antiderivative anti-aliasing (ADAA, F = ln cosh) so
the rasp can run near-full in the top register without breaking the alias floor.

## Conclusions (the commit-level decisions)

- **Stage-wise ADAA (per bias-tanh stage), NOT a composite antiderivative.** The composite
  ∫f2(f1(x))dx has no closed form → would need a 2–3D table over (x; k, a) whose interpolation
  error sets its own pseudo-alias floor. Stage-wise uses two closed-form ln-cosh antiderivatives,
  is exact per stage, and costs nothing when parameters ramp.
- **ADAA the `single` branch too** (closed form, nearly free) — it carries the high-f0 sound
  today and is needed for delay alignment.
- **Keep 2× oversampling.** Dropping to 1× moves the fold pivot to 44.1 kHz (+6–12 dB more
  pre-alias energy), moves the sinc zero, and deletes the 13.5 kHz decimation cliff that shapes
  every committed brass timbre. ADAA + 2× stack multiplicatively; 1× trades away the gain.
- **Floor:** raisable from 0.06 to ≥ 0.32 guaranteed (γ=2 pessimistic), 1.0 likely (γ=1).
  Ship design target **0.6**, then let oracle O-A push it up until the worst bin sits **6 dB
  under** BR-O11's 0.03 ceiling; commit that measured value. Keep the f0-dependent derate *law*
  with the higher floor (safety margin).
- **Fallback:** compute F and the divided difference in **f64 internally**; threshold
  **|Δx| < 1e-4**; fallback **f(midpoint)**. Seam mismatch < ~3e-9 — spectrally invisible.
  (f32-only variant: h ≈ 1e-2, seam ≈ −89 dB — still safe, but f64 is cheaper to reason about.)

## §1 — Why stage-wise ADAA suppresses the intermodulation aliasing (the part the lead must own)

First-order ADAA ≡ this continuous model (Parker/Zavalishin/Bilbao 2016): reconstruct input as
its **piecewise-linear interpolation** x̂(t), apply f in continuous time, convolve with a
one-sample boxcar (width T = 1/sr2), sample at sr2. Because x̂ is affine on the interval:

    y[n] = (1/T) ∫ f(x̂(t)) dt = (F(x[n]) − F(x[n−1])) / (x[n] − x[n−1]),  F = ∫f

Two consequences: (1) the boxcar has **H(f) = sinc(f/sr2)** — first-order rolloff with
transmission **zeros at every multiple of sr2**, applied to everything f generates *before*
sampling, regardless of where a component came from; (2) residual aliasing is whatever survives
sinc and folds → "+1 slope order."

Classify the naive sampled cascade's aliasing:
- **(A) stage-1 self-aliasing** — f1 harmonics above sr2/2 fold on sampling.
- **(B) stage-2 self-aliasing** — f2 on f1's legit in-band output creates >sr2/2 harmonics that fold.
- **(C) intermodulation** — f2 distorting stage-1's *folded* residue (the flagged class).

Under stage-wise ADAA: (A) and (B) sit near stage sinc **zeros** (components folding to low,
audible frequencies originate near sr2). (C): the folded residue entering stage 2 is *already*
attenuated to stage-1's first-order floor; f2 is bounded-gain (|f2′| ≤ k2/tanh(0.9k2) = O(1),
k2=2.6), so its intermod products are at most comparable in **relative** level to the residue
that seeded them — they **inherit stage-1's suppression** (they're made from the suppressed
residue) but are **not further suppressed** by stage 2 (they're in-band inputs, not stage-2-
generated supra-Nyquist content). Hence the cascade is **≈ first-order overall, not second** —
which is all §4 needs.

**Honest limitation:** the true continuous f2∘f1 also makes legit in-band components by f2
beating f1's supra-Nyquist harmonics *down*; stage 1's boxcar removed those before f2 saw them,
so stage-wise ADAA **omits** them rather than aliasing them. This is a timbre approximation, not
aliasing, bounded by f1's content above 44.1 kHz — doubly tiny here (lip_lp pre-filters the saw
before the shaper; tanh harmonics of an O(1) input at k≤3.2 decay fast). Negligible; tripwire #3.

**Morph structure helps:** g = single + a·(cascade − single) is a **linear mix**, and ADAA
commutes with linear mixing — so ADAA `single` and the cascade separately and mix. No composite
antiderivative needed for the morph either.

## §2 — Antiderivatives and per-sample update

For one bias valve (k, b), norm = tanh(0.9k):

    f(x) = (tanh(kx+b) − tanh b) / norm
    F(x) = [ (1/k)·lncosh(kx+b) − x·tanh b ] / norm

Stable lncosh (needed for |u| ≳ 8 in f32, better everywhere):

    lncosh(u) = |u| − ln 2 + ln1p(e^(−2|u|))

Per player, per sr2 sub-step (F/DD math in **f64**, in/out f32):

    x  = lip_lp.process(saw.next())                 // as today
    s  = ADAA{k,   b}     (x, x_prev)               // single valve
    sA = 0.5*(s + s_prev)                           // half-sample align (see §4)
    u  = ADAA{k1,  b}     (x, x_prev)               // cascade stage 1, k1 = k(1 − 0.6a)
    c  = ADAA{2.6, 0.55b} (u, u_prev)               // cascade stage 2
    y  = sA + a*(c − sA)                            // morph, unchanged
    x_prev = x; u_prev = u; s_prev = s

where ADAA{k,b}(x1,x0) = (F(x1)−F(x0))/(x1−x0) or the §3 fallback.

**Time-varying params (correctness):** the DD is only valid if **both** F evaluations use the
*same* (current-sample) parameters — mixing F_new(x1) with a cached F_old(x0) is meaningless.
So **do NOT cache F(x_prev); recompute it each sub-step with current params.** State = just
`{x_prev, u_prev, s_prev}` (three f32/player). Cost: 2 lncosh/stage; trivial.

**Note-on seeding (memoryless → exact):** at note-on set x_prev = first lip_lp output (or 0),
u_prev = f1(x_prev), s_prev = single(x_prev). Same when cascade_amt transitions 0→positive
(skip cascade stages while a=0). No spurious transient.

**Peak/gain invariance (BR-O12 premise):** at low freq the per-sample slew is small, DD →
f(midpoint), ADAA reproduces the static curve exactly incl. the ÷tanh(0.9k) norm. Only LF gain
effect is sinc(f/sr2): at 1480 Hz, sinc(1480/88200) = 0.9995 (−0.004 dB). BR-O12 reruns unchanged.

## §3 — Cancellation fallback

**Spec (f64 internals): threshold h = 1e-4 on |x1−x0|; fallback f((x0+x1)/2) with current params.**

Error budget: DD roundoff ≈ 2ε·|F|max/|Δx| ≈ 7e-12 at Δx=h (f64 ε=2.2e-16, |F|≤~1.5).
Midpoint mismatch: DD = f(m) + f″(m)·Δx²/24 + …, |f″|max = k²·0.7698/tanh(0.9k) ≤ 8.0 at k=3.2
→ ≈ 3.3e-9 at Δx=h. Branches agree to ~3e-9 — five orders below f32 output quantization, so the
switch is not representable in f32 output → no alias mechanism, and derivatives agree to O(Δx)
so no slope kink. (f32-only: h* ≈ 1e-2, matched error ≈ 3.5e-5 = −89 dB — still under BR-O11's
−30 dB. Recommend f64.)

## §4 — Oversampling, cost, delay, floor arithmetic

**Today's audible aliases:** sampling at sr2 = 88.2 kHz folds shaper harmonics about sr2; the
decimator's 13.5 kHz cliff keeps 0–13.5 kHz, so survivors are born near sr2 ± 13.5 kHz. For
BR-O11 (A5, 880 Hz), guard bins fold from f_orig = 88200 − {1246,2200,3080} = {86954,86000,85120}
Hz → harmonic p ≈ 97–99.

**ADAA gain at those bins** (≥ |sinc(f_orig/sr2)|, conservative single-sinc bound):

| BR-O11 bin | f_orig | f_orig/sr2 | sinc | extra suppression |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1246 Hz | 86954 | 0.9859 | 0.0143 | **−37 dB** |
| 2200 Hz | 86000 | 0.9751 | 0.0255 | **−32 dB** |
| 3080 Hz | 85120 | 0.9651 | 0.0361 | **−29 dB** |

**Floor arithmetic:** alias production grows with cascade_amt as amplitude^γ, γ ∈ [1,2]. ADAA
grants ≥ 29 dB at the worst bin → permissible multiplier 10^(29/(20γ)): γ=2 → ×5.3 → floor 0.32;
γ=1 → ×28 → 1.0 (capped). **Target 0.6, oracle-set** (raise until worst bin 6 dB under 0.03).
Keep the derate law with the higher floor.

**2× vs 1×: keep 2×** — 1× drops the fold pivot to 44.1 kHz (harmonic p≈48 vs ≈98 → +6–12 dB),
moves the sinc zero so 30–40 kHz content folds in with only −8 to −12 dB help, and deletes the
13.5 kHz cliff that shapes committed timbre. ADAA at 1× ≈ trades away its gain.

**State/cost:** three f32/player (`x_prev,u_prev,s_prev`); ~4 lncosh + 4 tanh f64/sub-step
= single-digit MFLOP/s offline — noise. **Group delay:** each boxcar = T/2 at sr2 = 5.67 µs.
Single path 0.5 (ADAA) + 0.5 (alignment average) = 1.0 sample; cascade 0.5 + 0.5 = 1.0 —
**matched** (why sA exists: without it the morph mixes branches 0.5 sample apart → moving comb
notch ~−1 dB by 13 kHz). The 11.3 µs is common-mode across all 5 players → per-player onset
decorrelation (ms-scale) untouched.

## §5 — Oracle design (no ears)

- **O-A. BR-O11 unchanged + BR-O11b worst case.** Keep BR-O11 (A5 dry+growl). Add BR-O11b:
  prog 56, key 90 (F♯6, 1480 Hz), vel 127, dry AND growled, new floor active. Non-harmonic
  guard bins {3840, 2360, 880} Hz (none a multiple of 1480; fold sources p=57–59 about sr2).
  Assert Goertzel mag ≤ 0.03× the 2nd harmonic (2960 Hz), **and ≤ 0.015×** (6 dB margin that
  licenses the committed floor value).
- **O-B. Unit-level differential alias oracle.** In `#[cfg(test)]` drive `brass_rasp` naive vs
  ADAA directly with a pure sine at a frequency whose harmonics p≈95–100 fold onto exact Goertzel
  bins; k=3.2, b=0.3, a=1.0. Assert ADAA's worst folded-bin/2nd-harmonic ratio improves ≥ 25 dB
  over naive. Pins §1 independent of lip_lp/decimator/derate tuning.
- **O-C. Fallback-seam oracle (the branch a spectral test never hits) — two parts, both required:**
  1. **Pointwise seam sweep:** for x0 over [−1.6,1.6] (~4001 pts) at each stage's (k,b) extremes
     {0.8,3.2}×{0.15,0.3} plus stage-2 (2.6, 0.165): evaluate DD at Δx = h(1+2⁻¹⁰) and fallback
     at Δx = h(1−2⁻¹⁰) about the same midpoint; assert |DD − fallback| ≤ 1e-6.
  2. **Forced-branch render + coverage canary:** render a low sine into the shaper (f=30 Hz at
     sr2, amplitude so peak slew ≈ 3h) so each cycle crosses the threshold four times.
     Instrument both branches with `#[cfg(test)]` hit counters and **assert both are nonzero**
     (this repo's lesson: a branch test that can't prove it took the branch is vacuous). Assert
     non-harmonic spur floor ≤ −80 dB re fundamental, and max|out − f(midpoint)| ≤ 1e-4.
- **O-D. BR-O12 rerun unchanged** (C4 forte/mp cascade differentials ≥ 1.18 / ≤ 1.06).
- **O-E. Render-diff inventory** (repo policy, not a gate): baseline-vs-new over every
  `render_opus.py::ALBUMS` MIDI. Expected: brass-bearing albums only (refresh their
  `listening/*.opus` in the same task). Any diff in a brass-free album, or in a 62/63-only
  (synth-path) piece, is a bug — stop.

## §6 — Tripwires (stop and re-escalate)

1. **BR_K_MAX > 3.2** → |f″|≤8 bound and fallback thresholds (§3) stale; recompute h ∝ (ε/|f″|)^⅓.
2. **A filter inserted between the cascade stages** → stage-wise ADAA still works per stage, but
   the §4 half-sample alignment changes and any composite-F shortcut is dead.
3. **lip_lp removed or moved after the shaper** → f1 supra-Nyquist content grows, §1's "omitted
   beat-down products negligible" weakens; re-verify O-B.
4. **f0 > ~2 kHz at full cascade, or 2× reduced to 1×** → +1 order plus the 13.5 kHz cliff no
   longer covers it; need 4× or 2nd-order ADAA (own ill-conditioning, rewrites §3).
5. **k or cascade_amt modulated at audio rate** (per-sample, not note-scale) → keep param
   smoothing time constants ≫ one sample.
6. **Morph restructured away from a linear mix** (e.g. cascade_amt inside a tanh) → the
   mix-after-ADAA decomposition dies; back to the composite-F problem.
7. **Anyone caches F(x_prev)** → silently wrong the moment parameters ramp (§2). The three-float
   state is the spec.

## Lead's verification note (Opus 4.8)

Verified by understanding, not blind-applied: antiderivative and |f″|≤8 checked by hand; BR-O11
guard-bin fold indices (~p99, sinc≈0.014/−37 dB) reproduce the §4 table; the intermod claim is
correctly *qualified* (first-order overall, not second) and — the reason I'm comfortable owning
it — the committed floor is **oracle-set with a 6 dB margin**, so correctness rests on O-A/O-B,
not on the analysis being exactly right. The load-bearing subtlety (recompute F each sub-step,
never cache — tripwire #7) and the branch-coverage canary (O-C, matching this repo's own lesson)
are the two things a naive implementation would get wrong; both are pinned.
