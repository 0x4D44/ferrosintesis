# Raw (un-normalized) render loudness — measurement findings

**Date:** 2026-07-13 · **Mode:** measurement/investigation (no shipped-code change)
**Question:** before we redesign loudness normalization, what does ferrosintesis
actually output *before* `normalize_to_i16` peak-normalizes it? Design against real
numbers, not guesses.

## Method

- Dev-only example `crates/ferrosintesis-cli/examples/raw_dump.rs` calls
  `offline::render()` (the raw f32 buffer, before normalization) and writes a 32-bit
  float WAV — the exact bytes the synth produces, at full precision. Render options are
  identical to `render_opus.py` (rate 44100, wet 0.32, tail 6.0, samples on, all
  channels, auto echo).
- `measure_raw.py` renders all 104 album MIDIs (99 under `albums/` + 5 Synth Feature
  Showcase under `demos/`), measures each with `ffmpeg ebur128=peak=true`, deletes the
  WAV immediately (a 60-min float WAV is ~1.3 GB). Output: `raw_loudness.csv`.
- Validated on *Five Fables*: predicted normalized loudness from the raw measurement
  (−20.9 LUFS) matched the committed opus (−20.7) within 0.2 LU. Method is sound.

## Findings (all measured)

### 1. The synth runs HOT and clips — quietness is mostly attenuation, not weakness
- Raw integrated loudness: min **−38.3**, median **−17.8**, max **−12.6** LUFS.
- **56 of 104 tracks CLIP raw** (sample peak > 0 dBFS; hottest +6.1 dBFS,
  Black Glass Pursuit). For these the current normalizer *attenuates*.
- 46 of 104 sit below −1 dBFS raw peak — the normalizer *boosts* them.
- So per-track peak-normalization is a crude bidirectional AGC: it pulls hot clipping
  mixes down and pushes quiet mixes up, both to a −1 dBFS peak. Loudness then falls out
  as `−1 − crest`, which is why it varies (the earlier census: 13 LU spread, −19.6 mean).

### 2. Native inter-track spread is extreme — album-relative gain is OFF the table
Per-album raw inter-track loudness spread:
- **Through Lines 25.7 LU** (Landing Lights −38.3 → finale −12.6)
- VIGIL (opus4-8) **17.5 LU** (Snow −32.4 → Bright Ruin −14.9)
- The Spark **12.3 LU**, Showcase 10.7, Atlas 9.5, Hours After Rain 7.8.

Simulating one gain per album (loudest track → −1 dBTP) ships the quiet tracks
**inaudibly**: Landing Lights at −44.3 LUFS, Snow at −32.2. **This refutes "just stop
peak-normalizing and use album gain"** — it is *worse* than today for sparse tracks.

### 3. But the native staging is musically FAITHFUL
Raw loudness vs composed note-energy, Spearman ρ per album: Burning Meridian +1.00,
Spark +0.95, Showcase +0.90, Hours +0.88, Big Weather +0.85, Through Lines +0.73,
VIGIL +0.67, Atlas +0.51 (Bright Matter −0.60, a 5-track outlier). The quiet tracks are
quiet *because they were composed sparse* — the intent is real; the **degree** (25 LU) is
just too extreme for casual listening, and −38 LUFS tracks rely entirely on the
downstream normalizer to be audible at all.

### 4. Per-track LUFS normalization to −18 is feasible with minimal limiting
Gain each track to a target LUFS, true-peak-limit the overshoot at −1 dBTP:

| Target | tracks needing any limiting | median depth | need >3 dB | need >6 dB |
|--------|-----------------------------|--------------|------------|------------|
| −16 (Apple) | 93/104 | 3.6 dB | 58 | 9 |
| **−18** | **77/104** | **2.0 dB** | **23** | **3** |
| −20 | 36/104 | 1.6 dB | 4 | 1 |

At **−18 LUFS** the median limiter action is 2 dB (transient-catching, transparent);
only 3 tracks need >6 dB, and those already clip raw. The *never-limit* alternative
(cap gain at ceiling) leaves **66/104 tracks short of target** (median 2.3 LU, worst
9.0) — i.e. it reintroduces the crest-driven variance we are fixing. So a bounded
true-peak limiter is *necessary*, and −18 keeps it gentle.

## Recommendation

**Per-track loudness (LUFS) normalization to −18 LUFS with a −1 dBTP true-peak limiter**,
replacing per-track peak normalization at `crates/ferrosintesis-cli/src/main.rs:128`.
- Directly fixes "some renders are quite quiet" (every track lands at −18).
- A scalar gain preserves every composed **within-track** arc — the Ninth Bell's 22.9 LU
  build/drop and every `analyze.py` ratio check survive untouched.
- Guarantees audibility (no −38 or −44 LUFS tracks).
- Limiting is minimal and bounded (median 2 dB) and only bites transients.
- Cost: the album-level **between-track** arc flattens — but that arc is today either
  scrambled (peak-norm) or unlistenably extreme (native 25 LU). Optionally emit
  `R128_ALBUM_GAIN` tags so album-aware players can restore relative levels.

## Caveats / follow-ups (need ears — this box has none)
- The **−38 LUFS tracks** (Landing Lights, Quiet Before Dawn, Last Train, Hour Before
  Dawn) need +18–20 dB makeup to reach −18. That much gain can surface reverb-tail hiss
  or the synth noise floor. Listen before shipping.
- **56/104 clip raw** — the synth has no master gain-staging headroom and relies on the
  normalizer. Fixing hot voices/buses at source (voices.rs/engine.rs) is a deeper,
  separate investigation; downstream LUFS-norm is the pragmatic fix now.
- A hand-written BS.1770 LUFS meter (K-weighting + gated 400 ms blocks) is needed in
  ferrosintesis (zero-third-party ethos). ~150 lines, and it comes with a natural
  differential oracle: agree with ffmpeg ebur128 to <0.5 LU.
