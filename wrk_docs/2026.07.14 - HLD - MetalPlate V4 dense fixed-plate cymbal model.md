# HLD — MetalPlate V4: the dense fixed-plate cymbal model

**Date:** 2026.07.14
**Author:** Fable 5 (design consult), verified and owned by Claude Opus 4.8 (lead)
**Supersedes the direction in:** `2026.07.13 - HLD - cymbal plate synthesis and its oracle.md`
(that HLD's multiband-*wash* idea is replaced here by "the modal bank IS the wash" — see §2)
**Scope:** ferrosintesis `MetalPlate` — channel-10 keys 49, 51, 52, 53, 55, 57. Every drums
album re-renders. Effort ≈ 1 week.

## 0. Why — four listener defects, one root cause

Arthur auditioned the crash cymbals and reported four things, all confirmed against the code:

1. **"A grain real cymbals don't have."** `metal_spec_for(49)` builds 28 log-spaced centers
   (12 single + 16 detuned pairs) over 650 Hz–14 kHz. At HF the center spacing (~550 Hz at
   5 kHz) is ≈ one per auditory filter (ERB ≈ 565 Hz at 5 kHz) — so each ERB holds **one
   detuned pair**, beating at df = 5–20 Hz at near-100% depth. 4–20 Hz AM is the
   fluctuation-strength band (Fastl & Zwicker): the ear resolves each beat as a discrete
   wobble = "grain". The `Shimmer` struct adds a *globally coherent* 10–24 Hz AM on top —
   coherent cross-band AM is the most salient kind. Two engineered grain sources, both in the
   worst band.
2. **"Weak / not good, especially the ride section" (01:40+).** The ride is pure model (no
   sample), a continuous 40+ hits/10 s pattern. Its "ping" is highpassed *noise* at 7.6 kHz
   (a tick of hiss, not a pitched attack); every hit re-rolls the plate (40 different
   cymbals); and the modal layer is buried ~17 dB under the wash.
3. **"Sounds like SSHHH not a cymbal" when cymbals stack (2:47).** The sustained sound is
   ~90% additive white noise through one static highpass. N independent white washes sum to
   white noise at +10·log₁₀N dB (valleys fill, flatness→1) → the stack reads as hiss. Matches
   the measured flatness: model 0.83 vs real crash 0.58 — our model is *closer to white noise
   than a real cymbal is*.
4. **Raising the recorded-crash sample gain only "slightly" helped** — because the sample is
   on the attack (the easy part) and every defect lives in the *body* (the hard part).

**One root cause:** the model is a static-filter white-noise wash with a sparse modal bank
(44 modes, engineered beats) buried underneath. Density, a fixed plate, frequency-dependent
damping, and demoting the wash fix all four at once.

## 1. Survey — what state-of-the-art cymbal synthesis does (and our verdict)

| Approach | Core idea | For ferrosintesis |
|---|---|---|
| **Nonlinear plate FDTD** (Bilbao; Chaigne/Touzé/Thomas; Ducasse; Edinburgh NESS) | Solve the von Kármán shell equations on a grid; the geometric nonlinearity gives the true bloom (energy cascade low→high) and, at ff, *wave turbulence* — a real crash literally is turbulence. | **Reject.** Elliptic solve every sample (no FFT), free-edge biharmonic BCs, energy-preserving nonlinear discretization; ~minutes CPU/second of audio; months of numerics. Research trap. |
| **Reduced nonlinear MODAL** (Ducceschi & Touzé, JSV 2015; Ducceschi/Hamilton/Russo real-time gong, DAFx-23 via SAV; Skare & Abel DAFx-19 crash, thousands of modes + cheap nonlinear approx) | Modal decomposition + explicit nonlinear coupling terms; SAV makes it explicit & provably stable. | **Park as phase 2** (2–6 wks). Buys physically-derived bloom + true ff crackle. Not needed for the four defects; our cascade already fakes the bloom to oracle standard. |
| **Analog 808/909** | 6 inharmonic square oscillators → HPF/BPF → dual-decay VCA. | Instructive not adopted: static spectrum, harmonically-locked → "sizzle" not bronze. But its **fixed oscillator bank** is why 808 hits are consistent — validates our fixed-plate move. (Even the 909 crash/ride are PCM.) |
| **Sampling / multisample** | 20–40 vel layers × RRs × articulations per cymbal; GB-scale. | Realism benchmark; wrong for a dense generative ride (round-robins machine-gun) and against the 16.68 MiB embedded-asset ethos. **Keep the crash-attack sample as a hybrid** — that's commuted synthesis (J.O. Smith), legit SoA. |
| **Spectral (SMS) / neural (DDSP, DrumBlender, DrumGAN)** | Analyze→resynthesize; learn the decomposition. | Wrong *runtime* for a zero-dep seeded synth. But **SMS is the right *offline calibration* method** — analyze the real crash WAV for the oracle targets. Their sines+noise+transient decompositions independently confirm our architecture. |
| FDN metal-reverb; banded waveguides; feedback-FM | pseudo-modal / efficient tricks | Fallbacks only (FDN if compute bites; the rest are wrong-tool or static-inharmonic). |

**Conclusion:** the practical SoA for *modeling* a cymbal is a **dense modal bank + a
phenomenological bloom + frequency-dependent damping + a hybrid sampled attack** — exactly
the architecture class we already have, run in a different regime. Not FDTD, not more samples.

## 2. The design — MetalPlate V4 ("dense fixed plate")

Runtime stays **linear and feed-forward** (no runtime nonlinearity; velocity→brightness is
computed once at note-on). BIBO-stable by construction; no feedback path anywhere.

**2.1 Fixed, dense mode set (per key, built ONCE from a per-KEY seed — not per hit):**
- Crash 49/57 ≈ **620 modes.** Low 650–2000 Hz: 60 log-spaced modes (4–6 boosted "ring
  modes" = the gong/bell body that survives the tail). High 2–16 kHz: **560 modes on a
  jittered grid** f_k = 2000 + 25k + U(−7,+7) Hz. Density: **9.6 modes/ERB at 2 kHz → 35 at
  8 kHz** (past the ≥8/ERB fusion target everywhere). A *jittered grid*, not Poisson (Poisson
  puts ~⅓ of gaps in the <10 Hz flutter band); every adjacent gap bounded to [11, 39] Hz.
- Ride 51/59 ≈ 460 (950 Hz–13 kHz); china 52 ≈ 450 *with deliberate 30–80 Hz near-pairs*
  (coarse roughness = the trashy china character — keep these); splash 55 ≈ 300; ride-bell 53
  reuses the ride table with a bell excitation.
- **Delete the detuned pairs and the per-cymbal `Shimmer` AM** — the dense bank self-generates
  smooth shimmer statistically (each pairwise beat has depth ~1/k; the within-ERB envelope
  approaches narrowband-noise = "shimmer" not "flutter"). This is half of the grain fix.

**2.2 Damping — t60(f) power law (the darkening):** t60(f) = t60_ref·(f/f_lo)^(−γ).
Crash t60(650)=3.2 s, γ=0.75 → t60(14 kHz)≈0.32 s. Calibrate γ *differentially* against the
real crash WAV's per-band t60s. Replaces today's two-point interpolation; gives the
centroid-fall (darkening) for free.

**2.3 Excitation — where ALL per-hit variation now lives:** random phases; **velocity→
brightness tilt** amp_i ×= (f_i/2000)^β(vel), β ≈ −0.9 (soft, dark) → +0.1 (hard, bright) —
the first-order cymbal nonlinearity done parametrically; **strike-position comb** wandering
±3%/hit. The plate never moves; hits still vary organically.

**2.4 The ride ping (fixes defect 2):** give each mode **two amplitude lanes** — a_sus (t60(f))
and a_ping (t60≈0.18–0.28 s), summed at the oscillator. Allocate a_ping only for ride/bell
keys and only on 1.8–4.5 kHz modes (~110), at 2.5–3× the local sustain amp, shaped by a
Gaussian-in-log-f formant at ~2.6 kHz. Result: a pitched, fast-decaying attack formant riding
over a controlled sustain — the classic ride "tick + spread", identical pitch structure every
hit (fixed plate).

**2.5 Demote the wash (fixes "SSHHH" + polyphony):** delete the sustained wash as an energy
carrier. Keep only a 3–8 ms contact **click** (the "chick"), and optionally a low "air" band
capped ≥12 dB *below* the modal 2–8 kHz RMS and **slaved multiplicatively to the modal
high-band energy** (so it decays with the modes, never outlives them). Rebalance so **modal ≥
85–90% of energy** in [0.2, 1.0] s (today the wash is ~17 dB *above* the modes). Polyphony then
composes correctly by construction: different keys → different fixed tables → peaks in
different places → peaked+peaked stays peaked, not summed white noise.

**2.6 Bloom — keep the P-C1/P-C2 cascade**, but interpolate its floors/depths smoothly in
log-f (per-mode, precomputed) instead of 4 discrete band steps (620 modes would make a 4-step
edge an audible seam). All existing `cymbal_bloom_cascade_v3` assertions stay green.

**2.7 Hybrid crash attack — keep `SampleOverlay` on 49/57** (commuted synthesis). Its job now
shrinks to the first ~150 ms; expect to *lower* its gain. **Run the timbre oracles on the pure
model** (`--no-samples`) so the sample can't mask a model regression.

**2.8 Compute:** ~1.2 Gflop/crash hit (~13× today), viable offline. Do **SoA layout from day
one** (parallel `Vec<f32>` for re/im/cr/ci/amp/decay, modes sorted by cascade stage) so LLVM
autovectorizes (~4–8× on AVX2); add deterministic **mode culling** (precompute each mode's
death sample, shrink the active span as the tail decays). `render-catalog` parallelizes tracks.

## 3. The oracle (ratio/shape-based, renderer-normalization-proof, differential vs the real WAV)

Fail-first doctrine: measure today's model AND `drum_crash1_ff_rr1.wav` with the *same*
estimator, print both, commit thresholds between them with margin. Pure-model (`--no-samples`),
dry (`render_drum_kit`).

- **O-1 GRAIN (headline).** Per-octave-band (2–4k, 4–8k) envelope → detrend → normalized
  autocorr peak over 4–25 Hz AM on [0.25, 1.75] s. Today (engineered beats + shimmer):
  ~0.45–0.8 FAIL; real ~0.10–0.30. Gate ≤ 0.35 AND ≤ real+0.10. **Companion:** cross-band AM
  coherence (Pearson of the two band envelopes) ≤ 0.25 — catches any resurrected global shimmer.
- **O-2 BRONZE vs NOISE.** (a) flatness(2–9k, [0.5,0.9]s): today 0.83 FAIL, real 0.58, gate ≤
  0.68. (b) tail modal salience (top-8 Goertzel peaks over median, [0.8,1.2]s) ≥ 8 dB.
- **O-3 STACK ("SSHHH").** Render 49+52+55+57 together; flatness(2–9k,[0.15,0.45]s) ≤ single
  crash + 0.06 (today whitens to 0.87–0.92 FAIL).
- **O-4 DARKENING.** Per-band t60 strictly decreasing with band; t60(1–2k)/t60(8–14k) within
  ×1.5 of the real WAV's ratio; centroid late/early ≤ 0.75 (today ~flat FAIL).
- **O-5 RIDE PING.** band(1.8–4.5k)/band(5.5–12k) over [5,65]ms ≥ 2.0 (today ~0.3–0.8 FAIL);
  ping flatness ≤ 0.55; two-decay structure (ping t60 0.12–0.4 s over a ≥1.5 s sustain).
- **O-6 RIDE CONSISTENCY.** 8 equal hits; min pairwise Pearson of [5,80]ms spectra ≥ 0.93,
  ping-centroid CoV ≤ 2.5% (today re-rolled plate ≈ 0.6–0.85 FAIL). Passes by construction on a
  fixed plate.
- **O-7 BLOOM regression:** existing `cymbal_bloom_cascade_v3` green. **O-8 HYGIENE:** DC/NaN/
  peak/choke.
- **Calibration tool** under `tools/` (not shipped): runs O-1…O-5 over the current render and
  the real WAV, prints the table; thresholds committed with measured numbers as comments.
  Calibrate *ratios and margins*, never equality (the sample has room/mic coloration).

## 5. Tripwires (re-escalation signals)

1. **Grain survives densification** → suspect the cascade band-stepping (disable to test), a
   re-introduced coherent AM, or the sample's room flutter — in that order. If dense+incoherent
   still grains, the psychoacoustic model is wrong, not the code.
2. **Fused but not BRONZE** → strengthen the low ring-mode families; if still generic metal,
   *that* is the trigger to fund the phase-2 nonlinear core (not more tuning).
3. **Ride ping reads as "marimba" (too pitched)** → widen the formant / add modes; if nothing
   satisfies, a ride *tip sample* is the honest fallback (needs sourcing).
4. **Fusion threshold** → if grain persists at ≥8/ERB, the offender is the 25–40 Hz adjacent-gap
   (roughness proper); reshape the grid to spread within-ERB gaps to 40–150 Hz.
5. **Compute** → micro-bench the vectorized loop before committing counts; if SoA doesn't
   materialize, halve counts (≥5/ERB still fuses) or fall back to a shared persistent ride plate
   / FDN wash.
6. **Over-fitting ONE recording** → all differential targets come from one ff crash with
   room+mic baked in; gate with margins, widen if a second reference contradicts.
7. **Mix interactions** → the stack oracle is dry; bus glue + reverb could smear a passing model
   in situ. The render-diff inventory + one listening pass over the ride section is the real
   acceptance test (this box has no ears; Arthur does).

## 6. Relationship to samples (Arthur's question) — and what the sourcing scan found

A CC0-sourcing scan (2026.07.14) **overturned the assumption that CC0 drum-kit cymbals are
unavailable.** They exist, GitHub-pinnable exactly like our VSCO bank, licenses verified from
the repos' own LICENSE files:
- **Virtuosity Drums** (`sfzinstruments/virtuosity_drums`, **CC0 1.0**) — a stick-played jazz
  kit with a real **ride (bow + bell)**, crash, and full hi-hat; ~4 round-robins on the
  cymbals. The one to sample a ride/crash from.
- **Big Rusty Drums** (`sfzinstruments/karoryfer.big-rusty-drums`, **CC0 1.0**) — ride
  (bow/bell/edge), **china**, crash, hi-hat.
- **VCSL** (`sgossner/VCSL`, **CC0**, same author as our VSCO bank) — a clean hi-hat.
- Excluded (attribution/ShareAlike/Sampling-Plus, against the "no attribution" bar): ltibbits'
  Zildjian ride (Sampling Plus), quartertone hats + FreePats Muldjord kit (CC-BY), Philharmonia
  (CC-BY-SA). "The Hat With The Phat" ride is license-ambiguous (GPL vs CC0) — unverified.

So both paths are open. The engineering nuance still holds and points MODEL-first for the
worst defect: **the ride is the main problem (the weak 01:40 section), and a dense generative
ride (40+ hits/10 s) MACHINE-GUNS a 4-round-robin sample set** — audible repetition — whereas
the fixed-plate model gives a genuinely different-yet-consistent hit every time. Sampling wins
for the SPARSE, EXPOSED crash; modeling wins for the DENSE ride. Recommendation: build
MetalPlate V4 (fixes crash + ride + china + splash + bell in one ~1-week pass, keeps the
from-scratch identity, no repo growth), keep the crash sample as a hybrid attack, and treat
Virtuosity Drums as the proven fallback if the modelled crash still doesn't convince solo
(tripwire 3). The sample path is viable but is a bigger, identity-shifting commitment — Arthur's
call. Full sourcing detail in the JRN.
