# HLD — Tremolo restrike: fast same-key plucked repeats re-pick the ringing string

**Origin:** Fable 5 consult (finite on-call specialist), commissioned by the lead
(Claude Opus 4.8) during the Tubular Bells voice-feedback task. This HLD captures
Fable's reasoning as the durable trace; the lead verified it by understanding the
diff, re-running the oracle, and measuring the real passage before adopting.

## Problem

A GM-25 (Acoustic Guitar steel) **tremolo** — one key struck ~13×/second
(inter-onset ~75 ms) — rendered as "someone clicking something very fast, like
rain," not a musical shimmering tremolo. Surfaced by the Tubular Bells reference
render at 3:56 (ch4). The same defect afflicts any fast plucked tremolo/roll
(mandolin tremolo, sitar jhala, banjo roll).

## Why the obvious fixes fail (the three stacked layers)

Each NoteOn spawns a **brand-new `Pluck` from silence**, so a tremolo is a train of
fresh re-attacks. But voice-reuse ALONE does not fix it (measured: broadband trough
depth 21.6 → 22.7 dB — no improvement). Three layers sit under the artifact:

1. **Fresh-spawn restart** (the obvious one): 13 broadband onset transients/s. Real,
   but *not the dominant term*.
2. **Inter-stroke release chop**: every mid-run NoteOff hard-releases the string
   (`rel_t60` 0.15 s = 400 dB/s ≈ −12 dB per 30 ms gap) **plus** the G6
   release-darkening glide (τ≈36 ms toward 600 Hz), which at treble keys compounds
   ~1000 round-trips/s and kills even the fundamental inside one gap. In a tremolo the
   mid-run gate-off is *notation, not damping* — no hand damps between strokes.
3. **The treble carrier is already dead**: the one-pole loop damper's *magnitude* at
   f0 dominates treble decay (STEEL B5: `damp_mag(f0)` ≈ 0.955/round-trip ≈ **−390
   dB/s**; the fundamental is −45 dB within 100 ms). The nominal `t60` only bounds
   `loop_gain`; the damper magnitude is separate. So between 75 ms strokes there is
   nothing left ringing whatever the engine does about voice identity.
   **DC-instability trap:** do NOT compensate by dividing `loop_gain` by `|H(f0)|` —
   that makes round-trip gain > 1 below f0 (|H(DC)|=1) and the loop is DC-unstable.
   The stable in-scope carrier hold is the house's band-limited saturating sustainer
   (`SusDrv` — bounded injection, bandpass zero at DC).

Two further discoveries: **h1 roulette** — one period of comb-filtered noise has a
Rayleigh-random fundamental (measured 20 dB stroke-to-stroke → a stuttering tremolo);
a real pick always carries strong h1, so the restrike burst tops its own h1 up to a
floor. **Phase-cancellation roulette** — additively injecting a fresh burst into a
comparable circulating carrier randomly cancels; a real pick *grabs* the string
(the "pick-catch": scale the delay line down so the new stroke owns the phase).

## Mechanism (all gated behind the tremolo path; inert otherwise)

- **Engine gate TREM1** (`engine.rs`): a per-block sample clock `now` + per-(ch,key)
  `key_on_at`; a same-(ch,key) NoteOn with IOI ≤ **`TREM_IOI_MAX_S` = 100 ms**
  (10–16 strokes/s tremolo vs 5–7/s picking at ≥143 ms) routes to `voice.retrigger()`
  on the most-recent same-(ch,key,program,alt) Active voice instead of spawning. On
  success it clears `held`/`sost_held` and **increments `voices_spawned` anyway** so
  every later spawn draws its pre-change seed (the render diff stays confined to the
  tremolo).
- **`Voice::retrigger` (default `false`)** — implemented ONLY by `Pluck` (a plucked
  string physically IS re-struck while ringing) and delegated by the `LaVoice` sampler
  wrap *without replaying the PCM attack* (identical PCM 13×/s IS the machine-gun).
  Winds/bowed/reeds/brass keep full re-articulation — their `legato_to` (a slur) is
  deliberately NOT conscripted. Preset flag `PluckPreset::trem` is `false` for the
  mechanically-damped keyboards (harpsichord, clavinet) and the frozen altbank PIZZ.
- **`Pluck::retrigger`** = a full RE-PICK of the ringing string: retune; restore the
  spawn-stroke damper brightness (undo G6 darkening, fresh per-stroke jitter); a fresh
  pick-law burst (pick_lp + position comb) at `v·TREM_RESTRIKE_LEVEL` (0.7) with the
  **h1 floor** (0.5); **pick-catch** `catch_damp(TREM_CATCH=0.35)`; re-trigger the
  onset click; un-release with a smooth `release_env` recovery (60 dB/40 ms, masked
  under the stroke attack; branch unreachable unless `retrigger` ran → all pre-existing
  renders bit-identical); first restrike installs the **carrier hold** (`SusDrv` at
  `TREM_SUS_FRACTION` = 0.35).
- **Tremolo-run release**: while `trem_hold`, gate-offs take `rel_t60 ×
  TREM_RELEASE_STRETCH` (8×; gap cost 12 dB → 1.5 dB, run-end still rings ~1.2 s), skip
  the stop thump and G6 darkening, and keep the driver through the gap; a CTRL-rate
  watchdog drops the hold `TREM_SUS_DROP_S` (0.16 s) after the last restrike.

## Oracle — `fast_same_key_restrikes_shimmer_not_click_train` (engine.rs)

Metric: the **f0-carrier p10/p90** (per-10 ms-frame Goertzel at f0 over the steady
run) — "does the tone persist between strokes," a pure ratio so loudness
normalisation can't move it. Explicitly rejected the broadband-envelope metric, which
moves the WRONG way (the baseline's overlapping released twins fill gaps with hash
that gross-envelope metrics credit as continuity; the same trap the lead hit
independently). Three self-guarding clauses:
- (a) carrier continuity > 0.10 — **measured 0.038 (fresh-spawn) → 0.215 (fix)**.
- (b) still articulates: broadband p10/p90 < 0.63 (guards against over-slurring to one flat note).
- (c) IOI gate pinned: 200 ms picking carrier < 0.05 (guards the gate from widening into picking).

**Lead's independent verification (real Tubular Bells ch4 key-76 tremolo, dry):**
carrier 0.097→0.357, 0.045→0.403, 0.102→0.466 across the three runs — a 4–9×
improvement. Byte-identical confirmed: single pluck, 200 ms picking, and
piano/flute/harpsichord fast repeats. Full gate green (`cargo test --workspace
--release`, clippy `-D warnings`, fmt).

## Invariants & tripwires (own these)

- Inert off the tremolo path (default-false capability + `trem_ok` preset flag).
- **Blast radius** (album MIDIs with ≤100 ms same-key Pluck repeats): only ~3 tracks —
  Hollow Hill Pt2 (32-note steel tremolo), Sub Rosa, The Signal Fire — all currently
  machine-gunning, so *improved*. Sitar jhala / banjo rolls engage (idiomatically
  correct; audition). Verify with the render-diff inventory.
- `Stats::voices_spawned` now also counts restrikes (deliberate, for seed alignment).
- Overlapping same-key notes, and tremolo under CC64 sustain, differ slightly from
  baseline (documented, unaudited edges).
- **Residual — separate task:** the treble-sustain defect itself (layer 3: every
  treble Pluck note is ~pick transient, fundamental t60 ≈ 130 ms at B5). The tremolo
  hold only *masks* it inside runs. A proper fix lives in the KsLoop damper design
  (a magnitude-flatter damper at f0 with phase compensation) and re-voices every
  guitar album — remember the DC-instability trap above.
