# HLD — Bass sub-octave (the XG "bigness")

Author: Fable 5 consult (design), verified + owned by the delivery lead.
Date: 2026.07.15. Task: tubular-bells-voice-polish (bass, round 3).

## Problem
Arthur wants the ferrosintesis electric BASS (GM33 finger) to match the "bigness"
of a real Yamaha MU-80 (XG) finger bass. Measuring the real XG (via the cycle-accurate
mdsc55 emulator) showed a sustained SUB-OCTAVE component (energy at f0/2, an octave
BELOW the played note) that our voice lacks. Our `sub` oscillator sits AT f0; there is
nothing at f0/2.

## The measured XG sub-octave (level relative to the fundamental)
Rendered single notes through the real MU-80, measured f0/2 vs f0 in the sustain:

| key | f0 (Hz) | fs=f0/2 (Hz) | sub-octave re fundamental |
|-----|---------|--------------|---------------------------|
| 40 (E2)  | 82.4  | 41.2 | −42.6 dB |
| 43 (G2)  | 98.0  | 49.0 | −47.9 dB |
| 45 (A2)  | 110.0 | 55.0 | **−11.0 dB** |
| 47 (B2)  | 123.5 | 61.7 | −27.5 dB |
| 50 (D3)  | 146.8 | 73.4 | **+1.5 dB** |
| 52 (E3)  | 164.8 | 82.4 | −0.1 dB |
| 54 (F#3) | 185.0 | 92.5 | **+6.6 dB** |

The level rises with pitch but is NOT perfectly smooth even in absolute frequency
(dips at fs=49, 61.7) — a mix of a fixed downstream envelope (recorded cab/body +
voicing EQ) acting on a roughly constant intrinsic sub-octave, plus per-multisample-zone
variation. Fable's first-principles point stands: pure resampling/transposition is
ratio-preserving and cannot manufacture an f0/2 line, so the sub-octave is a real
recorded/voiced component, not a transposition artifact. **Either way the design
conclusion is the same: implement a smooth, consistent, faded law — do NOT replicate
the jumpy per-key XG behaviour, and do NOT chase the F#3 "+9 dB" (it makes the sub
DOMINATE the fundamental → the note re-reads an octave down as F#2).**

## Design (Fable consult, verified)

**1. Mechanism.** New `sub_oct: f32` field on `PluckPreset` (default 0 → fully inert;
only bass-family presets author it). A pure SINE at f0/2 summed in PARALLEL post-body-EQ,
exactly like the existing f0 `sub` (own oscillator, own envelope field — NOT shared with
`sub_env`, which would couple it to `sub>0`). Velocity law reuses the sub's:
`p.sub_oct · v · (1.25 − 0.5·vn)`.

**2. Level law = constant intrinsic level × a frequency window W(fs), fs = f0/2.**
W is a precomputed per-note SCALAR (not a runtime filter), recomputed on retune
(`apply_pitch`) so bends/legato across the knee behave:
```
fs < 30 Hz:   W = 0
30–56 Hz:     W = ((fs − 30) / 26)^3       (cubic ramp 0 → 1)
56–95 Hz:     W = 1 + (fs − 56) / 39       (linear 1 → 2, i.e. +6 dB)
fs > 95 Hz:   W = 2                          (clamp)
```
`sub_oct` is voiced so A2 (fs=55) renders the sub-octave at **−9 dB re fundamental**.
Check: E2 (fs 41) → ≈ −30 dB (matches XG); A2 → −9 (anchor); F#3 (fs 92.5) → ≈ −2.3 dB
(rises, but capped so the fundamental still WINS — no octave flip). The top-slope cap
(≈ −3 dB, not the XG's +9) is the one deliberate deviation.

**3. Anti-rumble is the LOWER HALF of the same law, not a separate mechanism.** W=0
below 30 Hz kills the sub-octave for the lowest notes (E1 fs=20.6 Hz) where it would be
inaudible rumble. Why this matters: −18 LUFS normalisation is K-weighted and heavily
discounts <60 Hz, so a sub-20 Hz sine raises the TRUE PEAK without raising measured
loudness — the −1 dBTP limiter would then clamp the AUDIBLE signal to make room for
inaudible rumble. The scalar fade (steep, ~cubic) is exact and free; a one-pole HP
(6 dB/oct) is an order of magnitude too shallow.

**4. Pure sine — no waveshaping.** 2·fs = f0 exactly, so any even-harmonic shaping lands
coherently on the fundamental at the sub's RANDOM phase → ±1 dB note-to-note fundamental
wander. 3·fs = 1.5·f0 = a fifth = chord mud. The XG's "bigness" is a bare line at fs;
that is what we replicate. sub-octave shape fixed at (0,0); the preset's `sub_shape`
keeps applying to the f0 sub only.

**5. RNG gating.** Draw the sub-octave's random phase ONLY when `sub_oct > 0`
(`.then(|| …)`), placed immediately after the sub's phase draw. Zero draws when off →
every sub_oct-less preset is bit-identical. When ON, the extra draw shifts that voice's
own downstream RNG stream, so GM33 render realizations change everywhere → the GOLDEN
mix-balance fixture and the portable bass signatures WILL move and must be re-pinned;
render-diff on GM33 albums is expected; any NON-bass diff means the gating leaked.

## This-bassline caveat (delivery-lead finding)
The Tubular Bells finger-bass is very low: ~36% of its note-ons (incl. the most common,
A1/key33 at 22.8%, fs=27.5 Hz) fall in the W=0 fade zone, ~55% are partial, ~7% full.
So on THIS bassline the sub-octave is deliberately SUBTLE — its low-note weight already
comes from the v5 voicing (f0 sub + body EQ, +3 dB, signed off), and the real XG has no
sub-octave this low either. The sub-octave is a GENERAL voice improvement, most audible
on higher bass content. Knee is a tuning knob if Arthur wants more engagement here (at a
headroom cost); decide by ear on the A/B.

## Oracle: `bass_sub_octave_law` (cfg(test), voices.rs, differential vs `sub_oct:0` BASS)
Realization-tolerant (the extra RNG draw shifts the stream — same-stream differential is
NOT available). Sustain window 0.30–0.80 s (past the kick + ramp). seed 7, vel 64.
1. A2: peak_locate(40–70) within ±3% of 55.0 (freq at exactly f0/2).
2. A2: r45 = mag_at(55)/mag_at(110) ∈ [0.25, 0.50] (−12…−6 dB; scale-invariant).
3. A2: mag_at(with,55) ≥ 6× mag_at(off,55) (NEW energy, not floor leakage).
4. E2: r40 < 0.12 AND r40 < 0.4·r45 (monotone thinning).
5. F#3: 1.2·r45 < r54 < 0.75 AND peak(60–250) within ±4% of 185 (rise + cap, fundamental wins).
6. E1: spectral_band_rms(15–28 Hz) with ≤ 1.2× off (headroom clause — fs=20.6 windowed out).
7. A2: DC |mean over integer-period window (44 periods of 55 Hz)| < 5e-4.
8. A2 3 s: decay mag_at(2.2–2.7 s,55) < 0.15× mag_at(0.3–0.8 s,55).
9. A2: release — sub-octave band in last 0.3 s < 0.10× the 0.25–0.45 s band (summed before amp·release_env).
10. A2: bounded peak(with)/peak(off) < 1.45, rms ratio < 1.35.
11. (opt) click: ramped onset slew, first 90 samples < 2.5× steady slew, multi-seed.
12. (opt) phase randomness: inter_corr(seed7,seed8, 65 Hz-LP'd first 0.4 s) < 0.98.
