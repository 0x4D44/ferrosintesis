# HLD — *Slipstream* (ten displays)

**Composer/owner: Claude Fable 5.** Commissioned by Arthur off the back of
"Three-Sixty-One" (*Through Lines* T16): *"an album of tracks inspired by
Three-Sixty-One … same complex soundscape, multiple counterpoints, soaring and
sweeping synths, complex rhythm and fills, highly layered audio, dual guitars.
They should build and drop and build again. Richly textured, complex, fast
paced, ear catching. Ten tracks."* Occasional references to Three-Sixty-One
are wanted; the tracks must be largely different.

## 1. Concept

**An air display, told in ten acts.** Three-Sixty-One was the AquaTheater
spectacle; *Slipstream* is its sister show in the open sky. The mapping is
one-to-one with the brief:

| Brief | Device |
|---|---|
| dual guitars | **THE DUO** — a two-ship guitar display team (ch14 lead ship, ch15 wing ship), flying a *different machine-verified formation on every track* |
| build and drop and build again | every act climbs, passes at full power, climbs again — per-track contour oracles (strictly-rising windows; later drops strictly bigger) |
| complex rhythm and fills | the shared 8-shape fill library (material.FILL_LIB, A–F inherited note-for-note from T361, G/H new) scheduled into verified escalations; plus per-track metric devices (7/8, inserted bars, 3:4:5 polyrhythm, breakbeat) |
| soaring and sweeping synths | the saw-lead soars (long held high tones with CC1 blooms), CC74 macro-sweeps, portamento swoops, reverse-cymbal risers into every lift |
| multiple counterpoints | ≥ 2 simultaneous independent lines at every climax, with contrary/oblique-motion and downbeat-consonance oracles |
| highly layered | 14–16 active channels per track, layer-count oracles at climaxes |
| fast paced, ear catching | tempos 120–152, one cold-open track, hooks stated early, ~4–6 min per act |
| occasional T361 references | pinned quotes in T9 (the searchlight = ORBIT riff) and T10 (the flypast = orbit + scream); a two-bar wink in T1. Nowhere else. |

Home key E minor (T361's key) for the opener and finale; every other act in
its own key so the show never sits still.

## 2. Album anatomy

`albums/fable5/Slipstream/` in the **federated movements shape** (the Through
Lines machinery, copied): `conductor.py` (registry + `Part`), `material.py`
(shared DNA, self-proven), `movements/tNN_*.py` (self-contained tracks),
`build.py` / `verify.py` / `analyze.py` / `render_all.sh` / `test_verify.py`.

**material.py DNA** (all pinned by `verify_material()`):
- `ASCENT_CELL` — the album signature: 0, +7, +12, +19 semitone climb, three
  eighth pickups + a 2.5-beat hang. Every track states it at least once
  (pinned per track); T10 augments it four-fold.
- `mirror(p, axis)` — the reflection arithmetic shared by the formation
  oracles (T4 inversion, T6 mirror canon).
- `FILL_LIB` — shapes A–H (A–F verbatim from T361's fill bed — the lineage).
- **T361 quotes** — `ORBIT_RIFF_361` = [1,5,8,10,12,10,8,5] (aeolian
  sixteenths) and `SCREAM_*_361` (B5→A6, +2 bend), pinned verbatim to the
  Through Lines data.
- Morse lanes — `WHEELS UP` (T1), `CLEAR SKIES` (T10).

## 3. Global conventions (every track)

**Channel plan** (baseline; a track may re-purpose 0–8 and 12 with its own
programs, but 9/10/11/13/14/15 are fixed roles):

| ch | role | notes |
|----|------|-------|
| 0 | ostinato/arp voice | the track's motion engine |
| 1 | pad | CC74/CC11 choreography over the whole timeline |
| 2 | bass | synth bass (GM38/39) or picked bass |
| 3, 4 | antiphonal posts | fixed L/R (pan ~18 / ~110), **transient calls only** (≤ 0.75-beat notes) |
| 5 | soaring synth lead | GM81 saw; the "sweep" carrier |
| 6 | color | harp/bell/e-piano/steel per track |
| 7 | aerial strings | GM49 |
| 8 | choir | GM52/53; CC70 vowel morphs |
| 9 | kit | `program_changes=[(9, 0.0, 1)]` (the V3 kit) |
| 10 | melodic toms GM117 | fill lane (bank B via CC0=1) |
| 11 | synth drum GM118 | fill lane (bank B via CC0=1) |
| 12 | orchestra hit GM55 | (or track color) |
| 13 | riser GM119 | reverse cymbal (bank B via CC0=1) |
| 14 | **guitar — lead ship** | GM29 + CC0=1 (the sustaining DRIVE_LEAD voice; the T361 solo sound) |
| 15 | **guitar — wing ship** | GM30 (mid-scooped chug) or GM29 (+CC0=1 when it must sing); per formation |

**The duo discipline.** Both guitars are sustained tonal sources → **both
centred (CC10 64)**, contrast carried by program/register/role, never pan
(the mono-collapse pan-Haas lesson). Lead ship on GM29+bank1 for solos (T361
audibility lesson: program 30 is mid-scooped, wrong for a lead; 29 pushes
mids). Held lead notes must be re-picked or CC68-slurred so they never blip
out. Every track pins its formation with a falsifiable oracle (see §4).

**Pan discipline** (T361's, verbatim): sustained beds hold CC10 64 for the
whole piece; only transient sources (notes ≤ 0.5–0.75 beats) may leave
centre; antiphonal posts sit at fixed L/R values; any orbit-style moving pan
lane rides only a transient arp. Mirror in audio_checks: mono-sum loss ≤ 2 dB.

**Contour discipline.** Each track declares its build windows and drops in
its module; oracles enforce: per-build strictly-rising 8-bar velocity-mass
windows; every later drop's mean per-bar velocity mass strictly above the
earlier drop's (the T361 "second drop bigger" rule, extended per track); any
hush section under 50% of the neighbouring drop.

**Fills discipline.** Fills come from `material.FILL_LIB` via
`material.play_fill` (jt=0 keeps signatures exact). Per track: escalating
per-window counts through every build (strict rise), ≥ 5 distinct shapes per
build, a ≥ 20-note unbroken fill into every drop, drops thinned (window cap).
Big-lift moments may add the track's own bespoke BIG fill.

**Soar/sweep discipline.** Each track authors at least: one CC74 macro-sweep
covering ≥ 60 CC74 units on pad or lead; risers (GM119) into every drop; and
either a portamento swoop (CC5/CC65, interval ≥ 12 semitones) or a ≥ 6-beat
held lead soar with CC1 bloom. Oracle-checked per track.

**Counterpoint discipline.** At each track's largest section, ≥ 2 sustained
melodic lines sound simultaneously with: ≥ 50% non-coincident onsets,
≥ 60% contrary+oblique motion (against the reference line), downbeat
pairwise consonance (`{0,3,4,5,7,8,9}` on `(a−b) mod 12`, structural
downbeats only), and no pitch-class doubling above 25%.

**House rules** (the hard-won ones):
- jt=0 on every oracle-pinned lane (fills, formation lanes, quotes, hits).
- No same-pitch overlapping notes (check_overlaps); shave durations at seams.
- Bends: integer-semitone plateaus, recentred to 0 before every movement
  boundary (check_bend_hygiene); wing/lead vibrato via CC1, not the bend lane,
  when the bend lane carries marked gestures.
- Scored silences (T3's stall!) go in GAP_WHITELIST.
- DURATION_WINDOW is on the **file** seconds (write() adds a 2-beat pad).
- Builders write note-ons only inside their own movement (whole-timeline CC
  lanes are exempt and should be authored once, in the first builder that
  needs them); document seam carry-overs in BOUNDS_WHITELIST.
- PROGRAM_WHITELIST lists exactly the programs used (setup + scheduled).
- Drums (ch9) stay inside GM 35–81.
- Consult `crates/ferrosintesis/README.md` before voicing an unfamiliar GM
  program; prefer programs already proven in the fable5 catalog.

**Verification loop** (per track): write oracles FIRST, compose to pass;
`python build.py --track N --check` while composing; `python build.py
--track N` then `--track N --verify` when the file exists. Green = done.

## 4. The ten displays

Grids below are the *designed* shapes; the composer owns the final beat
arithmetic (and sets DURATION_WINDOW accordingly) but the architectural
claims, formation devices and oracle plans are contractual. Durations target
4:00–5:30 (T10 to 6:00), album ≈ 45–50 min.

---

### T1 — Wheels Up (the opener)

- **E aeolian, 132 bpm, 4/4, ~4:20** (≈ 576 beats). Seed 20261101.
- **Story:** the field wakes. Radio chatter (Morse `WHEELS UP` on a
  woodblock/sidestick lane), engines turn over (low bass swells), the duo
  taxis in on muted chugs, then the first full pass of the day.
- **Architecture:** INTRO (radio + engine start) → TAXI (ostinato in, duo
  chugs) → BUILD1 → DROP1 (hook in duo octaves) → STRIP (texture drops to
  bass+duo) → BUILD2 (fills denser) → DROP2 (hook + saw counter + choir; >
  DROP1) → OUTRO (throttle back; final ASCENT statement, solo lead ship).
- **Duo formation — OCTAVES:** through DROP1 the wing ship doubles the lead
  ship exactly one octave down, tick-for-tick (oracle: in the pinned span,
  onset sets identical, wing pitch = lead − 12, everywhere). In DROP2 the
  wing breaks formation into free counterpoint (counterpoint oracle applies).
- **Ear-catch:** the duo states ASCENT_CELL as the very first pitched notes
  of the album (pinned), before any drum.
- **T361 wink:** exactly two bars of ORBIT_RIFF_361 on the color channel
  (steel drums) buried in BUILD2, pinned to the quote data, ≤ 8 statements.
- **Morse oracle:** the `WHEELS UP` rhythm decodable from the intro lane
  (pinned to material.morse_rhythm).
- **Oracles:** `duo_octave_formation`, `ascent_first_pitches`,
  `radio_morse_lane`, `build_drop_contour` (D2 > D1), `fill_escalation`,
  `orbit_wink` (present, small, pinned), `drop2_counterpoint`.

### T2 — Knife Edge

- **A aeolian, 144 bpm, verses in 7/8 + drops in 4/4, ~4:20.** Seed 20261102.
- **Story:** the knife-edge pass — the aircraft rolls to 90° and holds it on
  rudder alone. Unstable, thrilling, always about to fall out.
- **Architecture:** cold taxi (short) → EDGE1 (7/8 verse: trades) → DROP1
  (4/4 locks) → EDGE2 (7/8, tighter trades) → BRIDGE (the wobble: bends) →
  BUILD → DROP2 (> DROP1) → snap ending on a single unison stab (the track
  simply stops — ear-catching).
- **Duo formation — TRADES:** in the EDGE sections the guitars alternate
  strict one-bar (7/8) trades and **never sound together** (oracle: zero
  overlapping sounding spans; strict L/R … i.e. lead/wing alternation of
  bars). In the DROPs they lock to rhythmic unison (≥ 80% coincident
  onsets, different pitches — power-chord pairs).
- **Metric oracle:** time-signature grid pinned (7/8 verses, 4/4 drops);
  kit accent pattern 2+2+3 in the 7/8 (pinned accents).
- **Ear-catch:** the snap stop — final beat is a single ≤ 0.5-beat unison
  stab followed by scored silence (gap-whitelisted) then one cymbal choke.
- **Oracles:** `duo_trades_never_overlap`, `duo_drop_lockstep`,
  `meter_grid_7_8`, `kit_223_accents`, `build_drop_contour`,
  `fill_escalation`, `snap_ending`.

### T3 — Hammerhead

- **D aeolian, 126 bpm (dive accelerates to 138), 4/4, ~4:45.** Seed 20261103.
- **Story:** the vertical climb until airspeed dies, the yaw pivot at the
  top, the vertical dive, the pull-out. Twice — the second higher.
- **Architecture:** run-in → CLIMB1 (16-bar single crescendo, register and
  velocity strictly rising; lead ship climbing line) → STALL1 (2 bars of
  near-silence: one high pedal tone only — gap-whitelisted) → PIVOT1
  (drum-fill cadenza, fills only, 1 bar) → DIVE1 (descending cascades,
  tempo map 126→138 across the dive) → PULL-OUT = DROP1 (tempo back,
  four-on-floor) → CLIMB2 (higher: peak register above CLIMB1's) → STALL2 →
  PIVOT2 (bigger) → DIVE2 → DROP2 (> DROP1) → victory pass OUTRO.
- **Duo formation — CONTRARY:** in each climb the lead ship rises while the
  wing ship *descends* (strict contrary motion on downbeats, oracle-pinned);
  they cross mid-climb (the crossing beat pinned) and swap registers.
- **Unique device:** the *stall* — the album's most extreme dynamics gesture
  (velocity mass < 10% of the climb peak, pinned) and the pivot cadenza
  (only ch10/11 sound, pinned).
- **Oracles:** `climb_monotone` (register+velocity rise, CLIMB2 peak >
  CLIMB1), `stall_hush`, `pivot_fills_only`, `dive_descent_and_accel`
  (tempo map pinned; cascade centroid falls), `duo_contrary_cross`,
  `build_drop_contour` (D2 > D1), `fill_escalation`.

### T4 — Cuban Eight

- **G dorian, 128 bpm, 4/4, ~4:40.** Seed 20261104.
- **Story:** the figure-eight: two loops joined by half-rolls. Loop two is
  loop one *upside down*.
- **Architecture:** entry → LOOP1: BUILD1 → over-the-top (suspension bar) →
  DROP1 (the 32-beat HOOK on lead ship + saw double) → HALF-ROLL (4-bar
  turnaround, harmony pivots) → LOOP2: BUILD2 → DROP2 where the **hook
  returns exactly INVERTED** (material.mirror about a pinned axis,
  tick-exact oracle over the whole 32 beats) and the mix is bigger →
  exit pass (hook + inverted hook in 2-part counterpoint — both at once,
  consonant on downbeats) → out.
- **Duo formation — INVERSION:** DROP1 hook on lead ship; DROP2 inverted
  hook on wing ship (lead harmonizes); exit: both simultaneously.
- **Ear-catch:** the exit's simultaneous hook + mirror-hook is the album's
  "aha" contrapuntal moment — pinned pairwise-consonant on every downbeat.
- **Oracles:** `hook_inversion_exact` (mirror map, tick-exact),
  `exit_double_hook` (simultaneity + consonance + both lanes complete),
  `duo_roles`, `build_drop_contour` (D2 > D1), `fill_escalation`,
  `halfroll_pivot` (key-area shift pinned via chord-root lane).

### T5 — Lomcevak (the tumble)

- **B phrygian, 152 bpm, 4/4 with inserted 2/4 hiccup bars, ~4:15.** Seed
  20261105.
- **Story:** the gyroscopic tumble — the aircraft departs controlled flight
  end-over-end. Controlled chaos; the album's rhythm laboratory, fastest
  tempo, densest fills.
- **Architecture:** launch → TUMBLE1 (the polyrhythm engine: kit accents
  every 4 beats, tom cycle every 3, syndrum cycle every 5 — coinciding every
  60 beats, the realignment crash pinned) → GATHER (build) → DROP1 →
  TUMBLE2 (hiccup bars inserted: the 2/4 bars pinned in the timesig grid) →
  BUILD2 → DROP2 (> DROP1; polyrhythm rides ON TOP of four-on-floor) →
  recovery OUTRO (all three cycles land together on the final downbeat,
  pinned).
- **Duo formation — HOCKET:** the guitars split one continuous sixteenth
  line between them — union forms an unbroken grid line, intersection empty
  (oracle: in hocket spans, no simultaneous onsets, and the merged onset
  grid has no holes). Phrygian bite (the ♭2) featured in the line.
- **Fills:** the album's densest — every FILL_LIB shape used (≥ 8 distinct
  signatures, highest per-window counts of the album), yet drops still
  thinned relative to their builds.
- **Oracles:** `polyrhythm_3_4_5` (cycle lanes + realignment), `duo_hocket`,
  `hiccup_bars` (timesig grid + kit pattern), `fills_all_shapes`,
  `build_drop_contour`, `phrygian_flavor` (♭2 usage count on melodic lanes).

### T6 — Mirror Pass

- **C aeolian, 120 bpm, 4/4, ~5:00.** Seed 20261106.
- **Story:** two aircraft fly toward each other, one inverted — the mirror
  pass. The album's contrapuntal centrepiece.
- **Architecture:** approach (pads, the axis tone alone) → PASS1: the canon
  (lead ship states a long cantus; wing ship plays it **inverted about a
  pinned axis, delayed 2 beats**, tick-exact for the whole section) →
  BUILD1 → DROP1 (canon continues OVER four-on-floor — the beauty-with-power
  moment) → interlude (choir + saw take over the canon pair, guitars rest) →
  BUILD2 → DROP2 (> DROP1; canon at 1-beat delay — tighter — plus choir
  third voice in free counterpoint) → resolution: both ships land on the
  axis pitch in unison (pinned).
- **Duo formation — MIRROR CANON:** `wing(t) = mirror(lead(t − lag), axis)`
  for every note in the canon spans (the oracle recomputes the entire wing
  lane from the lead lane and material.mirror; tick-exact, velocity-shaped).
- **Counterpoint:** the interlude's choir/saw pair must satisfy the full
  counterpoint discipline; DROP2 runs three simultaneous lines.
- **Oracles:** `mirror_canon_exact` (both lags), `axis_resolution`,
  `interlude_handoff` (guitars silent; choir+saw carry the pair),
  `three_lines_drop2`, `build_drop_contour`, `fill_escalation`.

### T7 — Vapour

- **F lydian, 138 bpm, 4/4 (breakbeat kit), ~4:30.** Seed 20261107.
- **Story:** wingtip vortices flashing into cloud — the sweep-and-shimmer
  act. Bright, airy, fast; the sweeping-synth showcase.
- **Architecture:** shimmer intro (arp + autopan on TRANSIENT arp only) →
  GROOVE1 (breakbeat: syncopated kit, no four-on-floor; duo in parallel
  sixths glide) → SWEEP1 (the big CC74 filter sweep + portamento swoop
  into…) → DROP1 → strip → GROOVE2 → SWEEP2 (bigger) → DROP2 (> DROP1;
  double-time hats) → dissolve outro (arp thins, sweep falls).
- **Duo formation — SIXTHS:** the ships fly parallel: wing = lead − (8 or 9
  semitones, diatonically correct in F lydian) for ≥ 80% of duo time
  (oracle measures the interval histogram); brief splits allowed at phrase
  ends.
- **Sweep oracles:** ≥ 2 CC74 macro-sweeps each covering ≥ 70 units with
  monotone rise then fall; ≥ 2 portamento swoops (CC65 on, interval ≥ 12);
  the lydian ♯4 featured in the hook (usage pinned).
- **Kit oracle:** breakbeat — snare displacement pattern pinned; NO
  four-on-floor in grooves (kick-on-every-beat forbidden there), drops may
  floor it.
- **Oracles:** `duo_parallel_sixths`, `cc74_macro_sweeps`,
  `portamento_swoops`, `breakbeat_not_four_floor`, `lydian_sharp4`,
  `build_drop_contour`, `fill_escalation`, `autopan_transient_only`.

### T8 — Split-S

- **F♯ aeolian, 134 bpm, 4/4, ~4:15.** Seed 20261108.
- **Story:** the escape manoeuvre — roll inverted, pull through, exit
  pointing the other way. The track that plays the album's shape backwards.
- **Architecture — COLD OPEN:** the track *starts* inside DROP0 at full
  power (its first 8 bars are within the loudest of the piece — pinned) →
  ROLL (strip-down over 8 bars: layers peel off one at a time, layer-count
  strictly falls — pinned) → the QUIET (bass + wing ship alone) → BUILD1 →
  DROP1 → BUILD2 → DROP2 (the pull-through; > DROP1 and ≥ DROP0) → EXIT:
  the opening hook returns in **retrograde** (tick-exact reversal about the
  section grid, pinned) as the outro.
- **Duo formation — RETROGRADE:** wing ship's EXIT lane = lead ship's OPEN
  lane time-reversed (oracle recomputes it; pitches identical, onsets
  mirrored about the section midpoint).
- **Ear-catch:** the cold open itself (no intro at all — bar 1 is the drop)
  and the strip-tease ROLL.
- **Oracles:** `cold_open_loudest_start`, `roll_strips_layers`
  (per-2-bar active-channel count strictly falls), `hook_retrograde_exact`,
  `build_drop_chain` (D2 > D1, D2 ≥ D0 mass), `fill_escalation`,
  `quiet_duet`.

### T9 — Night Launch

- **G♯ aeolian, 130 bpm, 4/4, ~5:00.** Seed 20261109.
- **Story:** the night display — afterburners, searchlights, pyro. Dark,
  cinematic, the album's dynamic-range act.
- **Architecture:** night field (low pads, distant choir, sparse bass
  pulses) → IGNITION (bass ostinato lights; duo enters low) → BUILD1 →
  DROP1 (dark four-on-floor; hits = pyro bursts on an off-beat schedule) →
  SEARCHLIGHT (the T361 quote: ORBIT_RIFF_361 on a soft bell/steel voice,
  ≥ 8 statements, pinned tick-exact to the quote data, while the choir holds
  and a slow autopan sweeps a TRANSIENT bell arp — the light panning the
  crowd) → BUILD2 → DROP2 (> DROP1; pyro doubles; choir full) → afterglow
  OUTRO (the choir resolves; one last distant hit).
- **Duo formation — SOAR OVER PEDAL:** wing ship holds low pedal fifths
  (long chug-pedal tones re-picked per bar); lead ship soars above with
  ≥ 6-beat held tones and CC1 blooms (≥ 3 soars ≥ 6 beats, min pitch floor
  pinned — the T361 soar idiom, new material).
- **Pyro oracle:** orchestra-hit lane matches a pinned off-grid schedule
  (accents on beats 1.5/3.5 style placements), density strictly doubles
  D1→D2.
- **Oracles:** `searchlight_orbit_quote` (pinned to material data),
  `pyro_schedule`, `duo_soar_over_pedal`, `night_dynamic_range` (quietest
  32-beat window ≤ half of DROP2 mass… and DROP2 the album act's peak),
  `build_drop_contour`, `fill_escalation`, `autopan_transient_only`.

### T10 — The Flypast (finale)

- **E aeolian, 128 bpm (T361's key AND tempo — deliberate), 4/4, ~5:45.**
  Seed 20261110.
- **Story:** every aircraft of the day airborne at once for the final
  formation flypast — and the show salutes its AquaTheater sister.
- **Architecture — three cycles, each bigger:** muster (roll call: quick
  2-bar cameos of T1's octaves, T6's mirror, T7's sixths — pinned brief
  quotes of *this album's own* formations) → BUILD1 → DROP1 → BUILD2 →
  DROP2 → BUILD3 (the longest riser chain) → DROP3/STACK (the flypast):
  **ORBIT_RIFF_361 ostinato** (steel drums, sixteenths, pinned tick-exact),
  **ASCENT_CELL in 4× augmentation** (choir + brass, pinned), the **lead
  ship soaring** with the **T361 SCREAM reprise** (B5 approach → held A6
  peak with +2 bend flicks, pinned to SCREAM_*_361), the **wing ship
  counter-line** (full counterpoint discipline vs the lead), all
  simultaneously over a tonic pedal, every structural downbeat pairwise
  consonant across the four lanes → sign-off: Morse `CLEAR SKIES` on the
  tinkle-bell/woodblock as the stage empties → final unison E.
- **Contour chain:** D1 < D2 < D3 in mean per-bar velocity mass (pinned);
  build windows rise within each build; fills escalate build-over-build
  (B3 > B2 > B1 peaks).
- **Oracles:** `flypast_stack` (all four lanes present + tick-exact quotes +
  pairwise downbeat consonance), `scream_reprise` (pitch pair, bend
  plateaus, hygiene), `ascent_augmentation`, `three_cycle_chain`,
  `muster_cameos`, `clear_skies_morse`, `fill_escalation_x3`,
  `wing_counterpoint`.

## 5. Verification & delivery plan

1. Scaffold (done with this HLD): machinery copied from Through Lines;
   conductor registry; material.py green.
2. Ten composers work `movements/tNN_*.py` concurrently (self-contained
   modules; scoped `build.py --track N` loops).
3. Full `python build.py` (all MIDIs + manifest) then `python build.py
   --verify` — every oracle green.
4. Render (`render_all.sh` after `cargo build --release -p
   ferrosintesis-cli`), `python analyze.py` — audio oracles green
   (calibrate thresholds against the real render where a speculative
   number proves mis-set; document any calibration).
5. Register the album in `crates/render-catalog/src/main.rs` `ALBUMS`
   (genre "Progressive Rock / Instrumental").
6. ALBUM.md, README.md, album_manifest.json, journal; one atomic commit.

**Out of scope:** no ferrosintesis (synth) changes — the album is composed
entirely to the shipped voice set, like Three-Sixty-One was.
