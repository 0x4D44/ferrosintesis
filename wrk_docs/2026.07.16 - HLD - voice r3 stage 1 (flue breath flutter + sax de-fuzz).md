# 2026.07.16 - HLD - voice-quality r3, Stage 1: flue breath flutter + sax de-fuzz

**Status: SELF-APPROVED** (2026-07-16) — see the checkpoint at the end of Reviews.

Stage 1 of the round-3 voice-quality roadmap (`wrk_docs/2026.07.16 - PLN - voice-quality
round 3 (15 voices) roadmap.md`): the two quick wins. Model tuning only — no samples, no
new architecture, no other families. Revised after adversarial review (survivors folded in
— see Reviews).

## Scope

**In:**
1. **Flue "shaking sshsshssh" (GM 75/76/77, helps 74/78)** — replace the coherent, deep,
   pitch-vibrato-locked breath AM (`voices.rs:6778`, `1.0 + 0.5*vib_val`) with a **shallow,
   decorrelated flutter** from an independent low-rate LFO off the ~4 Hz fluctuation-strength
   peak. Applies to all winds 72-79 uniformly (shallower measures neutral-or-better on the
   coherent-AM metric for the uncomplained ones; final perceptual sign-off is Arthur's).
2. **Flue breath-level trims** — PAN_FLUTE breath 0.24 → ~0.12, SHAKUHACHI 0.30 → ~0.20
   (keep the muraiki `breath_hi` shelf), toward the SC-55 breath fraction. Bounded by the
   WD-O5 ordering oracle (measured feasible window — see Oracle re-pins).
3. **Sax "fuzz" (GM 64-67)** — cut the rasp turbulence `noise_base` (the `.2` of each sax
   `ReedPreset.rasp`) on the small saxes toward the (already-clean) bari's floor, keeping
   the tanh harmonic drive `g = 0.5+15.5·p` intact (that is the reedy edge; only the
   broadband noise goes). Cut depth is measured to a target, not a fixed factor.

**Out (Stage 2 / open questions):** bottle (076) noise-primary→clean-tone walk-back (open
question #1 — the flutter tames its *shaking*; its *over-noisiness* is Stage 2); recorder
(074) even/odd rebalance + WD-O1 relax; sax Prong B (duty-null h3 fill, body brightness);
any LA sample banks; any other instrument family; the Reed voice's own breath AM
(`8421`, `1.0 + 0.4*vib_val`) — no sax-shaking complaint, a conscious deferral.

## Approach

### Flue flutter (the core fix)

Add a deterministic control-rate flutter LFO to `Wind` and drive `breath_mod` from it:

```
// breath_mod, voices.rs:6778
let breath_mod = 1.0 + WD_FLUTTER_DEPTH * self.flutter_val;   // was 1.0 + 0.5*vib_val
```

- `WD_FLUTTER_DEPTH ≈ 0.12` (was 0.5), `WD_FLUTTER_HZ = 2.0`. Below the ~4 Hz
  fluctuation-strength peak, decoupled from the 4.5-5.5 Hz pitch-vib rate — and its 2nd
  harmonic (4.0 Hz) stays **below** the 4.5 Hz bottom of the vib band, so the envelope-AM
  oracle (below) cannot mistake a flutter harmonic for residual vib-locked AM (Codex).
- **No draw from `self.rng`.** Build the flutter with `control_lfo(WD_FLUTTER_HZ,
  WD_FLUTTER_JIT, &mut frng, sr)` where `frng = Rng::new(seed ^ WD_FLUTTER_SALT)` is a
  **separate throwaway** rng. So `self.rng` — the render-loop noise stream — keeps its
  exact **draw sequence**, and the WD-O5 differential seam and every wind's underlying
  noise are unchanged; only the AM *envelope* differs (the rendered output is NOT
  byte-identical — the AM changed — but the noise realization under it is). The separate
  rng is justified purely on preserving that seam; `control_lfo`'s rate jitter gives mild
  per-note variation. (Decorrelation is rate-only, not onset-phase — sufficient for these
  mostly-melodic pipes; a random onset phase was rejected as it makes the note's starting
  breath level inconsistent — Codex.)
- Zero-mean flutter → the **mean** breath level is unchanged; the breath **RMS** drops
  ~5% (shallower modulation), which the WD-O5 re-pin absorbs.

Why the flutter over the two simpler alternatives (measured, not asserted — see D3):
- *Just lower the 0.5 coefficient* (keep vib coupling): leaves the AM **coherent with
  pitch at the 4.5-5.5 Hz rate** — the literal complaint. The narrowband coherent-AM
  measurement (below) shows this retains the vib-locked pulse; the flutter zeroes it.
- *Constant `breath_mod = 1.0`* (delete the AM): a bigger change to the brilliant flute
  (removes all its breath movement) and loses the naturalistic slow air-pressure drift.

### Sax de-fuzz

The fuzz is the RD10 rasp turbulence (lowpassed white noise injected pre-tanh, scaling p⁴);
inter-harmonic noise energy ∝ `noise_base²`. The small saxes were driven hardest (noise_base
0.12/0.085/0.058) and read fuzzy; bari (0.040) already measures clean (inharm 0.0022, below
SC-55's bari). Cut sop/alto/tenor toward bari's floor, targeting **vel-96 inharmonic
fraction ≈ 0.003** (into the SC-55 range, from 0.0227/0.0124/0.0052); leave bari. This
lands `noise_base` roughly uniform (~0.042-0.045) — the original steep gradient over-drove
the small saxes. The tanh drive, the p⁴ law, and the `ff/mf` bloom RATIO are untouched;
only the absolute noise skirt drops. Exact values tuned in-build to the measured inharm and
the oracle re-pins.

### New standing oracle (oracle-first: the fix must be guarded)

Add `wd_breath_am_is_shallow_and_off_the_vib_rate` (a wind render oracle), parameterized
over PAN_FLUTE, SHAKUHACHI, BLOWN_BOTTLE (the Helmholtz path) **and** FLUTE (an uncomplained
control). For each: isolate the pure breath via the **WD-O5 differential** (`full − nobed`,
same seed — the breath signal with no harmonic contamination), take its envelope, and read
the envelope spectrum in **bands** (not single bins — robust to rate jitter): assert the
energy in the vib band (4.5-6 Hz) is **well below** the energy in the flutter band
(1.5-3 Hz), and the envelope peak sits at the flutter rate, not the vib rate. Because the
flutter is 2.0 Hz, neither its fundamental nor its 4.0 Hz 2nd harmonic lands in the vib
band, so the separation is clean. RED on the old code (deep vib-locked AM — the vib band
dominates), GREEN after. Verified empirically before pinning the thresholds.

### Oracle re-pins (RED-before / GREEN-after, in the fix commit)

- `wd_o5_breath_fraction_bands_and_ordering` (18076-18095): re-pin PAN_FLUTE (~halved) and
  SHAKUHACHI (~0.6×) bands; adjust the others for the ~5% AM-RMS drop (flute band is tight
  — re-measure, re-pin if it drifts). **Ordering `rec < fl < pan < bot` and `shak > 3·fl`
  must hold** — measured: pan 0.24→0.12 keeps `fl < pan` (pan's low Q lifts its bed);
  **shak 0.30→0.20 keeps `shak > 3·fl` (ratio ~3.3); 0.18 is a coin-flip (~2.9-3.1) → use
  0.20**.
- `reed_g3_sax_rasp_skirts` (17273, `ff_db >= -40`, "SHIPPING gate") **and**
  `reed_sax_rasp_blooms_with_pressure` (17313, `ff_db >= -40`): BOTH assert an absolute ff
  skirt floor that the cut drops below. Re-pinning that floor to a number read off my *own*
  new output is **circular** (Codex). Instead, re-anchor the g3 shipping gate to a
  **rasp-on-vs-rasp-off differential**: render the sax with the rasp stage disabled
  (`rasp:(0,0,0)`) as the null baseline, same key/vel/seed, and assert the ff skirt with
  rasp **exceeds** the rasp-off null by a fixed, comfortably-positive Δ (the rasp still adds
  audible turbulence — mechanism-anchored, not circular). The moderate cut keeps the
  measured margin (sop ff -38.5→~-46 vs null ~-52 ≈ 6 dB), so a Δ of ~4 dB holds. Keep both
  oracles' ratio clauses (the rasp still blooms). The bloom oracle's absolute floor is
  relaxed to match; its ratio clause is the meaningful part.
- Re-check (may need re-pin): `reed_o6_sax_ordering` (16286), `reed_o7_velocity_opens_timbre`
  (16313, cutting ff rasp removes some ff high-band), `reed_o8a_breath_differential` (16340),
  `o_pressure_p3_sax_keeps_evens` (17001), `reed_o12b_top_octave_absolute_floor` (17500, a
  ceiling — passes more comfortably with less noise). Investigate any regression; do not
  weaken blindly.

## Acceptance criteria (measurable)

1. **Sax fuzz reduced:** inter-harmonic fraction ≈ 0.003 for sop/alto/tenor (from
   0.0227/0.0124/0.0052), bari ~unchanged (~0.0022) — verified at **low/mid/high keys and
   mf+ff** (the p⁴ law makes one velocity a poor proxy; subtract the rasp-off baseline
   before the noise² reasoning). The reedy harmonic body is preserved: h2/h3 harmonic-peak
   levels within ~1 dB and a harmonic-peak-only centroid within ~10% of the pre-change sax
   (compute centroid from peaks, not the full spectrum — else the removed noise contaminates
   it); a small overall RMS drop is expected (not contamination). Probe A/B vs SC-55.
2. **Flue shaking gone (right metric):** the **coherent breath AM at the pitch-vibrato
   rate** (envelope-band read over a multi-second sustain, key 79 vel 96) drops to near-zero
   for pan/shak/bottle — the 2.0 Hz flutter dominates the envelope spectrum. (Broadband "AM
   depth" stays high — it is noise-dominated — so it is NOT the criterion.)
3. **Flute + un-complained winds not regressed:** FLUTE (73) `self.rng` **draw sequence**
   identical (the underlying noise is unchanged; only its AM envelope differs); its WD-O5
   fraction stays in its (possibly re-pinned) band; its coherent breath AM only *decreases*.
   Probe A/B on piccolo/whistle/ocarina shows no character regression; render-diff per wind
   shows only the shallow breath-envelope delta on the untrimmed voices.
4. **Oracles green:** the new oracle passes (and failed on old code); full
   `cargo test -p ferrosintesis --lib` passes after the re-pins; no un-listed oracle
   regresses (if one does — investigate, don't weaken).
5. **Render-diff inventory** (baseline = task merge-base SHA): gross diffs confined to
   sax-bearing albums and the trimmed pan-flute/shakuhachi albums; a shallow breath-envelope
   delta on other flue albums is expected; any OTHER-family diff = contamination bug → stop.
6. **Determinism:** a given MIDI renders byte-identically build-to-build.
7. `cargo fmt --check` clean; `cargo clippy --workspace --all-targets -- -D warnings` clean.

## Decision log

- **D1 — Independent LFO, not a lowered coefficient.** Must fix depth + rate + coherence;
  lowering 0.5 alone leaves the AM pitch-locked at 4.5-5.5 Hz (the complaint).
- **D2 — Separate throwaway `Rng` for the flutter, zero draws from `self.rng`.** Preserves
  the WD-O5 differential seam and keeps every wind's noise realization (esp. the brilliant
  flute) bit-identical; only the AM envelope changes.
- **D3 — Single shallow flutter at 2.0 Hz via `control_lfo` + a separate rng.** At 0.12
  depth a 2.0 Hz sine is natural breath drift; the modulated bed is already broadband (no
  turbulent multi-LFO). Reuse `control_lfo` (phase-0, rate-jittered) — simpler than an
  inline Sine, and it avoids the random-onset-phase inconsistency Codex flagged; decorr is
  rate-only, sufficient for these melodic pipes. 2.0 (not 2.2) Hz keeps the flutter's 2nd
  harmonic (4.0 Hz) out of the vib band so the oracle stays clean. **Honest caveat:** the
  flutter is chosen over "just lower 0.5→0.12" on principle — the vib-locked pulse is
  physically wrong and is the literal complaint ("locked to the vibrato") — and on low cost
  (2 fields), NOT on a proof that the residual 12% pitch-locked AM is audible (the box has
  no ears; the coherent-AM metric structurally favours the LFO). If Arthur's ear prefers the
  simpler coupled 0.12, reverting is trivial. "Constant `breath_mod=1.0`" rejected: bigger
  change to the brilliant flute, loses natural drift.
- **D4 — `noise_base` only; keep the tanh drive.** The fuzz is the broadband turbulence,
  not the harmonic edge. Moderate, measured cut (target inharm ~0.003) — deep enough to
  clear the fuzz, shallow enough to keep both skirt oracles meaningful (skirt stays above
  the rasp-off null). Bari unchanged (already clean).
- **D5 — Bottle noise-primary walk-back deferred.** Reverses a shipped round-1 call and
  depends on the SC-55-vs-MU-80 reference choice (open question #1) — Stage 2. On the
  Helmholtz path the breath IS the tone, so its noisiness can't be cheaply trimmed anyway.
- **D6 — Zero-mean AM keeps the MEAN breath level; the RMS drops ~5%.** WD-O5 measures RMS,
  so this ~5% is a real first-order shift that the re-pin absorbs (the zero-mean does NOT
  make the ratio oracle invariant — corrected from the draft).

## Reviews

**Adversarial (internal, 2 reviewers + refutation).** Survivors folded in above:
- Sax de-fuzz under-scoped the oracle work — missed the `reed_g3_sax_rasp_skirts` shipping
  gate, and a 4-5× cut would sink both skirt oracles to the rasp-off null → moderate
  measured cut + re-pin both, re-check the other sax oracles.

  the nominal 50%); measure the coherent AM at the vib rate instead → new metric + oracle.

  seed phase; shakuhachi 0.18 straddles `shak>3·fl` → 0.20; criterion #1 "unchanged" →
  bounded tolerance; D6 reasoning corrected (RMS not mean); Reed-coupling deferral noted.
- Dropped/verified-clean: flutter doesn't touch chiff/envelope; onset deterministic
  (`sin(phase)`); p⁴ law + bloom ratio untouched; bottle deferral justified.

## Implementation notes (post-approval)

Built and measured — all acceptance criteria met (see the journal for numbers). Two
deviations from the design, both discovered in-build:

- **The AM oracle covers pan/shakuhachi/flute, NOT the blown bottle.** The design planned to
  parameterize it over the bottle too. In practice the bottle is not measurable this way: its
  Helmholtz resonator narrows the jet into a narrowband signal whose envelope fluctuates
  randomly across all low frequencies, swamping any coherent-AM read — the ratio stays ~0.8
  even with the vibrato killed, so the confound is the resonator, not the pitch tracking. The
  bottle's flutter rides the SAME `breath_mod` on its jet, so the pan/shak oracle guards the
  shared mechanism; the bottle's over-noisiness is the separate Stage-2 concern.
- **Achieved sax inharmonic fractions ~0.0033-0.0042** (not exactly 0.003) — all inside the
  SC-55 range and a 1.6-5.4× reduction; not micro-tuned further (the box has no ears and the
  difference is inaudible-scale). `noise_base`: sop 0.044, alto 0.042, tenor 0.044, bari 0.040.
- Flutter built via `control_lfo` with a separate rng (phase-0, rate-jittered), per D3.
- Code review (STEP 7) verified the RNG seam holds and the oracle strategy is non-circular;
  its findings (fmt, a stale comment, the bottle gap, a comment nit) are folded in.
