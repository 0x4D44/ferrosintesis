# HLD — Voice R3 Stage 2: harp octave-hollow (GM46)

**Status: SELF-APPROVED** (2 adversarial + Codex reviews folded in — §9) · **Author:** Claude
(Opus) · 2026.07.16 · Effort: S · New samples: no · Brass-loop slice 2 of 4

## 1. Problem
The Orchestral Harp (GM46, a `Pluck`/Karplus-Strong voice, `HARP` preset voices.rs:2817) is
**octave-hollow**: its 2nd harmonic dominates the fundamental. Measured (baseline vs SC-55, dB re f0):

| key | h2 ferro | h2 SC-55 | error |
|---|---|---|---|
| C4 (60) | **+3.5** | −27.7 | ~31 dB hot |
| C3 (48) | −8.3 (**h4 +5.2**) | −20.7 | mid hot |
| C5 (72) | −49.8 | −17.9 | f0-dominant already |

**Root cause = the pick-position comb.** The KS excitation applies `exc[i] = raw[i] −
0.9·raw[(i+pos·period)%period]` (voices.rs:3522-3525). At `HARP.pos = 0.35` the comb transfer
`|1 − 0.9·e^(−j2π·k·pos)|` **peaks at h2** (+3.7 dB, matching the measured +3.5) — and at h4 for
C3. The octave-hollow is an **even-harmonic** defect from an **off-centre pluck**.

## 2. Goal & non-goals
**Goal:** restore f0 dominance on the harp (h2 clearly below f0, toward SC-55's −20..−28), keeping
the odd-harmonic shimmer, on **GM46 only**.
**Non-goals:** the shared excitation *code* / the other ~23 plucked-bass presets (byte-identical —
`pos` is per-preset, so only HARP changes); the loop damper `bright`/`pick_lp`; the body peak; the
C5 short-ring (a separate roadmap item).
**Caution (voices.rs:3497):** a full `sin(nπp)/n²` displacement excitation was TRIED AND REVERTED
(buries the pluck, broke 10 oracles). A low-pass tilt has the same flaw — Codex + both adversarial
reviewers proved a monotonic lowpass can't reach the octave target without starving the shimmer
(max f0→2f0 correction is 12 dB, requiring cutoff ~0.6·f0 which craters h4–h8). **So the lever is
the even-selective comb, not a tilt.**

## 3. Reference (SC-55-anchored)
Harp: f0-dominant, h2 ≈ −20..−28 dB re f0 at C3/C4, with live ODD upper partials (h3/h5/h7 = the
shimmer). Physically = a central-ish pluck (a string plucked at its centre has no even harmonics).

## 4. Approach — move the harp pluck toward centre (`HARP.pos` → ~0.5)
The comb already selects harmonics by pick position. **Move `HARP.pos` from 0.35 toward 0.5.** A
half-period comb (`pos = 0.5`) nulls the EVEN harmonics (h2 → `|1−0.9| = 0.1 = −20 dB`, near SC-55's
−27) while the ODD harmonics survive (`|1+0.9| = 1.9 = +5.6 dB`) — so f0 dominates, h4 (C3) also
drops, and h3/h5/h7 keep the shimmer. This is the **one-knob, existing-field, physically-correct**
fix: no new field, no filter, no steady-state buffering, no gate.

### Constants (calibrated by a `pos` sweep + re-measure)
`HARP.pos` ≈ **0.47** first-cut — near centre for a deep even-null, backed slightly off 0.5 so a
touch of even colour remains (not a pure-odd clarinet tone). Sweep 0.35→0.50 across C3/C4/C5 and ≥2
seeds; land C4 h2 ≤ −8 dB (must-win, toward −20..−28) with the odd-shimmer floor intact. Re-trim
`HARP.amp` (0.56) to the **measured** RMS delta (direction unknown — the peak-normalize at
voices.rs:3526 rescales after the comb, so a smoother burst can normalize LOUDER; measure).

## 5. Testability & oracles (tests-first)
- **Recal `harp_46_shimmers` (14804) — the defect-encoding oracle** (asserts `spectral_centroid >
  580`; the hot octave INFLATES that centroid, so it rewards the defect — a f0-dominant fix fails
  it). **Replace (SC-55-faithful, STRONGER)** with a decoupled pair, measured at BOTH an early
  (0.05–0.15 s) and late (0.30–0.45 s) window (so the octave isn't hot in the attack either):
  1. **f0 dominance:** `20·log10(band(2f0)/band(f0)) ≤ −8 dB` at C4, where `band(fh)` sums Goertzel
     magnitudes over a small window around `fh` (± the course_detune spread, ~±1%) so the two
     detuned KS loops don't read low at the exact bin (Codex/DSP review).
  2. **Odd-shimmer floor:** at least 2 of {h3,h5,h7} above a small floor re f0 (ODD uppers survive
     the comb; decoupled from h2 so a hot octave can't satisfy it, and NOT h4/h6 which the comb nulls).
- **Re-verify (recal only if genuinely shifted, never weaken):** `harp_46_has_soundboard_and_harp_
  wound_law` (350-500 Hz body mode + HF-retention differential at 1200 Hz — `pos` preserves odd HF
  so this should HOLD, unlike a lowpass which would starve it), `dulcimer_level_vs_harp` (±2 dB —
  HARP.amp re-trim), pizz-vs-harp (±10 dB).
- **No harp-only gate oracle needed:** `pos` is a per-preset comb parameter — only HARP's comb
  changes; the render-diff proves the other 23 plucks are byte-identical (the true no-leak gate).

## 7. Decision log
- **D1 — Move the pluck toward centre (`pos`→~0.5)**, NOT a lowpass tilt or the reverted 1/n²: the
  octave is EVEN-harmonic; the comb is the even-selective lever; a lowpass can't reach the target
  without starving shimmer (all 3 reviewers). Physically correct (centre pluck = no even harmonics).
- **D2 — Use the existing `pos` field** — no new field, no gate, no filter, no steady-state buffer.
  Only HARP's comb changes → harp-only by construction.
- **D3 — Recal `harp_46_shimmers` to f0-dominance + ODD-shimmer floor** (stronger; measures the
  actual defect), band-Goertzel around 2f0 for detune, two temporal windows.
- **D4 — Re-trim `HARP.amp` by the measured RMS delta** (all level oracles are RELATIVE to harp).
- **D5 — Pre-bump** (deltic doesn't add bumps — evidence overrides doctrine here).

## 8. Risks
- **Over-centre → pure-odd clarinet tone.** Mitigated by backing `pos` off 0.5 + the odd-shimmer
  floor + Arthur's ear. Primary aesthetic risk.
- **Register/seed variance** (the KS noise burst gives random per-mode projection — Codex). The
  comb shift is systematic (seed-independent); measure ≥2 seeds × C3/C4/C5 to confirm the fix isn't
  a one-seed artifact.
- **amp re-trim direction unknown** — measure, don't assume.
