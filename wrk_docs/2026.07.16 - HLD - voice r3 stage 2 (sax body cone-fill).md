# HLD — Voice R3 Stage 2: sax body cone-fill (GM 64–67)

**Status: SELF-APPROVED** (2 adversarial + Codex reviews folded in — §9) · **Author:** Claude
(Opus) · 2026.07.16 · Effort: M · New samples: no · Brass-loop slice 1 of 4

## 1. Problem
Sax "low-fi/hollow" (round-3). Prong A (rasp de-fuzz) shipped Stage 1. **Prong B**: the bore is
a variable-duty rectangle (`ReedPulse`, duty `w`≈0.27–0.33) whose sinc envelope
(`2|sin(πnw)|/(πn)`) puts a **null on h3**, and the whole upper-mid is thin. Measured (baseline
vs real SC-55, alto C4 / tenor G3, mf; dB re f0):

| | centroid | h2 | **h3** | h4 | h5 | h6 | h7 | h8 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| alto ferro | 1411 | −5.0 | **−17.0** | −15.5 | −16.9 | −22.7 | −25.9 | −22.6 |
| alto SC-55 | 1905 | −11.0 | **−7.2** | −7.8 | −12.6 | −18.1 | −11.7 | −14.9 |
| tenor ferro | 1067 | −3.0 | **−19.2** | −11.2 | −15.0 | −24.9 | −23.9 | −21.2 |
| tenor SC-55 | 1819 | −6.0 | **−5.8** | −11.6 | −7.4 | −3.9 | −13.2 | −18.0 |

Two facts drive the fix: (a) ferro's h3 is notched ~10–13 dB below SC-55; (b) ferro's whole
h3–h8 upper-mid is thin (centroid gap 490/750 Hz) **but its h2 is already over-hot** (ferro
−3…−5 vs SC-55 −6…−11). The RD10 rasp *deliberately re-lands* the duty rectangle (`bias=2w−1`),
so it preserves the null — h3 must be filled by a **parallel** source.

## 2. Goal & non-goals
**Goal:** fill h3 (toward SC-55's ≈ −6 dB) and enrich h3–h8 **without adding h1/h2** (ferro's
even balance is already hot), on the four **saxophones only**, preserving the `bias=2w−1` rasp
identity, without dragging the centroid down.
**Non-goals:** the rasp/duty chain (untouched); h1/h2 (must NOT rise); the double reeds /
clarinet / harmonica / bagpipe / shanai sharing `ReedPreset` (byte-identical, `cone=0`);
f0-tracked resonance (a wah).

## 3. Reference (SC-55-anchored)
Alto/tenor: h3 ≈ −6 dB re f0; centroid up toward ≈ 1800 Hz; h2 unchanged; a full, tapered
h3–h8 upper-mid (a conical bore's complete series, shaped by the body).

## 4. Approach — a targeted upper-harmonic residual (NOT a saw)
**Why not a saw (reviews):** a full `1/n` saw adds h1+h2 — worsening the already-hot h2 and
risking phase-cancellation of the fundamental — and, being low-harmonic-dominated (its power
centroid ≈ 2.5·f0 ≈ 860–1290 Hz), it would *lower* the composite centroid ~60 Hz and **widen**
the SC-55 gap. It fixes the notch but fails the brighten half of the goal.

**The fix: a `ConeResidual` — an additive bank of upper partials only (n = 3..8), no h1/h2.**
Summed into the signal **right after `s` is formed (voices.rs:8730), before the formant bank
(8732)**:
```
let mut s = /* rasp arm — unchanged */;
if let Some(cr) = &mut self.cone_res { s += cr.next(); }   // NEW: Σ w_n·sin(2π·n·φ), n=3..8
for b in &mut self.formants { s = b.process(s); }
```
- **`ConeResidual`** holds a fundamental phase accumulator `φ` (retuned per CTRL tick to the
  composed `f = base·bend·scoop·(1+vib)` beside voices.rs:8689) and a fixed weight array
  `w[3..8]` scaled by the per-preset `cone`. `next()` = `Σ_{n=3}^{8} w_n·sin(2π·n·φ)`, skipping
  any partial with `n·f > 0.45·sr`. Following `f` (incl. vibrato) means each partial FMs by n×,
  the physically-correct behaviour.
- **Fills h3 + lifts the upper-mid, no low-harmonic drag.** Omitting h1/h2 means: no
  fundamental inflation / phase-suppression, no worsening of the hot h2, and the residual's
  energy sits at h3–h8 (786–2094 Hz alto) so the centroid holds/rises rather than dragging.
- **Alias-free & DC-free by construction.** Pure sines, only partials below Nyquist summed → no
  fold-back (no polyBLEP needed — this sidesteps the saw's 1×-rate alias-floor erosion). Sines
  are zero-mean → no DC offset for the downstream biquads.
- **Preserves `bias=2w−1`.** Downstream of and parallel to the tent→bias-tanh→decimate chain;
  the bias oracle rebuilds the tent in isolation and never sees `s`. Additive (not crossfade) →
  the full reed pulse/edge is kept; the RMS rise is small (~+0.4 dB) and cancels in the
  within-render `reed_o2` sustain ratio.
- **Gating (sax-only):** `Reed` gets `cone_res: Option<ConeResidual>` (Some iff `preset.cone>0`).
  Non-sax presets keep `cone=0` → `None` → the `if let` never fires → byte-identical, no
  `self.rng` draw (phases seeded deterministically from the fundamental, not `self.rng`).

### Calibration (first-cut; re-measured)
Per-preset `cone` (start ~0.15–0.25) + a shared taper `w[n]` (first-cut mild, e.g. flat-ish so
the centroid does not drop). Tune each sax to hit output h3 ≈ −6 dB, h2 unchanged, centroid ≥
its cone-off value and toward SC-55, RD-O6 brightness order + span preserved. The re-measure
harness (§6) is the tuning oracle. Double reeds/clarinet/harmonica/bagpipe/shanai: `cone=0`.

## 5. Testability & oracles (tests-first)
- **O-H3 `reed_sax_fills_h3_without_dulling`** — money oracle. Alto (65,C4) + tenor (66,G3),
  **vib forced off, fixed base_f, steady post-scoop window**, cone ON vs cone OFF (`cone=0`,
  same seed → bit-identical off-arm). Via Goertzel `mag_at` at f0/2f0/3f0 + `spectral_centroid`:
  (a) h3/h1 rises ≥ +6 dB into [−12,−2]; (b) **h2 within ±3 dB of off** (no even-collapse / no
  saw-h2); (c) **f0 magnitude within ±1.5 dB of off** (no fundamental suppression); (d)
  **centroid_on ≥ centroid_off** (the anti-saw-drag guard, ties to the "brighten" goal); (e)
  broadband RMS rise < 1.5 dB. Runs at ≥2 velocities.
- **O-SCOPE `reed_cone_is_sax_only`** — enumerate **all 11** reed presets (incl. **HARMONICA**,
  which the existing REED_PRESETS/OPRESSURE arrays omit): assert `cone>0` for the 4 saxes,
  `cone==0` for the 7 non-saxes. (Byte-identity of the non-saxes is proven by the full render-diff,
  §6 — the "force cone_amp on a None voice" check is a tautology and is dropped.)
- **Existing reed/sax oracles must stay green (re-verify; the cone is a static spectral change,
  recal only if genuinely shifted, never weaken an assert):** `o_pressure_p3_sax_keeps_evens`
  (even-frac ≥0.35 — filling odd h3 lowers ferro's 0.89 toward SC-55's 0.47, big margin, but
  don't overshoot below 0.35), `reed_rasp_bias_lands_duty` (unaffected — parallel),
  `reed_rasp_bias_is_load_bearing`, `reed_g3_sax_rasp_skirts` + `reed_sax_rasp_blooms_with_pressure`
  (the residual is ON-lattice → adds to the null-referenced denominator; **verify the delivered-
  output number**, the "common-mode cancels" reasoning was refuted), `reed_o6_sax_ordering`
  (watch the sop/bari **span ≥1.3**, not just order — per-sax `cone` likely needed),
  `reed_o13` DC guard (progs 64-71, DC/rms<1e-3 — residual is DC-free, verify), `reed_o12*_alias_floor`.

## 7. Decision log
- **D1 — Upper-harmonic residual (h3–h8, no h1/h2)**, not a saw and not a duty-shift: fills the
  null AND lifts the upper-mid without inflating the fundamental or the already-hot h2, and
  without a wah.
- **D2 — Additive sine bank, alias-free by construction** (only sub-Nyquist partials): no
  polyBLEP, no 1×-rate alias-floor erosion, no DC.
- **D3 — Inject post-rasp, pre-formant (8730):** preserves `bias=2w−1`; formants+tilt shape the
  added partials. The tilt passes h3 at all playable pressures (cutoff = f0·N(p), N>3 for any real
  note; only a pp whisper darkens ~3 dB — physically correct).
- **D4 — Per-preset `cone` + `Option<ConeResidual>` gate** — mirrors the existing
  `rasp: Option<ReedRasp>` idiom exactly. `ReedPreset` is **crate-internal** (`pub(crate) mod
  voices`, lib.rs:92) — NOT public API — so the new field cannot break semver (patch bump correct).
  All 11 preset consts + the ~12 test literals must add `cone:` (compile-enforced).
- **D5 — Deterministic phases (no `self.rng` draw)** → non-target reed streams untouched.

## 8. Risks
- **Centroid still lags / character too bright (aesthetic).** The residual lifts but may not reach
  1800 Hz; the taper/partial-range are tunable. Final `cone` + brightness need Arthur's ear.
- **Rasp differential compresses.** The residual is on-lattice → inflates the null-referenced
  denominator of `reed_g3`; verify the delivered number, re-measure the ratio pre-blend if needed
  (Stage-1 precedent), never weaken.
- **o6 span / p3 even-frac.** Per-sax `cone` tuning; keep even-frac ≥0.35 (toward SC-55's 0.47).

## 9. Reviews (2 adversarial + Codex — findings & resolutions)
Strong convergence; the design was substantially reworked before implementation.
- **[BLOCKER, Codex+DSP] A 1/n saw is the wrong source** — adds h1/h2 (worsens hot h2, risks f0
  phase-suppression) and is low-harmonic-dominated so it *lowers* the centroid, widening the SC-55
  gap. Both reviewers prescribed an upper-harmonic residual. → **Adopted: `ConeResidual` h3–h8, no
  h1/h2.** (D1)
- **[MAJOR, DSP] Oracle was blind to the centroid + f0** — an h3-only ratio ships the saw's
  brightness failure green, and coherent addition can fake the ratio by suppressing f0. → **O-H3
  now asserts centroid ≥ off, f0 stable, h2 unchanged, vib forced off.** (§5)
- **[MAJOR, blast] O-SCOPE was a tautology + harmonica missing** from the reed preset arrays; and
  the render-diff `--program 64-67` never renders the non-sax material it claims stays identical. →
  **O-SCOPE enumerates all 11 presets incl. harmonica; render-diff is full-catalog.** (§5/§6)
- **[MED, Codex+DSP] "common-mode cancels" for the rasp differential is false** — the cone
  compresses the null-referenced rasp margin. → **Verify the delivered-output number.** (§5/§8)
- **[LOW, blast] semver rationale wrong-but-lucky** — `voices` is `pub(crate)`, so `ReedPreset`
  isn't public API. → **D4 corrected.** DC (DSP §3), level (DSP §4), tilt-rolloff (DSP §1),
  rate/sum (DSP §2) all **refuted** — not problems; but `reed_o13` added to the verify list.
