# 2026.07.16 - PLN - ferrosintesis voice-quality round 3 (15 voices) roadmap

**Mode:** Design-only (investigation + methodical plan). No `voices.rs` edits and no
version bump in this doc — this is the roadmap Arthur approves before any build.

## The ask

Round-3 feedback on the *ferrosintesis Reference Audition* renders flagged 15 GM voices
(0-indexed program numbers, confirmed against the audition index). Arthur asked to note
them all, dig into *why* each is weak, and produce a methodical plan — hypothesis on the
table: "perhaps lack of LA synthesis?" A follow-up sharpened it: the woodwind section is
mostly excellent (**the flute — GM72/73, which carries the LA sampled attack — is
brilliant**); the weak ones are "obvious gaps, perhaps because we lack the samples."

**Headline answer.** The LA-synthesis hypothesis is *half* right, and the half it gets
wrong is the good news. LA sampling in ferrosintesis covers only the **onset** — every
held note is 100 % model from ~0.3 s on (proven: brass holds render bit-identical with
`--no-samples`). So:

- Where the complaint is about **identity / attack**, a missing sample really is the
  cause and the fix is a new CC0 bank (viola sameness, recorder identity, timpani strike).
- Where the complaint is about the **sustain or the noise character** — which is *most*
  of round 3 (brass holds, sax fuzz, flue "sshsshssh", choir, harp balance) — **no
  sample would help**: sampling more onsets cannot fix a sustain, and these are cheap
  in-model bugs. Arthur's two most vivid complaints (the flue "shaking sshsshssh" and the
  sax "fuzz") are **a handful of constants**, not a sample campaign.

The plan therefore splits: cheap model fixes first (most of the wins), a targeted CC0
sample campaign only where the onset is the cue.

## Method — A/B against real hardware

The methodological upgrade this round: every root cause is measured against a **real GM
module**. `mdmidiemu` (on PATH; ROMs at `D:/language/mdsc55/roms`) renders the actual
**Roland SC-55mkII** (`sc55`) and **Yamaha MU-80 / XG** (`mu80`). A dev-side harness
(`probe.py` + `analyze_wav.py`, kept out of the repo) renders each flagged program through
*our* synth and both real modules at the audition register, and A/Bs the spectra
(tonal ratio, spectral centroid, partial levels dB re f0, decay, noise-AM depth,
sustain centroid-wander). The box has no ears — every audible claim below is a number or
a cited line.

Reference discipline learned: **SC-55 is the reliable spectral reference** (clean ~40 ms
onset, `tonal_ratio` ~1.0, no swell). **MU-80 is frequently reverb/swell-dominated**
(peaks 0.002–0.004, ~420 ms pre-roll) — use it for *character*, not absolute levels.

This is round 3. Prior art (07.14 voice-quality overhaul HLD, 07.14 round-2 CR, 07.11
woodwind/LA comprehensive design, 07.13 anchor-fidelity HLD) is built on, not re-trod;
per-program prior status is folded into the findings below.

## Per-family findings (evidence + fix)

### Solo strings — 040 Violin, 041 Viola, 043 Contrabass  · P1 · effort M · new samples: yes (viola)
- **40 == 41, proven two ways.** (1) *Identical onset*: both route through `violin_bank`
  at the same `LA_VIOLIN` gain 0.30 / fade (0.12, 0.38) (`voices.rs:10206` vs `:10248`).
  Windowed diff of the two renders: [0,120 ms) diffRMS/RMS = 0.059 (−24.6 dB) at key64 —
  the recorded violin attack, the strongest "which instrument" cue, is bit-identical for
  both. Round-2 deliberately gave viola the violin bank (it was the only bare solo-bowed
  default) — so today's sameness is a direct consequence of that call. (2) *Tail too
  subtle*: `BODY_VIOLIN` vs `BODY_VIOLA` gives centroid 3812 vs 3605 Hz (viola only 6 %
  darker); SC-55 makes it **1653 vs 2195 → 33 % darker**, via a 1.5–2 kHz formant bump,
  not a smooth tilt. We separate them ~4–5× too weakly, and only in the region the shared
  sample covers.
- **43 unconvincing = too bright.** Centroid ours 1279 vs SC-55 701 Hz at E2 (1.82×), 914
  vs 469 at E1 (1.95×). Mechanism: the contrabass arm inherits the cello's in-loop damping
  `refl_sustain=2600` Hz **but has less output damping than the cello** (`out_lp=None` vs
  cello 2100 Hz) — so our double bass is brighter than our cello, backwards from real
  acoustics (heavy wound bass strings damp HF *more*). Body resonance at 700 Hz
  (`voices.rs:7230`) is too high for a bass corpus. The weak E1 fundamental is *not* a bug
  (SC-55 also has weak f0 there — correct missing-fundamental physics).
- **SoA.** Waveguide + arco LA is already the SoA-ish shipped approach (matches Spitfire/VSL
  sample-modelling philosophy for the onset). The gap is spectral tuning, not architecture.
  A full per-string waveguide section was rejected in the anchor HLD.
- **Fix.** (a) **Viola bank** (the real 40==41 fix): wire a dedicated CC0 `viola_bank` from
  VSCO-2-CE Viola Section susvib (already-pinned source), route GM41 to it, pair with a
  darker `BODY_VIOLA` toward the ~0.75× violin centroid ratio. (b) Contrabass: darken the
  waveguide (`refl_sustain` 2600→~1400, add `out_lp` ~1200, `body_f` 700→~250) to
  concentrate energy in low harmonics. Re-verify the distinctness oracles (a darker viola
  makes them *more* distinct). **Wolf-band** keys 46-50 (waveguide mode-locks to 3·f0) is a
  known pre-existing defect — park as its own slice.

### Orchestral harp 046 + Timpani 047  · P1 · effort M · new samples: no (model); optional bank Stage 4
- **Timpani — severe: no highs at all.** Attack power band-split (0–150 ms) key45: **ours
  100 % below 300 Hz, 0 % above**; SC-55 56 % <300 / 38 % in 300 Hz–1 kHz / 5 % >1 kHz.
  Modal bank tops at 2.245·f0 (247 Hz at key45) and the only broadband part — the mallet
  strike — is lowpassed at 300 Hz (`voices.rs:1469`). No felt-beater "thwack." Velocity
  brightness is negligible (centroid 178→179 Hz across v96→v120). Mode ratios 1.742/2.245
  also depart from the canonical kettledrum set 1:1.5:2:2.44:2.89 and stop two modes short.
- **Harp — octave-hollow.** 2nd harmonic +5.7 dB over f0 at C4 (SC-55 −26.6, MU-80 −19.4)
  → our octave is ~32 dB hot. KS excitation is flat-spectrum shaped only by the feed-forward
  comb (`voices.rs:3345`): at pluck pos 0.35 the comb gives h2 only −0.8 dB vs h1, so h2 ≈
  h1 instead of a real string's 1/n rolloff; the round-2 shimmer raise keeps those flat
  uppers ringing and the 400 Hz body-EQ peak (`voices.rs:2661`) tips h2 over f0. Upper harp
  (key72) also rings 2.7× too short (T60 1.02 vs 2.71 s).
- **Fix.** Timpani: add a bandpassed felt-mallet strike ~800–1500 Hz over the tuned modes
  (reuse the `drums.rs` membrane strike), extend the modal bank to canonical ratios.
  Harp: shape the KS excitation to a 1/n (−6 dB/oct) tilt behind a **harp-only** preset
  field (the excitation code `voices.rs:3319-3347` is shared by *all* Plucks — must not
  contaminate guitars/bass), undo the shimmer over-raise, move the body peak off the octave.
  **Both need their blocking oracle recalibrated** (see Risks). Optional Stage-4 LA onset
  banks (VCSL harp / VSCO timpani strike) layer fidelity over the corrected models.

### Vocal ensemble — 052 Choir Aahs, 053 Voice Oohs, 054 Synth Voice  · P1 · effort M · new samples: no CC0 source exists
- **Wrong formant topology.** The parallel narrow-Q bandpass bank (qs [9,10,9],
  `voices.rs:5671`) carves deep valleys *between* formants: GM52 aah key48 h3/h4 =
  −21/−29 dB where **both real modules peak** (SC-55 +8.1/−5.3). A cascade formant
  topology (Klatt 1980) auto-corrects formant amplitudes; a parallel bank distorts the
  vowel. This is the textbook "hollow/synthetic vowel" cause.
- **Voiced as a bright solo singer.** Always-on singer's-formant cluster + strong F2/F3 +
  a raw saw source (−6 dB/oct vs a real glottal flow's −12) push our centroid far above
  both references: GM52 2479 vs SC-55 1829 / MU-80 1522.
- **GM54 inverted** — ours is the brightest (2962 Hz); both modules render it *darkest*
  (1023–1168). Untouched by prior rounds, and an oracle (`choir2_formant_placement_per_vowel`,
  `voices.rs:5176`) actively pins it bright — encoding a premise the hardware refutes.
- **Fix.** Re-voice GM54 dark; add a −6 dB/oct glottal source tilt on the summed voices;
  widen qs [9,10,9]→~[5,6,5] to fill the notches; trim 52/53 singer's-formant. Recalibrate
  the oracle. No CC0 choir source exists (VSCO has no choir), so this stays model-only. If
  the parallel-bank approximation underdelivers on A/B, a full **Klatt cascade port** is the
  L-effort fallback (parked).

### Brass holds 056–061  · P1 · effort M · new samples: no
- **Frozen sustain is the tell.** With no authored CC/aftertouch (committed album MIDIs
  author none) every timbre driver is constant during the hold: bloom at sustain 1.0,
  pressure 1.0, growl 0, bite decayed. The radiated spectrum does not move — centroid
  p5–p95 wander **2–13 Hz** (under one 10.8 Hz FFT bin) vs SC-55's 88–191 Hz (7–75×); RMS
  ripple 0.1–0.4 dB vs 0.5–4 dB. And solo naturals have **no vibrato** (`BR_DEFAULTS.vib =
  (0,0,0)`, `voices.rs:8762`; only sections vibrate). A perfectly periodic waveform with
  zero micro-variation is the textbook synth signature — and it only affects the *hold*,
  which is exactly why attacks (LA + rasp) sound good and holds don't.
- **Fix.** Fold a shallow, decorrelated, off-4-Hz modulator into `L` (`voices.rs:9166`) so
  lip fc / kws / cascade / out_lp / amp / pitch all breathe together — target SC-55's
  wander band. This *reuses the same modulator* built for the flue quick win (see Shared
  infrastructure). A secondary spectral-balance miss (our h2–h5 ~17 dB weaker than real
  brass's low-mid formant ring) is a bigger, riskier body-EQ change — parked behind the
  liveness fix, which should recover most of the "synthetic" perception on its own. Leave
  synth brass 62/63 untouched.

### Saxophones 064–067  · P1 · effort S (fuzz) / M (body) · new samples: no
- **"Fuzz" = rasp turbulence.** Lowpassed white noise injected pre-tanh (`voices.rs:8174`)
  intermodulates through the g≈16 tanh into broadband skirts, scaling **p⁴** with pressure
  (velocity sweep: tenor inter-harmonic fraction 0.0010→0.0052→0.0433 at vel 30/96/120). At
  album mf soprano carries 0.0227 inter-harmonic energy vs SC-55's **0.0001** (~200× more).
  Not aliasing (nyquist energy 0.0 on every render — the 2× oversample/ADAA works).
- **"Low-fi/hollow" = duty-null.** The variable-duty rectangle at w≈0.30 puts its first
  sinc null at h3, gutting it to −15…−20 dB across all four saxes; a real conical-bore sax
  has no such null (SC-55 h3 = +1…+4 dB).
- **Fix.** Prong A (Stage 1, quick win): cut rasp `noise_base` ~4–6× keeping the tanh
  harmonic drive → removes the fuzz (target inter-harmonic <0.005). Prong B (Stage 2):
  blend a fuller-harmonic component to defeat the h3 null and lift the mid-register centroid
  toward SC-55 (~2200 Hz), preserving the `bias = 2w−1` identity.

### Flue/whistle pipes 074–078  · P1 · effort S (breath) / M (bodies) · new samples: optional Stage 4
- **The "shaking sshsshssh" is one line.** `breath_mod = 1.0 + 0.5*vib_val`
  (`voices.rs:6778`) applies a fixed ±50 % AM to every noise component, driven by the
  *same* LFO as the pitch vibrato (so it is coherent, not turbulent) at 4.5–5.5 Hz. That is
  **exactly the ~4 Hz fluctuation-strength peak** (Fastl/Zwicker) — the modulation rate the
  ear is most sensitive to as "shaking." Measured noise-AM depth: pan 66 %, bottle 102 %,
  shakuhachi **187 %**, vs SC-55's steady 14–36 %. Why the flute doesn't suffer despite the
  identical coefficient: its breath *level* is quiet (0.09) so the pulsing is masked; the
  complained pipes carry 0.24–0.80 breath, exposing it. **Root cause = loud breath × deep
  coherent AM at the 4 Hz peak — not "too much breath" per se.**
- **Per-program secondaries.** Bottle is genuinely over-noisy (tonal 0.68 vs SC-55 1.00 —
  round-1's Helmholtz redesign made noise the *primary* source; a real blown bottle is a
  fairly pure Helmholtz self-oscillation). Recorder's harmonic balance is inverted (ours
  even-dominant, dies after h4; SC-55 odd-tilted to h10) — and an oracle hard-asserts the
  wrong `h2 ≥ h3`. Whistle is the least broken.
- **Fix.** Stage 1 (quick win): replace the pitch-locked ±50 % AM with a shallow (~0.12)
  **decorrelated** flutter from an independent deterministic LFO off the 4 Hz peak (a
  deterministic LFO adds no rng draw, so the differential seam oracle stays aligned — low
  risk); trim pan-flute breath 0.24→~0.12 and shakuhachi 0.30→~0.18 (keep the muraiki
  shelf). Stage 2: correct the recorder even/odd body and relax its oracle; walk the bottle
  back toward SC-55's clean tone (see open question). Optional Stage 4: a VCSL Baroque
  recorder onset bank to settle its identity.

## Cross-cutting themes

1. **LA is onset-only → "holds synthetic."** The load-bearing fact. It splits the whole
   roadmap: *animate/correct the model sustain* (cheap; covers brass, winds, choir, sax)
   vs *extend sampling into the sustain* (a new looped-sampler architecture — deferred,
   probably unneeded). A new sample fixes an *onset/identity* complaint, never a sustain.
2. **The "living signal" axis — frozen vs over-modulated, same physics.** Brass is frozen;
   flues are the opposite (a coherent breath AM on the 4 Hz peak). **One shared primitive
   solves both:** a shallow, per-voice-decorrelated, off-4-Hz modulator — winds turn it
   down and decorrelate, brass turns it up and routes it through the timbre coupling.
3. **Noise reads as artifact.** The flue "sshsshssh" and the sax "fuzz" are both
   noise-*character* errors. Real reediness/breathiness lives in harmonics plus subtle
   *incoherent* turbulence. Shared philosophy: move the "edge/air" budget off broadband
   noise onto harmonics (sax) or a decorrelated shallow flutter (flues).
4. **Wrong spectral balance / octave errors.** Harp octave-hollow, choir inter-formant
   notches, sax h3 duty-null, viola 4× too weakly separated. Shared metric: partial-levels
   re-f0 vs the hardware.
5. **Oracles that encode the defect (the deepest risk).** Three provably-wrong oracles
   actively block the correct fix: `harp_46_shimmers` (rewards the octave-heavy balance),
   choir `CH2-O2` (pins GM54 bright), recorder `WD-O1` (asserts h2 ≥ h3 backwards). Past
   rounds optimized wrong oracles — this is why complaints persisted. **Calibrate each
   oracle against hardware A/B *before* fixing.**
6. **A/B-against-hardware is itself shared infrastructure** — the harness that nailed every
   root cause becomes the calibration reference for every fix.

## Shared infrastructure (build once, serve many)

- **A. The living-breath / turbulence micro-modulator** — one DSP spec (slow ~2–3 Hz,
  deliberately off the 4 Hz peak, zero-mean, per-voice-decorrelated). Zero-mean protects
  every ratio-based oracle. First built for the Stage-1 flue quick win (deterministic, no
  new rng draw), then reused by brass in Stage 2. Each family draws from its own rng — keep
  it deterministic or gate the draw so no family's stream shifts.
- **B. The `prepare.py` CC0 sample pipeline + orchestral-crate growth** — the
  locked-but-never-executed "grow CC0 ~3× to 45–55 MiB" (woodwind HLD §6.2). VSCO-2-CE is
  already pinned → viola + contrabass banks are *incremental*. VCSL is a *new* CC0 source
  (recorder/harp/timpani) needing a fresh SHA pin. `contrabass_bank` is the wiring template.
  Samples are **source**, never git-ignored.
- **C. The hardware-calibration harness + per-voice target table** — `probe.py` +
  `analyze_wav.py` (dev-side, out of the repo) plus the measured SC-55 target deltas per
  voice. These numbers become the re-pin values for recalibrated oracles and the ear-in-the-
  loop A/B targets.
- **D. Oracle-recalibration + render-diff discipline** — recalibrate defect-encoding
  oracles from hardware, RED-before/GREEN-after in the *same* fix commit; full
  `cargo test -p ferrosintesis --lib` at broad scope (harp is the level reference for the
  whole pizz/bowed family); render-diff inventory from the task **merge-base** SHA per
  shipped change.

## Risks & oracles

- **Render-diff inventory** (report, not gate) for every `voices.rs`/`sampler.rs`/`engine.rs`
  change: baseline = task **merge-base** SHA (never HEAD). Expected diffs *only* on the
  touched programs' albums; a diff on an untouched family = contamination bug — bisect,
  don't ship. `render_diff.py` keys on the program byte only, so adjudicate bank-dependent
  changes (viola/contrabass/recorder/harp/timpani) **per bank**.
- **Oracle recalibration is the deepest risk.** The three defect-encoding oracles must be
  re-derived from hardware in their fix commits (RED-before/GREEN-after), never weakened
  standalone. Run the *full* lib test suite at broad scope — harp is the BW-O9 level
  reference for the whole pizz/bowed family (round-2 brightening already broke 3 of its
  level oracles; trim amp ratio-invariantly). Audio oracles stay **ratios**, never absolute
  floors (the CLI peak-normalizes). Regenerate the golden mix fixture after choir/broad
  changes.
- **RNG / shared-struct.** Brass/Wind/Reed each own their rng (no cross-family
  contamination), but adding an rng *draw* within a family shifts that family's stream and
  breaks its determinism/seam oracles → prefer deterministic LFOs or gate the draw. ChoirV2
  is forked off SawStack (protected) — don't add per-Layer fields/draws (would
  un-byte-identical pads/leads). The harp excitation tilt must be a harp-only preset field
  or it contaminates all Plucks.
- **Licensing / semver.** viola/contrabass = VSCO-2-CE (already pinned, CC0). recorder/harp
  = VCSL (CC0, new pin — vet + SHA-pin). timpani = VSCO Timpani (CC0). Choir = **no CC0
  source exists** → model-only. Hard constraint: no CC-BY (Salamander), no
  Sampling-Plus/Iowa. All model tuning = patch bump; sample-bank additions add no public
  API → still patch/minor; one bump per integrated task.
- **The box has no ears.** Every root cause is measured, but no prototype was rendered — the
  exact fix depths (brass wander, flue flutter, sax skirt, viola darkening, choir centroid)
  are first-cut targets from the SC-55 delta and need one build-iteration re-measure +
  Arthur's ear-in-the-loop A/B to land inside the target band.
