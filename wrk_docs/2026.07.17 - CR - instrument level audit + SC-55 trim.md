# 2026.07.17 — CR — instrument level audit + SC-55-referenced per-program trim

## The question

Arthur: "I'm not convinced all the instruments are at the same level. Are they all
normalized to the same levels?"

## Diagnosis: no per-instrument normalization exists

ferrosintesis has **no per-program loudness normalization**. Each voice produces
`vel_amp(vel) * gain`, where `gain` is a hand-tuned per-voice constant; the mix's only
per-voice scaling is the authored `volume * expr * at_gain * breath` (CC7/CC11/pressure/
breath). The single global stage normalizes the whole *mix* to −18 LUFS — it sets album
loudness but does nothing about instrument-to-instrument balance. A few pairwise oracles
pin relative levels within the percussion/mallet/alt-bank world (dulcimer-vs-harp,
tinkle-vs-glock, …), but nothing pins the melodic families against each other.

### Measured imbalance (128 GM programs, equal key/velocity/CC, dry, peak-normalized)

Peak-normalize is a single linear scalar (verified: files peak at −1.00 dBFS, 0 clipped
samples), so relative dB across programs is exact. Whole-note RMS is the fair loudness
proxy for sustained voices; early-window (0–250 ms) RMS for plucked/percussive (whole-note
RMS unfairly penalizes fast decay). Register-robust across C3 and C4.

Systematic offsets found: flutes/pipes ran ~+8 dB hot, solo strings & low brass +8..+12,
string/synth sections and choir −5..−9 quiet, reed already near-median. Struck/plucked/
percussive families read very quiet on whole-note RMS but that is envelope, not level —
their attack peaks sit within ~10 dB of the sustained voices.

## Decision: match the SC-55mkII balance, conservative / damped

Arthur chose to level toward a real GM module's balance (keeps flute-quieter-than-trumpet)
rather than flatten to equal loudness, and chose the **conservative / damped** scope.

### Method

- Reference: Roland SC-55mkII via `mdmidiemu --synth sc55` (ROMs at
  `D:/language/mdsc55/roms/sc55`), same probe MIDIs, CC91/CC93 = 0 (dry).
- Each engine's per-program level expressed re its own median (absolute level cancels);
  `delta = ferro − sc55` is how much hotter ferro is. Averaged over C3+C4.
- **Correction = −delta**, restricted to **sustained families only** (whole-note RMS is
  trustworthy there), × **0.70 strength**, clamped to **±6 dB**, 1 dB dead-band, round 0.5.
- Left untouched (0.0): Piano, ChromPerc, Guitar, Bass, pizz/harp/timpani, orchestra-hit,
  SynthFX, Ethnic, Percussive, SoundFX — envelope-confounded or noise/FX.

Result: 48 programs trimmed. Flagship moves — string/synth sections & choir **+5..+6 dB**,
solo strings/brass/flutes/organ **−4..−6 dB**.

## Implementation

`engine.rs`: `PROGRAM_TRIM_DB: [f32; 128]` + `program_trim_lin(program)`, applied at the
melodic channel strip (`g *= trim`, ch 9 drums exempt — they are key-indexed and levelled
by `kit_balance()`). `g` feeds both the dry mix and every FX send, so the trim scales the
whole voice together: **pure level, timbre- and wet/dry-neutral**.

Oracle: `program_trim_scope_and_calibration` pins the conservative scope (the corrected
set is exactly the sustained ranges; its complement must stay 0.0) plus the flagship
values, the ±6 dB clamp, and the dB→linear math.

## Verification

- **Containment** (baseline 302b6a6 vs new, probe re-render): untouched programs move
  worst 0.019 dB (bit-identical); corrected programs land within 0.51 dB of their trim
  (the ≤0.5 dB wobble is the master bus-glue compressor reacting to the level change —
  expected, benign).
- **Convergence** (family residual vs SC-55, before → after): Ensemble +6.3 → +2.5,
  Organ −5.1 → −1.4, Pipe −5.1 → −1.6, Brass −4.1 → −1.4, Reed −0.8 → −0.1. Residuals of
  ~1–2.5 dB are the intended 0.70× damping, not error.
- **Blast radius** (static program scan + real-album render-cmp): only albums using a
  nonzero-trim program change (choir/sections/organ/brass/flute are ubiquitous → 106/109
  tracks). "Ten Thousand Watts" (no corrected program) renders **bit-identical**; choir/
  organ tracks differ at **identical length** (level-only).
- Full workspace suite green; `golden_mix_balance_holds` re-captured for ch 8 (StringEns1,
  +5.5 dB; centroid unchanged — level-only), all other rows bit-exact.
