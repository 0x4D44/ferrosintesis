## 1. Problem
The choir voices are too bright and hollow vs the real modules (a `ChoirV2` voice; GM52/53/54 are
the only programs reaching `choir()`, voices.rs:10799). Measured (centroid Hz, ours vs SC-55 vs
MU-80, keys 60/55):
- **GM52 Choir Aahs:** 1990 vs 1078/1056 — **~1.85× too bright** (always-on singer's-formant + a
  raw −6 dB/oct saw source; a real glottal flow is −12).
- **GM53 Voice Oohs:** h2 −11/−5 vs SC-55 −20/−10 — **~9 dB too hot** (SC-55 ooh is fundamental-only).
- **GM54 Synth Voice — INVERTED:** 2718/2323 = the brightest of our three, but SC-55 1003/763 and
  MU-80 827/899 render it the **darkest** — **2.7–3.3× too bright**. The flagship defect.
- **Inter-formant notch:** GM52 h3/h4 = −37/−37 where SC-55 = −12/−20 and MU-80 = −22/+1 — a narrow-Q
  parallel formant bank carving **17–38 dB notches where the modules peak** ("hollow/synthetic vowel").

**Klatt verdict:** the investigation confirms the cheap fix (dark + glottal tilt + Q-widen) reaches
SC-55 without a Klatt cascade — the tilt roughly halves the centroid, GM54 dark/GM53 ooh are pure
preset moves, and Q 9→5 + the existing chest-branch parallel source fill the notches. **Klatt stays
parked** (Arthur's A/B is the only remaining Klatt trigger — escalate only if the render still reads
hollow after these knobs).

## 4. Approach — 5 choir-scoped knobs (no RNG draws)
1. **Glottal source tilt −6→−12 dB/oct** (all three, the biggest centroid lever). Add
   `tilt_lp: [OnePole; 4]` to `ChoirV2` (one per section), `OnePole::lowpass(CH2_TILT_HZ, sr)`, applied
   to the voiced section sum at voices.rs:6135 **before** the tract + before the dry/chest. A one-pole
   is −6 dB/oct; on the saw's −6 that's −12 above the corner. `CH2_TILT_HZ` ~180 Hz first-cut (tune to
   the centroid target; too low muffles the fundamental).
2. **Widen vowel Qs** voices.rs:5971 `[9,10,9] → ~[5,6,5]` — broaden the bandpass skirts so they
   overlap and lift the inter-formant floor (h3/h4).
3. **Trim always-on singer's-formant** on GM52 + GM54 (`sf_gains`): removes a fixed 2.9–3.25 kHz
   brightness injector (GM52 `[0.30,0.18]→~[0.18,0.10]`).
4. **Re-voice GM54 dark** (the `_ =>` preset, voices.rs:5960): F2 `1900→~1050`, F3 `2600→~2400`;
   vgains `[1,0.70,0.40]→~[1,0.35,0.12]`; sf `[0.35,0.22]→~[0.12,0.06]`; **chest_gain `0.0→~0.45`**
   (the fundamental restore the modules show); re-trim `amp_mul` to hold CH2-O6.
5. **GM53 h2**: `vgains[1]` (F2 gain) `0.45→~0.30` — pull h2 down ~5–10 dB toward SC-55.

All 5 are `voices.rs`/`choir()`/`ChoirV2` only; the dispatch is a single `52..=54` arm → choir-scoped
by construction. **Calibrate by an eprintln sweep + re-measure at keys 60 + 55** to hit §3.
