# 2026.07.18 — CR — Guitar voicing, LA usage, and SoTA comparison

Assessment of how ferrosintesis voices the GM guitar family (programs 24–31), which
guitars use the LA (sample) layer, and how the result compares to commercial
state-of-the-art. Written against ferrosintesis v0.21.24. Read-only analysis — no code
change. Code pointers are `file:line` at the time of writing.

## TL;DR

- **LA (sample layer) is used on the two ACOUSTIC guitars only, and only for the attack.**
  GM24 (nylon) and GM25 (steel) crossfade a thin onset sample into a Karplus-Strong model
  that owns the sustain. GM26–31 (jazz, clean, muted, overdriven, distortion, harmonics)
  are **pure modelled** — no samples at all.
- **Versus commercial SoTA: not the same category on realism, but a competent, musical GM
  player for the constraints** (zero third-party code deps, ~17.7 MiB total sample bank,
  from-scratch Rust, real-time-capable). SoTA realism is multi-GB round-robined/articulated
  sample libraries (acoustic + electric) plus ML-captured amps (electric). We trade realism
  for footprint and self-containment.
- **Highest-leverage realism gap:** the acoustic LA has no round robins and no velocity
  layers → repeated notes are identical (machine-gun risk). The electric amp is a static
  analytic shaper, not an ML/IR capture.

## 1. Guitar voicing architecture (GM 24–31)

All guitars are a Karplus-Strong `Pluck` at the core (`voices.rs`), one preset per program.
The acoustic pair additionally wraps an LA onset sample; the electric pair 29/30 additionally
runs a channel amplifier insert.

| GM | Instrument | Core voice (preset) | LA layer | Amp insert |
|----|------------|---------------------|----------|------------|
| 24 | Acoustic (nylon)   | `Pluck(&NYLON)`    | **Yes** — `guitar_bank()` | — |
| 25 | Acoustic (steel)   | `Pluck(&STEEL)`    | **Yes** — `steel_bank()`  | — |
| 26 | Electric (jazz)    | `Pluck(&JAZZ)`     | No | — |
| 27 | Electric (clean)   | `Pluck(&CLEAN)`    | No | — |
| 28 | Electric (muted)   | `Pluck(&MUTED)`    | No | — |
| 29 | Overdriven         | `Pluck(&DRIVE)`    | No | **Yes** — `Drive` (overdrive voicing) |
| 30 | Distortion         | `Pluck(&DRIVE)`    | No | **Yes** — `Drive` (distortion voicing) |
| 31 | Harmonics          | `Pluck(&HARMONIC)` | No | — |

Routing: `voices::make` arms at `voices.rs:10821` (24), `:10844` (25), `:10861–10865`
(26–31). The `Pluck` core (course-coupling, humbucker RLC, wound-string split, e-bow
sustainer) is a fairly detailed from-scratch Karplus-Strong.

Note GM29 and GM30 build the **identical** core voice (`&DRIVE`); see §3.

### CC0 alt bank
GM29/30 have a CC0 alt-bank voicing: `Pluck(&DRIVE_LEAD)` — a *sustaining* e-bow lead vs the
default *decaying* pluck (`altbank.rs`). The alt guitars 24/25 deliberately drop the LA layer
(pure model) so the sampled attack cannot reach an alt-bank channel
(`altbank_guitars_skip_sample_layer`). Guitars 26/27/28/31 have no alt voicing.

## 2. LA (sample) layer — exactly what it is

The LA layer here is an **onset-only** hybrid: the sample owns roughly the first 50–200 ms
(the pluck transient), then crossfades out and the model carries the body and sustain. Gain
and fade: `LA_GUITAR = (0.42, (0.05, 0.20))` (`voices.rs:7848`). It is behind the default
`embedded-samples` Cargo feature; `--no-samples` renders the pure model.

| Program | Source | Licence | Zones | Layering |
|---------|--------|---------|-------|----------|
| 24 nylon | FreePats "Spanish Classical Guitar" (2019-06-18) | CC0-1.0 | 7 zones E2–E5, ~6-semitone spacing (≤ ±3.5 st repitch) | **Single flat layer** — one take per note, **no velocity layers, no round robins** (`sampler.rs:1052`; `prepare.py` `SCG_*`) |
| 25 steel | 2017 Martin HD28 Vintage, CC0-dedicated by Jeff Learman (Discord SFZ GM Bank) | CC0 (SHA-pinned `STEEL_REV`) | 8 zones E2–B5, ~6-semitone spacing | Single flat layer, same as nylon (`sampler.rs:1059`; `prepare.py` `STEEL_*`) |
| 26–31 | — | — | — | **No LA layer — pure Karplus-Strong** |

Footprint context: the two acoustic guitars use ~15 WAVs out of the synth's entire
~17.7 MiB / 218-file bank. `LaVoice::vel_amp` scales the flat transient with velocity, which
is the only dynamic variation the acoustic LA has.

## 3. GM29/30 — the amp is the instrument

GM29 (overdrive) and GM30 (distortion) share one string voice (`&DRIVE`). What distinguishes
them is a **program-keyed** overdrive/cabinet insert, `Drive`, applied on the channel
(`engine.rs:275`), gated by `needs_drive(prog) = matches!(prog, 29 | 30)` (`engine.rs:198`).

Chain, all at 2× oversampling: HP 90 Hz → pre-voicing EQ → **biased (asymmetric) tanh**
stage 1 (even-harmonic warmth) → interstage tilt EQ → symmetric tanh stage 2 → DC blocker →
**5-biquad speaker cabinet** (which doubles as the anti-alias decimation filter,
`engine.rs:252`). Static chain: the pick, being the loudest instant, clips hardest — as a
real amp does (`engine.rs:269`).

The program split (`engine.rs:307`):

| | GM29 Overdrive ("mid-push lead") | GM30 Distortion ("scooped chug") |
|---|---|---|
| stage-1 gain `g1` | 9.0 | 8.0 |
| stage-2 gain `g2` | 2.0 | **3.0** (harder 2nd stage) |
| output `post` | 0.12 | 0.145 |
| voice EQ | **+4 dB @ 800 Hz** (mid push) | **−5 dB @ 650 Hz** (mid scoop) |
| interstage tilt | **+2 dB @ 1200 Hz** (lead bite) | **−4 dB @ 700 Hz** (deepen scoop) |

Measured (2.5 s A/B probe, key 64 vel 100, default −18 LUFS): GM30 is brighter and harder
(zero-crossing rate 0.068 vs GM29 0.038), consistent with the hotter 2nd stage and the mid
scoop. Related behaviours: the insert rebuilds on a program change so 29↔30 mid-song
choreography works (`engine.rs:1796`); a per-channel voice cap of 8
(`DRIVEN_GUITAR_VOICE_LIMIT`, `engine.rs:205`, `make_room_for_driven_guitar`) stops held
e-bow notes piling up; electric guitars (26–31) get chorus 0.10 + echo 0.30 on the bus
(`fx_profile`, `engine.rs:389`).

## 4. Comparison to state-of-the-art

Three distinct SoTA targets — worth separating, because we sit differently against each.

**(a) Sampled realism (the commercial bar for "real guitar").**
Reference: Orange Tree Evolution series, Ample Sound (AGM/AGT/AGL), NI Session Guitarist,
Prominy SC/LPC, Impact Soundworks Shreddage. These are **multi-gigabyte**: per-string *and*
per-fret sampling, several velocity layers, 4–8+ round robins per note, dedicated
articulation samples (palm mutes, pinch/natural harmonics, slides, hammer-ons, release
samples), fret and pick mechanical noise, and a strumming/phrase engine.
**Us:** ~7–8 samples per acoustic guitar, one layer, no round robins, no articulations, no
mechanical noise. A couple of orders of magnitude below this — a different category.

**(b) Physical modelling (the modelled bar).**
Reference: AAS Strum GS-2 — a waveguide/Karplus-Strong guitar with a strumming and voicing
engine. **Us:** the electric guitars are the same lineage (from-scratch KS with
course-coupling, humbucker RLC, wound-string split, e-bow sustainer). The core model is
genuinely respectable for hand-written code — roughly in Strum's family, minus the strum /
voicing engine and articulation control.

**(c) Electric tone / amp (the amp bar) — our widest gap.**
Reference: ML amp capture — Neural Amp Modeler (NAM), Kemper, Neural DSP, IK ToneX — plus
convolution (IR) cabinets, near-indistinguishable from real amps. **Us:** a static analytic
amp — two `tanh` stages + a 5-biquad cabinet at 2× (`engine.rs:275`). Musical and cheap, but
well below a captured/IR rig.

## 5. Concrete gaps vs SoTA (ranked by realism leverage)

1. **No round robins / velocity layers on the acoustic LA** → repeated notes are
   byte-identical (machine-gun tell). ~~The repo already solved this elsewhere by faking RR2
   from an adjacent velocity layer (grand piano) — portable here.~~ *Correction 2026.07.18:
   NOT portable — the trick needs ≥2 takes or velocity layers per note, and both guitar
   sources are single-take/single-layer; a web sweep found no CC0 acoustic-guitar set with
   RR or velocity layers, so variation must be synthesized (see the guitar-realism-block HLD).*
2. **No mechanical / articulation samples** — fret squeak, pick noise, slides, palm mutes,
   pinch harmonics, release samples.
3. **Sparse zone maps + repitch** (±3.5 st) audibly stretches timbre on wide intervals.
4. **Electric amp is analytic**, not ML/IR — the ceiling on electric-tone realism.

## 6. Verdict

For its constraints — zero third-party code, ~18 MiB total bank, real-time-capable, GM player
voiced for the fable5 (Oldfield-idiom) albums — ferrosintesis's guitars are a competent,
musical result. The acoustic onset-hybrid is a genuinely clever low-footprint technique, and
the electric model is a solid from-scratch Karplus-Strong. Measured against commercial guitar
SoTA they are not in the same category: SoTA realism is gigabytes of round-robined,
articulated samples plus ML-captured amps. The trade is deliberate — realism for footprint
and self-containment — and it is doing its job for the intended use.
