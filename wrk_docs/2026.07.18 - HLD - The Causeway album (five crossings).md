# HLD — *The Causeway* (five crossings for band, strings, choir and tide)

**Artist:** Claude Fable 5 · **Idiom:** the listener's two poles made into a
geography. A tidal island and a mainland village, joined by a causeway that only
shows at low water; two players, one on each shore, send music instead of letters
across a winter. The **island** writes in late-ABBA ice (The Visitors / Super
Trouper — incantatory repeated notes, off-beat pushes, sequenced chill) wrapped in
Enigma/Delerium weather (breath flutes, vowel choirs, echo throws). The
**mainland** writes in McCartney warmth (Band on the Run / Flowers in the Dirt —
melodic protagonist bass, piano pump, clavinet strut, suite-form pivots), layered
with Oldfield patience (additive builds, tubular bells). **Original material
throughout** — vocabulary of the idioms, quotes nothing.

**Story arc (the machine-verified spine):** the two shores' themes begin a tritone
apart and converge track by track — 6 → 4 → 3 → 2 → 0 semitones — while every
cadence is modally withheld (no leading-tone V–I anywhere in tracks 1–4) and the
themes are **never allowed to sound at the same time**. Track 5 crosses at dawn:
both themes in D, first simultaneity, invertible counterpoint, a fusion phrase
that plants the mainland's leap inside the island's incantation, the album's only
melodic tonic landing, and a IV–I plagal Picardy. Estrangement → storm → turning →
darkest hour → union.

**Shape:** The Remaining's federated grid, verbatim machinery — `conductor.Part`
per track, one module per track under `movements/`, shared parameterized
`verify.py`, per-track oracles, `analyze.py` audio checks. Album dir:
`albums/fable5/The Causeway/`.

## 1. Global material (all data in `material.py`, oracle-pinned)

### 1.1 THE ISLAND THEME (the ABBA pole — an incantation that refuses to land)
Ten notes, 8 beats, aeolian degrees. Repeated-note head, off-beat pushes,
stepwise fall, and a hang on **degree 2** (never the tonic):

```
(onset, dur, degree): (0,.5,5) (.5,.5,5) (1,1.5,5) (2.5,.5,4) (3,1,3)
                      (4,.5,4) (4.5,.5,5) (5,1,3) (6,.5,1) (6.5,1.5,2)
contour: 5-5-5-4-3-4-5-3-1-2   (B B B— A G / A B G E F#— in E minor)
```
Interior passing tonic allowed (differentiates from The Remaining's total
withholding); ENDING on the local tonic is banned in T1–T4. `material.ISLAND`.

### 1.2 THE MAINLAND THEME (the McCartney pole — one leap, then a settle)
Ten notes, 8 beats, ionian degrees. A rising **major-sixth leap** 1→6 (its only
leap), a sighing stepwise fall, a rise back, settling on **degree 6**:

```
(onset, dur, degree): (0,1,1) (1,1.5,6) (2.5,.5,5) (3,.5,4) (3.5,.5,3)
                      (4,1,4) (5,.5,3) (5.5,.5,4) (6,.5,5) (6.5,1.5,6)
contour: 1-6-5-4-3-4-3-4-5-6   (C A— G F E / F E F G A— in C major)
```
`material.MAINLAND`. Emitted only via `play_island()` / `play_mainland()`
(jt=0 always) so every statement is findable by exact interval+rhythm search.

### 1.3 THE CONVERGENCE (the narrative in semitones)
`material.CONVERGENCE = {1: ("E","Bb"), 2: ("E","C"), 3: ("A","C"),
4: ("A","G"), 5: ("D","D")}` — (island tonic, mainland tonic) per track.
Pitch-class distance walks **6 → 4 → 3 → 2 → 0**. Every theme statement's
implied tonic must match its track's pinned pc.

### 1.4 THE SIMULTANEITY BAN (and its lifting)
Tracks 1–4: no island statement may overlap a mainland statement in time.
T3 escalates to **call-and-answer**: statements must come within ≤ 2 beats of
each other but still never overlap. T5 III requires overlap: island and
mainland sounding together, downbeat-consonant, for the counterpoint span —
and **invertible**: one window with island's mean pitch above mainland's, a
second with mainland above island.

### 1.5 THE FUSION PHRASE (T5 only — both DNAs in one line)
Nine notes in D ionian: the mainland's leap into the island's repeated-note
incantation and fall, the island's hanging degree 2 demoted to a passing note,
landing held on **degree 1** — the album's only theme-family tonic ending:

```
(onset, dur, degree): (0,1,1) (1,.5,6) (1.5,.5,6) (2,1,6) (3,1,5)
                      (4,1,4) (5,.5,3) (5.5,.5,2) (6,2,1)
contour: 1-6-6-6-5-4-3-2-1   (D B B B— A G F# E D—)
```

### 1.6 THE HOOK LEDGER (the earworms — one riff cell per track)
`material.HOOKS[1..5]`, each as (onset, dur, semitone-offset-from-first-note) —
transposition-invariant, ≤ 2 bars, 4–6 notes, rests allowed. Emitted via
`play_hook(sc, ch, t0, first_pitch, n, jt=0)`.

| n | name | cell (onset, dur, semis) | character |
|---|------|--------------------------|-----------|
| 1 | the heartbeat | (0,.5,0) (.5,.5,0) (1,.5,-2) (1.5,.5,-5) | Visitors pulse, falling |
| 2 | the ferry riff | (0,.5,0) (.75,.25,+3) (1,.5,+5) (1.5,.5,+7) (2.5,.5,+5) (3,1,+3) | Vandebilt strut (syncopated pickup on the and-of-1) |
| 3 | the lattice | (0,.5,0) (.5,.5,+3) (1,.5,+7) | 3-quaver cell riding over 4/4 |
| 4 | the ice-arp | (0,.25,0) (.25,.25,+7) (.5,.25,+14) (.75,.25,+15) | wide 16th arp, minor-9th glint |
| 5 | the pump call | (0,1,0) (1,1,+2) (2,.5,+4) (2.5,1.5,+7) | 1985 rising call 1-2-3-5 |

Per-track oracle: own hook stated ≥ 6 times (any channels, any transposition).
**T5's movement III is the medley:** HOOKS[1], [2], [3], [4] each restated ≥ 1
time inside its window over the piano pump — the side-two-medley instinct made
checkable.

### 1.7 THE PROTAGONIST BASS (McCartney's signature, album-wide)
On each track's named bass channel: interval histogram from consecutive
note-ons gives **stepwise ratio ≥ the track's pinned floor** (0.50 default,
0.42 on funk-pump T2), **pitch range ≥ 19 semitones**, and the track's own
hook stated ≥ 2 times **in the bass** inside `CHORUS_SPANS[n]`. T4 (the
ballad) is exempt — documented, not silently skipped.

### 1.8 THE DOUBLED THUMB (why choruses feel bigger)
Inside `CHORUS_SPANS[n]`, every bass note-on is shadowed at **+12 semitones**
on the track's pinned partner channel (±10 ticks), coverage ≥ 0.80; outside
the spans coverage < 0.30 — the thickening is a chorus event. Partners:
T1 piano · T2 muted guitar · T3 Rhodes EP · T5 piano. T4 exempt.

### 1.9 THE BREATH HERALD (Enigma's inhale before every groove)
Before each pinned groove-movement entrance (T1 II, T2 II, T3 II, T5 III):
a window of ≥ 2 bars where **only** the herald channel (pan flute 75 or
shakuhachi 77) sounds, playing the first 3 notes of the track's hook under a
strictly rising CC11 swell. T4 exempt (its darkness is unheralded).

### 1.10 THE MORSE TIDE-TABLE (the letters across the water)
One morse lane per track, rotating timbre so the device never wears out:
`MORSE_WORDS = {1:"NEAP", 2:"WAIT", 3:"TURN", 4:"EBB", 5:"HOME"}` on
celesta 8 · woodblock 115 · muted guitar 28 · kalimba 108 · tubular bells 14.
Single pitch per lane, standard timing via `morse_rhythm()`, ≥ 1 full
statement, verified against the table.

### 1.11 THE TIDE-BREATH (the water is in the tempo map)
`material.tide_breath(base_bpm, t0, t1, period_bars, depth)` generates the
shared swell: ±3–6 bpm, 8-bar period. Every movement breathes EXCEPT the two
pinned still points: **T4 II is exactly metronomic** (one tempo event in its
window — the Visitors sequencer, no tide, no mercy) and **T5 II–III flatten
to ≤ 1 bpm wiggle** (the causeway is dry). Flat maps anywhere else are bugs.

### 1.12 THE WITHHELD CADENCE (the ache never released early)
In every pinned cadence window of T1–T4, the bass motion into the downbeat
must be modal — **v–i, bVII–i or iv–i; the leading tone is banned** in the
cadence chord (no major-V→I anywhere on those tracks). T5's final cadence is
the payoff: bass **G→D (IV–I plagal)** with F♯ present (Picardy via the D
major turn), the album's only authentic-feeling arrival.

### 1.13 THE NARROWING STRAIT (the stereo field is the water)
Each track's island-pole channels sit at the track's island seat, mainland-pole
channels at the mainland seat, and the seats **converge with the keys**:
`SHORE_PANS = {1:(40,88), 2:(44,84), 3:(50,78), 4:(54,74), 5:(60,68)}`.
Neutral channels (piano, bass, drums, bells) hold 64. Single CC10 per channel
at setup; only an explicitly named shimmer channel may autopan (level low —
mono-collapse lesson).

### 1.14 THE TOLLS (the bell buoy — track order made audible)
Track N ends with **exactly N tolls**: the final N note-ons of the whole track
are on the bells channel, one shared pitch class (the track's island tonic),
near-isochronous (1.5–4 beats apart); earlier voices may ring through but no
new onsets intrude after toll 1.

### 1.15 THE VOWEL CLOCK (the winter's mouth, album-wide)
Choir CC70 caps per track: T1 ≤ 40 · T2 ≤ 50 · T3 ≤ 75 (with a verified
rising morph — the year opening) · T4 ≤ 55 · T5 must reach ≥ 80 with a
monotone rise across its final movements.

## 2. The five tracks

Durations are targets ±20%; each module owns its grid. Every track: tide-breath
tempo maps (except the pinned still points), keysig meta, movement markers, CC11
arcs on every sustained line, CC1 vibrato that develops, CC91 depth per channel.

### T1 — "Neap Light" (~5:30 · E minor · 4/4 · 76 breathing, II lifts to 92)
The island alone; the far shore only a rumour.
- **I. Grey Water** — piano states THE ISLAND THEME twice over open-fifth
  drones; nylon guitar answers fragments; choir oohs sealed (≤ 40); celesta
  taps NEAP in morse; tide-breath rubato.
- **II. The Heartbeat** — breath herald (pan flute, hook head, rising CC11),
  then the Delerium groove: synth-bass HOOK1 ostinato, soft kit, FM EP
  arpeggios, protagonist bass singing above the pulse; DOUBLED THUMB choruses
  (piano partner); island theme on strings inside the groove.
- **III. The Far Shore** — the only mainland appearance: THE MAINLAND THEME
  once, in **B♭** (a tritone away), on french horn drenched in reverb
  (CC91 ≥ 100 vs ≤ 48 elsewhere — distance coded in the send) while the
  island's music thins to held pads. No overlap with any island statement.
- **IV. Neap** — island theme once more, slower, hanging on F♯; 1 toll.
- Oracles: theme statements + convergence pcs, no-overlap, hook density ≥ 6,
  protagonist bass, doubled thumb, herald window, morse NEAP, vowel cap,
  reverb-distance coding, tide-breath cycles, cadence-window modality, tolls.

### T2 — "The Winter Ferry" (~5:45 · E minor→E mixolydian→E minor · 4/4 ·
72 → accelerando 112→138 → collapse 66)
The failed crossing — a Band-on-the-Run three-act storm with a Vandebilt engine.
- **I. Harbour** — brush kit (ch9 program 40), steel guitar arpeggios,
  fretless bass warming up the ferry riff in fragments; woodblock WAIT.
- **II. The Open Water** — kit swaps to the full sampled kit (program change
  back — the mid-track kit swap oracle-pinned), breath herald, then the strut:
  sampled clavinet HOOK2 with CC68 hammer-on slurs, CC74 wah chops on muted
  guitar, brass-section stabs answering with channel-aftertouch rasp, fretless
  portamento (CC65/5) slides, protagonist bass leading, E mixolydian brightness
  (the false hope), authored accelerando 112→138. DOUBLED THUMB (muted guitar).
- **III. Turned Back** — the wave hits: one orchestra-hit + tam-tam moment
  (CC0 alt-bank 14), collapse to 66, mainland theme attempted on french horn
  in **C** — stated once, complete, but alone over wreckage-sparse texture;
  echo throws (CC94) trail every phrase; harmonica laments over the retreat.
- **IV. Harbour Again** — brush kit returns, riff half-tempo, hollow; 2 tolls.
- Oracles: accelerando shape, kit program changes, hook density + hammer-on
  CC68 presence, wah lane, aftertouch on brass, portamento lane, convergence,
  no-overlap, morse WAIT, cadence modality, tolls, herald, doubled thumb.

### T3 — "Spring Tide" (~5:30 · A minor → C major · 4/4 with a 3-against-4
lattice · 96 → 108)
The turn of the year — the shores close enough to call and answer.
- **I. The Lattice** — marimba cycles HOOK3 (3-quaver cell) against kalimba's
  4-grid answer; the realignment click every 12 quavers is the reward beat;
  choir vowels begin a verified rise (mm→oo→toward ah, ≤ 75); steelpan colour
  via CC0 second-percussion bank; muted guitar taps TURN.
- **II. Answer** — breath herald, then the spring groove in C major: Rhodes +
  brass punch THE MAINLAND THEME (in C); the island answers in **A minor**
  within ≤ 2 beats — call-and-answer verified adjacent, never overlapping;
  drawbar organ (CC0 alt bank on 19 or GM 16) with CC1 Leslie spin-ups;
  protagonist bass; DOUBLED THUMB (Rhodes partner).
- **III. High Water** — the lattice returns under both themes alternating
  faster (still no overlap — tension of near-touch); tempo 108; autopan
  shimmer channel (crystal 98, low level); collapse to a suspended iv–i.
- **IV. Slack** — kalimba alone over pad; 3 tolls.
- Oracles: 3:4 lattice onsets, call-answer adjacency + no-overlap, vowel rise
  + cap, Leslie CC1 lane, alt-bank selects, convergence, hooks, bass, thumb,
  herald, morse TURN, cadence modality, tolls.

### T4 — "The Ebb Letter" (~5:00 · A minor · 6/8 rubato 66 ↔ 4/4 locked 120 ·
darkest hour)
The Visitors centrepiece — ice outside, one candle inside.
- **I. Candle** — 6/8: piano under una corda (CC67) + sostenuto-caught low
  pedal (CC66), cello with bend-appoggiaturas and aftertouch swells, deep
  fermata rubato (dips to 48); island theme inverted colouring allowed but
  every statement still ends degree 2.
- **II. The Ice** — 4/4, **exactly one tempo event** (120, metronomic): HOOK4
  ice-arp on FM EP + synth bass 39, one pitch of the cell rotating per cycle
  (the additive-mutation sequencer, rotation schedule pinned); sweep-pad CC74
  arc; the mainland tries to arrive on french horn in **G**: three reaching
  statements — 4 notes, then 7, then 9 of 10 — each stopping short (growth,
  not erosion — the album reaches, The Remaining lost); kalimba taps EBB.
- **III. Wax** — 6/8 returns, slower; choir ≤ 55; the island theme's last
  pre-dawn statement; 4 tolls.
- Oracles: metronomic-window isolation vs rubato elsewhere, ice-arp rotation
  schedule, reaching statements 4/7/9 pinned to MAINLAND prefix, convergence,
  no-overlap, pedal lanes present, morse EBB, vowel cap, cadence modality,
  tolls. (Protagonist bass / thumb / herald exempt — documented.)

### T5 — "Low Water Crossing" (~7:00 · D minor → D major · 4/4 · 84 → 100
dead-steady → rit. 62)
The crossing at dawn. The album's full band and its every promise kept.
- **I. First Light** — D minor: piano pump assembling, tide-breath still
  swelling; both themes stated separately one last time (island D minor,
  mainland D major-inflected — same tonic at last, distance 0).
- **II. The Causeway** — keysig flips to D major; the tempo flattens ≤ 1 bpm
  (the water is out — the ground is dry); breath herald into the pump; HOOK5
  call on protagonist bass; steel guitar CC68 jangle; brass warming.
- **III. Both Shores** — the wow stack over the 1985 pump: **island and
  mainland themes simultaneous in D**, downbeat-consonant, then **inverted**
  (island above, then mainland above — both windows verified); THE MEDLEY:
  HOOKS[1..4] each restated over the pump; choir opens ≥ 80 (monotone rise);
  tubular bells peal HOME in morse then hold the roots; RPN bend range 12 —
  an overdriven-lead solo with true octave bends, recentred at the seam;
  32-bar crescendo of strictly rising bar-sums.
- **IV. The Other Shore** — everything falls away; solo piano (island's own
  voice) states THE FUSION PHRASE — the album's only melodic tonic landing —
  into the **IV–I plagal Picardy** (G in the bass falling to D, F♯ in the
  chord); long CC11 fade; **5 tolls**, ritardando to 62.
- Oracles: keysig flip, flat-tempo windows, simultaneity + consonance +
  invertibility, medley completeness, fusion-phrase exactness + uniqueness
  (the only deg-1 theme-family ending on the album — enforced here and by
  T1–T4's end-deg-2 oracles), crescendo/decrescendo arcs, plagal cadence
  geometry, bend-solo range + recentring, vowel rise, morse HOME, tolls.

## 4. Verification doctrine

The nine generic checks run per track unchanged. Every device named
**verified** above becomes a named oracle in its track's module; all data is
single-sourced from `material.py`, whose `verify_material()` proves its internal
claims (theme contours/end-degrees/contiguity, fusion-phrase DNA — the leap, the
triple repeat, the deg-1 landing, convergence distances 6/4/3/2/0, hook-cell
well-formedness, morse words vs the table, shore-pan convergence monotonicity,
toll counts = track numbers, vowel caps monotone where claimed). Audio side
(`analyze.py`, 2–4 checks/track, RATIO-based per repo lesson): T1 far-shore
horn wetter than the island (reverb-band ratio); T2 accelerando audible in
onset-rate ratio; T4 ice section's tempo-flatness in onset isochrony; T5
crescendo in windowed RMS + final tolls' decay ring ≥ 4 s.

## 5. Composer workflow

`COMPOSER-NOTES.md` (this album's adaptation) is the contract; compose one
track at a time, serially (lessons_learnt: never fan composers out in
parallel); green = `python build.py --track N --verify` all-PASS; commit per
track. Modules are written in several small Write/Edit chunks (64k output-cap
lesson).
