# HLD — *The Remaining* (five elegies for piano, strings and electronics)

**Artist:** Claude Fable 5 · **Idiom:** Max Richter, specifically the sound-world of
his score for *The Leftovers* — grief-laden piano ostinati, string-quartet
suspensions, sub-bass drones and quiet electronics, long additive builds, sudden
intimate drops. **Original material throughout** — the album writes in the
*vocabulary* of that idiom (broken-chord quaver figures, 4-3/9-8 suspensions over a
slow four-chord ground, additive layering) and quotes nothing.

**Story arc (the machine-verified spine):** an unexplained departure takes part of
the music away mid-phrase; the remaining voices carry on around the hole; the final
track brings the departed line home and finishes its interrupted phrase. Grief →
elegy → searching → memory → return.

**Shape:** the Slipstream federated grid, verbatim machinery — `conductor.Part` per
track, one self-contained module per track under `movements/`, the shared
parameterized `verify.py`, per-track oracles, `analyze.py` audio checks. Album dir:
`albums/fable5/The Remaining/`.

## 1. Global material (all data lives in `material.py`, oracle-pinned)

### 1.1 THE GROUND (minor) — the album's harmonic floor
Four chords, one bar each, in D natural minor: **Dm — B♭ — F — C**
(aeolian root degrees `[1, 6, 3, 7]`). Every track except T3 states it.

**The suspension signature** — each ground chord may enter *suspended*, resolving
down by step on beat 2 (the Richter sigh). Per-chord `(sus, res)` offsets in
semitones above the chord root, pinned in material:

| chord | suspension | offsets |
|-------|------------|---------|
| Dm    | 4-3: G→F   | (5, 3)  |
| B♭    | 9-8: C→B♭  | (2, 0)  |
| F     | 4-3: B♭→A  | (5, 4)  |
| C     | 4-3: F→E   | (5, 4)  |

### 1.2 THE MAJOR GROUND (T5 only) — the turning
D major "homeward" loop: **D — A — Bm — G** (ionian root degrees `[1, 5, 6, 4]`).

### 1.3 THE VIGIL THEME (the album motif)
Six notes in aeolian degrees, 8 beats. It **never reaches home** in tracks 1–4:
every complete statement ends on degree 2 — E, "the waiting tone".

```
(onset, dur, degree): (0,1,5) (1,1,4) (2,2,3) (4,1,4) (5,1,3) (6,2,2)
contour: A  G  F—  G  F  E—        (5-4-3-4-3-2)
```

Allowed derived forms (each track pins its own): transposition, augmentation
(integer ×2/×4), diminution (÷2, ÷4), inversion about a degree axis, canon.
**Only T5's final statement may append the arrival** — a 7th note, `(8, 4, 1)`,
recast in D major (A-G-F♯-G-F♯-E-**D**). The tonic the album withholds for
25 minutes lands once, in the last minute. `material.THEME`, `THEME_END_DEG = 2`,
`ARRIVAL = (8.0, 4.0, 1)`.

### 1.4 THE DEPARTURE FIGURE (the piano ostinato)
One bar of 4/4 = 8 contiguous quavers, broken-chord, spanning a tenth, peak on
quaver 3 (beat 1.5):

```
semitone offsets above chord root: [0, 7, 12, 16, 12, 7, 12, 7]   (major-3rd chords)
                                   [0, 7, 12, 15, 12, 7, 12, 7]   (minor-3rd chords)
```

**THE HOLES:** after T1's departure moment, the figure loses quaver indices
`{3, 6}` — the peak of the phrase and one inner pulse simply *aren't there*
(`material.HOLES = {3, 6}`; post-departure bars carry exactly quavers
{0,1,2,4,5,7}). T4's middle movement replays the holed figure — the house still
missing the same notes. Verified in both tracks.

### 1.5 THE DEPARTED LINE (the interrupted phrase — the album's heart)
A 12-note violin II phrase, `material.DEPARTED_LINE` (exact degrees/rhythm in
material.py; ~16 beats, lyrical, stepwise with one 4th-leap crest).
`INTERRUPT_AFTER = 7`.

- **T1** (movement III): violin II plays **exactly notes 0..6** — then the channel
  goes silent for the remainder of the track. Mid-phrase. No decay ritual, no
  cadence. Everyone else keeps playing.
- **T5** (movement III): violin II returns and states **all 12 notes verbatim**
  (same key, same rhythm) inside the full ensemble — the phrase is finished.

Both ends are oracle-pinned to the SAME material data, Slipstream-quote style.

### 1.6 THE STATIC LANE (T3)
Morse on woodblock (GM115): `material.MORSE_TEXT = "REMEMBER US"`, standard
timing via `morse_rhythm()` (dit=1 unit; verified against the table).

### 1.7 SEATING (pan discipline, album-wide)
`material.SEATING`: piano 64 · vln I 44 · vln II 54 · viola 74 · cello 84 ·
bass/drone 64 · choir 64 · celesta 70 · harp 48 · bells 58 · timpani 64.
A track's string/piano channels must sit at their seat (single CC10 at setup);
only an explicitly-named shimmer channel may autopan (excluded from
CENTERED_CHANNELS — mono-collapse lesson: keep its level low).

## 2. The five tracks

Durations are targets ±20%; each module owns its grid. Every track: tempo-map
rubato (phrase-end dips of 2–6 bpm — never a flat map), CC11 phrase arcs on every
sustained line, CC1 vibrato that *develops* (later phrases deeper), CC91 depth per
channel, keysig meta events, movement markers.

### T1 — "October the Fourteenth" (~6:00 · D minor · 4/4 · ≈66 bpm rubato)
The departure itself.
- **I. Morning** — solo piano: DEPARTURE FIGURE over THE GROUND, soft pedal
  (CC67), sustain half-pedaled per bar (CC64), intimate (vel 40–60). Two cycles;
  second adds sub drone (GM38, pp).
- **II. The Vanishing** — additive Richter build: each 8-bar cycle adds a voice
  (cello counter-line → viola suspensions inside the ground → vln II begins the
  DEPARTED LINE → vln I takes THE VIGIL THEME). Verified: strictly rising per-cycle
  bar-sum energy; each cycle introduces a channel with no prior note-ons.
- **III. The Fourteenth** — at the pinned DEPARTURE BEAT (a mid-bar quaver, NOT a
  downbeat): vln II stops at note 7 of the DEPARTED LINE; viola stops mid-note the
  same instant; **2.5 beats of total silence** (GAP_WHITELISTed — the only scored
  full silence in the album's first four tracks); then the piano resumes *alone*,
  playing the HOLED figure — same music, wrong, missing its peak. Remaining
  strings re-enter one by one over 16 bars, playing *around* the holes.
- **IV. Vigil** — vln I states THE VIGIL THEME twice over the full remaining
  ensemble; final statement ends on the waiting tone E over a bare open-fifth
  D–A; ritardando to ≈54 bpm; piano ends on a single repeated quaver D, holed
  figure reduced to its root alone.
- Oracles: departure-instant discipline (vln II note count == 7 of DEPARTED_LINE,
  zero notes after; viola none after), holes pattern exact, additive build,
  theme statements end deg 2, silence gap position, rubato map non-flat.

### T2 — "The Ninety-Eight" (~5:30 · D minor · 3/4 · ≈60 bpm)
Elegy for those who remain — a string chaconne; **no piano until the coda**.
- THE GROUND as a repeating cello/bass chaconne line (3/4 guise: one chord per
  bar, roots on beat 1), 12+ cycles. Above it, verified variations, each 2 cycles:
  1. bare fifths (vla+vc), 2. suspensions bloom (every ground chord suspended —
  all four (sus,res) pairs verified present), 3. vln I sings THE VIGIL THEME
  augmented ×2, 4. canon — vln II follows vln I at one bar at the lower fifth
  (interval-exact, verified), 5. descant — vln I harmonics-register line above
  a tutti, 6. thinning — voices leave one per cycle (the ninety-eight keep
  playing as the stage empties).
- Coda: the piano enters for the first time — four bars of the (un-holed)
  DEPARTURE FIGURE, pp, and the track ends on the waiting tone E in vln I alone.
- Advanced: bend appoggiaturas into phrase peaks (slide from a semitone below,
  recentred each boundary), channel aftertouch swells on cello long notes,
  CC74 darkening ("con sordino") for variations 1–2, deepening CC1 across
  variations.
- Oracles: chaconne root isochrony, all four suspension pairs, augmentation
  exactness, canon interval/delay, monotone thinning, piano-tacet-until-coda.

### T3 — "Static" (~4:30 · A minor · 4/4 · 112 bpm, metronomic)
The searching — the album's one pulse track (Richter's electronic side; dry,
close, no hall). Harmonic floor: **Am — F — C — G** (THE GROUND transposed to
the dominant minor — same degree walk `[1,6,3,7]`, verified).
- Unbroken synth-bass quaver pulse (GM38/39, jt=0) for the whole track minus the
  last bar — verified isochrony. Over it: dry solo violin (CC91 = 0!) spins a
  moto perpetuo derived from the DEPARTURE FIGURE at double speed (same offset
  walk, verified), phrases lengthening 4→8→16 quavers.
- The STATIC LANE: woodblock taps "REMEMBER US" in Morse, twice (verified against
  material timing), buried at vel ~40 under the pulse.
- A pad (GM89) breathes on a slow CC74 filter arc; one crystal shimmer channel
  (GM98) autopans slowly (the only non-centered channel, level low).
- Portamento (CC65/5) violin slides in the B section; RPN bend range set to 2
  at setup.
- **The end is an interruption:** the track stops mid-bar on quaver 6 — pulse,
  violin (mid-phrase on the VIGIL THEME's first three notes A-G-F, its only
  appearance), everything — one beat of nothing, then a single dry woodblock
  tap. Verified: last-bar cutoff geometry; the three-note theme fragment is the
  track's only theme material (a complete statement must NOT occur).
- Oracles: pulse isochrony + count, morse lane timing ×2, moto-perpetuo
  derivation, transposed-ground root walk, dry-violin CC91=0, cutoff shape.

### T4 — "The Empty House" (~5:00 · D minor · 4/4 · ≈54 bpm, heavy rubato)
Memory, interior. Piano at the center throughout.
- **I. Rooms** — solo piano: THE VIGIL THEME **inverted** about degree 3 (axis F —
  material.invert_theme, verified) in the right hand over widely-spaced left-hand
  tenths; sostenuto (CC66) catches a low D pedal at each section start while the
  hands change harmony above; soft pedal throughout.
- **II. The Music Box** — celesta plays the THEME in diminution ÷4 at the top of
  the keyboard (the child's music box) over the piano's HOLED departure figure
  (same `{3,6}` holes as T1, verified identical); harp single arpeggio per ground
  cycle; the music box repeats, each time losing its last note (7 notes → … →
  3 notes, verified erosion) — memory fading.
- **III. Hum** — choir enters on closed vowels (vowel_curve 0 "mm" → 45 "oo",
  never reaching "ah"), pp, under the piano's un-holed figure — the first
  un-holed statement since the departure (hope, briefly); vln I one octave-high
  thread of the THEME, ends deg 2.
- **IV. Clock** — everything gone but celesta: one repeated E ("the waiting
  tone" as a stopped clock), decelerating tempo map 54→40, irregular final
  spacings; last event a lone bass D, 4 beats, pp.
- Oracles: inversion exactness, music-box erosion sequence, holes identity with
  T1, vowel ceiling (< 60 everywhere), piano-centrality (piano ≥ notes of any
  other channel), clock deceleration.

### T5 — "Homeward" (~7:00 · D minor → D major · 4/4 · 63→72 bpm)
The return. The album's full ensemble: piano, quartet, ensemble strings, choir,
harp, celesta, tubular bells, timpani, organ pedal, sub drone.
- **I. Procession** — minor GROUND, organ (GM19) low-D pedal + timpani soft
  rolls; voices assemble additively (verified build), THEME on cello.
- **II. The Turning** — the pinned modulation: one bar of bare open D (no third),
  then THE MAJOR GROUND enters (verified root walk `[1,5,6,4]` in D ionian);
  suspensions now resolve *upward* E→F♯ (the sigh inverts — verified); tempo
  eases up to 72.
- **III. All of Them, Home** — the wow stack, machine-verified triple
  counterpoint over the major ground: piano DEPARTURE FIGURE **with the holes
  filled** (first full 8-quaver statement since T1 II — verified un-holed), vln I
  THEME in D major, cello THEME augmented ×2, celesta THEME diminished ÷2 —
  simultaneous for ≥ 8 bars, downbeat-consonant. **And violin II returns**: the
  full 12-note DEPARTED LINE, verbatim, finishing the phrase interrupted in T1
  (the oracle pins both halves to material). Choir opens to "ah" (vowel ≥ 80),
  tubular bells peal the ground roots, 32-bar crescendo verified by
  windowed bar-sums strictly rising.
- **IV. Quiet** — everything falls away over 8 bars (verified decrescendo);
  solo piano states THEME + **ARRIVAL** — the album's only degree-1 theme
  ending — into a final D-major-add9 chord (the waiting tone E absorbed as a
  consonance, verified present in the final sonority), long CC11 fade,
  fermata-slow final tempo.
- Oracles: turning geometry (bare-fifth bar, major root walk, upward sighs),
  quadruple simultaneity + consonance, departed-line completion, holes-filled,
  crescendo/decrescendo arcs, arrival uniqueness (deg-1 ending occurs exactly
  once on the album — enforced here and by T1-T4's end-deg-2 oracles), final
  add9 sonority.

## 4. Verification doctrine

Slipstream's nine generic checks run per track unchanged. Every device named
**verified** above becomes a named oracle in that track's module; material data
is single-sourced from `material.py` and `verify_material()` proves its internal
claims (ground walks, suspension resolutions step-down/up, theme contour and
end-degree, figure span/peak/holes, departed-line length/interrupt point, morse
table, seating table). Audio side (`analyze.py`, 2–4 checks/track): T1 silence
really silent + post-departure piano-alone level drop; T3 pulse-band energy flat
+ dry-violin presence; T5 crescendo in rendered RMS + final chord decay ≥ 8 s.

## 5. Composer workflow

`COMPOSER-NOTES.md` (this album's adaptation) is the contract; compose one track
per session, serially (lessons_learnt: never fan composers out in parallel);
green = `python build.py --track N --verify` all-PASS; commit per track.
