## 1. Goal and scope

One sequential build improving GM guitar realism on five fronts, ranked by leverage
in the 2026.07.18 SoTA analysis:

1. **Onset variation** for the acoustic LA banks (GM 24/25) — remove PCM
   bit-repetition and the velocity-brightness mismatch at the crossfade.
2. **GM 29/30 held-note decay** — investigated; resolved as a *record
   correction*, not a code change (§3).
3. **Cabinet fine structure** ("MicroCab") for the Drive insert (GM 29/30).
4. **Mechanical noise** on the acoustic guitar presets + a modest LA fade widen.
5. **Commuted-synthesis excitation** for GM 24/25 (prototype; ship gate in §7/§10).

**Honesty note (review S3/D4):** synthetic variation removes *bit-identity* and
adds the velocity axis, but the drum-kit history (HAT_JITTER: worst-pair NCC 0.494
*still* machine-gunned) proves numeric decorrelation ≠ audible variety. The
machine-gun *percept* is judged at the audition checkpoint (§10), not by AC1.
Real round robins remain impossible under current licensing (§2).

**Non-goals:** LA layers for electrics (excluded by design, LA HLD 2026.07.10 §5);
strum/articulation composition helpers; neural amp; new sample assets or crates;
any controller/CC surface; version bump (integration owns it).

## 2. Sourcing decision — the block is asset-free

Web verification (asset scout, 2026.07.18) found the intersection
(CC0 licence × RR/velocity layers × matching acoustic instrument) **empty**:

- Karoryfer's relicensed-CC0 libraries are all *electric* (Shinyguitar = archtop
  jazz box, Emilyguitar = solid-body, Black & Green = hollowbodies).
- FreePats' steel-string acoustic is **GPLv3**; its nylon set is the single-take
  CC0 source we already use. Pianobook forbids redistribution.
- Soundwoofer's "public domain" cab IRs carry **no licence deed** — a community
  claim only; embedding one contradicts our SHA-pinned CC0 provenance culture.

Consequence: variation is *synthesized* (items 1, 5) and the cabinet upgrade is
*authored* (item 3). No new crates, no prepare.py sources, no licence work.
Correction rider (review S11): the companion CR's §5 claim that the grand-piano
"RR2-from-adjacent-velocity-layer" trick is "portable here" is false — both
guitar banks are single-take, single-layer; this branch amends that CR text.

## 3. Item 2 — GM 29/30 decay is deliberate; record correction only

- `DRIVE.sustain = 0.0` is round-3 U2's explicit decision (`voices.rs:2503-2511`):
  the default bank is a *decaying* overdriven pluck; the sustaining voice is the
  CC0 alt bank's `DRIVE_LEAD` (0.6). The e-bow driver is never installed
  (`voices.rs:3600-3603`) and the latch block is unreachable (`voices.rs:3854`).

"Fixing" it would erase a designed contrast and break an oracle. **Change:** amend
the misleading lessons_learnt 2026.07.18 clause; no code change.
(All three reviewers affirmed this resolution.)

## 4. Item 1 — onset variation for the acoustic LA layer

**Problem.** Single-take banks: repeated equal-velocity notes replay identical PCM
for the whole fade window. And the model darkens with velocity on three levers
(`voices.rs:3522-3528`) while the sample only scales in gain (`sampler.rs:1613`).

**Approach (revised after review):**

- **Narrow factoring (S4/D8, integ affirmation).** A new guitar-only constructor
  `LaVoice::wrap_var(…, var, seed)` used by exactly the two guitar arms
  (`voices.rs:10827, 10850`). `wrap`/`wrap_fx` signatures are untouched, so the
  ~16 other LA call sites cannot change by construction — the inert path is a
  *branch that doesn't exist*, not a "transparent" filter (a wide-open one-pole
  still perturbs bits). The vel-brightness one-pole lives in `LaFx` (sibling of
  the existing `shelf`); the per-note stochastic jitter is a small `OnsetVar`
  spec consumed only by `wrap_var` (different lifetime than static `LaFx` —
  split, don't fully fold; refutation-round consensus).
- **Locked detune, not sample-only rate jitter (D7).** The per-note pitch offset
  is applied to *both* the sample step and the model's fundamental, so they
  cannot beat through the crossfade. Spec (as-built): 5 strata across ±5 cents
  (hat-lesson strata pattern, `sampler.rs:2144-2149`) + white ±1 cent — max
  ~±6 c, sized as a *musical* intonation scatter (Codex C3: the ±45 c oracle
  bound is a detection tolerance, not a tuning budget; implementation found the
  O-PITCH ±15 c lattice bar stacked on the model's own few-cent tuning residue
  binds first, so ±6 c is the honest budget). **Composition rule (Codex C3):**
  the offset is stored as a variation multiplier and every performance pitch
  update composes `variation × performance_pitch` on BOTH paths — the model is
  constructed detuned and `LaVoice::set_pitch` forwards the composed
  multiplier, so bends/vibrato/glide cannot silently unlock the pair.
- **Onset-delay jitter 0–6 ms + gain jitter ±6 %** — per D4 these carry most of
  the transient decorrelation (rate jitter is pitch-bounded on a pitched
  instrument; the pitched yardstick is DRUM_JITTER ±2.5 %, not the hats' ±8 %).
- **Velocity-brightness:** `LaFx` one-pole whose corner follows the model's
  excitation law shape; **exact bypass (branch, no filter call) at vel ≥ 100**
  (I10 — the LA oracles all run at vel 100/seed 5 and `la_guitar_attack_sharpness`
  has only a 5 % margin).
- Same seed ⇒ bit-identical (determinism preserved); seed is the engine's
  existing per-voice seed (`engine.rs:1493`).

**Guards:** existing LA oracles stay green at their vel-100/seed-5 fixtures; a new
FNV byte-identity pin on one non-guitar LA program (S5) proves the blast radius
is the two guitar arms and nothing else. **Low-velocity seam coverage (Codex
C4):** the existing continuity fixtures all run at vel 100 — where the new filter
is bypassed — so add low/mid-velocity differential seam rows (wrapped vs
model-only at the same velocity) covering the filter's active range and
worst-case jitter seeds.

## 5. Item 3 — MicroCab (cabinet fine structure)

**Approach (revised; as-built numbers):** a **standalone, impulse-testable
linear unit** (mirroring `cab_biquads`, engine.rs:252 — review I6/S10): direct
path + 5 sparse FIR taps, response normalized to the **900–3600 Hz band
average** (D9 — sum-normalizing pins DC, not the band the ripple rides).
As-built rider on I8's draft "≤ 0.55 ms" delay cap: implementation showed a
delay tap ripples equally at *every* frequency, so the cap neither ensures nor
is needed for low-band cleanliness — the taps (0.36–0.90 ms) were instead
**numerically optimized against the real bounds jointly** (250 k-candidate
search): ≤ 1.6 dB span below 800 Hz (the voicing/asymmetry protection I8
actually wanted), 3→6 kHz tilt −0.07 dB (cliff margin untouched), |H| ≤ 1.52
(alias margin). AC4's drafted "≥ 6 alternations ≥ 2 dB" was likewise
physically over-spec'd for this architecture — recalibrated to **≥ 6
alternations ≥ 1.5 dB, total swing ≤ 10 dB** (measured 6.2 dB), recorded here
per the no-silent-weakening rule. Placed in `Drive::chain` **before the two cab lowpass biquads**,
so the anti-alias cliff is genuinely the last element (refutation-round
resolution of I5/D6: measured alias floor has ~14 dB margin even after the FIR,
but pre-cliff placement makes it unconditional at zero cost).

This deliberately revives the guitar-v2-deferred "ripple comb" (S10 — provenance
noted, not a contradiction: v2 deferred it as polish, this block is that polish).

**Guards:** new ripple oracle on the standalone unit (≥ ~6 alternations ≥ 2 dB
across 900–3600 Hz); plus a **combined-response oracle (Codex C5)** — impulse
through MicroCab → cab biquads in series (what listeners actually receive),
pinning the existing macro anchors (presence/cliff/low-resonance ratios), a
maximum ripple depth, and broadband level parity vs the current cab within a
stated tolerance. `cabinet_response_shape`, `drive_alias_floor`,
`drive_asymmetry_and_dc`, `drive_pre_voicing_direction`, and the
`driven_sustain_stays_distorted` THD floors all stay green. "Does it sound like a
cab, not a phaser" is an ear call → §10.

## 6. Item 4 — mechanical noise + fade widen

**4a — REDUCED to release thumps (external review C1/C2).** Two Codex findings,
both verified in code, collapse the original plan:

- **C2 (verified by exhaustive arm enumeration):** the `make()` dispatch
  explicitly covers all 128 GM programs — the `_ => STEEL` arm is unreachable
  for valid MIDI (only invalid `u8` 128–255 reach it). The internal review's
  "preset split" requirement (I2) protected a path that cannot be hit; it is
  **dropped**. Editing STEEL affects GM25 only.
- **C1 (verified structurally):** `LaVoice` discards all model output before the
  50 ms fade start, and the click/attack-noise bursts die in 3–4 ms — so
  `attack_noise` and `click_hp` changes are **inaudible on the default sampled
  path** (whose recorded onsets already carry the real mechanics). They would
  only polish the opt-in alt bank / modeled-only builds, at the price of the
  riskiest oracle-recalibration surface in the block. They stay **deferred**
  (the `voices.rs:2426-2432` comment stands, now with the sharper reason).

What remains — the piece audible on every path because it fires at note-off,
when the model owns the note:
- STEEL: `stop_thump ≈ 0.4` (palm damp lands with a thud).
- NYLON: `stop_thump ≈ 0.3` (fingertip damp).

New presence oracle (post-note-off low-band energy bump vs stop_thump=0);
verify the onset-window oracles (`pick_click_is_audible`,
`wound_strings_darker`) are untouched — stop_thump fires outside their
measurement windows, so no recalibration should be needed (assert, don't
assume). The original deferral's "listen" half is §10 (S7).

**4b. Fade widen 0.20 → 0.30 s** (revised down from 0.35 — D5): consumption is
`fade_end × step` with step ≤ 2.05, and `steel_B5` is only 0.706 s, so 0.35 s
could run the zone dry ~6 ms before fade-end at extreme repitch (0.30 × 2.05 =
0.615 s clears every zone with margin). Guards:
- Static guard test in the **source domain** (Codex C8: `step` folds in
  `44100/sr`, so a rate-relative bound lies at 48/96 kHz):
  `fade_end × 44100 × (f/root)_max ≤ zone_samples`, asserted at the default
  44.1 kHz configuration.
- A short **end taper** on the sample read (~5 ms fade as the read approaches
  the zone's last samples) so any dry-out at *any* sample rate degrades
  gracefully instead of stepping — the robust fix for the non-44.1 k case.
- **GM25 steel rows added to `la_level_continuity`** (it currently exercises
  only nylon — the dry-out case was unguarded). Sequenced after item 1.

## 7. Item 5 — commuted-synthesis excitation (prototype)

**Reframed benefit (D1/I7/S9):** the PCM overlay owns [0, fade_start = 50 ms) by
design — `LaVoice` discards the model there, so commuted excitation cannot and
need not improve the *first attack*. Its audible contribution is **body colour
through the model-owned region** (from the crossfade on), and **body-coloured
legato/restrikes** (which today re-excite with plain white noise).

**Derivation** — string canceller `y[n] = x[n] − g·H(x)[n−N]` over the zone's
first ~100 ms; N from the zone root (already the autocorrelation-measured
recording f0, `sampler.rs:76-77` — refutation of D3's premise); H/g from the
preset's damping at that pitch. A single-N canceller is a deliberate
approximation of the two-loop plant (Codex C6): the vertical loop is detuned
only 0.13 % (sub-sample in N) and the coupling is weak (k = 0.02), so the
mismatch lands in the residual — which is exactly what the
residual-energy-at-f0 gate measures; if the canceller leaves > threshold f0
ring, the zone falls back to the noise burst. **Cached per (zone, preset) and
warmed by `prewarm()`** (D10/I3) — never derived per note-on on the realtime
thread. The residual stays in the 44.1 kHz source domain and is read with the
same stepped-cubic interpolation the sampler uses (Codex C8), so it is
sample-rate-independent by construction. **Double-body correction (C6):** the
residual already contains the recorded body, so the preset's body-EQ biquads
are bypassed while commuted excitation is active — otherwise the body is
applied twice.

**Injection (D1/I4 — the critical correction):** `KsLoop::tick` returns the
delayed tap and pushes `input`, so a silent loop rings up over one period and
under-levels the crossfade seam. Therefore: the excitation's **first period is
pre-loaded as KS initial state** (the existing `KsLoop::new(&exc)` path — no
silent-start construct, no change for the ~40 other `Pluck::new` callers, via a
separate constructor for the commuted case) and only the **tail** trickles in
through the hammer/`inject` port (`voices.rs:3796-3803, 3845-3846`).

**Level parity (D2, sharpened by C6):** the injected tail's gain is calibrated
so output level at the crossfade seam matches the burst path — measured as
**band-split RMS (3 bands), not whole-band RMS alone**, so a spectral step at
the seam can't hide inside a level match.

**Restrikes (S8, sharpened by C7):** `retrigger()` builds a fresh white-noise
burst today (`voices.rs:4092-4132`) — i.e. today's restrikes are *random*;
replacing them with one cached wavetable would make them MORE identical, a
regression. When commuted excitation is active, restrikes draw from the cached
wavetable **with per-strike deterministic variation** (rate/gain jitter from
the voice rng, on top of the existing pick-catch). Tests + audition cover both
~75 ms retriggers and ~150 ms fresh-note repeats.

**Preregistered oracles (C9):** before the mechanism lands, a calibration
commit measures the current path and a deliberately-broken counterfactual, and
freezes the exact windows, metrics, thresholds, and key/velocity/zone coverage
for: the body-energy oracle, the 3-band seam parity tolerance, and the
residual-at-f0 threshold — amended into this HLD. Mechanism-after-oracle, per
the repo's method; no tuning the bar to the result.

**Gating:** armed only where the LA wrap is armed (default bank, samples on);
alt bank and `--no-samples` keep the noise burst — `altbank_guitars_skip_sample_layer`
untouched; a unit assertion pins that the modeled-only build never derives
(mirror `clavinet_default_is_sampled_alt_is_modeled`).

**Ship rule (revised — S2):** numeric gate (all guitar oracles + body-energy
oracle + seam-parity + residual check green, two-iteration stop rule) is
*necessary*; the *sufficient* condition is Arthur's ear at the audition
checkpoint (§10). Ships default-on only past both; otherwise revert the commit
and keep findings in the journal.

**AS-BUILT OUTCOME: PARKED (not shipped).** The canceller itself works
(−16…−41 dB f0 cancellation on all 15 zones, gate frozen at −12 dB), but the
injection mechanism failed its preregistered gates through three iterations —
pole-zero mismatch × ~10³ resonant gain (swell), spike-flat period seeding
(profile regression), unnormalized projection fold (overshoot). Prototype +
probe preserved at commit `a9764e0` (reverted `f76d1a2`); full post-mortem and
the redesign lead ((1−g)-normalized harmonic projection) in the journal.
Items 1–4 are unaffected and ship.
