# 2026.07.18 — PLN — saxophone looped sustain, model as modulator (GM 64-67)

## Problem (measured)

Past 0.24 s the sax `LaVoice` gates the sample off (`sampler.rs:1599`), so a held sax
is 100% modeled reed. The modeled sustain is a static drone: liveness (framewise RMS
CoV) **0.024 vs the SC-55's 0.050** (~2.3× less movement). Each 0.628 s sample already
carries ~0.4 s of real, breathing sustain that we discard.

## Approach (Arthur-approved; SoTA-verified)

Attack sample → **pitch-synchronous looped real sustain**; the **model is demoted from
a sound source to a modulator** (vibrato/brightness/breath applied TO the looped sample),
NEVER summed as a second tonal oscillator at the same f0 (the comb trap the attack seam
already avoids with its sum-to-one crossfade). The looped real recording IS the hold, so
the hold becomes real sax timbre.

## Key architectural decision — loop is computed at RUNTIME from the committed WAV

Rather than re-bake the sample bank (network + committed-asset change), the seamless loop
is derived from the existing zone `data` + its measured `root`, once per zone, cached in a
`OnceLock` (mirroring the sample-bank caches). Rationale: no network dependency, the
committed CC-BY WAVs are untouched, and the loop math is deterministic. The research's
"bake offline" advice was about keeping the runtime hot path a dumb modulo wrap — a
once-per-zone cached bake satisfies that.

### Feasibility — PROVEN (prototype on the committed samples)

| file | f0 | loop | periods | seam cost | spectral drift |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| sax_alt_C#4_f | 281 | 0.414–0.471 | 16 | 0.0016 | −2% |
| sax_ten_C4_f | 264 | 0.339–0.373 | 9 | 0.0006 | +1% |
| sax_sop_C5_f | 531 | 0.334–0.552 | 116 | 0.0012 | −3% |
| sax_bar_C3_f | 130 | 0.316–0.423 | 14 | 0.0020 | +0% |

Every zone yields a low-seam, spectrally-steady integer-period loop. The material is
sufficient; bias selection toward the LARGEST clean loop (fewer repeats → weaker "looping
tell").

## Increments

- **Inc 1 — core looped sustain (hearable milestone).** `Zone` gains an optional cached
  loop; a `SaxLoopVoice` plays `data` 0→loop_end then wraps loop_start↔loop_end
  (contiguous attack→loop, wrapped-neighbour interp `data[(j+1)%n]`). Loop found by an
  `extract_loop`-style seam search (integer-period from `root`, value+slope cost), with a
  short linear (sum-to-one) crossfade + zero-cross snap, cached per zone. Wire GM 64-67
  (samples on) to it; `--no-samples` unchanged. Model NOT summed for the hold.
  *Validate:* renders; sustain liveness ↑ toward SC-55; `la_sax_audible` + pitch integrity
  green; loop-seam oracle (RMS-flat across ≥3 wraps, per `bagpipe_loop_sustains_across_wraps`).
  **← natural review point: Arthur listens before the modulator work.**
- **Inc 2 — model as modulator.** Vibrato on loop read-rate (CC1, delayed/ramped),
  brightness one-pole LP corner from CC11×velocity (reuse the rasp/cone-fill dynamic
  intent as a control curve), additive HP breath noise tracked to CC11. CC semantics:
  engage only when a channel authors the controller. Loop-tell mitigation: slow bounded
  (±0.1–0.3%) read-rate random walk.
- **Inc 3 — guards + integrate.** Extend `la_sax_level_continuity` for the attack→loop
  seam; render-diff (only GM 64-67 albums differ past 0.24 s; any sax-free diff is a bug);
  README/DESIGN + journal. Version bump at integration (not on branch).

## Risks / watch

- **Loop-tell** on the shorter loops (9 periods): rely on the largest-clean-loop bias +
  Inc 2 modulators; a pure Inc-1 loop may sound slightly static until Inc 2 lands.
- **No ears on this box** — the numeric oracles (liveness, pitch, seam RMS) are the proxy;
  Arthur's listen after Inc 1 is the real acceptance test for a timbre change.
- Repitch stays inside the 0.5..=2.05 clamp (sax zones ~a major third apart).
