# HLD — *The Causeway*, Act Two (tracks 6–10: the tide returns)

**Addendum to** "wrk_docs/2026.07.18 - HLD - The Causeway album (five
crossings).md". Act One (T1–T5) stands as composed and committed. The album
becomes a ten-track, two-act record (~55 min). **Revision 2** — after a
two-critic adversarial pass; the migration duties in §4 are load-bearing.

**Story arc (machine-verified):** the dawn crossing happened; then the tide
came back, as tides do. The causeway drowns again — the shores part
*physically* (the stereo strait re-opens) — but the two voices never part
again *musically*: from T6 onward the themes may, and must, sound together,
and the leading-tone ban is lifted (the ache was resolved). Act Two's new
material all grows from THE FUSION PHRASE — the one line both voices own —
and Act Two runs on a new audible tension engine: **THE ROAD HOME** (the
fusion retrograde) assembles across the act, 3 → 5 → 6 → 8 notes, each track
reaching further and stopping short, until T10 finally walks it whole —
paired with a forward fusion statement as an audible palindrome — and the
**island theme turns major** for the first time on the record.

Act One's discipline: separation. Act Two's discipline: weather. The water
rises and falls (tide-breath everywhere, a gale at the centre), and the
music holds.

## 1. Act Two material (extends `material.py`, oracle-pinned)

### 1.1 THE CONVERGENCE, ACT TWO — never apart again
`CONVERGENCE` extends: `{6: ("G","G"), 7: ("C","C"), 8: ("D","D"),
9: ("F","F"), 10: ("D","D")}` — distance **0 on every Act Two track**. The
pair travels *together* through D's plagal neighbourhood — G, C, back
through the home D under the gale, F for the autumn, D to leave — and every
theme statement's implied tonic must match its track's pc. One pinned
exception: T9's MEMORY states the island theme once in its original
**E minor**, whitelisted by T9's own oracle, never silently.

### 1.2 THE SIMULTANEITY LAW, INVERTED
Act One banned overlap until T5. Act Two **requires** it: every track 6–10
must contain ≥ 1 overlapping island+mainland statement pair
(`material.overlapping_pairs`), downbeat-consonant across the overlap. T8
requires ≥ 2 overlaps *inside its storm movement* — the storm cannot part
them; that is the thesis of the act.

### 1.3 THE FUSION AS SEED — the hook ledger, second five
`HOOKS[6..10]`, each derived from THE FUSION PHRASE. **Derivation
predicates are per-hook** (proven in `verify_material()` as data):

| n | name | cell (onset, dur, semis) | derivation predicate |
|---|------|--------------------------|----------------------|
| 6 | the flood bell | (0,1,0) (1,.5,9) (1.5,.5,9) (2,1,9) | interval+onset sub-pattern of FUSION (= fusion[0:4] verbatim) |
| 7 | the noon fall | (0,1,0) (1,1,-2) (2,.5,-3) (2.5,.5,-5) (3,1,-7) | interval+onset sub-pattern of FUSION (= fusion[4:9]; final dur shortened — durations are not matched by the searcher) |
| 8 | the gale riff | (0,.5,0) (.5,.5,0) (1,.5,0) (1.5,1.5,-9) | pitch-retrograde of fusion[0:4] only (own rhythm) — the incantation slamming down the sixth |
| 9 | the road-home head | (0,.5,0) (.5,1.5,2) (2,2,4) | pitch head of FUSION-RETRO (rels = retro[0:3] = 1-2-3 rising; own rhythm at onset fractions 0/¼/1, deliberately searcher-disjoint from the retro, the mainland's 4-5-6 run, the island's 3-4-5 run AND hook 2's inner b3-4-5 run — the last alias was caught by the round-trip matrix) |
| 10 | the sail | (0,1,0) (1,1.5,9) (2.5,1.5,12) | fusion frame: first note, crest, last note lifted an octave |

Consequences (by design, documented for composers): every forward fusion
statement auto-registers **hooks 6 and 7 exactly once each** (they are
literal sub-patterns; `verify_material` asserts those matches as the
derivation proof, and asserts hooks 8, 9, 10 do NOT match inside any
theme-family emission — 8 and 9 are rhythm-saved, hand-checked and
machine-pinned). Therefore **hook-density and medley oracles on Act Two
tracks count only hits NOT time-nested inside a fusion-family statement
span** — use the new helper `material.hook_statements_unnested(sc, n)`,
never raw `find_statements`, for those counts. Own-track density stays ≥ 6
(unnested); the protagonist bass still sings the track's own hook ≥ 2× in
its choruses.

### 1.4 THE SHARED LANGUAGE — fusion statements everywhere
Every Act Two track states THE FUSION PHRASE ≥ 1 time (tonic = the track's
pinned pc). On T10 the fusion statement(s) live **only in movement IV** (the
palindrome — §1.5); the medley movement must stay fusion-free so its hook
hits are genuine. T5's "exactly one" stays a T5-local law; the album docs
re-scope "the record's only melodic tonic landing" to Act One (docs change,
not an oracle change).

### 1.5 THE ROAD HOME — the act's tension engine and its two payoffs
**THE FUSION RETROGRADE** (`play_fusion(..., retro=True)`, searchable as
`"fusion_retro"`): (0,2,1) (2,.5,2) (2.5,.5,3) (3,1,4) (4,1,5) (5,1,6)
(6,.5,6) (6.5,.5,6) (7,1,1) — the held tonic, the climb, the incantation,
home. The same road walked backward.

- **THE REACH** (T6–T9): `RETRO_REACH = {6: 3, 7: 5, 8: 6, 9: 8}` — each
  track states **exactly its pinned prefix** of the retrograde
  (`play_fusion(retro=True, count=N)`) ≥ 1 time, and NO longer prefix and
  no full retrograde (oracle: prefix cells of length pinned+1 … 9 all have
  zero matches). The listener hears the road home assembling across the
  act, four times reaching, four times stopping short — Act Two's version
  of T4's 4/7/9 device, at album scale.
- **THE PALINDROME** (T10 IV): one forward FUSION statement immediately
  followed by the full RETROGRADE, **exactly once on the album** — the road
  out and the road back as one audible mirror. Banned on T6–T9.
- **THE ISLAND IN MAJOR** (T10 III): `play_island(..., major=True)`
  (ionian; it *still hangs on degree 2* — even melted, the island keeps its
  character), searchable as `"island_major"`. Banned on T6–T9; required ≥ 1
  on T10, in a **becalmed, cleared window of its own** (§2 T10) — the payoff
  gets the stage, not a corner of the medley.

### 1.6 THE RE-OPENING STRAIT (and the one boat)
`SHORE_PANS` extends: `{6: (56,72), 7: (54,74), 8: (42,86), 9: (46,82),
10: (64,64)}` — widths 16, 20, 44, 36, 0. Claims: the gale (T8) is the
widest water of the act; the water is never again as wide as the first
winter (all < 48); the island-left / mainland-right rule applies to 6–9.
**T10's one-boat seats apply to the two THEME-CARRYING channels only**
(both at 64 — one point on a wide sea); T10's accompaniment gets an
explicit symmetric seating plan around centre (its module pins it; e.g.
brass 54/74, strings 48/80, bells 58/70) so the album's fullest track is
not mono (repo mono-collapse lesson).

### 1.7 THE TIDE-TABLE, ACT TWO
`MORSE_WORDS` extends: `{6: "FLOOD", 7: "NOON", 8: "GALE", 9: "WANE",
10: "SAIL"}` on five NEW rotating timbres — vibraphone 11, steel drums 114,
**timpani 47** (the gale's morse is thunder), music box 10, glockenspiel 9.
All ten morse programs distinct across the album.

### 1.8 THE VOWEL CLOCK, ACT TWO
`VOWEL_CAPS` extends: `{6: 90, 7: 95, 8: 45, 9: 70, 10: 127}` — summer
opens, the gale seals the mouths again (T8 < T7 pinned), autumn mellows,
and T10 must REACH ≥ `VOWEL_FLOOR_T10 = 100`: above every other track's
cap outside T5 (whose committed *reach* is 90) — the album's fullest voice
as they sail, and machine-comparable: `VOWEL_FLOOR_T10 > VOWEL_CAPS[7]`
and `> VOWEL_FLOOR_T5`.

### 1.9 CADENCE LAW, ACT TWO — the plagal signature, spent carefully
The leading-tone ban stays scoped to tracks 1–4. New helper
`plagal_final_failures()` — **new logic, NOT built on `cadence_failures`**
(that helper's window-wide leading-tone ban and permissive {0,5,7,10}
approach set are Act One law): bass lands the tonic pc at the final
downbeat, and its last prior note is pc offset **5 exactly**; no
leading-tone ban. Scope: **plagal finals on T6, T7, T9, T10 only.** T8 is
pinned to end UNRESOLVED — final bass on the iv (pc offset 5 *as the last
note*, no tonic landing on any melodic channel in its final window; the
bell-buoy toll channel is exempt: the buoy tolls the D the music refused).

### 1.10 UNCHANGED AND CLARIFIED LAWS
Tolls continue (`TOLLS[n] = n`; `play_tolls` no longer validates the
phantom gap after the final strike, so T10's widening fits the law).
Tide-breath everywhere EXCEPT the pinned authored shapes: **T6 II's
accelerando and T8's storm map own their movements** (the T2 pattern: the
authored shape gets its own tempo oracle; tide-breath covers the rest).
Breath heralds: **T6's herald window sits at the very top of the track**
(the act opens with an inhale, before the gait begins), T7 and T10 herald
their grooves; T8 arrives unannounced and T9 is exempt (both documented).
Protagonist bass + doubled thumb on 6, 7, 8, 10 (T9 exempt). Bank-select
order, pan discipline, gaps, bends, bounds — generic checks unchanged.

## 2. The five tracks

### T6 — "The Flood" (~5:30 · G major/E minor lean · 4/4 · ≈112)
The morning after: they walk back as the tide chases them — jaunty
McCartney domestic pop (the Another Day gait) breaking into a stacked
Super Trouper chorus.
- **Herald** (track top): pan flute alone ≥ 2 bars, HOOK6's head, rising
  CC11 — the act inhales.
- **I. Dry Shod** — piano + acoustic gait, HOOK6 bell on vibraphone
  (which also taps FLOOD); first easy overlap of the themes; REACH: the
  road-home's first 3 notes, stated and left hanging.
- **II. Water at the Heels** — authored accelerando + rising CC74 (the
  water audible as brightness); the chorus stacks (doubled thumb, piano).
- **III. The Last Stones** — the sprint; a pan-flute ECHO of HOOK6's head
  rides over the run (an echo, not a herald — it is not alone).
- **IV. Landed** — soaked and laughing: half-tempo chorus reprise, fusion
  statement, plagal final, 6 tolls.
- Oracles: overlap ≥ 1, herald-at-top, accelerando + CC74 rise, unnested
  hook density, reach(3, no longer), protagonist bass, doubled thumb,
  morse FLOOD, vowel ≤ 90, plagal final, pans 56/72, tolls, fusion ≥ 1,
  no island_major / no full retro.

### T7 — "Noon Water" (~5:30 · C major · 4/4 · ≈88 downtempo)
High summer — the Delerium Karma track, Feel-the-Sun warm: lush downtempo
groove, breathy shakuhachi and pan-flute leads, choir high (≤ 95), steel
drums tapping NOON, both themes overlapping lazily in C.
- **I. Glare** — pads + shakuhachi haze, HOOK7 falling on Rhodes.
- **II. The Long Afternoon** — herald, then the groove: HOOK7 on bass
  (protagonist), doubled thumb (Rhodes), themes overlapping, fusion on
  flute; CC94 echo throws on phrase tails; REACH: 5 notes, further up the
  road, stopped.
- **III. Heat Shimmer** — autopan crystal (named, low), harmonica lazy
  solo with draw bends (the album's only harmonica feature this act).
- **IV. Evening Water** — HOOK7 augmented; plagal final; 7 tolls.
- Oracles: overlap, unnested hook density, reach(5, no longer), bass,
  thumb, herald, morse NOON, vowel ≤ 95, echo throws, plagal final, pans
  54/74, tolls, tide-breath, fusion ≥ 1, no withheld payoffs.

### T8 — "The Equinox Gale" (~5:45 · D minor · 4/4 · authored storm map)
The album's heaviest track — Stranglehold horns, rock organ 18, driven
guitars 29/30, the causeway drowned for weeks, the strait at its widest
(42/86). The thesis oracle: **the storm cannot part them** — ≥ 2 overlaps
INSIDE the storm movement.
- **I. Glass Falling** — pressure drop: low drones, timpani morse GALE,
  sealed choir (≤ 45), HOOK8 assembling.
- **II. The Gale** — HOOK8 (B-B-B slam down the sixth) on driven guitar +
  bass octaves, horns answering with aftertouch rasp (the Stranglehold
  engine), rock-organ Leslie fast; authored lurching tempo map (its own
  oracle); both themes stated INTO the storm, overlapping twice.
- **III. Eye** — near-silence (whitelisted): fusion alone on piano — the
  language holds in the eye; REACH: 6 notes, snatched away as the back
  wall hits.
- **IV. Blown Out** — collapse; the riff halved, hollow; **no benediction**:
  the final window lands on the iv and refuses the tonic (bells exempt) —
  then the lone buoy tolls the D the music would not, 8 times.
- Oracles: storm overlaps ≥ 2, unnested riff density, reach(6, no
  longer), authored storm map, aftertouch horns, organ CC1 fast, eye gap
  whitelist, morse GALE (program 47 lane), vowel ≤ 45, UNRESOLVED final
  (iv, no tonic outside bells), pans 42/86, tolls, fusion ≥ 1 (the eye),
  no withheld payoffs, unheralded (documented).

### T9 — "The Wane" (~5:00 · F major · 4/4 bossa-lite + rubato · ≈76)
Autumn letters — the Distractions / Don't Be Careless Love ballad with
Footprints fingerpicking: nylon + fingerpicked steel guitar, brush kit,
strings, **cello** as the marquee voice (harmonica stays in T7); music box
taps WANE.
- **I. Shorter Days** — nylon fingerpicking, bossa brushes, strings low.
- **II. The Letters** — THE MEMORY, oracle-pinned: HOOK1 (the first
  winter's heartbeat) ≥ 1 at stretch ≥ 2 (slow memory), and the island
  theme ONCE in its original E minor — Act Two's only off-pc statement,
  explicitly whitelisted by this track's convergence oracle.
- **III. Don't Be Careless** — the warm chorus: themes overlapping gently
  in F, fusion on cello, HOOK9 (the road-home head, rising 1-2-3) closing
  every phrase — hope, not sigh; REACH: 8 notes now, almost the whole way
  home, held back at the last step.
- **IV. First Frost** — music box alone (WANE again), a breath of HOOK4
  ice (unpinned); plagal final; 9 tolls.
- Oracles: memory quotes (HOOK1 stretched, island-at-E whitelist),
  overlap, unnested hook9 density, reach(8, no full retro), morse WANE,
  vowel ≤ 70, plagal final, pans 46/82, tolls, tide-breath deep, fusion
  ≥ 1, ballad exemptions documented, no island_major.

### T10 — "Out on the Tide" (~7:00 · D major · 4/4 · 96, breathing, never flat)
The second crossing: both leave together at low water, and the album ends
on open sea — Band-on-the-Run gallop + Pipes-of-Peace pomp. **Not a T5
remake:** the payoffs get their own movements, and the ending is a fade at
full sail, not a fall-away.
- **I. Eight Bells** — dawn assembly; herald (HOOK10's head — the last
  inhale); glockenspiel taps SAIL; act-one echo allowed (HOOK5 unpinned).
- **II. The Boarding** — the gallop: HOOK10 rising on protagonist bass,
  doubled thumb (piano), brass + bells; themes overlapping in D; THE
  ACT-TWO MEDLEY: HOOKS 6–9 each ≥ 1 in this movement's window, counted
  UNNESTED — and the movement is fusion-free by law, so every hit is
  genuine.
- **III. The Slack** — becalmed, cleared window: the gallop stops; **THE
  ISLAND IN MAJOR** (≥ 1), the ice melting as a scene of its own, choir
  rising toward the floor (≥ 100 reached here or in IV, monotone rise).
- **IV. Hull Down** — the wind returns and crests to full sail; **THE
  PALINDROME**: fusion forward, then the full RETROGRADE (exactly once),
  into the plagal IV–I in D; the full ensemble holds its D-major chord, a
  seashore texture (GM 122, one held key, started before the tolls so it
  legally rings through) swells beneath, and **10 tolls** widen into the
  wash while CC11 carries everything below the horizon — the fade is
  distance, not diminuendo of the band. The sea sounds last.
- **Tempo law inverted from T5:** every movement breathes (they are ON the
  water); the deepest swell is the last.
- Oracles: medley 6–9 unnested + movement-II-fusion-free, island_major ≥ 1
  in III's window, palindrome (fusion in IV only; retro == 1, adjacent
  after a forward fusion), overlap, crescendo-then-distance-fade arcs,
  herald, bass, thumb, morse SAIL, vowel floor 100 monotone, plagal
  final, theme-channel seats both 64 + pinned symmetric accompaniment
  seating, 10 tolls + seashore ring-through, tide-breath every movement,
  unnested hook10 density.

## 4. Verification doctrine — MIGRATION DUTIES (land atomically, then regression-verify T1–T5)

Extending the tables breaks four committed exact-shape checks and the hook
round-trip harness in `verify_material()`; the extension commit must
rewrite them, and `python build.py --verify` (all five committed tracks)
must be green again before any Act Two track is composed:

1. **Morse counts** → the ten-word table {1:9, 2:8, 3:9, 4:9, 5:10, 6:17,
   7:10, 8:10, 9:8, 10:11}; **MORSE_PROGRAMS** → sorted keys [1..10], ten
   distinct programs; **TOLLS** → `{n: n for n in range(1, 11)}`;
   **VOWEL_CAPS** → keys [1..10] (Act One's 1–5 shape claim unchanged;
   Act Two claims per §1.8).
2. **Round-trip harness** → per-hook expected-match map: hooks 1–5
   exactly-1 own hit and zero in any theme-family emission (unchanged in
   spirit); hooks 6 and 7 additionally REQUIRED to match exactly once
   inside a forward-fusion emission (the derivation proof); hooks 8, 9, 10
   zero matches in every theme-family emission including the retrograde
   and island-major (rhythm-distinctness pinned).
3. **New machinery** with self-tests: `FUSION_RETRO` (+ `play_fusion`
   retro/count params; prefix cells for the REACH, incl. the proof that a
   count-N < 9 prefix emission never matches the full retro cell),
   `play_island(major=True)` + `"island_major"`/`"fusion_retro"` search
   keys, `hook_statements_unnested()`, `plagal_final_failures()` (new
   logic per §1.9), `RETRO_REACH` (strictly increasing, all < 9),
   Act Two pan/vowel/convergence claims, `play_tolls` phantom-gap fix.
4. **Act One scope-guards**: the strictly-narrowing strait claim and the
   isl<64<main rule iterate tracks 1–5 / 6–9 explicitly; CONVERGENCE
   distance claims: [6,4,3,2,0] for Act One (unchanged), all-zero + pinned
   pc walk for Act Two.

Audio side per track (2–3 RATIO checks): T6 rising brightness across II;
T7 groove warmth vs glare; T8 storm loudest + eye quietest on the album
track; T9 intimacy (chorus only modestly above verse); T10 tolls into sea
wash with an audible ≥ 6 s tail and III (becalmed) quieter than II.
