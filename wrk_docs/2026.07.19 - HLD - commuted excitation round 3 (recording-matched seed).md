4. **Honest gate reclassification** (S-F2 MAJOR + D-F5). Gate 2 is as

## 2. Mechanism v2 — recording-matched excitation

The KS loop's delay line is seeded with ONE period synthesized per note from
the zone recording's measured harmonic profile. No residual, no tail, no
inverse comb. New symbols do not use the word "commuted" (suggested family:
`RecMatch*` / `recmatch_*`); the HLD keeps the round's lineage name only.

### 2.1 Seed law (IC decay compensation — D-F1)

- **Per zone, at prewarm**: measure the recording's complex harmonic
  phasors `rec_k` (k = 1..cap) at `k·f_root` by Goertzel over the gate-2
  sustain window family, from the zone take (needs the r2 `pub(crate)`
  zone-field edit in sampler.rs).
- **Per note, at spawn**: synthesize one period at the played f0:

  `seed_k = rec_k · (w_k / w_1)^(−t_c·f0)`,  `w_k = g·|H(k·f0)|`

  where t_c = 0.475 s (gate-2 window center), w computed from the note's
  actual t60/bright (treble_hold already folded into `bright` at the build
  site — verified by the integ lens). The relative form (ratio to w_1)
  compensates the *profile* — absolute level is Part 2's job.
  **Compensation is a CLAMP, not a disable** (external C1 resolved the
  v2.1 ambiguity): every harmonic gets
  `min((w_k/w_1)^(−t_c·f0), 10^(18/20))` — saturating, continuous across
  keys (also retires the dsp micro-note's binary-flip step), never
  plain-rec_k fallback. The external pass computed that **past-cap
  harmonics occur through the MID rows, not just the edges** (nylon 52
  crosses the cap at k=6 — ≈+22/+23 dB needed; nylon 58 at k=4) — the cap
  therefore under-compensates gate-measured harmonics on celebrated rows,
  by design: boosting a fast-dying harmonic +40 dB at onset to win a
  sustain window is onset harshness, not realism. The frozen gates judge
  whether clamped compensation is enough; §2.3's freeze record discloses
  exactly where the cap binds. Harmonic cap: `k·f0 < min(8 kHz, 0.45·sr)`
  (an engineering pick: ear band + take SNR + anti-alias) and k within
  the measured profile.
- **Seed synthesis is continuous over the loop's actual fill length**
  (external C5): `KsLoop::new` fills `ceil(target)+1` samples, and r2
  wrapped the excitation modulo a rounded period — a phase seam that was
  inaudible for noise but would not be for phase-exact resynthesis. The
  new builder synthesizes harmonics with continuous phase across the full
  fill length at the loop's tuned frequency — no modular wrap, seam
  retired.
- **Profile sanity check** (external C4): retiring the residual gate
  removed the only check that touched the recording itself; every
  surviving diagnostic is preset-physics-only. A unit test asserts every
  zone's measured `rec_k` profile is sane (finite, non-NaN, rec_1 above a
  floor relative to the take peak) so a corrupted/mistagged take fails
  loudly at the profile, not conflated with model error inside gate 2.
- Phases: the recording's measured phasors. Both gates are phase-invariant
  (D-F6) — phase affects audition only. No peak-norm on the spawn seed.
- **No by-construction claim**: the seed *encodes* the recording's profile
  under the correct IC decay model; gate 2 decides (measured end-to-end).
- Synthesis by phase-recurrence rotation (2 mul/harmonic/sample; worst case
  key 40 ≈ 97 harmonics × 535 samples ≈ 52k mul — microseconds, bounded,
  fine for the realtime spawn path; S-F5).

### 2.2 Level calibration (two-phase, full-model, gate-twin target)

- **Phase 1** (per zone): derive the profile-bearing struct into its
  OnceLock (recording data only — no circularity).
- **Phase 2** (per zone): with the now-'static profile, render the FULL
  model via direct constructors — `Pluck::new_matched(preset, key_root,
  100, 44100, 5, ce, /*calib=*/1.0)` vs `Pluck::new(preset, key_root, 100,
  44100, 5)` — never through `make()`/`recmatch_for` (OnceLock reentrancy =
  deadlock, I-M3). **`calib` is an explicit constructor parameter**
  (re-review I-M2b): phase 2 passes 1.0 literally; the `make()` guitar arm
  reads the calib static (populated by a plain phase-2 pass, never a
  get_or_init that constructs via the arm) and passes it through — so
  `new_impl` never reads a half-built static, cold or warm. Measure the
  gate-3 bands (300/1000/3000 Hz, q=0.7, 0.25–0.35 s) on both, store
  `calib` = the matching scalar in a second static (I-M2's lifetime
  ordering).
- **Target = the exact gate-twin construction (fixed seed 5)**, per D-F4:
  averaging over seeds leaves ±1.5 dB of the ±2 dB budget on the table.
  The probe test prints seed-5-vs-multi-seed-mean deviation per zone as a
  diagnostic, so an outlier twin is visible in the record.
- calib multiplies the seed only (there is no tail). Gate 2's rel-f0
  profile is scalar-invariant, so calib cannot mask a profile error.

### 2.3 Coverage rule (loop-sustain physics — D-F3; replaces +12 st)

A key engages recording-matched seeding iff **at least 2 of harmonics
k ∈ {1..6} at the played f0 have per-harmonic ring time
`t60_k = 3/(f0·|log10 w_k|) ≥ 0.35 s`** (the gate-2 window start) —
otherwise the mechanism is inert there (nothing shapeable survives to the
sustain window) and the burst path is used. Constants: 0.35 s is
definitional (the window the round exists to shape); "≥2" = fundamental
plus at least one partial, the minimum for "profile" to mean anything.

**Freeze protocol (anti-gate-shopping, S-F1 + external C2):** the rule
depends only on preset constants and loop physics — computable a priori.
During bring-up, BEFORE the first gate run, a probe computes and journals
the FROZEN record from the actual preset code (authoritative; the dsp
lens's Python model is provisional only):
1. the engagement map for the full gate population;
2. a sensitivity disclosure — whether any row flips under 0.35→0.40 s or
   ≥2→≥3 (scope re-review ask);
3. **the needed-compensation-vs-cap table** at k = 2..6 per frozen row,
   plus where the three gate-3 bands sit relative to the cap, with
   "engaged-but-past-cap" as a named, disclosed category (external C1);
4. the spawn-vs-restrike level diagnostic (§2.4).
**ALL FIVE constants freeze together at that moment** — 0.35 s, "≥2",
t_c = 0.475 s, the +18 dB clamp, and the 8 kHz cap (external C2: labeling
a constant an "engineering pick" must not leave it tunable after gate
results; that would reopen exactly the S-F1 hole). Gate rows where the
rule disengages assert `..._for(prog, key).is_none()` (A1, exact shape per
I-M4); engaged rows must pass unchanged thresholds. After the freeze: any
red ⇒ repark. No constant may move afterward.

Re-review notes on the map: the dsp lens's provisional model says **all
gate keys engage** (survivor counts: nylon 6/6/6/6/3/2/2/2, steel
6/6/6/6/5/3/3/3/3); the integ lens's note-t60-law reasoning suggests nylon
88 may disengage in the real code — **nylon 88 is an explicitly-checked
line in the probe**, and if it engages and then fails a gate, that is a
repark (no fallback rescue exists any more, by design). Because no frozen
gate row may exercise the disengage branch, one **synthetic high-key unit
test** (e.g. steel ~key 100, where t60_1 < 0.35 s) asserts the fallback
fires — an added oracle, allowed, so the branch isn't dead-reckoned (dsp
re-review 1c-i). The 1-partial engagements at the top of the population
(nylon 70/76/88, steel 88) are recorded by the AC1 surviving-harmonic
diagnostic — greens there are parity evidence, not spectral-match evidence.

### 2.4 Restrike

Retrigger rebuilds the seed with the same builder plus r2's per-strike
jitter, then the existing peak-norm bound (`v·TREM_RESTRIKE_LEVEL`). The
external pass (C3) noted this discards the calib level on restrike while
spawn is calib-governed — a spawn/restrike level-law divergence gate 4
(relative RMS) is blind to. **Decision: keep the peak-norm** — it is the
SHIPPED restrike law for the burst path too (all pluck restrikes
peak-normalize to `v·TREM_RESTRIKE_LEVEL`), so matching it keeps the
matched path consistent with the behavior the gates twin against, and
redesigning shipped restrike semantics is out of this round's scope.
Honesty mitigations: the §2.3 freeze record includes a spawn-RMS vs
post-restrike-RMS diagnostic for matched vs burst at key 52 (a divergent
level law is visible pre-freeze, not hidden), and the early stems include
a restrike passage. Both call sites (spawn + retrigger) move to the new
builder in lockstep (I-M6).

## 7. Risks v2

1. **The seed may simply be inaudible** vs today's tuned KS sustain — the
   round optimizes distance to one flat take per zone as a proxy. The early
   stems + kill condition bound the sunk cost (S-F3).
2. **Off-root drift** (steel 88 at +5.16 st): calib derived at the zone
   root; ±2 dB must hold off-root. Genuine red if it misses.
3. **Sub-f0 seam bands at the top keys** (nylon 88 f0 = 1318 Hz vs
   300/1000 Hz bands): parity there measures common-mode LA + floor for
   both paths — likely trivially green, honestly noted as weak evidence.
4. **1-harmonic race at the top of the population** (D-F3): the relative
   gate can legitimately pass on h2 alone; the per-key surviving-harmonic
   diagnostic (AC1) keeps that visible, not hidden.
5. **Detuning vs exact-multiple Goertzel**: measured common-mode and small
   (≤ ~0.4 dB at k ≤ 6) — retired as a risk by the dsp lens.
