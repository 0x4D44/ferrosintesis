# HLD — Natural (gentle) pluck excitation redesign

**Status: APPROVED** (Arthur signed off the phased plan + self-paced-loop execution, 2026.07.19.)
Date: 2026-07-19
Task: `task/20260719-TSK-HUM-natural-pluck-excitation-redesign`
Design: **Fable 5** (consult); verified, owned, and to be implemented by Claude Opus 4.8 (1M).
Complaint (Arthur): our plucked-string voices sound FIERCE — "someone really digging into the
strings," not a gentle finger pluck; "the sustain is quiet compared to the pluck." Across every

## 0. Diagnosis (measured, not asserted)

**The defect is attack-to-sustain ENERGY BALANCE, not spectral harshness.** A differential harness
(single isolated notes, rendered through ferrosintesis AND FluidSynth+FluidR3_GM as a natural
reference; attack RMS over [0, max(15 ms, 1.5/f0)] ÷ sustain RMS over [100, 250] ms) shows:

| note | bare KS **model** (`--no-samples`) | samples-on | FluidR3 |
|------|-----|-----|-----|
| steel A3 f | **2.81** | 3.44 | 1.34 |
| nylon A3 f | **5.09** | 6.50 | 1.30 |
| harp  A4 f | 1.51 | 1.90 | 1.21 |

**Verification (Claude) — where the fierceness lives (both layers):**
- The **bare KS model is genuinely fierce** (att/sus 2.8–5.1 vs FluidR3 ~1.3) AND spectrally FLAT at
  the onset (steel tilt −3.1 dB/oct vs FluidR3 −9.6). An earlier "spectrally at parity" reading was
  measuring the LA *sample*, not the model — corrected here. So Fable's core diagnosis holds on the
  actual synthesis: flat, peak-normalized noise excitation → too many near-equal harmonics in the
  attack, decaying away by the sustain.
- The **LA sample layer ADDS fierceness** on top (samples-on ≈ +0.5…+1.4 att/sus over the model).
  **Tripwire 8 confirmed** — a companion fix in `sampler.rs` (LA gain/fade staging) is also needed
  for default (samples-on) renders. The redesign is two-pronged: excitation (voices.rs) + LA layer.

**The physics (load-bearing).** The KS delay line is filled with one period of LP'd white noise,
peak-normalized. Below `pick_lp` the spectrum is FLAT: at A3 forte nylon `pick_lp`≈3500 Hz → ~16
near-equal harmonics; attack RMS ∝ a·√16 = 4a. By ~100 ms the in-loop damper has stripped everything
above ~h4; sustain RMS ∝ ~a·√3. **Flat excitation alone predicts att/sus ≈ 2–2.5 — the surplus is
literally twelve extra harmonics dying in the first 50 ms.** A natural −12 dB/oct (1/n²) displacement
distribution gives Σh² ≈ 1.1× the h1–h4 energy → ratio ≈ 1.1 before decay. This is why *tilt* can look
near-parity while the *ratio* is 5× off (tilt is a slope; the ratio integrates the area under it).
The **click** (HP noise, gain 2.0–2.2 ≈ up to ~2× the string peak, 3 ms, super-linear in velocity)
is steel/electric's dominant term; it sits at 2.5–5 kHz so it's invisible to a >5 kHz HF metric and
barely moves crest — matching "guards at parity, ratio broken." **nylon (small click) → the
front-load dominates; steel (big click) → the click dominates. Both get both, proportioned per
family.**

**Scoping fact (default renders).** GM 24/25/46/6/104/105 attacks are the **LA sample** (the model is
discarded before `fade_start`). The MODEL attack is audible for: never-wrapped plucks (26–37, 45,
15, 7-alt), CC0 alt banks (oud, ukulele), `--no-samples`, extreme-repitch fallbacks, and **every
tremolo restrike**. The **driven guitars (29/30) are NOT LA-wrapped** → the amp-sim/drive processes
the bare model → the excitation fix reaches ch 14's lead directly.

## 1. Lever choice: ~100% attack-side taming, 0% body-raising

The sustain levers (`t60`, `bright`, release laws) are COMPOSITIONAL — 109 albums were written
against those decay times and they are pinned by `harp_46_shimmers`, the class-identity `sustain_db`
axis, and the decay oracles. FluidR3's 1.1–1.3 is partly loop-flattened (an artifact) so "hold the
body up" would chase it. So: redistribute the excitation's harmonic energy (carries nylon/harp/
finger) + dispose of the click (carries steel/electrics) + normalize on the sustain band (guarantees
no re-leveling). A sustaining driver stays an *articulation*, never a global realism floor.

## 2. Redesigned excitation — a harmonic-domain build (behind `exc_model: Legacy | Shaped`)

Per-preset opt-in so migration is preset-by-preset; un-migrated presets are bit-identical.

**2.1 Backbone (deterministic, position- and slope-shaped).** With N = `exc_len` (one period,
`.max(4)`), f0_buf = sr/N, n_max = floor(0.45·sr/f0_buf):
- Weights n=1..n_max: **w_n = |sin(nπβ)| / n^s · P(n·f0_buf)**
  - β = the existing jittered `pos` (position enters analytically via sin — NOT the FIR comb; the
    comb stays on the noise only; both use the same β so notches coincide).
  - s = **slope knob** (1/n^s = −6.02·s dB/oct): finger 2.0, picked 1.3–1.5. This sets att/sus (§0).
  - P(f) = one-pole magnitude 1/√(1+(f/pick_lp)²) using the EXISTING velocity/wound-scaled `pick_lp`
    → the velocity→brightness law and `wound_strings_darker` survive by construction.
- Phases φ_n = 0 ± 0.4 rad jitter (n≥2), φ_1 = 0 → physical displacement snapshot (low crest ~1.7),
  not a noise smear.
- Amplitude jitter (1 + 0.25·white()) for **n≥2 only** — h1 deterministic (retires the harp weak-h1
  Rayleigh problem structurally).
- Zero-mean, Nyquist-capped, exact periodic components → band-limited + DC-blocked for free.
- Cost O(N·n_max) once per note-on — negligible.

**2.2 Noise component (texture).** Exactly today's `raw → FIR comb (delay round(βN), coeff 0.9)` from
the note rng (or the fixed rng for `vel_sense<1` presets — harpsichord plumbing untouched).

**2.3 Blend (energy-domain).** Normalize backbone and noise each to unit RMS, then
**exc = √(1−ρ)·backbone + √ρ·noise**, ρ=`noise_mix`: finger 0.20, picked 0.30. Preserves per-note
liveliness (+ pos/bright/t60 and w_n/φ_n jitters).

**2.4 Normalization — the anti-re-leveling core.** Anchor the SUSTAIN BAND, not the peak: scale so
the energy of h1..h4 (Goertzel on the buffer) equals **K_SUS·v** (v = existing `vel_amp`). One global
K_SUS calibrated once so RMS(0.05–0.35 s) matches the HEAD median across the preset/key grid (Oracle
G7 enforces ±1.0 dB). Per-preset `exc_trim` allowed ±1.5 dB (beyond = Tripwire 1). Peak guard: if
max|exc|>1.6·v, scale down. Side benefit: the harpsichord's ±1.5 dB seed-luck spread collapses to
~±0.4 dB.

**2.5 h1 floor.** `top_up_h1` stays after normalization — now a near-no-op (h1 deterministic);
`harp_octave_outliers_are_floored` should STRENGTHEN.

**2.6 Parameter table (finger↔pick knobs).**

| Family | s | noise_mix | pick_lp base | pos base | click | knock (§3) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Finger-soft: NYLON, HARP, PIZZ, FRETLESS, BASS, UPRIGHT, alt oud/ukulele | 2.0 (harp start 1.7 — Tripwire 4) | 0.20 | lower to 2000–2800 where >3k | 0.25–0.30 where <0.2 | **0 (deleted)** | 0.35–0.6·v |
| Picked: STEEL, JAZZ, CLEAN, DRIVE, PICK, DULCIMER | 1.3–1.5 | 0.30 | unchanged | unchanged | 35–50% of current | 0.2·v |
| Mechanical: HARPSICHORD, CLAVINET, SLAP, SLAP_POP, SITAR, BANJO | **Legacy** | — | — | — | unchanged | — |

Predicted (arithmetic, harness confirms): nylon 6.5 → ~1.4–1.8; steel 3.44 → ~1.6–2.2.

**2.7 Restrike/hammer.** Factor the excitation builder so the tremolo restrike gets the Shaped law
automatically (the most-exposed modeled attacks in default renders). Legato hammer stays phase-2.

## 3. Click disposition (commuted synthesis REJECTED, with reasons)

- **Finger presets: cut the click.** No physical basis for a fingerpad; pure attack-window energy.
- **Replace with a "body knock":** a short pre-EQ transient — Burst through `bandpass(f_knock, Q≈1)`
  at the preset's strongest body-mode ≤400 Hz (~200–300 Hz guitars, ~110 Hz upright), T60 10–15 ms,
  gain 0.35–0.6·v **linear**, via the existing `onset_pre` pre-EQ insertion so the body EQ colors it
  like a top-plate tap. This is the codebase's own bass-`kick` thump pattern generalized.
- **Picked presets: reduced HP click (35–50%) + a small knock.**
- **Full commuted synthesis rejected:** (a) the preset `body` chains are near-allpass (IR ≈ delta),
  so convolving folds in ~nothing; (b) any body transient ADDS attack energy — the opposite of the
  measured need.
- SLAP/SLAP_POP/CLAVINET keep their transients (pinned articulations).

## 4. Velocity law, crest-bounded

Level unchanged (v = vn^1.6). Brightness laws unchanged (pick_lp×(0.10+1.30·vn), bright×(0.22+0.98·
vn)); s is velocity-INDEPENDENT (the corner, not the slope, carries "forte=brighter"). Transients:
knock ∝ v linear; residual click ∝ v·(0.5+0.5·vn) — replacing today's super-linear v·vn ("forte=
spikier"). Enforced by G2 (crest(vel120)/crest(vel50) ≤ 1.4) + att/sus band holding at vel 50 AND 120.

## 5. Oracle suite (definition of done) — measured on the BARE MODEL (`--no-samples`)

Spectral tools: existing Goertzel `mag_at`/`centroid`/`hp_rms` + one hand-rolled radix-2 FFT (4096,
Hann) for flatness/inter-harmonic. Thresholds marked (cal) are frozen in the gap: RED on HEAD ≥10%
margin, GREEN on redesign ≥10% margin.

**P — Primary: attack-to-sustain energy ratio.** Windows: att = RMS[0, max(15 ms, 1.5/f0)]; sus =
RMS[100,250] ms. Physics-anchored band (NOT a magic number): measure t60_eff per note (log-RMS fit
100→400 ms); single-exponential ring gives R_phys = 10^((60/t60_eff)·0.1675/20). Require
**att/sus ∈ [max(1.15, 0.85·R_phys), 1.55·R_phys]**. Lower bound = anti-overshoot (K2 trap a). This
formula *quantifies the FluidR3 caveat*: Fluid's 1.1–1.3 sits below R_phys → confirmed loop-flattening
→ FluidR3 is a direction, physics is the floor. Grid: progs {24,25,26,27,32,33,34,35,45,46} + alt
oud/ukulele; keys {low,mid,high}; vel {50,100,120}; mean over seeds {3,7,11} in band, each seed ±15%.

**D — Differential vs FluidR3 (committed goldens, hermetic).** Dev-only script renders single notes
via fluidsynth+FluidR3_GM (progs 24/25/32/33/45/46, keys {40,52,64}, vel {60,100}), extracts
{att/sus,tilt,crest,flatness} to a committed JSON (regeneration script + soundfont SHA documented;
the test compares to the committed numbers — no fluidsynth at test time). D1: ratio_ours/ratio_fluid
∈ [0.95, 1.8] (may exceed — real decay — never undercut). D2: tilt within ±4 dB/oct. D3: energy
E(0–30ms)/E(100–300ms) within [0.5×, 2.0×] of Fluid.

**G — Secondary guards (parity; must not regress).** G1 tilt (robust regression, drop 2 deepest comb
notches, h1–h12): finger ∈[−15,−8], picked ∈[−12,−5], ≤ HEAD+2 (no compensatory brightening) (cal).
G2 crest ≤3.3, crest(120)/crest(50)≤1.4. G3 flatness ±0.06 of HEAD (cal). G4 HF fraction ≤0.02.
G5 slew ≤1.2× HEAD. G6 inter-harmonic floor ∈[−55,−22] dB (anti-sterile — the blend survives) (cal).
**G7 loudness parity (anti-re-leveling): RMS(0.05–0.35 s) within ±1.0 dB of a captured HEAD table;
seed spread ≤1.0 dB.** G8 h1 sanity (anti-burying): mag(h1) ≤ max(h2..h6)+14 dB. G9 variation+
determinism: seeds pairwise mean |Δ| ≥0.5 dB over h2..h10; same seed → bit-identical. G10 DC <1e-4.

## 6. How each K2 trap is avoided
- (a) Burying under h1: sin(nπβ) means h1 does NOT dominate (β=0.25 → h2>h1); s<2 for picked; noise
  blend + pick_lp corner keep upper-partial life; G8 bounds it; P-band lower edge fails an attack-less
  pluck.
- (b) Re-leveling: normalize the SUSTAIN BAND (what loudness tracks) to the same v-law; K_SUS
  calibrated to HEAD; **G7 machine-checks ±1.0 dB** → re-leveling can't ship silently.
- (c) Ten-oracle destabilization: per-preset Legacy|Shaped opt-in (un-migrated bit-identical); the
  velocity/wound/damper/t60 laws untouched; explicit disposition table (§7).

## 9. Tripwires — stop and re-escalate
1. K_SUS can't hold G7 without >±1.5 dB per-preset trims → sustain-band energy model is wrong.
2. P-band green but still sounds fierce → residue is downstream (bus-glue/chorus/reverb onset) —
   stop cutting excitation, re-diagnose at the mix layer.
3. velocity_opens_pluck_timbre < ~1.25 → velocity law needs s velocity-dependent (design change).
4. Harp shimmer vs slope irreconcilable at s≥1.6 → harp needs a damper-side re-voice (owner call).
5. FluidR3 bands disagree with physics bands by >30% per family → recalibrate to measurement, say so.
6. Render-diff touches any non-plucked album → BUG, stop.
7. steel sparkle bar (0.085) unreachable with click ≤60% → surface the sparkle-vs-gentleness conflict
   (the owner's own oracle defines steel as pick-fierce) rather than weakening either silently.
8. **Samples-ON att/sus for GM 24/25/46 still >2.5 after the model fix → the fierceness in default
   renders lives in the LA bank gain/fade (sampler.rs), a different fix.** (CONFIRMED partially active
   by Claude's verification — the LA layer adds +0.5…+1.4 att/sus. Phase 4 addresses it.)
