# 2026.07.20 — HLD — velocity-law alignment: one k=2 level law

Design consult: **Fable 5**. Owner: Claude Opus 4.8. Companion to
`2026.07.20 - HLD - instrument balance oracle + drum-forward recalibration.md` (the balance
HLD). This fix **precedes and unblocks** that document's M-CAL static-trim derivation: a

Anchors: `crates/ferrosintesis/src/dsp.rs:726` (`vel_amp`), `voices.rs` (floor sites, §4),
`sampler.rs:2653` (LA velocity law), `sampler.rs:3028` (sax composite), `drums.rs:588`,
`engine.rs:2860` (strip — velocity-blind, untouched), `voices.rs:14084` (organ exemption).

## 1. Scope

Make the velocity→level law of every velocity-sensitive voice — melodic, sampled and drum —
the GM-hardware convention `amp ∝ (v/127)²`, measured as an aggregate at the rendered
output, so composed dynamics render at their authored magnitude and drums stop out-gaining
the band as passages get louder.

**Non-goals:** `DRUM_FORWARD` / `kit_balance` recalibration (balance HLD §7);
`PROGRAM_TRIM_DB` re-derivation (M-CAL — unblocked by, not part of, this task); the
CC7/CC11 channel-gain curve (a separate axis, not yet measured); emulating the SC-55's
below-v≈24 low-end lift (§2.3); any public-API change (none needed — `dsp`/`voices`/
`sampler` are private modules, so this is a patch bump).

## 2. The measurement, and what it says

Probe: 12 programs × 2 keys × 9 velocities (8…127), notes isolated and dry, bus glue
bypassed, program trims inert. Level = max BS.1770 momentary block over a 1.2 s window.
Fit `amp = (v/127)^k` by least squares on level vs `20·log10(v/127)`, v ≥ 32.

| engine | median k | k range |
|---|---|---|
| SC-55 | **1.997** | 1.67 – 2.06 |
| S-YXG50 | **1.981** | 1.96 – 2.05 |
| ferrosintesis | **0.660** | 0.36 – 2.03 |

### 2.1 Both references ARE the square law — verified to 0.22 dB

`(v/127)²` is `40·log10(v/127)` in dB. Against the measured SC-55 medians:

| v | law | SC-55 | error |
|---|---|---|---|
| 32 | −23.95 | −23.93 | 0.02 |
| 48 | −16.90 | −17.04 | 0.14 |
| 64 | −11.90 | −11.86 | 0.04 |
| 80 | −8.03 | −8.25 | **0.22** |
| 96 | −4.86 | −4.91 | 0.05 |
| 110 | −2.50 | −2.55 | 0.05 |

Worst error 0.22 dB, and the S-YXG50 agrees independently. Two independently-implemented
modules from different manufacturers landing on the same law to within a quarter of a dB is
a specification (the DLS/SoundFont convention), not a preference. There is no free parameter
to argue about.

### 2.2 The defect is bimodal — "median k = 0.66" under-describes it

- **39 bare sites** are a wrong *exponent* (1.6): still a clean power law, just flat —
  19.2 dB of range over v=32…127 instead of 23.9.
- **The floored sites** are a wrong *functional form*: `X + Y·vel_amp(v)` asymptotes to `X`
  as v→0, so it is not a power law at all. The 0.4-floor voices bottom out at −6.6 dB where
  the SC-55 sits at −23.9.

**Deleting the floors alone leaves k = 1.6; changing the exponent alone leaves the floors
dominating.** Both halves are required.

### 2.3 The v=16 anomaly is a mixture artifact

The differential was non-monotonic (+7.76 at v=16 vs +14.77 at v=32). Two things collide:
the SC-55 departs from its own square law at the bottom (−28.44 measured vs −35.99 by the
law — a deliberate ~7.5 dB low-velocity lift the S-YXG50 does not share), while ferro's bare
k=1.6 sites land coincidentally on top of it. The per-program differential at v=16 is
therefore bimodal and its median straddles two clusters.

Below v≈24 **the two references disagree by >6 dB**, so there is no stable target there.
Decision: adopt the pure square law all the way down, matching the S-YXG50. Revisit only if
ghost-note passages in the corpus read as missing.

### 2.4 Census correction — three "floors" are timbre, not level (verified)

Of the 18 floored sites, three drive timbre parameters and **must be kept**:

- `voices.rs:6914` — lead filter **cutoff** `spec.cutoff * (0.55 + 0.45·vel_amp)`.
- `voices.rs:8080` — BowedString **bow speed** `max_vel: 0.03 + 0.22·vel_amp` ("bow SPEED
  drives brightness — a harder note is a faster bow").
- `voices.rs:5367` — pipe **chiff** at 0.012 scale: onset-transient colour, negligible level.

And one true level floor was **missing** from the original census because it uses non-literal
constants: `voices.rs:8098` BowedString `amp: amp_base + amp_span·vel_amp(vel)`.

`sampler.rs:3028` (sax) is not a mistake either but a deliberate composite — its comment
says the p/f banks already carry the bulk of the dynamic and the taper is gentle on top.

Blind floor deletion would have converted a filter cutoff and a bow-speed mapping into level
laws. All four classifications were re-verified against the source before adoption.

## 3. Approach — one global law; floors reclassified, not merely deleted

### 3.1 The change

1. **`dsp.rs:726`: exponent 1.6 → 2.0.** One definition site; every bare caller — all of
   `drums.rs`, most of `voices.rs`, the sampled-kit gains — inherits k=2 with no further
   edit. **This is the whole drum fix**: drums and melodic voices now share one law, so the
   drums/band ratio becomes velocity-invariant and the "drums lurch forward as the passage
   gets louder" instability dies structurally rather than by tuning.
2. **Fold the 14 level floors by their pair sum** (§4):
   `X + Y·vel_amp(vel)` → **`(X+Y)·vel_amp(vel)`**.

   This is one rule with no exceptions, and it keeps **v=127 output unchanged by the fold**
   — `vel_amp(127) = 1`, so the folded form and the original agree exactly at full velocity.
   The curve rotates about fortissimo; the hand-tuned top-end voicing does not move.

   For the 13 sites whose pair sums to exactly 1.0 the fold *is* plain deletion. Two sites
   do not sum to 1.0 and must be folded explicitly:
   - `voices.rs:1347` GM5 — `0.22 + 0.82` = 1.04 (`0.47·(…)` → `0.489·vel_amp`).
   - `voices.rs:8098` BowedString — `amp_base + amp_span` = **1.18** (GM42 cello) and
     **1.80** (GM43 contrabass), from the match at `voices.rs:8008`.

   > **Adversarial review caught this** (§10). An earlier draft said "delete the floors;
   > every *literal* pair sums to 1.0". The word "literal" silently exempted the one site
   > §2.4 had just been proud of finding — `:8098` uses computed constants. Plain deletion
   > there is a flat **−1.44 dB on the cello and −5.11 dB on the contrabass at full
   > velocity** (`amp` is a straight output multiplier at `voices.rs:8191`, and nothing
   > renormalizes it), which would violate AC7 and trip T5. It would also collapse cello
   > and contrabass to identical gain, erasing the deliberate 3.7 dB register balance
   > documented at `voices.rs:7992`. Folding by the pair sum makes the class of bug
   > impossible rather than fixing one instance of it.
3. **Keep the 3 timbre floors**, each gaining a comment naming it exempt from the level law.
4. **`voices.rs:3861`** — the Pluck `vel_sense` literal `vn.powf(1.6)` moves to 2.0 in
   lockstep; its comment already declares it mirrors `vel_amp`.
5. **Sampled composite paths** (§3.3).

~20 production line-edits, one load-bearing. Deterministic by construction: pure
arithmetic-constant changes, no RNG or ordering change.

### 3.2 Where the floors' musical intent goes

The floor's purpose — a quiet note keeping some body — survives where it belongs:

- **Timbre floors stay.** A quiet note is still articulate and present-but-dark. That is what
  the ear actually wanted; the *level* floor was compensating for models that once lacked
  this timbral response.
- **Genuinely compressed instruments** use the existing `vel_sense` mechanism
  (`voices.rs:3856`): compress velocity *before* the global law (`vn′ = 1 − s·(1−vn)`, then
  square). This mirrors how GM hardware works — a global concave curve × a per-patch
  sensitivity — and explains why the SC-55's own k dips to 1.67 on some patches. Exactly one
  instrument warrants it today (GM6 harpsichord, already wired). Grant it to nothing else here.
- **The composition layer** (CC7/CC11) is where "keep this *pp* line present" is authored
  intent, and several album engines already do this.
- The −18 LUFS normalize keeps whole-piece loudness pinned; what gets quieter is quiet
  *passages within* a piece — which is the authored dynamic finally rendering at full size.

### 3.3 Sampled voices: composite law, thresholds untouched

Layer **selection** compares raw `vel` against thresholds (`piano_bank` 51/95,
`cello_bank`/`contrabass_bank` 80…). A level-law change does not touch `vel`, so selection is
structurally unaffected; AC5 pins it anyway.

Level is subtler — the contract is that the **aggregate output** follows k=2, composed from
embedded layer loudness × synth-side gain law:

- **LA-wrapped voices** (`sampler.rs:2653`): the sample-gain law must track the wrapped
  model's law or the onset/sustain crossfade ratio drifts with velocity. Both branches
  simplify — generic `0.35 + 0.65·vel_amp(v)` → `vel_amp(v)`; guitar
  `(vel_amp(v)/ref)^exp · (0.35+0.65·ref)` → `(vel_amp(v)/ref)^exp · ref`.
  `GUITAR_VEL_LEVEL_EXP` is re-derived by its existing seam oracle.
- **Layer-carrying voices** (sax `:3028`; the piano/cello/contrabass banks *if* their pp/mf/f
  recordings are not loudness-equalized): the caller's per-layer gain constant absorbs the
  embedded step so the composite fits k=2. **Never** move thresholds; **never** re-bake
  assets (they are source, not output). AC2/AC3 arbitrate whether a bank needs this — do not
  pre-derive constants the oracle does not demand.

### 3.4 Alternatives rejected

- **Correct the aggregate at the channel strip.** The strip is per-channel and per-block and
  **cannot see velocity** — velocity is per-note. Wiring it in means new plumbing plus a
  registry inverting ~50 heterogeneous internal laws from a distance. Wrong layer.
- **Make voices velocity-blind for level; engine owns note gain.** The correct greenfield
  factoring — the invariant would be structural instead of oracle-enforced — but it is a
  57-site refactor for zero acoustic difference versus §3.1. Rejected per the simplicity
  brief; revisit only if a third velocity pass ever happens.
- **A per-voice "velocity depth" everywhere.** A depth preserving aggregate k=2 is just a
  trim (pointless); a depth that doesn't recreates the six-constant zoo that caused this
  defect.
- **Naive "change exponent, delete all 18 floors".** Silently converts a filter cutoff and a
  bow-speed mapping into level laws and breaks the sax composite. §3.1 is this plan *plus*
  the classification that makes it safe.

## 5. Acceptance criteria (tests first)

Shared harness: render `voices::make(program, key, vel, 44100.0, seed, samples=true)`
(drums: `drums::make`) for 1.2 s; level = max 400 ms RMS block on a 100 ms hop, in dB. Fit
`k` = least-squares slope of level vs `20·log10(v/127)` over V = {32, 48, 64, 80, 96, 110,
127}, averaged over two keys. Voice-level measurement is valid because the strip is
velocity-blind (verified) and the defect definition already bypassed glue. K-weighting is
unnecessary — the fit compares one note against itself across velocity.

- **AC1 — law pin.** `vel_amp(v) == (v/127)²` for all v; `vel_amp(127) == 1.0`.
- **AC2 — melodic aggregate law.** Probe set spanning families and both sampled and modeled
  paths (0, 4, 5, 11, 24, 33, 40, 42, 48, 52, 56, 60, 65, 73, 80, 89). `|k_p − 2.0| ≤ 0.2`
  per program; `|median k_p − 2.0| ≤ 0.1`. *Why:* every failure mode is far outside (bare 1.6;
  any floor with X ≥ 0.2 fits ≤ ~1.2; layer double-count reads ≥ ~2.4), while
  velocity-tracked brightness adds only ~0.05–0.15 of legitimate slope to an RMS measurement.
  The S-YXG50's own 1.96–2.05 spread shows the law is this tight in practice.
- **AC3 — layer guard.** For layered sampled programs: every probe point's residual from the
  fitted line ≤ 1.0 dB, and level strictly increasing across V, including across the 51/52,
  79/80, 95/96 boundaries. *Why 1.0 dB:* an uncompensated raw pp↔mf recording step is ≥ 3 dB;
  fit scatter from round-robin jitter is ~0.3 dB. This is what catches double-counting.
- **AC4 — exemptions.** `cathedral_organ_steady_level_is_velocity_independent` passes
  **unmodified**. GM6 asserted separately: 72→110 span < 3 dB (the `vel_sense` contract).
  Both excluded from AC2's probe set, with a comment naming this doc.
- **AC5 — layer selection pinned.** For v ∈ {51, 52, 79, 80, 95, 96} each bank function
  returns the same bank identity as today. Structural, cheap, pins the constraint forever.
- **AC6 — drum law and symmetry.** Same harness on drum keys {36, 38, 42, 45, 46, 49, 51}:
  `|k_d − 2.0| ≤ 0.2` per key, and `|median k_d − median k_p| ≤ 0.15`. *Why 0.15:* both
  medians derive from one function and the two hardware references' medians differ by only
  0.016, while the old asymmetry (1.6 vs 0.66) exceeds the bound six-fold.
- **AC7 — fortissimo anchor** (render-diff step, not a unit test). Baseline binary from the
  branch's base commit in a throwaway worktree; per-program v=127 probe level within ±0.25 dB
  of baseline. Documented exceptions only: GM5 (fold rounding ≤ 0.1 dB) and acoustic guitars
  (LA anchor rework ≤ ~1 dB). Plus the standard render-diff inventory: every album with
  v<127 content diffs (expected, total); a v=127-only probe and cathedral-organ-solo material
  must be **byte-identical**.
- **AC8 — end-to-end reference cross-check** (offline, once). Re-run the velcurve probe on the
  fixed build: ferro median k ∈ [1.9, 2.1]; ferro−SC55 differential within ±1.5 dB per point
  for v ∈ {48…110}, ±2 dB at v=32, v ≤ 16 excluded. Tolerance from the references' own mutual
  disagreement (up to ~0.9 dB at these points) plus probe noise.

## 6. Decision log

- **Exponent 2.0, not a lookup of the SC-55 curve** — both references are the square law
  within 0.22 dB for v ≥ 32; below v=24 they disagree with each other, so there is no second
  target worth modelling.
- **Pure power law below v=32** (no SC-55-style low-end lift) — simplicity; matches the
  S-YXG50; revisit only on corpus evidence.
- **Fix at the voice layer, not the strip** — the strip cannot see velocity; the defect lives
  where velocity is consumed.
- **Delete level floors rather than re-anchor them at v=100** — anchoring adds per-site
  constants and raises v=127 levels (clipping risk); plain deletion keeps v=127 bit-stable.
- **Keep the three timbre floors** — they are the legitimate home of "quiet notes keep body".
- **`vel_sense` is the only sanctioned reduced-depth mechanism**, sole user GM6 — mirrors
  hardware's global-curve × per-patch-sensitivity split.
- **Drums get no special treatment** — the shared exponent *is* the symmetry.
- **Layer thresholds and sample assets untouched**; composite law corrected at caller gain
  constants only.

## 7. What this does to the existing albums

Almost every note below v=127 gets quieter. The exceptions are the deliberately
velocity-independent cathedral organ and the compressed GM6 harpsichord, which barely move.
The committed `.mid` files need **no edits** — the synth becomes correct under them. Note
this is a *policy* (synth-side defects are fixed synth-side) rather than a proof: albums
authored while monitoring the old curve may carry velocities that compensated for it, so a
per-album CC re-touch remains a plausible follow-up.

Magnitudes: bare-law voices (including all drums) shift by `8·log10(v/127)` dB — −0.5 at
v=110, −1.0 at 96, −2.4 at 64, −4.8 at 32. Floored voices shift more; the figures −1.4 at
110, −2.7 at 96, −7.5 at 64 and −11.3 at 48 are for the `0.4 + 0.6` pair, which covers 11 of
the 14 sites — the other pairs differ, and the 0.55+0.45 sites move about half as far.

After the −18 LUFS normalize, loud sections come *up* correspondingly: dynamics widen to
authored size. **Expect the first renders to read drums relatively more forward at mezzo
dynamics** — that is the floored voices losing their inflation, not a drum change, and the
static answer is the companion `DRUM_FORWARD` work. Per-album CC re-touches are
composition-layer follow-ups, exactly the class already accepted on 17 July.

## 8. Risks / tripwires — stop and re-escalate

- **T1** — a sampled program's fitted k lands ≥ 2.4: embedded layer loudness is
  double-counting. Compensate at the caller gain constant; if compensation would exceed 6 dB,
  **stop** — that is an asset problem, not a constant.
- **T2** — `guitar_low_velocity_seam_continuity` cannot be satisfied by any
  `GUITAR_VEL_LEVEL_EXP`: the model's energy law changed *shape*, not just slope. The LA law
  design needs revisiting, not a bigger constant.
- **T3** — a **foreign** GM file sounds over-dynamic where an SC-55 render of the same file
  does not: suspect the measurement chain, not the law. Our own albums reading over-dynamic
  is *expected* and routes to the re-mix lane, never back to floors.
- **T4** — judging drum balance from this change's renders alone: forbidden. The drum verdict
  waits for `DRUM_FORWARD` recalibration on top of this law.
- **T5** — any diff in v=127-only or cathedral-organ-only material beyond the documented GM5
  and guitar exceptions: a bug, full stop.
- **T6** — a re-pinned test whose shift does *not* match the analytic law delta for its site
  and velocity within ~0.3 dB: you are re-pinning a bug. Stop and diagnose.
- **T7** — Arthur ear-rejects k=2 wholesale after living with it: that contradicts the
  standing corpus-fidelity decision and is his call to re-make. The resolution would be a
  render-side option, never per-voice floors returning.

## 11. External review — Codex (`gpt-5.6-sol`, xhigh), one pass

Deliberately outside the lead's model family. **Degraded run:** this machine's
`codex-windows-sandbox-setup.exe` is missing, so Codex could not read any repo file; it
correctly refused to review rather than fabricate, and the pass was re-run with the design
inlined. It could therefore review the *judgement* and the acceptance criteria, but could not
independently verify any `file:line` claim. **Residual risk: no external reviewer has checked
this design against the actual source.**

Verdict on the core: *"The core change is basically right… preserving the old defect because
135 tracks depend on it would be the wrong architectural choice."*

Accepted and folded in:

- **The oracle must use the same estimator as the evidence.** AC2 originally specified
  unweighted RMS while the reference measurement used BS.1770 momentary loudness. Codex
  correctly noted these diverge once velocity changes spectrum — which the *retained* timbre
  floors do by design. The test harness now uses `momentary_lufs`. Measuring a fix with a
  different instrument from the one that found the defect is how you get a green suite and a
  wrong synth.
- **AC8 must compare normalised curves** `ΔL(v) = L(v) − L(127)`, not absolute levels;
  otherwise an unrelated program-gain offset can fail a correct implementation.
- **Assert per key, never averaged** — averaging two keys hides one fitting low and one high.
- **AC3 must sample across the layer seams.** The shared fit set contains neither 51/52 nor
  95/96, so as written it could not see a seam at all. Now uses a dedicated seam set.
- **16 programs cannot prove "every velocity-sensitive voice"** — `voices.rs:3861` carries its
  own `powf` literal, so a voice can bypass the shared helper entirely. Added a full
  128-program sweep (AC2b); it runs in ~15 s and immediately surfaced GM24/25 at k=2.26 and
  GM109 at k=0.00, neither of which the 16-program probe would have caught.
- **§7 overclaimed.** "Every note below v=127 gets quieter" excludes the velocity-independent
  organ and compressed GM6; the quoted deltas describe one floor pair, not all six.
- **T3 was dogmatic.** A foreign GM file sounding over-dynamic does not *imply* measurement
  failure — investigate, do not prejudge.

Noted, not adopted: a demand to pre-specify holdout keys and stop conditions for sampled-bank
constant fitting. The simpler answer that the same review implies is to **fit no constants in
this task at all** — measure, and classify any sampled bank that misses the law rather than
tuning it until the judging test goes green.
