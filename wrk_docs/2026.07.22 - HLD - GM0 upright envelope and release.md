# HLD — GM0 upright envelope and release (MM-BUG-KILN-00042)

**Status: SELF-APPROVED**

## Goal and scope

Make the default GM0 VSCO upright carry the Ferrosinthesis Reference Audition's
short piano phrase coherently: normalize slow amplitude-envelope differences
between its round robins and neighbouring sample zones, then add only the GM0
piano release needed to bridge the phrase's 62.5 ms key-up gaps.

- Condition all 54 default upright assets (nine zones × three velocity layers ×
  two round robins) as one bank.
- Preserve each recording's hammer transient, spectrum, pitch, and natural
  post-attack decay slope.
- Calibrate release after the conditioned bank is rendered, not against the
  inconsistent source takes.
- Keep the change deterministic and generator-owned.

### Non-goals

- Rewriting the FRA MIDI, adding pedal/legato controllers, or changing note
  scheduling.
- Making GM0 monophonic or suppressing legitimate re-articulation.
- Conditioning or retuning GM1, GM3, GM0 alternate-grand, orchestral, or other
  LA sample banks.
- Spectral EQ, transient synthesis, compression, a runtime feature flag, or a new
  dependency.
- Matching the SC-55 or S-YXG50 timbre. Their release behaviour is a calibration
  reference, not a request to clone either instrument.

## Diagnosis

The FRA opening piano figure is D4/F#4/A4 at velocity 104 and 96 BPM. Each note
lasts 312.5 ms and the next starts 375 ms later, leaving 62.5 ms of key-up time.
There is no sustain pedal or CC68. Voice-seed parity selects C4/G4 zones and
alternates the G4 round robin on the second and third notes.

The current bank is onset-trimmed and peak-normalized one file at a time. That
does not normalize time shape. Across the forte takes, the 8–38 ms attack versus
100–180 ms early-body RMS ratio ranges by more than 20 dB; several same-zone
round robins differ by 4–9 dB. The delivered Ferro phrase then loses 16–23 dB
during its short gaps, while a matched retained SC-55 probe loses about 6 dB.

Two independent causes therefore stack:

1. source takes expose different attack/body balance as the phrase crosses a
   zone and round-robin boundary;
2. both the sampled onset and GM0 model use very short note-off releases
   (60 ms and 100 ms T60 respectively), so each new key arrives after the prior
   voice has largely cleared.

## Approach

### 1. Deterministic bank-level macro-envelope conditioning

prepare.py will keep trimming each source exactly as today, but defer writes for
the 54 piano-prefixed segments until the complete bank is available.

Across all 54 takes:

1. Detect the onset again in the trimmed segment with the existing 3-percent
   rule, anchored to the untouched first 40 ms rather than the whole take. Then
   measure attack/body dB as RMS from 0–30 ms after onset divided by RMS from
   140–220 ms after onset. Onset-relative windows handle the source files whose
   pre-roll clamps below the nominal eight milliseconds, while the early peak
   anchor prevents a raised body from moving the onset threshold.
2. Fit one minimax straight register trend through all three velocity layers,
   both round robins, and all nine roots. Cap its slope at 0.26 dB per semitone,
   then choose the intercept that minimizes the largest residual. One shared
   target prevents a timbral layer change from becoming an amplitude-envelope
   jump while limiting root-to-root movement to a gradual 1.82 dB.
3. For each take, compute the body gain that makes its ratio meet the shared
   trend. Apply unity gain through 40 ms after onset, a smoothstep ramp from
   40–140 ms, then hold the gain. This leaves the hammer waveform untouched
   apart from a later uniform file scale and leaves the post-140 ms decay slope
   unchanged.
4. Treat 13 dB as an emergency generation stop, not usable headroom. The
   measured minimax solution reaches 12.55 dB; the previous 12 dB stop could not
   represent one whole-bank envelope without splitting the velocity layers. A positive
   correction must also stay below the take-specific gain that would make any
   post-150 ms peak overtake the first 150 ms attack/body event. A 20 ms envelope
   scan rejects a ramp step above 4.5 dB when conditioning adds more than 2 dB
   to the source's own adjacent-window movement.
5. Shape consistency alone is insufficient: equal ratios can have different
   absolute RMS because recording crest factors differ. Fit one robust absolute
   body-level trend across the root medians after shaping, shared by all three
   layers, and uniformly scale every take to it. Shift that whole trend down if
   needed so no file exceeds 0.9; do not independently peak-normalize the files
   again. This gives round robins and adjacent zones coherent absolute attack
   and body levels while runtime velocity remains the loudness law.

This is slow envelope shaping only. It never filters, mixes, stretches, or
resynthesizes a recording. The piano-only bake and a full bake take the same
path; every other family retains its present immediate-write path.

### 2. Measure the conditioned phrase before changing release

Regenerate only the default upright, build the synth, and render the exact FRA
opening. Record each note's short-gap decay and compare it with the pre-change
Ferro and retained SC-55 measurements.

If the conditioned bank already loses at most 10 dB over the 62.5 ms gap, stop:
release code is unnecessary. If it still clears faster, proceed with the narrow
GM0 release below. The envelope and release changes remain separately measured
in the journal even if they land in one shipped-code commit.

### 3. GM0-only damped release

Use one calibrated release T60 for both owners of a GM0 note:

- the acoustic_piano model's note-off decay;
- the default-inert LaVoice sample note-off multiplier, selected only by the
  explicit default program-0 acoustic-grand route.

Start from 0.60 s because an exponential T60 of 0.60 s falls about 6.25 dB over
62.5 ms, matching the retained SC-55 probe before the model's natural decay.
Treat that value only as an initial estimate: intrinsic recording/model decay
and the moving LA weights also affect the audible fall. Tune once from dry,
direct conditioned-GM0 fixtures to meet the acceptance window, then use the full
FRA render as listening evidence.

Thread an explicit optional note-off T60 through acoustic_grand_with_bank and a
new LaVoice wrapping entry point into the internal builder. The program-0 default
arm alone passes the calibrated value, including its modeled-only path. GM0
alternate-bank calls, their undefined-CC0 fallback, GM1 Bright, GM3 Honky-tonk,
and every existing LaVoice caller pass no override and therefore keep the 100 ms
model / 60 ms sample defaults. This avoids using the non-bright flag as a proxy
for the default bank: that flag is also false for every GM0 alternate.

The conditioned bank also needs its default-GM0 handoff recalibrated against the
unchanged model. Its matched settings are sample gain 4.0 and a 180–450 ms
sample-to-model fade. The forte recordings are 4.9 dB louder than the shared
unweighted macro envelope through the repository's K-weighted velocity oracle,
so the default sampled route trims both sample and model owners by the same
factor at the v95/v96 layer boundary. This preserves the handoff and release
shape while retaining the global square velocity law. Alternate banks retain
their legacy gain, fade, and release.

The calibrated release is 0.45 s. This remains ordinary key-up damping, not
pedal sustain: direct sampled fixtures lose 4.13–6.77 dB over 62.5 ms, modeled
fixtures lose 7.32–8.12 dB, every path is at least 28.53 dB down by 250 ms, and
all voices clear the 2 s reap probe.

## Acceptance criteria

1. A piano-only bake routes all 54 default-upright takes through the conditioner;
   two consecutive bakes produce identical hashes for all 54 WAVs. A full bake
   uses the same path, and a tracked-asset inventory proves that no non-piano
   asset changes.
2. Across all velocity layers, conditioned take ratios are within 0.35 dB of
   one fitted gradual register line and same-zone round robins differ by at most
   0.35 dB. Absolute 140–220 ms body RMS is also within 0.35 dB of the shared
   register trend across round robins, adjacent roots, and layer boundaries;
   attack RMS follows from the shape ratio. The two low-pp duplicate sources do
   not count as sole evidence: every non-duplicate pair and synthetic unequal
   fixtures must pass.
3. Conditioning changes no sample before 40 ms after detected onset except one
   uniform per-file scale, never lets a post-150 ms peak overtake the first
   150 ms event, applies no correction above 13 dB, and introduces no 20 ms ramp
   step above 4.5 dB when that is more than 2 dB beyond the source movement. It
   preserves length, channel count, 44.1 kHz rate, pitch, and post-140 ms decay
   slope; every peak is at most 0.9.
4. A dry direct-voice oracle covers D4/F#4/A4 at velocity 104 and both round
   robins. It compares equal 20 ms RMS windows immediately before note-off and
   over 42.5–62.5 ms after note-off: each voice falls 4–10 dB and round-robin
   spread is at most 2 dB. A separate engine-level three-note fixture proves the
   default route and RR order and pins the same gap to at most 10 dB after the
   real dry-send/master path. The full FRA render remains listening evidence.
5. Without CC64, sampled and modeled-only GM0 voices at low, middle, and high
   keys are at least 24 dB below note-off level after 250 ms and reach the
   existing dead/reap threshold within 2.0 s. CC64 and all-notes/sound-off
   semantics remain unchanged.
6. The calibrated T60 is 0.45 s. GM0 alternate banks (including an
   undefined-CC0 fallback), GM1, GM3, and a non-piano LA canary render
   byte-identically in focused tests. Only default GM0 sampled and modeled paths
   opt into the coherent release.
7. Runtime sample-body seam checks span all physical roots, both sides of every
   zone boundary, all three velocity layers, and both round robins; each adjacent
   boundary stays within 3 dB. Source-time attack checks cover every take, while
   the existing composite attack-dominance checks retain representative low/high
   runtime coverage. This split keeps pitch-shifted spectral differences out of
   an amplitude-envelope oracle. Existing la_level_continuity, zone/fade-budget,
   velocity-layer seam, and deterministic-render tests also pass. Any
   piano handoff gain affected by conditioning is recalibrated rather than
   weakening an oracle.
8. The required all-MIDI render-diff uses a binary built from this branch's
   exact base commit and reports zero contamination. Every NOT REACHED row is
   triaged explicitly: its static scanner does not model event timing or CC0 bank
   selection, and a low-rate probe may deliberately exceed the sample repitch
   guard. The FRA key track is regenerated locally for listening and metric evidence.
9. Focused Python and Rust tests, the full workspace test suite, cargo fmt check,
   and workspace lint pass with closed stdin and bounded runtimes.

## Decision log

- **Normalize shape and absolute level:** static gain cannot fix attack/body
  differences, but matching only their ratio still leaves crest-factor-driven
  RMS steps. The bank needs one shape trend and one absolute body-level trend.
- **One whole-bank shape fit:** the first implementation retained separate
  dynamic-layer trends, but the forte target produced a 3.42 dB v95/v96 jump.
  A single target across all layers preserves their spectra while removing the
  amplitude-envelope discontinuity.
- **Slope-bounded minimax shape trend, robust linear level trend:** the measured
  C6 acceleration made the first robust line demand +13.65 dB, while a quadratic
  retained abrupt zone movement. A minimax intercept keeps every correction under
  13 dB, and the 0.26 dB/semitone slope cap makes the trend genuinely gradual.
  Absolute body level remains a robust line.
- **Onset-relative scalar envelope after 40 ms:** the hammer and spectrum are
  identity cues; only slow body level needs correction. A smooth 100 ms ramp and
  first-150 ms peak ceiling prevent the conditioner manufacturing a late bloom.
- **Generator-owned assets:** editing WAVs without the bake rule would make the
  next regeneration restore the defect.
- **Condition before release:** release calibration against inconsistent takes
  would hide bank defects in a runtime tail.
- **One GM0 T60 for sample and model:** the crossfade has one audible owner at
  each instant; mismatched note-off laws would make release depend on note age.
- **Calibrate handoff and perceived layer level separately:** shared unweighted
  envelopes do not make a brighter forte recording K-weighted-level neutral.
  A matched owner trim fixes that bank boundary without weakening the existing
  attack, seam, or velocity-law oracles.
- **No release feature flag:** GM0 program semantics are the product change, not
  an optional experiment.
- **Explicit default-bank release opt-in:** CC0 selects recordings, so it would
  be aesthetically defensible for every GM0 bank to share articulation. The
  available evidence calibrates only the default upright, however; preserving
  alternate byte identity is the smaller correct change.
- **No MIDI rewrite:** the dry, unpedalled articulation is valid input and
  exposes a synth defect.

## Adversarial review record

Two independent reviewers critiqued the draft from the sample-conditioning and
release/routing sides. Each then received the other's findings and checked them
against the cited code before a finding could survive.

Surviving findings folded above:

- expand seam and attack coverage from two GM0 fixtures to every root, layer,
  round robin, and zone edge;
- guard the slow correction ramp against creating a secondary swell;
- make the dry per-voice release windows, seeds, and round robins executable,
  with the mixed FRA render used only for listening;
- prove two-bake determinism and inventory non-piano assets;
- treat the fixed source-time metric as a proxy and make repitched runtime
  root/edge checks authoritative;
- keep uncalibrated GM0 alternate banks on their existing release;
- name the 2.0 s reap bound and require explicit render-diff NOT REACHED triage.

Refuted after code inspection:

- A longer configured release would also lengthen GM0 legato/retrigger transient
  retirement. The wrapped Modal piano returns false from both optional voice
  methods, so only note-off applies the configured multiplier.
- The 0.60 s estimate was presented as a final audible decay. The draft already
  made it a starting value; the wording now states the intrinsic-decay and
  crossfade qualification more directly.

## Cross-family review record

One fresh Claude Opus review ran read-only in plan mode with no subagents. It
confirmed the diagnosis, sample ownership at FRA note-off, and the two internal
refutations, then found one critical routing ambiguity and several useful oracle
gaps.

Significant/useful findings folded above:

- non-bright is not a default-bank selector: all GM0 alternate calls also pass
  false, so release must be an explicit argument authored only by the default
  program-0 arm;
- shape-ratio equality cannot prove absolute round-robin level parity, so add a
  shared absolute body-level trend and same-root/layer checks;
- positive correction must stay under each take's no-late-peak ceiling, and the
  existing velocity-layer seam oracle must remain green;
- the engine-level dry path must carry an executable at-most-10 dB gap criterion,
  not merely routing evidence;
- state the 0.45–0.625 s tuning interval implied by the short-gap and 250 ms
  clear-tail requirements;
- make analysis windows onset-relative, guard both rise and droop during the
  ramp, and prove non-piano-only modes are no-ops.

Rejected or narrowed with rationale:

- Applying one articulation to every GM0 alternate is musically defensible, but
  it requires calibration evidence for those recordings. This task keeps them
  byte-identical.
- Independently re-peak-normalizing every conditioned file was rejected because
  it recreates crest-factor-driven RMS steps. A common headroom shift preserves
  the fitted level trend instead.
- The review predicted an attack-dominance failure arithmetically but could not
  measure it in read-only mode. It is treated as a real risk with take-specific
  headroom and bank-wide/runtime guards, not recorded as a demonstrated failure.

Because the external pass classified the original routing ambiguity as critical,
one targeted re-review checked only the revised override path against the default,
alternate, fallback, and GM1 call sites. It returned RESOLVED: the default GM0
arm alone authors the calibrated override and every other call retains None.

## Residual risks

- Envelope and level trends are useful oracles, not a listening verdict. The
  conditioner can still make a technically smooth piano feel too hard or soft;
  runtime seams, the engine FRA metric, and listening must catch a poor balance.
- A longer default-GM0 release changes dense piano writing even though alternate
  banks remain inert. Render-diff gives the blast-radius inventory; focused
  long-gap and polyphony/reap checks guard against pseudo-pedal smearing.
- Default and alternate GM0 banks intentionally retain different key-up damping
  until the alternates receive their own calibration evidence.
- The SC-55 probe is retained calibration evidence. No fresh S-YXG50 capture is
  available, so equality with that device remains Arthur's listening comparison.
