# HLD — driven-guitar amp+cabinet voicing: give the CC0 alt bank its own lead amp/cab

**Author:** Claude Opus 4.8 (1M) · **Date:** 2026-07-23 · **Area:** ferrosintesis synth
**Task branch:** `task/20260723-TSK-HUM-driven-guitar-alt-bank-lead-amp-cab-voic`
**Consulted:** Fable 5 (imaginative) and gpt-5.6-sol at `max` (convergent, cross-family)

## 1. The problem (Arthur's ear)

> "GM029 main sounds the same as GM029 alt — need more difference" — and the same for GM030.
> The leads also sound **thin**.

## 2. Why they sounded alike — measured, not assumed

The channel insert `Drive` (`crates/ferrosintesis/src/engine.rs`) is a program-keyed amp+cab:
pre-HPF → biased asymmetric tanh (g1, bias) → interstage tilt EQ → second tanh (g2) → DC
blocker → 5-biquad cabinet with a cone-breakup ripple FIR (`MicroCab`), the nonlinear path
oversampled 2×.

`alt` (CC0 bank ≠ 0 → the sustaining `DRIVE_LEAD` string) changed **only the `MicroCab` ripple
depth**. Measured on the unmodified baseline, feeding identical input through the two inserts:

| baseline, lead − main | prog 29 | prog 30 |
|---|---|---|
| body-vs-presence tilt (800−2800 Hz) | +1.17 dB | +1.08 dB |
| low-end focus (100−800 Hz) | **+0.05 dB** | **+0.20 dB** |
| fizz (6000−800 Hz) | −0.65 dB | −0.56 dB |

A low-end delta of **0.05 dB** is the cleanest statement of the defect: the two banks ran the
*same amp*. Everything that distinguished them lived on the string side.

**And the string side does not rescue it.** The main-vs-alt *sustain* gap is large and grows
with pitch (14 → 60 dB over keys 45–69). But a lead line's notes last a few hundred
milliseconds, so nobody ever hears that tail — over the span that is actually audible, both
banks went through one amp. That is the whole complaint, and it is why the fix belongs at the
amp/cab layer.

## 3. Design

Keep the architecture; enrich it. No new DSP primitive, no impulse-response convolution —
`Biquad`, the existing tanh stages and `MicroCab` only.

**`Drive::new` gains an `alt` branch** returning a complete lead voicing (own gain staging,
voicing EQ and cabinet). `from_stages` now takes the cabinet array and the pre-shaper high-pass
corner as parameters. **The main bank is untouched and stays bit-identical** — verified by
byte-comparing six probe renders (§7), not merely asserted.

### 3.1 The three levers that make a lead amp

Both consults independently corrected the first draft here, and the corrections are the design:

1. **FOCUS — cut lows *before* the shaper** (`pre` 90 → 120 Hz). The biggest single lever and
   the one the draft missed. Low end entering a clipper eats headroom and smears
   intermodulation across every harmonic; that mush is generated *inside* the nonlinearity, so
   no post-cab EQ removes it. 120 rather than 130 Hz is a deliberate compromise — 12.4% of every
   alt-bank note in the repo is E2–B2, and those lose ≈7.5 dB of fundamental at this corner.
   **This is the one number most likely to need Arthur's ear** (§9).
2. **BODY — carried by the cabinet**, because at these operating points both tanh stages are
   deeply saturated, so the pre-clip EQ mostly decides *which partials dominate the clipper*
   rather than acting as tone control. The cab is the only truly linear tone stage in the chain,
   so curing "thin" is a cab job.
3. **A DARKER feed into stage 2.** The draft pushed +4 dB at 2.3 kHz into the second shaper for
   "cut". That is the recipe for **fizz**: boosting presence into a clipper multiplies its
   harmonic/IMD skirt. Real lead channels go darker into the last stage and add presence
   *after*, linearly. So `tilt` moved down to 1400 Hz / +2.5 dB and all presence lives in the cab.

**Explicitly NOT a lever:** extra `g2` does not buy sustain. Amp compression lengthens a
*decaying* note by holding it at the knee, but the alt bank's string is the e-bow sustainer
(`DRIVE_LEAD`, `sustain: 0.6`) which already holds at constant level. Both consults flagged this
independently; the draft's rationale was simply wrong. `g2` is raised only modestly, for edge
thickness.

### 3.2 Shipped values

| | 30 lead (distortion) | 29 lead (overdrive) |
|---|---|---|
| pre-HPF | 120 Hz | 120 Hz |
| g1 / g2 | 9.0 / 3.4 | 8.0 / 2.8 |
| bias | 0.85 | 0.85 |
| `voice` (pre-clip) | peak(650, 1.0, **−4.5**) — scoop | peak(1000, 0.8, **+4.0**) — push |
| `tilt` (into stage 2) | peak(800, 0.9, −2.0) | peak(1400, 0.8, +2.5) |
| `post` | 0.0714 | 0.0789 |
| cabinet | body +4.5 @600, presence +3.0 @2600 | body +2.5 @600, presence +5.0 @2800 |

Shared lead cab elements: low resonance peak(120, 1.0, +2.0); anti-alias cliff
lowpass(4000, 0.9) + lowpass(3800, 0.8) — **identical to the rhythm cab**.

**`bias` is deliberately 0.85 on both.** 0.8 vs 0.9 was tempting as a 29≠30 axis, but Sol
quantified that dropping to 0.8 shrinks the duty asymmetry generating H2 against floors
carrying only 1.4–4.1 dB of margin. Measured at 0.85, 29's H2 moves −18.5 → −18.2 dBc and 30's
*improves* −23.3 → −19.0 dBc. Taking the caution cost nothing.

**"Air" was considered and dropped.** Raising the cab cliff for sparkle is the least valuable
lever (on a driven guitar 5–7 kHz is cone fizz, not air) and is the first thing to retreat on if
alias margin tightens. Both consults agreed; the cliff is unchanged.

### 3.3 Why the lead cabinet is keyed on program (and the rhythm cab is not)

The first cut shared one lead cab and **failed**: 30's lead sat only 0.68 dB from its own main
at keys 63/65. 30's lead deliberately keeps a deep pre-clip scoop — that is what makes it 30 and
not 29 — which cancels a shared cab's body fill. It also creates a constraint clash: 30's lead
must differ from 30's *main* (wants more mid body) **and** from 29's *lead* (on that axis, less).
Per-program lead cabs resolve both: 30's puts back more body and less presence, 29's the
reverse.

### 3.4 The 29-vs-30 main sharpening — considered, declined

Optional per the brief. In the main bank the two already sit 6.62 dB apart on the 600:2000 mid
axis. Sharpening would break the bit-identical property and move every main-bank album track.
**Left untouched.**

## 4. Oracles

The synth is oracle-first and this box has no ears, so the numeric contract *is* the design.
Layered deliberately, after both consults rejected the draft's "presence-band ratio + spectral
centroid" as too correlated and too gameable (both are brightness detectors; centroid can rise
*because* a voice got thin). Centroid is not used at all.

| oracle | what it pins | RED at HEAD? |
|---|---|---|
| `alt_drive_is_a_distinct_lead_voicing` (engine) | **The contract.** Same input through both inserts — no string, no samples. Signed, bounded body-vs-presence tilt, low-end focus, and anti-fizz. | **Yes — all 6 clauses** |
| `alt_bank_leads_stay_distinct_from_each_other` (engine) | 29≠30 *inside* the alt bank: spectral distance, push-vs-scoop direction, gain staging | n/a (new axis) |
| `driven_banks_diverge_at_lead_pitches` (engine) | End-to-end integration: level-normalised 6-band spectral distance, keys 57–69 | integration guard |
| `driven_main_and_alt_banks_diverge` (voices) | String-side sustain/onset contrast, **extended to keys 57/62/69** | pre-existing |
| `drive_alias_floor` | **extended to alt**, 3 fundamentals × both programs | safety |
| `drive_asymmetry_and_dc` | **extended to 16 points**, both banks, two drive levels | safety |
| `driven_sustain_stays_distorted` | floors kept; **new −12 dBc harshness CEILING** | safety |

Every bound is two-sided. A floor with no ceiling invites ratchet-creep ("more presence is
better") until the lead is a caricature; the ceilings stop that.

**Three metric traps found by measuring, recorded so they are not re-walked:**
- A **signed two-band ratio through the full engine does not work** — it swung −1.7 to +8.8 dB
  across adjacent keys for a *fixed* pair of amps, because at these fundamentals it mostly
  measures which harmonics land in which band, and the two banks also play different strings.
  Replaced with a direction-agnostic multi-band *distance*, scored as a register mean plus a
  per-key non-collapse floor.
- **Band sets must reach below 200 Hz.** The first calibration script started at 200 Hz and was
  structurally blind to the pre-HPF and low-resonance moves — it reported the change as doing
  almost nothing (+0.08 dB). With bands from 80 Hz the same comparison reads +0.80 dB.
- **Deep saturation is legitimately odd-harmonic dominated**, for the shipped rhythm amps too
  (H2−H3 at amp 0.5: 29-main −14.4 dB, 30-main −15.6). So "H2 must exceed a tenth of H3" only
  tests the asymmetry mechanism at *moderate* drive. Asserted where the claim actually lives.

## 5. Measured result

Same input through both inserts (the amp, isolated):

| lead − main | 29 baseline | 29 new | 30 baseline | 30 new |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| body-vs-presence tilt | +1.17 | **+2.93** | +1.08 | **+6.62** |
| low-end focus | +0.05 | **−5.40** | +0.20 | **−7.13** |
| fizz | −0.65 | **−2.45** | −0.56 | **−5.44** |

On musical probes (un-normalised, 6-band level-normalised distance): main↔alt separation rose
**2.60 → 3.40 dB mean, on every probe**; the alt voice itself moved **3.22 dB mean** (up to
5.12); the main bank measured **bit-identical** on all six.

Level: the lead is matched to its own rhythm amp to **0.01 dB** on a 300–3400 Hz band at the
loud operating point, so the A/B isolates timbre. This is deliberately **not** a loudness lift —
the ledger records Arthur discarding a +6 dB GM29/30 trim (KILN-00042), to be re-derived
separately.

## 6. Interaction with KILN-00042 / 00049 — not disturbed

KILN-00042 gave main `DRIVE` a register-scaled `DamperHold::Derived`; `DRIVE_LEAD` is excluded
(`Off`, KILN-00049). Nothing here touches either: no preset field changed, and the main bank is
bit-identical. The amp/cab axis is purely additive.

## 7. Render-diff inventory — clean, and fully explained

Baseline built from `origin/main` (c75f9d2) in a throwaway worktree; all 124 album MIDIs
rendered with both binaries (`--program 29 --program 30`).

```
EXPECTED  changed :  11
EXPECTED  same    :  87
CONTAMINATION     :   0   <-- the one that matters
NOT REACHED       :  26
```

**The prediction was exact.** Because the main bank is bit-identical, only tracks that *sound* a
driven guitar on a nonzero-CC0 bank may move. An independent scan of all 124 MIDIs finds exactly
**11** such files — the ten `fable5/Slipstream` tracks plus `fable5/Through Lines/16 -
Three-Sixty-One` (itself the track cited in the `MICRO_CAB_LEAD_DEPTH` doc comment, so its
alt-bank use is expected) — and exactly those 11 changed. No track moved that should not have,
and none failed to move that should have.

The 26 **NOT REACHED** are the expected harness artifact, not a defect: `render_diff` classifies
by *sounded GM program*, not by bank, so every main-bank-only driven-guitar track (Big Weather,
Hollow Hill, Seven Kinds of Sunlight, and others) is predicted-changed yet must render
byte-identical — which is precisely the bit-identity property this change promises.

## 8. Verified tripwires

- **Chords through a hotter lead amp → intermodulation** (Sol). Measured: Slipstream's alt
  channels are **monophonic** — max polyphony 2, 80–100% strictly single-note. Cleared.
- **Alias margin** (Fable: retreat if under 6 dB). Measured over 3 fundamentals × both programs:
  worst −54.4 dBc against a −40 bar — **14.4 dB margin**, and the alt is no worse than the main.
- **THD too hot / fuzzy.** Worst row −15.5 dBc against the new −12 dBc ceiling.
- **Alt bank re-voiced under an existing album** (Fable). Live: Slipstream's character *does*
  change. This is the intended improvement, but it means Slipstream needs a listen, which is
  why `03 - Hammerhead` is the Set C A/B track.

## 9. Open question for Arthur

**The 120 Hz pre-shaper high-pass on the lead.** It is the main "focus" lever, but it thins the
12.4% of alt-bank notes that sit at E2–B2 by ≈7.5 dB of fundamental. `low_29_alt` / `low_30_alt`
in the A/B set exist precisely so this can be judged. If low leads sound too light, this is the
first retreat (120 → 105 Hz), not the cab.
