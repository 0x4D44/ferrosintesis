### Oracles — the derived-oracle discipline is compounding

**147 test functions added, 0 removed.** Test count across `crates/`: **733 → 880 (+20 %)**, plus
a new libFuzzer harness at `fuzz/fuzz_targets/parse.rs`.

Six new `#[cfg(test)]`-only modules (3,641 lines, zero public API):

- **`balance.rs`** — `PROGRAM_TRIM_DB` reaches the audio at exactly its tabled value. Two
  oracles: the dB→gain conversion is exact **and** the channel strip applies it. A correct
  conversion nothing calls is a silent no-op.
- **`inventory.rs`** — every packaged WAV is accounted for by a counted row in its own crate's
  `PROVENANCE.md`. Derived from the filesystem.
- **`render_profile.rs`** — `Options::default()` is the single definition of the shipping
  profile; the entry-point set is re-derived, not listed.
- **`payload.rs`** — no shipped document states a stale payload figure. Reads the feature list
  as *text* so it still asserts under `--no-default-features`.
- **`provenance.rs`** — every committed upstream source is pinned by a SHA-256 recorded in a
  packaged document. 53 of 74 had none. Ships a dependency-free SHA-256.
- **`parse_robustness.rs`** — every `MidiError` variant reachable with its correct payload from a
  minimal corrupted SMF. Coverage derived from an exhaustive match, so a new variant **fails to
  compile** until a fixture exists. `is_err()` is deliberately never used.

Two are explicitly **non-vacuity** tests — `the_curve_oracle_rejects_a_flat_damper` and
`the_oracles_reject_the_prose_they_were_written_to_catch`. That is the "write the adversarial
document that *should* fail your oracle" discipline CLAUDE.md calls for, actually applied. The
prewarm oracle was also re-founded after KILN-00073 showed its `pub fn *_bank` predicate was a
hand-maintained assumption wearing a source-scan's clothes.

### The largest source-of-truth change

**`ff3928c fix(albums): clamp stale same-pitch overlaps (KILN-00056)`** — 49 files, 30 committed
