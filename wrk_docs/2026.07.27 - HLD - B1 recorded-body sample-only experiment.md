# B1 recorded-body sample-only experiment

Status: SELF-APPROVED

## Intent

Determine whether the Yamaha B1 capture sounds natural when its recorded hammer and
early string/soundboard body play without either offline handoff conditioning or the
generic randomized modal piano.

## Scope

- Keep the existing 25 normal and 27 hard zones, the velocity-59/60 layer split,
  measured roots, high-pass, resampling, peak normalization, and runtime attack gain.
- Re-bake 1.5 seconds of each B1 body without the handoff conditioner and with only a
  10 ms end taper.
- Route samples-on B1 notes through a bounded whole-note sample voice. The recording
  owns the complete voice until the asset ends; note-off applies the existing
  register-dependent felt-damper T60.
- Keep the samples-off modal piano, VSCO first alternate, every other GM0 alternate,
  CC64 handling, sympathetic bus, and non-GM0 paths unchanged.
- Produce direct/dry capture-chain controls plus normal-engine Tubular Bells and
  isolated short/long-note context files.

Out of scope:

- A shippable long-sustain design, looping, granular continuation, additive/modal
  resynthesis, new recordings, new velocity layers, or changing VSCO's hard character.
- Increasing the packaged B1 asset duration beyond the existing 1.5-second body.
- Integration before Arthur has auditioned the experiment.

## Approach

1. Preserve the exact generated-inventory guard, but remove the B1 handoff
   conditioner from the bake. Call the existing onset trimmer with a 10 ms end taper
   rather than the current 600 ms taper, retaining the natural recorded body through
   almost the whole asset.
2. Add one small B1-specific sampled voice, following the existing damped
   `ClavinetSampled` playback shape: nearest-zone cubic read, pitch bend, velocity
   gain, bounded end, and exponential note-off release. Do not generalize the two
   voices during this diagnostic.
3. In the samples-on B1 route, construct that voice instead of constructing or
   rendering the modal sustain. Keep the model as the complete samples-off path.
4. Hold the established audible attack scale: peak-normalized PCM at runtime gain
   `1.30 × dsp::vel_amp(velocity)`. The failed conditioned bank's 0.25 storage scale
   and 5.20 make-up gain retire together.
5. Establish a direct localization ladder at representative root notes and one
   worst-case intermediate repitch: onset-aligned archival slice, baked WAV, and
   dry sampled-voice output. Apply and report each stage's own peak-normalization
   scalar so pure recorder/bake/runtime gain changes do not dominate the comparison;
   do not apply whole-file loudness matching, limiting, or bus processing.
6. Produce the dry voice rung with a deliberately ignored, test-only diagnostic in
   `sampler.rs` that constructs and renders the B1 voice directly to a temporary WAV.
   It does not enter `EngineCore`, so it bypasses the unconditional sympathetic comb
   bank, sends, `BusGlue`, and output normalization. Judge the unbent held body in a
   common 0-1.0 second window, then judge the terminal boundary and note-off damper
   in separate controls.
7. Render the pinned Tubular Bells piano excerpt plus a diagnostic MIDI containing
   short repetitions, layer-boundary notes, and long holds through the normal engine.
   These are musical-context auditions, not sample-only evidence, because the
   unchanged engine adds sympathetic resonance.

## Acceptance criteria

1. `prepare.py --only=b1upright` succeeds from committed sources, preserves the exact
   25-normal/27-hard inventory check before writes, and produces identical hashes on a
   second bake.
2. Every baked B1 file equals the ordinary peak-normalized 1.5-second onset trim with
   a 10 ms end taper; no B1 handoff conditioner or 0.25 storage scale remains.
3. A samples-on B1 note is seed-invariant, remains recording-owned past 75 ms, and
   contains no modal voice contribution over the covered 88-key range. The seed pair
   used by this oracle must first produce materially different output on the
   samples-off model, which is byte-identical to the exact parent's model owner:
   over 100-500 ms, `rms(a-b) / max(rms(a), rms(b)) >= 0.05`. Small xorshift seeds
   that collapse to similar first draws are invalid. The samples-on outputs for the
   calibrated pair must be bit-identical. A samples-off B1 note remains the seeded
   model.
4. Note-off applies the existing B1 felt-damper curve; pitch bend and nearest-zone
   tuning remain functional; held voices reap at the bounded asset end.
5. While every source frame touched by cubic interpolation remains inside the
   parent's 30 ms source-domain identity region, the proposed path matches the exact
   parent's output within a tight numeric tolerance at representative root and
   intermediate notes on both sides of the velocity-59/60 layer boundary. At
   44.1 kHz the safe condition is
   `floor(read_position) + 2 <= floor(0.030 × 44_100)`. The tolerance permits only
   expected full-scale 16-bit requantization and equivalent interpolation; it does
   not compare a read whose cubic support enters the parent's conditioner ramp.
6. Direct controls print the peak scalar used to level-align each stage by
   construction, then use fixed playback gain with no whole-file loudness
   normalization, limiting, dither, engine bus, or send. They separate the
   onset-aligned Opus-decoded/channel-L archival slice, processed baked WAV, and dry
   sampled-voice playback. A worst-case intermediate note exercises repitch. The
   held-body verdict uses the common unbent 0-1.0 second window; asset-end,
   pitch-bend, and early note-off/damper behavior are assessed separately.
7. Full-length pinned Tubular controls prove the VSCO first alternate and samples-off
   model are byte-identical to the exact parent.
8. Focused Python/Rust tests, formatting, clippy, and the appropriate workspace checks
   are green, including `cargo check -p ferrosintesis --no-default-features`.
9. Arthur first auditions the dry controls, then the normal-engine Tubular and
   diagnostic renders. A natural first-second body supports a same-recording
   continuation design. An unnatural dry result implicates the retained
   capture-to-playback chain and is localized by the three direct stages; it does not
   by itself identify the microphone capture as the cause. Numeric success cannot
   substitute for this result.
10. A catalogue-wide exact-parent render inventory changes only GM0 samples-on
    routes. Any non-GM0 or samples-off movement is a defect.

## Decision log

- This is a causal experiment, not a sustain solution. Removing the conditioner and
  modal voice isolates the committed-source-to-playback chain. The committed source
  is already lossy Opus and the slicer has already selected channel L, so those
  common-mode stages and the original recording cannot be distinguished without an
  uncommitted pre-Opus master. The ladder localizes only its two explicit boundaries:
  onset-aligned archival slice → filtered/resampled/normalized/quantized baked WAV →
  repitched/velocity-scaled dry voice.
- Keep the asset duration unchanged for the experiment. The archival slicer retains
  up to eight seconds per note, but committing that expansion before proving the
  capture's ceiling would spend bytes without information.
- Use a 10 ms terminal taper only to prevent an end click. The current 600 ms fade
  starts around 0.9 seconds and would erase much of the body being tested.
- Keep both B1 hardness layers. VSCO's single hard layer is a useful good-sounding
  control, not a reason to discard the B1's recorded dynamic distinction.
- Do not abstract the B1 diagnostic with the clavinet voice. The mechanisms may
  converge later, but their release policy and expected future continuation differ.
- Keep audition gain fixed. Loudness-matched copies may be secondary conveniences,
  but they cannot replace the causal fixed-gain comparison.
- Peak-align the direct stages by reporting and applying their own scalar before the
  fixed-gain audition. This removes a timbre-inert level step; it is not whole-file
  loudness matching.
- Repitch changes wall-clock duration, so “1.5 seconds” describes source material,
  not every played note. The shared 0-1.0 second judgment window avoids ordinary
  nearest-zone end effects; the boundary is an explicit separate limitation.

## Review record

Two independent reviewers critiqued the draft and each ran a refutation pass over
the combined findings.

Surviving findings, folded into this design:

- **Critical, resolved:** the normal engine always adds a synthetic sympathetic
  comb bank, so it cannot be the sample-only oracle. Add direct/dry controls and keep
  the normal render only as musical context.
- The experiment retains several capture-to-playback transformations. Narrow the
  inference and use an archival-slice → baked-WAV → dry-playback localization ladder.
- Quarter-scale-parent/full-scale-experiment requantization and the new voice require
  tolerance-bounded first-30-ms parity checks rather than assumed or byte parity.
- Repitch changes asset wall time. Use a common sub-second body window and judge the
  terminal boundary separately.
- Fixed-gain comparisons must not be hidden by per-file loudness normalization.
- Held body and note-off damper are separate listening questions.

Refuted findings:

- Revalidating newly measured roots against the unchanged runtime tables is useful
  maintenance, but exact-parent staged-scope comparison already pins those tables and
  it is not required for this experiment.
- CC68 does not create a new behavioral difference: neither the existing acoustic
  piano voice nor the proposed whole-sample voice accepts legato reuse.

After the direct-control correction and narrowed inference, no critical finding
remained from the adversarial layer.

The single external Claude review found three additional critical criterion defects,
all resolved in this revision:

- The parent conditioner preserves 30 ms of **source** time, not output time on a
  repitched note. The parity oracle now stops when source position reaches 30 ms.
- Raw archival, baked, and runtime rungs had incompatible levels. Each direct stage
  is now peak-aligned with its scalar reported, followed by fixed-gain playback; this
  removes a pure scalar without disguising spectral or envelope differences.
- Seed invariance could pass with xorshift seeds that barely move the parent. The
  seed pair must now demonstrate sensitivity on the exact-parent route first.

Useful external findings also added an intermediate repitch control, named the
test-only direct renderer and its excluded processing, stated the Opus/channel-L
common-mode limit, required the catalogue render-diff and no-default-features check,
and made the velocity law explicit. The baseline-capture finding was already
satisfied by the preserved exact-parent worktree at commit `3b0dfaf`; its outputs
and binary freshness remain validation preconditions. No external finding was
rejected as mere preference.

Because the external pass found critical defects, one targeted native re-review
checked only those three corrections. It passed the direct-rung level construction
but caught two remaining boundary errors, now resolved without another review round:

- Cubic interpolation reads through `floor(position) + 2`, so the parity oracle now
  bounds the furthest source frame touched rather than the nominal read position.
- A merely non-identical parent seed pair could differ negligibly. The oracle now
  requires at least 5% normalized RMS divergence over the model-owned 100-500 ms
  window before demanding bit identity from the experimental sample-only path.

No unresolved critical design finding remains. The targeted re-review allowance is
exhausted.
