# Sampled brass section

**Status: SELF-APPROVED**

## Goal and scope

Make default-bank GM 61 read immediately as a sampled brass section instead of a
five-player waveshaper. Use the already pinned, MIT-licensed MuseScore MS Basic
Brass Section preset for the attack and early body, then hand over to the existing
modeled section for indefinite sustain and MIDI expression.

In scope:

- Bake the ten dedicated GM 61 PCM zones from the pinned `MS Basic.sf3`.
- Package them in the existing `ferrosintesis-samples-musescore` crate.
- Wrap only default-bank GM 61 in the normal LA handover.
- Preserve the existing model as the no-samples fallback and sustained body.
- Add focused source, routing, pitch, handover, inventory, and provenance checks.

Out of scope:

- New dependencies, sample sources, feature flags, or runtime controls.
- Timbre changes to solo brass GM 56-60, synth brass GM 62/63, or
  alternate-bank brass. The shared `LaVoice` slur-handover correction can affect
  an early CC68 slur on any sampled voice and is validated as a separate,
  explicitly censused blast radius.
- Replacing the expressive brass model or looping the MuseScore recordings.
- Versioning, integration, publication, or release.

## Acceptance criteria

1. `python3 tools/ferrosintesis-samples/prepare.py --only=brasssection`
   reproducibly emits exactly the ten committed MS Basic preset-61 zones from
   the pinned source as `brasssection_F2.wav` through
   `brasssection_C6.wav`, asserts unique exact source roots and mono sample
   types before writing, and rejects stale family-owned output.
2. Every packaged brass-section WAV is mono 16-bit 44.1 kHz, non-empty, named
   by sounding pitch, and documented under the existing MIT notice. Runtime
   bank tests bind all ten embedded WAVs to measured `Zone.root` values and
   prove the section bank is not any solo-brass bank.
3. With embedded samples enabled, GM 61 differs materially from its modeled
   fallback in low, middle, and high attack/early-body probes. GM 56-60 and
   GM 62/63 retain their existing routing, and the CC0-nonzero alternate remains
   the pure model.
4. All ten roots and both sides of every nearest-zone boundary stay on pitch.
   Every engaging key retains PCM through the handover at its actual repitch
   ratio. The invariant uses the unfaded source budget,
   `(PRE_S + keep_s - bake_fade_s) / ratio > runtime_fade_end`; no zone can
   dry out while the model is still suppressed.
5. Fine envelope windows around both fade boundaries show no material click,
   trough, or gain step across low/mid/high keys and representative velocities.
   Short-note probes before, across, and after `fade_start` show no
   gap-then-blip release.
6. After the handover, samples-on GM 61 is the existing expressive model.
   Engine-level probes cover held life, note-off, early/mid/late CC68 legato,
   pitch updates, and authored breath/expression through the wrapper. Fine
   windows at each slur prove the shared sum-to-one handover has no dropout or
   gain bump. Channel aftertouch still changes level, pitch, and tonal energy,
   but must not apply one coherent 25–40 Hz amplitude flutter to all five
   section players.
7. `--no-default-features` keeps the current GM 61 model bit-identical to the
   task base across representative keys and velocities, and builds/tests
   without touching embedded PCM. Out-of-range fallback retains the same
   velocity calibration.
8. Sample prewarming initializes the new bank before realtime NoteOn, and the
   derived inventory/licensing oracles remain green.
9. A bank- and event-time-aware census of the exact `render-catalog` `ALBUMS`
   inputs identifies default-bank GM 61 reachability. The required catalog
   render-diff changes the union of that set and any track that authors an early
   CC68 slur on another LA-wrapped program, including demos; its program-only
   classifier is supporting evidence, not the bank-isolation oracle. The prior
   modeled candidate's measured reach—25 of 124 album MIDIs and 4 of 17 demos—
   is the expected GM61 starting set and must be reconciled against current
   `ALBUMS`, not trusted as timeless.
   Low/mid/high audition WAVs are produced for Arthur.
10. The all-program velocity law remains green. A focused GM61 census proves
    the sampled composite and the model-only/out-of-range fallback each use
    their own measured exponent.
11. Focused tests, formatting, and clippy are green. The bug moves to **Fixed**,
   never Closed, pending independent listening verification.

## Decision log

- **Use MS Basic preset 61.** It is a dedicated ten-zone Brass Section bank,
  distinct from the soundfont's solo-brass presets, and the exact source,
  checksum, licence, and acknowledgements already ship in this repository.
- **Reuse the MuseScore sample crate.** This adds no dependency or licensing
  surface and keeps one source pin in one package.
- **Use LA replacement, not additive layering.** One sampled section should own
  the onset; stacking it over the modeled onset would preserve the synthetic
  transient and create phase/level ambiguity.
- **Keep modeled sustain.** It preserves unlimited note length, controllers,
  determinism, and the no-samples build while the PCM establishes instrument
  identity where the current model is weakest.
- **Give GM 61 separate calibration.** Reusing the solo-brass gain/window would
  assume unlike recordings have identical envelope and level.
- **Bound the handover by source duration.** A one-second take at 2.05x lasts
  under 0.5 seconds. Retaining 1.9 seconds and capping the handover at 0.80
  seconds gives an explicit safety margin that every engaging key must prove.
- **Do not growl the whole section as one player.** Aftertouch retains the
  existing engine gain/vibrato and per-player tonal response, but GM61 bypasses
  the solo-brass post-sum 30 Hz flutter that pumps five players coherently.
- **Fix early legato at the wrapper contract.** `LaVoice` already says a slur
  retires the transient and glides the model, but its blend clock did not
  promote the model. A short rebased sum-to-one handover preserves level;
  abrupt promotion would stack or step. Correcting that common invariant is
  simpler and safer than a GM61-only mode flag.
- **Separate sampled and modeled velocity calibration.** GM61's current table
  exponent was measured on the pure model. The real PCM owns the analysis
  window after this change, so the composite is re-derived while the old model
  exponent becomes the explicit fallback override.
- **Do not add a per-key gain table speculatively.** One reviewer correctly
  noted that peak-normalized zones can differ in seam level. Start with one
  measured scalar; add only the smallest register trim that the exhaustive seam
  measurements prove necessary.

## Review record

Three independent adversarial reviewers covered architecture, source/provenance,
and audio-oracle adequacy. Each then cross-checked the combined findings once.
The following survived refutation and are folded above:

- PCM duration must cover `fade_end * accepted repitch ratio` for every key.
- Early legato must promote model ownership when it retires the PCM transient.
- Default/alternate bank isolation needs a direct oracle.
- All public inventory, architecture, count, notice, and provenance surfaces
  must be updated.
- The exact ten source zones, embedded files, and measured runtime roots need
  binding evidence.
- Seam checks need fine boundary-centred windows, not only the generic 100 ms
  level-continuity guard.
- Samples-off preservation needs task-base parity, not determinism alone.
- Catalog validation needs an exact `ALBUMS` list and bank-aware event census;
  the existing render-diff classifier alone cannot prove the boundary.

One proposed separate finding—“controller/lifecycle acceptance is missing”—was
rejected as duplicating an existing criterion. That criterion is now more
explicit about the concrete sampled-GM61 probes it requires.

No unresolved critical finding or architectural fork remains. The source-length
defect is resolved by the bounded handover plus an exhaustive runtime duration
oracle.
