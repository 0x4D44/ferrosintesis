## Confirmed findings

### CR-1 — Medium: published credits omit a supplied Original Author

- **Confidence:** High
- **Where:**
  - `D:\worktrees\ferrosintesis\20260731-REV-CLA@CRUCIBLE-code-review-005314\crates\ferrosintesis-samples-ydp-grand\NOTICE:4`
  - `D:\worktrees\ferrosintesis\20260731-REV-CLA@CRUCIBLE-code-review-005314\crates\ferrosintesis-samples-ydp-grand\PROVENANCE.md:20`
- **Tracked as:** `MM-BUG-CRUCIBLE-00001`
- **Problem:** The authoritative FreePats YDP page names Dr. Mikhail Krishtal
  and his Zenph Studios team as the original performers and recordists. The
  package credits `roberto@zenvoid.org`, Zenph Studios, and OLPC, but omits
  Krishtal's supplied name. The parent consolidated `NOTICE` repeats the
  omission.
- **Impact:** Both independently distributable packages can ship incomplete
  CC BY 3.0 attribution. Section 4(b) requires the Original Author's name when
  supplied.
- **Evidence:** The YDP section at
  <https://freepats.zenvoid.org/Piano/acoustic-grand-piano.html> supplies the
  name and role. CC BY 3.0 section 4(b) is at
  <https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/legalcode.en>.
- **Why current checks miss it:** The licensing oracles derive acceptable
  credit tokens from the already-incomplete crate `NOTICE`. They prove that
  some real credit travels, not that every upstream identity travels.
- **Concrete fix:** Add Dr. Mikhail Krishtal and the Zenph team's recording
  role to the crate `NOTICE` and `PROVENANCE.md`, then propagate it to the
  parent `NOTICE` and guide. Derive required identities from retained upstream
  evidence and add a negative control that removes Krishtal while preserving
  Roberto, the title, and URL.
- **Effort:** Small

### CR-2 — Low: published zone spacing names the wrong interval

- **Confidence:** High
- **Where:**
  - `D:\worktrees\ferrosintesis\20260731-REV-CLA@CRUCIBLE-code-review-005314\crates\ferrosintesis-samples-ydp-grand\PROVENANCE.md:10`
  - Context:
    `D:\worktrees\ferrosintesis\20260731-REV-CLA@CRUCIBLE-code-review-005314\tools\ferrosintesis-samples\prepare.py:881`
- **Tracked as:** `MM-BUG-CRUCIBLE-00002`
- **Problem:** The selected roots are MIDI
  `36/42/48/54/60/66/72/78/84`. Adjacent roots differ by six semitones, a
  tritone, but the packaged provenance calls them "minor thirds."
- **Impact:** Runtime data is correct, but published provenance overstates the
  selected zone density and can mislead later rebake or interpolation work.
- **Concrete fix:** Say "C/F-sharp roots every six semitones" or
  "tritone-spaced." Keep the separate statement that these roots were selected
  from an upstream layer sampled in minor thirds. Add a source-derived interval
  check and a negative documentation fixture for the stale phrase.
- **Effort:** Extra small

  cache, stages extraction, rejects stale owned outputs, and writes WAVs
  atomically. Earlier bugs cover those fixed classes.
- **Packaging:** The crate allowlist includes all nine tracked WAVs plus its
  README, NOTICE, and provenance. No dependency, build script, runtime
  filesystem access, unsafe code, or path-traversal surface exists.
