### Medium — the soft gong starts with an unfaded NoteOn step

- **Confidence:** High
- **Where:** `D:\worktrees\ferrosintesis\20260812-REV-MM-CDX@CRUCIBLE-code-review-092603\crates\ferrosintesis-samples-gong\samples\gong_ageng_soft.wav`; processing contract at `D:\worktrees\ferrosintesis\20260812-REV-MM-CDX@CRUCIBLE-code-review-092603\crates\ferrosintesis-samples-gong\PROVENANCE.md:112`; faulty zero-lead path at `D:\worktrees\ferrosintesis\20260812-REV-MM-CDX@CRUCIBLE-code-review-092603\tools\ferrosintesis-samples\prepare.py:4173`; direct consumer at `D:\worktrees\ferrosintesis\20260812-REV-MM-CDX@CRUCIBLE-code-review-092603\crates\ferrosintesis\src\sampler.rs:5297`
- **Problem:** the soft asset's first PCM sample is `-2769`, or 8.45% full scale. The transition from silence is 12.7 times the 95th-percentile adjacent step over its first 2 ms. `trim_lead_and_ring` computes `fin == 0` when the onset is frame zero, applies no fade, and publishes that moving frame first. `GongOneShot` then emits it directly.
- **Impact:** every soft-layer strike (velocities below 84) begins with a large waveform discontinuity. Static evidence proves the step and its shipped path; perceived loudness was not auditioned under this review contract.
- **Concrete fix:** reuse `trim_to_onset`'s slope-bounded zero-lead micro-fade in `trim_lead_and_ring`; regenerate the gong outputs; refresh their derived pins; add a negative fixture and a committed-WAV oracle that bounds the initial step against ordinary first-window motion without crushing a tight attack.
- **Effort:** Small
- **Tracking:** `MM-BUG-CRUCIBLE-00024`
