## Confirmed defects

### CR-1 — Two dulcimer assets jump away from silence at NoteOn

- **Severity:** Medium
- **Confidence:** High for the waveform and render path; audible click perception unverified
- **Location:** `D:\worktrees\ferrosintesis\20260816-REV-MM-CDX@KILN-code-review-131612\crates\ferrosintesis-samples-ccby\src\lib.rs:34,42`; `D:\worktrees\ferrosintesis\20260816-REV-MM-CDX@KILN-code-review-131612\crates\ferrosintesis\src\sampler.rs:3815,3851`
- **Tracked as:** Open `MM-BUG-KILN-00215`
- **Effort:** Small

`dulcimer_D5.wav` begins at signed PCM16 value `-1272`; `dulcimer_F#4.wav`
begins at `+2120`. Both peak at `29490`, making their opening edges 4.31% and
7.19% of the sample peak. `LaVoice` starts at source position zero and reads
that frame while the sample owns the onset, so a GM 15 NoteOn steps directly
from the preceding silence to the nonzero asset value.

**Concrete fix:** rebake the dulcimer family through the current zero-frame
de-click path. Strengthen the opening-continuity oracle with an adversarial
fixture for these exact shapes. Its current maximum-first-window comparison is
too permissive: larger motion later in the attack lets both files pass.

### CR-2 — Fresh sampled voices allocate scratch inside realtime rendering

- **Severity:** Medium
- **Confidence:** High
- **Location:** `D:\worktrees\ferrosintesis\20260816-REV-MM-CDX@KILN-code-review-131612\crates\ferrosintesis\src\sampler.rs:3378,3832,3852`; `D:\worktrees\ferrosintesis\20260816-REV-MM-CDX@KILN-code-review-131612\crates\ferrosintesis\src\live.rs:295`
- **Tracked as:** Open `MM-BUG-KILN-00216`
- **Effort:** Medium

`LaVoice::build` initializes `buf` with `Vec::new()`. Its first `render()` calls
`resize(out.len(), 0.0)`, so every newly constructed sampled GM 4 or GM 15 voice
allocates scratch in the deadline-bearing audio callback. `prewarm_samples()`
warms PCM caches and `reserve_realtime_storage()` reserves engine voice/index
vectors; neither reserves this per-voice buffer.

**Concrete fix:** use bounded inline/chunked scratch or reserve it before the
realtime path, then bypass the scratch after the sample handover. Add an
allocation-count oracle for fresh sampled NoteOns after both setup calls.
