Two things I initially got wrong here, corrected after an adversarial re-check:

- **The 9 deleted `-headroom` WAVs are sound-neutral.** `0f54b1a` established by SHA-256
  grouping that every `headroom_*_f_rr2.wav` was byte-identical to its `_mf_rr2` counterpart,
  and replaced them with an `ALIASES` file. They are deduplication, not a timbre change, and
  should not be cited as evidence the sound moved.
- **`sampler.rs` is overwhelmingly test code.** 226 of its 255 changed lines sit inside
  `#[cfg(test)] mod tests`; the single production change is `headroom_f_rr2()` delegating to
  `headroom_mf_rr2()`, byte-identical by construction. The sound claim rests on `engine.rs` and
  the 55 WAVs, not on `sampler.rs`.

**Was the change verified? Substantially yes — but not the way `CLAUDE.md:183` asks.** Each fix
commit documents its own render check against a CLI built from that commit's base, and the
