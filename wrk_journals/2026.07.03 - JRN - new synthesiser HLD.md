# JRN — new synthesiser HLD (hollowsynth realism)

Design-flow journal for the "new, awesome synthesiser" HLD. Following the
deltic-config **Ultracode – design (HLD)** flow (shared phrases, page 2 · D).

## Intent (confirmed read)

- **Goal:** raise hollowsynth's realism for **guitars, drums and bass** to the
  point where the three families read as recorded instruments, not a modelled
  approximation — driven by the 41-finding review that landed 2026-07-03.
- **Success looks like:** the five systemic gaps (no cabinet; velocity→timbre;
  attack transients; cymbal/tom modelling; kit/ensemble glue) are closed by a
  coherent architecture, with per-finding acceptance oracles a build run can verify.
- **Scope read (proposed, open to correction):** *evolve* hollowsynth — keep the
  proven render engine, bus/FX chain and LA sampler; rebuild the guitar/bass/drum
  voice models and the drum engine. **Lean hybrid** allowed: expand the embedded
  LA-style sampled-attack / one-shot layer within a few MB, staying a single
  self-contained binary. Not a greenfield rewrite.
- **Out of scope:** the composition engines (opus4-8, gpt5-*, python); non-guitar/
  bass/drum families except where shared DSP touches them; distribution/fleet.

## Environment adaptations (remote Linux container, not the Windows fleet)
