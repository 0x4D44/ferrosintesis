# 2026.07.06 - JRN - The Signal Fire build

Task: a new ~17-minute Oldfield-idiom piece (Incantations IV × TSDOE
Ascension × TB3 × Mastermind) plus hollowsynth v0.6. Worktree
`20260706-DEV-HUM-the-signal-fire-album-and-hollowsynth-v0`.

## Decisions

- **Arthur's choices** (AskUserQuestion): one continuous ~17-min piece;
  hollowsynth v0.6 with all three controller features; title *The Signal
  Fire*.
- **Unifying device**: one bass riff, three rhythmic guises (funk 4/4,
  10/8 additive 3+3+2+2, augmented) with a machine-checkable shared
  skeleton; three themes over the same i–♭VII ground with chord tones on
  every bar downbeat so the finale can stack them. The counterpoint rules
  are an *oracle* in material.py — the material was written to pass, and
  passed first try after two design-time fixes (parallel unisons between
  THEME_B and THEME_C at downbeats 0/4; fixed by moving C's bar-2 downbeat
  to degree 4).
- **The lattice IS the riff**: rather than a separate lattice figure, M3's
  three interlocking guitars split RIFF_10's ten quavers between them
  (indices {0,3,6}/{1,4,8}/{2,5,7,9}). Fewer ideas, deeper use — very
  Oldfield.
- Morse easter egg: M1 woodblock taps "CQ CQ CQ" (radio general call —
  a signal fire calling anyone); M6 answers "K" (go ahead). Story resolves.

## Build mechanics

- Phase 1 (workflow, 2 parallel agents): hollowsynth 0.5→0.6 (CC1
  vibrato/Leslie with 1.5 s rotor inertia, CC74 resonant lowpass insert
  with true bypass, CC64 sustain, `--solo` stems; 21/21 tests, fmt/clippy
  clean, v0.6 renders Hollow Hill byte-identically to v0.5) and the album
  scaffold (engine helpers, conductor-as-data, verify oracles, analyze.py).
  Committed dba4c54 + 866a4da.
- Phase 2 (workflow, 6 parallel agents): one composer per movement, each
  owning exactly one movements/*.py file. Race avoidance: composers never
  run build.py (shared outputs); they self-check via
  `build.build_score()` + per-movement `midi/_selftest_mN.mid` +
  hollowsynth `--solo` renders + analyze.py.

## Surprises / gotchas

- My roadmap prose said "~17:20" but its own tempo map integrates to
  ~16:54 — the scaffold agent caught the contradiction because it encoded
  the duration window exactly as written. Amended the HLD and
  verify.DURATION_WINDOW (16:30–17:30) rather than stretching M6.
- The two roadmap M1 tempo tables disagreed in detail (drift vs spin-up
  beats); conductor.py reconciles them (74/76 @152/160, 80/88 @168/172).
- hollowsynth CC74: a channel that once dips below 127 keeps the filter in
  path afterwards (slewed to 12 kHz, ~+3 dB shelf at the top) — true
  bypass is only for channels that never received CC74. Negligible but
  documented.

## Phase 2: the movements (and two rate-limit strikes)

- The six-composer fan-out hit the account session-token limit twice: first
  all six died mid-flight (~70 min in), then on relaunch M2 and M4 — the two
  biggest — died again. Both times the interrupted agents left syntactically
  whole drafts; a "salvage note" added to the shared preamble told relaunched
  agents to verify-and-complete rather than rewrite. Verdict: salvage worked
  well — M1's draft needed zero changes, M5's needed three targeted fixes,
  M2's reviewer found and fixed two real render clicks the draft had.
- Workflow resume semantics paid off: on the final resume only M2/M4 re-ran;
  the four completed movements replayed from cache.
- Cross-movement couplings the composers discovered and handled peer-to-peer
  via reports: the shared seeded RNG stream means any upstream event-count
  change re-rolls all downstream jitter (so per-movement click scans don't
  transfer to the final mix — re-measure the assembly); the dynamics-arc
  oracle made composers negotiate their velocity bands like a real section.

## Adversarial verification (4 lenses) and fixes

- Lenses: source code, binary MIDI (independent strict parser), rendered
  audio (full mix + solo stems), musical judgment. Three lenses had to be
  re-run on Opus after the Fable 5 model quota ran out mid-flight.
- Positive confirmations (measured, not claimed): wah LFO 3.34 Hz = exactly
  quaver rate at 100 bpm; double-track correlation drops 0.89 -> 0.05 at W3;
  mandolin tremolo 14.4 Hz = exactly 32nds at 108; the +2-semi seam bend in
  the data and recentred at 1312.0; peak -1.00 dBFS, zero rail-clips; both
  Morse messages decode from raw event times; all 31 program changes and 23
  tempo events tick-exact vs conductor.py.
- Confirmed defects -> fixed:
  1. [major] 777 same-pitch overlapping note pairs (ambiguous on kill-newest
     GM synths; worst ~8 beats across the M5->M6 pad seam). Fixed at the
     engine layer: Score._resolve_overlaps() write-time pass clamps an
     earlier note's off to the re-strike tick; unit-tested on in-order,
     out-of-order, nested and non-overlap cases; idempotent.
  2. [major] The Leslie "spin-up" was inaudible: prog-18 organ idles its
     tremulant at 6.5 Hz and LESLIE_FAST is 6.8 — CC1 swept only ~0.3 Hz.
     Fix in hollowsynth: channels that ever receive CC1 get rate =
     lerp(0.9 Hz, 6.8 Hz, mod); CC1-free files (Hollow Hill) keep program
     idle exactly (byte-identity re-verified).
  3. [major, musical] M4-W1's lyrical solo entry sat over a 6.6-notes/beat
     funk engine; W1 accompaniment thinned to half-time.
  4. [minor] M3's opening trough (-45..-59 dBFS for ~9 s after the loud M2)
     lifted ~+10 velocity; M3 656-720 plateau gets a crystal-loop breath;
     M5 gains CC11 breathing + Theme C phrase ebbs + a pad re-strike at the
     second peal (was a single 248-beat tied B4); W5 bass fills vary again.
  5. Oracle gap closed: the finale stacks THEME_A+C in IONIAN; the material
     oracle now re-checks that pair's downbeat intervals in ionian too.
  6. Manifest now reports the file's integrated duration (incl. the 2-beat
     end pad); HLD section 7 corrected where measurement disproved prose
     (M6 mix centroid rises — the bright finale IS the story; RMS ladder is
     not the velocity ladder).
