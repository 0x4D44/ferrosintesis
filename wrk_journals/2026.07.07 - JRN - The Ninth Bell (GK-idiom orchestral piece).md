# 2026.07.07 — JRN — The Ninth Bell (a GK-idiom orchestral piece)

## Task (Arthur)

> We've got a demo part — "orch_stem_ch0" — I love the string chords. Can
> you take that as the intro to a new piece of music — and then build on
> it? It feels like it goes somewhere dramatic and builds and drops —
> something like the intro to Gabriel Knight?

## What shipped

`fable5/The Ninth Bell/` — an original single-track orchestral piece,
5:46, A aeolian, 101 bars, 1,796 notes, in the *Gabriel Knight: Sins of
the Fathers* title-music idiom (Robert Holmes' dark-cathedral gothic).
The loved gesture (`demos/orchestral_demo.py` ch0 — voice-led Am–F–C–G
string chords swelling on CC11) is reproduced **verbatim** as movement I,
then the piece compresses one dramatic spring twice.

Dynamic arc, measured on the render (dB RMS/section):
Veil −42.7 → Processional −31.4 → Ascent −25.0 → **HIT** → **void −60.1**
→ Rising Tide −28.7 → **climax −18.5** → embers −47.1. The void is 35 dB
below the ascent (a cliff, not a fade); a **feint drop** at bar 62 (−11 dB)
re-arms the ear before the real climax. Exactly the "builds and drops"
Arthur asked for, made numeric.

## How it was built

1. **Design panel** (Workflow, 3 lenses — film-dramaturgy / gothic-idiom /
   motivic-architecture — + a judge). Winner: the film-dramaturgy design
   ("trained expectation, violently betrayed, then made beautiful"), with
   8 grafts from the losers (the nine-bell frame that names the piece, the
   pre-hit CC91 tail push, the organ Leslie arc, the phrygian gate, the
   theme-derived bell motif, leap-cell stretto, portamento discipline,
   hit markers). Synthesized into the HLD.
2. **Oracle-first scaffold** (repo lesson): `material.py` + `verify.py`
   written and green **before** the music — 13 oracles including intro
   fidelity (recomputes the demo gesture from the engine and pins ch0
   note-for-note), a GM 55–71 program whitelist (hollowsynth can't voice
   brass/reeds), the nine-bell ledger, two scored silences, and the
   build/drop dynamic-arc contour as inequalities on per-bar velocity sums.
3. **Composition fan-out** — 6 parallel composer agents, one per dramatic
   unit (m3+m4 paired: setup+betrayal is one gesture). Each edited only
   its own movement file against the HLD + oracles, self-validating with
   `build.py --check`.
4. **Assembly + render** — all 13 MIDI oracles green; hollowsynth v0.8
   render; `analyze.py` (new) confirms the audio arc, both reverb-tail
   silences, mono compatibility (0.46 dB loss — no collapse), and clicks.

## The material (one DNA)

A 13-note theme: opening rising minor sixth (the anguish leap E→C), a
stepwise sigh down, ending **hanging on degree 2** — the tonic withheld.
Its downbeats are chord tones of both the home ground (Am|F|C|G) and the
hardened climax ground (Am|Dm|Em|E7); the collapsing-staircase
countersubject is consonant over both too, so it runs unbroken through the
peak. The bell toll is the leap cell inverted. The withheld resolution is
granted only by the **ninth bell** — a lone A, the piece's last note (the
title is structural: 8 section tolls + the final A = nine).

The two betrayals verified clean in the note data: beat 128 every pitched
channel plays pure E major (E/G#/B, E1–E6); beat 352 pure B-flat, the
violin holding the theme's D as the chord's third while its resolving
B-natural never comes.

## Surprises / notes

- **The composer agents "failed" but had already succeeded.** All six
  workflow agents returned `out of usage credits` (Fable 5 quota) — yet
  five of the six movement files were fully written and the whole piece
  passed every oracle first assembly. The credit wall hit at the *final
  report* call, after the work and the agents' own `--check` iterations.
  Salvage-and-verify (the interrupted-agent lesson) cost nothing here.
- **Two engine copies exist.** Used the Sub Rosa / Winter Guests engine
  (has `keysig`, `vowel`, `portamento_*`, RPN, aftertouch), not the older
  Signal Fire one. The intro-fidelity oracle recomputes `pad_block` +
  `voice_lead` from that engine rather than hardcoding pitches, so it's
  robust to jitter (sort by jitter-rounded onset, not raw beat).
- The worktree has no built hollowsynth (`target/` gitignored), so the
  render used the main clone's binary and the Opus was encoded from that
  WAV directly with the same tag scheme render_opus.py uses.
