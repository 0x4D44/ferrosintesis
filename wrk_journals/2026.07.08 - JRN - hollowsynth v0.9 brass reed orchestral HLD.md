## Headline findings the verification earned

- **The plan's "brass is fully opt-in-safe" was wrong.** The safety scan found
  French Horn (60) and Brass Section (61) *are* used — both in `The Iron Tide`.
  So the brass voices re-render that one committed file; needs a waiver, not a
  free pass. Everything else in 55–71 is genuinely unused.
- **Strings and choir collide on the shared `SawStack` struct** (the critic's
  #1 finding): strings' ST2 writes a stack-level `vib_depth` that choir's CH-3
  deletes (moving depth per-`Layer`). Resolved by unifying: depth goes
  per-layer, one combined field set, one 16-arg ctor, `set_vib` writes all
  layers. Byte-safe because every new field is copied-not-drawn on the v1 path.
- **Four verification oracles measured the wrong thing** (mechanisms were fine):
  brass program-spread confounded by pitch (100 Hz centroid floor inverts the
  tuba); reed anti-alias tested the best case not the worst; choir shimmer grid
  overlapped the static detune cluster; crash-twin oracle measured a window
  where the twins are already 70 dB down. All corrected in the master §6.
- **Canary gap:** if strings ships default ST1, ch8 (the SawStack canary) becomes
  a feature row and no SawStack canary remains — exactly where the collision
  would show. Added a pad channel (ch14) as the untouched SawStack canary.
