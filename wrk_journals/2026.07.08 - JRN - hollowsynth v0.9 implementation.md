## Decisions / notes

- **REGRESSION POLICY RELAXED (Arthur, 2026.07.08).** Byte-identity of untouched
  committed renders is NO LONGER an absolute requirement. It stays the DEFAULT
  and the primary **contamination tripwire** — an *unexplained* byte diff must
  still be investigated as a possible bug. But a render change you can EXPLAIN and
  JUDGE improves (or at least doesn't degrade) the audio is now ACCEPTABLE — it is
  NOT a hard-stop or an architectural fork. This machine has no ears, so ground the
  judgment in design intent + the numeric oracles + physics, DOCUMENT the rationale
  here, and FLAG it for Arthur's listen. Still NEVER weaken/delete a test, soften an
  assert, or suppress a lint to hide a diff (the fmt/clippy/test gate stays strict);
  if a byte-identity test (drum fingerprint, golden canaries) fails, investigate —
  update it only when the change is intended-and-beneficial, recording why. The
  opt-in seams (kit-v2, CC73, program-gated voices) stay as the safe default, but a
  beneficial *default* change no longer has to hide behind one. Net effect on the
  remaining build: P3 strings ST1-default and P4 bowed 42/43 stop being "waivers"
  and just become documented quality improvements; a shared-struct refactor that
  perturbs an untouched channel is investigated (why?) but accepted if beneficial.
- **Reed deviations (2026.07.08, accepted & verified):** the oracles caught 3 real
  design bugs, fixed at root — (1) RD4 needs a DC guard (the pulse is zero-mean but
  ASYMMETRIC, so tanh biases); (2) the appendix's inert-formant-slot `(0,1.0,0.0)`
  builds `Biquad::peak` at f=0 = a DOUBLE POLE on z=1 (marginally-stable
  integrator, clarinet drifted to 17% RMS) — build the identity slot at a stable
  mid-band freq; (3) chiff outside the amp envelope (onset), matching Brass. Flag
  the inert-slot hazard for the reeds appendix + any future formant-bank voice.
- **Brass deviations (2026.07.08, accepted & verified):** (1) The brass appendix
  §3.3 signal chain omits an output-side brightness filter, but BR2's
  loudness→brightness only manifested at ~1.19 (needed ≥1.30) because the fixed
  bore/bell EQ masks the input lip LP. Added an L-driven OUTPUT lowpass
  (`out_base·2^(out_oct·L)`, trumpet 400/4.2, synth-gated) — the Bowed
  `900+5200·env` precedent the design itself cites. Load-bearing; flag for the
  brass appendix at finalize. (2) BR-O9 in HLD §6 was wrong twice — the
  matched-pitch fix had horn/trombone swapped (trombone h_max 7.0 > horn 5.5 ⇒
  brighter, per physics) and used a bell-shelf-confounded absolute-centroid
  metric. Final oracle: `hp_rms(2k)/rms` ordered trumpet>trombone>horn>tuba at
  C3; HLD §6 updated. (3) Brass amps use appendix starting values; finalize at
  BR-O15 fixture capture (engine-wiring unit) — they + out_base/out_oct tune
  together. (4) Deferred engine oracles: BR-O4 (CC11), BR-O12 (album compare),
