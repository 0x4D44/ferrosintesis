# 2026.07.10 — Atlas listening-note lyrics tags

Arthur asked for the GPT-5.6 listening files to expose each piece's motivation,
timestamped instrumentation, and showcased MIDI effects through the player's lyrics
view.

## Decisions

- Added one exact-MIDI-stem UTF-8 sidecar under `albums/gpt5-6/Atlas of
  Becoming/lyrics/`. `render_opus.py` embeds an optional sidecar as a multiline
  `LYRICS` Vorbis comment.
- Kept the prose separate from generated `album_manifest.json`. The renderer rejects
  blank, invalid-UTF-8, NUL-containing, and orphaned sidecars before it starts work.
- Treated `LYRICS` as an unsynchronised listening guide. Written timestamps describe
  section starts; they are not seekable lyric cues, and players that hide the
  conventional `LYRICS` field may not expose the guide.
- Distinguished static CC7/10/91/93/94 mix placement from changing controller
  showcases. The guides also identify two automation lanes aimed at silent channels,
  so authored MIDI is not misrepresented as an audible effect.

## Verification

- Six focused `unittest` cases cover optional loading, Unicode/newline preservation,
  invalid input, orphan detection, encoder comments, and the complete 14-track Atlas
  sidecar set.
- `cargo fmt --all -- --check` and `cargo check --workspace --locked` passed after the
  required shipped-code version bump to 0.11.1.
- All 14 tracks rendered successfully. A packet-level OpusTags parser confirmed every
  embedded `LYRICS` value exactly matched its sidecar, including multiline Unicode.
- `ropusdec` fully decoded all 14 refreshed listening files.
