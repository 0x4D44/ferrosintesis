## 2026.07.10 — adversarial design review

- Ran two independent read-only reviewers: one DSP/acoustic lens and one
  integration/routing lens. Each then attempted to refute the other's concrete
  findings. All refutation checks ran; none were degraded or unverified.
- One critical finding survived: default and secondary GM19 cannot retain
  separate held-note room/chorus/controller semantics after both are summed
  into the current single channel buffer. Revised the HLD to classify every
  active note before aggregation into ordinary, legacy-GM19, or
  cathedral-GM19 effect sources, with dry mix joined only after route-specific
  sends are derived.
- Other surviving findings tightened the design: replace the 72-rotator plan
  with at most 14 additive-generated rank wavetables; pin spectra, gains,
  mixtures, and breaks; derive physical pipe identity from rank/key; compose
  pitch/wind/tremulant once; add 10 Hz infrasonic safety and multiband FDN
  bounds; pin the Hadamard FDN topology; verify MIDI bank/program ordering;
  use a differential low-frequency route oracle; and recheck the alias ceiling
  after runtime pitch controls.
- One proposed extra monolithic legacy golden was refuted as redundant. The
  standalone legacy hash, focused overlap/controller/effect tests, and full
  old/new-binary *Ninth Bell* identity cover the promise.
- Because a critical finding occurred, the user-prescribed single targeted
  re-review will check only the revised pre-sum route containment. No general
  second review round will run.
- The targeted reviewer returned **RESOLVED**. It confirmed classification now
  occurs before aggregation and both bank-switch directions exercise CC1,
  CC91, CC93, chorus defaults, room routing, and CC121. No further internal
  review round is permitted or needed.

## 2026.07.10 — implementation

- Added the default GM19 `CathedralOrgan`: fourteen generated rank wavetables,
  a fixed English full-organ registration, stable rank/key pipe identities,
  low-key 32-foot foundation, velocity-independent sustain, 5.5 Hz division
  tremulant, wind-load sag, and a 10 Hz dry-path safety filter.
- Preserved the former GM19 voice exactly in CC0=1. Its standalone regression
  hash remains `11750116236685652893`.
- Added an isolated eight-line cathedral feedback-delay network with 40 ms
  pre-delay, frequency-dependent decay, decorrelated stereo taps, and 10 Hz
  return blockers. The existing global hall remains unchanged.
- Split active notes into ordinary, legacy-organ and cathedral-organ sources
  before deriving effect sends. Held notes therefore keep the room, chorus and
  controller semantics of the bank that created them when CC0 changes.
- Made GM19 cathedral chorus-free by default, while preserving explicit CC93.
  CC1 controls fixed-rate cathedral tremulant depth; GM16–18 and CC0=1 GM19
  retain the Leslie speed path.
- Added focused voice, reverb, routing, controller and bank-overlap tests. The
  initial integrated library run passed 222 tests with 4 ignored benchmarks.
  The release-only organ render benchmark passed at 179.3 ms versus 86.4 ms
  for the legacy voice, a 2.08x ratio against the 3x ceiling.
- Migrated *The Ninth Bell* to author CC0=1 before Program 19 because its organ
  scene explicitly depends on Leslie. Extended *Cathedral Mechanica* with a
  default-cathedral pedal/plenum scene followed by a CC0=1 legacy contrast.
- Updated public synth documentation and raised both crates to 0.12.0. Final
  review, render-diff inventory, listening-asset refresh and complete gates are
  still in progress.
