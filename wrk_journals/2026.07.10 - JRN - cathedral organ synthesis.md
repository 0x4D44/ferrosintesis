## 2026.07.10 — adversarial design review

- Ran two independent read-only reviewers: one DSP/acoustic lens and one
  integration/routing lens. Each then attempted to refute the other's concrete
  findings. All refutation checks ran; none were degraded or unverified.
- One critical finding survived: default and secondary GM19 cannot retain
  separate held-note room/chorus/controller semantics after both are summed
  into the current single channel buffer. Revised the HLD to classify every
  active note before aggregation into ordinary, legacy-GM19, or
  cathedral-GM19 effect sources, with dry mix joined only after route-specific
  sends are derived.
- Other surviving findings tightened the design: replace the 72-rotator plan
  with at most 14 additive-generated rank wavetables; pin spectra, gains,
  mixtures, and breaks; derive physical pipe identity from rank/key; compose
  pitch/wind/tremulant once; add 10 Hz infrasonic safety and multiband FDN
  bounds; pin the Hadamard FDN topology; verify MIDI bank/program ordering;
  use a differential low-frequency route oracle; and recheck the alias ceiling
  after runtime pitch controls.
- One proposed extra monolithic legacy golden was refuted as redundant. The
  standalone legacy hash, focused overlap/controller/effect tests, and full
  old/new-binary *Ninth Bell* identity cover the promise.
- Because a critical finding occurred, the user-prescribed single targeted
  re-review will check only the revised pre-sum route containment. No general
  second review round will run.
- The targeted reviewer returned **RESOLVED**. It confirmed classification now
  occurs before aggregation and both bank-switch directions exercise CC1,
  CC91, CC93, chorus defaults, room routing, and CC121. No further internal
  review round is permitted or needed.

## 2026.07.10 — external design review

  `CathedralOrgan` output before dry/send split, keep a return DC blocker, and

  low pedal note present.
