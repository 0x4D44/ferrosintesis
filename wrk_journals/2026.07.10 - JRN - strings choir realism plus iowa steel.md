## Unit 2 — choir formant-engine v2 (GM 52-54) — DONE

- **Design (HLD option C, DSP-only, zero sample bytes)**: a dedicated
  `ChoirV2` voice in voices.rs — deliberately NOT on `SawStack` (the
  shared-struct collision lesson), so strings/pads/leads render
  bit-identically by construction (oracle 7b's surviving pad/strings pins
  plus the diff inventory prove it). Eight singers in four SATB section
  pairs (intonation leans, pitch scatter, per-singer delayed/ramped
  decorrelated vibrato, drift, register weighting with mean-gain renorm);
  per-section 5-band tract = three CC70-morphable vowel formants + a fixed
  singer's-formant cluster (2.9/3.25 kHz) with ±3% per-section formant
  scatter; breath injected pre-tract (vowel-coloured onset puff ~90 ms +
  low air floor); soft consonant onset generalised from the alt-bank
  `hum_hold` prior art (morph frozen at schwa, mouth-open level lift +
  brightening lowpass). 54 splits from 53: uniform synth-stack scatter and
  a brighter "eh". CC70 `set_vowel` semantics preserved (engine anchors
  drive the vowel bands; the cluster shades with the third band's gain so
  "mm" closes it); legato = melisma (retune, no fresh consonant). CPU
  ≈ 2.5-3× v1 (8 saws + 20 biquads vs 4 + 3), inside the HLD envelope.
  Level calibrated: `CH2_AMP = 1.55` lands sustained RMS 0.032-0.082 vs
  v1's 0.032-0.070 across keys 45/57/69.
- **Alt bank untouched**: altbank routes 52-54 to its own frozen
  `choir_v2` factory with its own local SawStack — no shared code with the
  new default voice; its golden pin still passes.
- **Oracles** (CH2-O1..O6, thresholds pinned from measured values): default
  routing kind "choir2"; formant placement on a FIXED cross-program probe
  grid (P870 ooh≫eh, P1120 aah≫eh, P1900 eh≫both; ooh darkest by
  centroid); singer's-formant cluster prominent vs the 4.1 kHz band on all
  three programs AND closed by a CC70 "mm"; consonant/breath onset (soft +
  dark + flatness ≥0.12, harmonic sustain ≤0.30); ensemble shimmer
  (structural rate/delay decorrelation + live-window 2.6-3.6 s envelope AM
  floor, grid clear of static detune); pitch integrity (Goertzel <45
  cents, bend path) + level continuity vs strings 48 (2.4× window,
  measured 0.80-2.07). Retired: v1 choir golden hash in oracle 7b and the
  `(52,"sawstack")` routing pin — intentional voicing change. Existing
  engine CC70 vowel tests (52 and choir-pad 91) pass unchanged.
