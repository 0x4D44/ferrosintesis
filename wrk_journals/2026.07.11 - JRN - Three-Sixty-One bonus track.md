# 2026.07.11 — JRN — Three-Sixty-One (bonus track 16 on *Through Lines*)

## Task
Arthur loves T10 "Three-Sixty" and wanted a variant, **"Three-Sixty-One"**, that adds
(1) an overdriven/distorted guitar solo building in and soaring over the finale,
(2) a choir counterpoint later on, and (3) more varied, near-constant drum fills as the
track builds (he liked the big tom fill at ~1:14). Placement (his call): **track 16 on
*Through Lines***, a bonus reprise; the original T10 stays untouched.

## Approach
- Scouted the track + machinery: `t10_three_sixty.py`, `conductor.py`, `material.py`,
  `build.py`, `verify.py`, and the render path. Key finding: **no synth change needed** —
  ferrosintesis already renders GM 29/30 through a real tanh+cabinet distortion path;
  channels 14/15 were free.
- De-risked structurally first: cloned T10 → `t16_three_sixty_one.py`, registered it as
  track 16, added the two empty channels, and confirmed the 17-track file verified green
  (music + audio) before writing any new notes.
- **Fable 5 consult** (it composed T10 originally): briefed it precisely — grid, invariants,
  channel budget, the finale stack, consonance rule — and got back a full composition design
  (guitar solo / choir counterpoint / 6-shape fill library) *with oracles and tripwires*,
  measured against the real in-memory score. Captured as
  `wrk_docs/2026.07.11 - HLD - Three-Sixty-One additions.md`.
- **Verified by understanding, not blind-applied:** re-derived the "A-beat trap" independently
  ({E,A,C} on beats ≡4 mod 8 because the walker onsets A5 there) and the escalation counts
  (22<39<66, 28<47<71) — both matched Fable's numbers. Transcribed the note tables, wired the
  emitters, wrote the 3 new oracles, and **negative-controlled** the subtle consonance check
  (an injected B5 across the 604 A-beat correctly fails `guitar_solo_arc`).

## Surprises / decisions
- **RNG-stream boundary bleed.** The ~405 new `note()` calls shifted the shared seeded RNG,
  re-rolling the *outro* harp arp's jitter so its first note (beat 640, `en.arp` jt=4) landed
  at ~639.99 — inside the dive lane's [544,640) count → `finale_stack_fidelity` 385 vs 384.
  Fixed by emitting the outro arp deterministically (jt=0) in T16. Merged the gotcha into the
  existing 07.06 RNG lesson.
- **Sparse finale defeats an average-RMS audio threshold.** Fable's speculative stem check
  "finale ≥ build+6 dB" is wrong for a deliberately sparse soaring line (the finale guitar
  stem averages −38 dB vs drop two's −30, because it's full of rests). Dropped that check;
  the structural `guitar_solo_arc` oracle verifies register/velocity/soar instead, and I
  verified the stems manually (guitar silent pre-320, rising into drop two; choir present in
  the finale at −18.5 dB).
- **Opus blast radius.** Adding track 16 makes a full `render_opus.py` retag all 15 existing
  opus files' TRACKTOTAL 15→16. To keep the change minimal I rendered **only** track 16's opus
  (via `render_one`, TRACKTOTAL=16) and left the 15 untouched. Flagged for Arthur (HLD §8).
