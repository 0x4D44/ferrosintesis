# 2026.07.11 — JRN — Three-Sixty-One: making the lead guitar audible

## Symptom
Arthur, on listening: "I like the toms and fills. But I don't hear any lead guitar."
The solo was in the MIDI and rendered (the `--solo 14` stem had sound), yet it was
inaudible in the full mix.

## Diagnosis (reproduce → measure → root cause)
1. **Program 30 was mid-scooped.** `engine.rs` `Drive::new`: program 30 (distortion)
   applies a −5 dB peak at 650 Hz (a "scooped chug" for power chords) with post-gain
   0.30; program 29 (overdrive) applies a +4 dB push at 800 Hz (a "mid-push lead") with
   post-gain 0.42. Mids are exactly where a lead cuts through — 30 was the wrong voice.
   Fable's "30 for harmonic mass" was backwards for an audible lead.
2. **The guitar is NOT too quiet at the source.** Per-channel peaks: guitar 0.53, saw
   lead 0.28, orbit 0.45, harp 0.22, orch-hit 0.62, **drums 1.27**. The guitar is the
   *second-loudest* melodic voice; the drums dominate the mix (mix peak 1.51–1.58).
3. **It's a decaying pluck.** The guitar voice (Karplus-Strong) decays a beat after the
   pick, so the sparse held finale notes were brief blips, not a singing lead.

**Measurement trap corrected:** `--solo` stems are independently peak-normalized to
−1 dBFS, so a solo-stem RMS reflects crest/decay, NOT level in the mix — my first "gap"
numbers were meaningless. Un-normalizing via the CLI's reported `peak` gave the true
gap (~20/27 dB below in drop2/finale), and a band-limited (700–2500 Hz) measure gave the
real audibility proxy (a lead lives or dies in its own band, not against the full mix).

## Fix (composition layer; no synth change)
- **Program 30 → 29** (mid-push lead that cuts through) + channel louder/drier (vol 104→118,
  reverb 26→20) + a +12 velocity boost across the solo.
- **Re-articulate** held drop-two/finale notes (re-pick every 0.5 beat) so a decaying pluck
  keeps singing. Done in the emitter, so note-off spans / phrase counts / pitches — and
  therefore every `guitar_solo_arc` oracle — are unchanged.
- **Duck the maskers** in the solo sections: drum crashes 116/118→98, orchestra hits
  106/108→92, opening a pocket for the lead.
- Fixed the `choir_counterpoint` independence oracle to measure against the guitar's
  MELODIC onsets (the note plan), not the sustain re-picks (which now blanket the finale).

**Result:** band-limited (700–2500 Hz) gap 18 → **11–13 dB** below the mix; the mid-push +
sustain put the lead in its niche. All structural + audio oracles green.

## Honest limitation
Still ~11–13 dB below in-band — present, but not dominant. The remaining competition is
the finale's *signature* bright stack (orbit steel-drums, walker saw). Closing it further
means either compromising that identity (ducking the orbit/walker) or a synth change (give
the prog29 lead more drive gain for level+sustain — broad blast radius, needs the render-diff
inventory + a version bump). Both are Arthur's call. Shipped this version for his ear first.
