# 2026.07.11 — JRN — Three-Sixty-One: from marimba to a soaring distorted lead

## Arc of the session
Arthur's feedback drove three passes on T16's guitar solo:
1. **"I don't hear any lead guitar."** Diagnosis: program 30 (distortion) is mid-*scooped*
   (−5 dB @ 650 Hz — a lead disappears there) and the pluck decays. Fix pass 1 (committed
   a0d7c5c): program 30→29 (mid-push), louder/drier, re-picked to sustain, ducked the drums.
2. **"Sounds like a wooden glockenspiel [marimba]... not a soaring lead with held notes,
   bends, hammer-ons."** Root cause: the distorted guitar is a decaying Karplus-Strong pluck;
   my re-picking made repeated mallet strikes. This needed a real sustaining voice.
3. **This pass:** built an opt-in sustaining synth voice + Fable re-composed the solo as a
   true soaring lead. Verdict: "sort-of OK" — good enough to commit; a dedicated
   guitar-synthesis review is deferred to a separate pass.

## What I built
- **Synth `DRIVE_LEAD` voice** (`voices.rs` + `altbank.rs`): opt-in (CC0 bank-select), so the
  4 other guitar albums stay byte-identical (render-diff confirmed, 299 tests pass, clippy
  clean). The sustain levers, in order of impact: `bright` 4800→11000 (the harmonics ring —
  `t60` alone did nothing because it clamps at pitch and only governs the fundamental), then
  `amp` 0.70→1.5 (drive the tanh hot → compression sustain, the only lever that reaches the
  fast-decaying high notes).
- **Fable's soaring-lead re-composition** (consult): held/ringing tones, two 9-beat soars
  across chord changes on bridge tones, a bent A6 finale peak, CC68 legato runs, 12 bends,
  CC1 blooms, plus a plucky under-layer on the same ch14. Every held tone consonant on every
  spanned downbeat incl. the finale {E,A,C} walker trap; no correctness rides on a bend.
  Captured in `wrk_docs/2026.07.11 - HLD - Three-Sixty-One soaring lead.md`.

## Surprises / decisions
- **Measurement trap #2:** `--solo` stems are peak-normalized, so a solo-stem RMS is NOT the
  guitar's level in the mix. Un-normalize via the CLI's reported peak; and audibility is a
  *band-limited* (700–2500 Hz) question, not broadband — a lead sits ~18–24 dB below a full
  mix's RMS by nature.
- **The guitar is not too quiet at source** (per-channel peaks: guitar 0.53 > saw-lead 0.28);
  it was mid-scoop + decay + drum masking, not level.
- **Fable flaked** (session limit, then auth error) mid-design, then returned complete. It
  delivered the harmonic framework first — which is the load-bearing part — so the notes
  followed. Resumed via SendMessage (retained its context).
- **Deferred:** the guitar timbre is only "sort-of OK." Arthur wants a separate guitar-synth
  review — logged in `scratchpad.md`, not chased here.
