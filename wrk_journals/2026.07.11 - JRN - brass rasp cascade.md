# 2026.07.11 — JRN — brass rasp cascade (BR12)

**Task.** Arthur: "how can we further improve the brass instruments … to get that
really sharp rasp from a real brass instrument?" → chose to **prototype the cascade
(Fork A)**. This log covers the analysis, the design, and the tuning.

## What the rasp actually is

The sharp brass edge (cuivré / "brassing up") is **nonlinear wave-steepening in the
bore**: at high blowing level the pressure wave steepens toward a shock as it
travels the tube, cascading energy into a slow-rolloff high-harmonic tail whose
spectral centroid climbs *super-linearly* with loudness. It is level- **and**
geometry-dependent (Gilbert/Campbell/Myers "brassiness potential" — trombone/trumpet
rip easily, horn only at ff, tuba barely).

## Why the pre-BR12 model fell short (diagnosis, not guess)

The `Brass` voice made its edge with a single memoryless `brass_valve` bias-tanh
whose drive `kws` rose **linearly** with the loudness scalar L and was clamped at
`BR_K_MAX = 3.2` for aliasing under **2× oversampling**. Two verified facts:

1. **Aliasing-limited, not drive-limited.** At forte the shaper already sits near
   the cap (trumpet `kws ≈ 3.0–3.1`). "Turn it up" just aliases.
2. **L ≤ 1.0 in sustain.** `L = 0.10 + 0.90·(vel/127)` at default pressure, so forte
   lives at L ≈ 0.8–0.95 and only vel127 reaches 1.0. Any "kick in above L=1" gate is
   near-dead, and a naïve convex `L^p` law would make forte *darker*. (This was the
   adversarial-critique catch that reshaped the plan; re-verified against
   `control_tick`.)

So the fix had to reshape the harmonic **rolloff** at the real forte operating
point, within the 2× envelope — not add drive.

## Design (BR12), all in `voices.rs`

- **`brass_rasp`** — morphs from the bare BR1 valve (`cascade_amt = 0`, pre-BR12
  sound, exact) toward a **two-stage cascade that SPLITS the drive**: stage 1
  softens as the cascade opens (`k·(1 − SPLIT·amt)`), a second knee (`K2`) re-hardens
  it. Splitting (not stacking) is the crux — stacking a 2nd stage on an
  already-hard forte note barely changes it (measured on/off ratio 1.04); splitting
  gives a genuinely slower, shock-like rolloff. Both stages k-normalised ⇒
  level-preserving.
- **`cascade_amt`** (control-rate) = `brassiness · MAX · smoothstep(LO,HI,bright) ·
  hf_derate`. Gated on `bright` (spans ~0.3–1.0 in sustain, >1 only under authored
  growl) so **mp stays clean and forte/ff/growl rip**; the growl term means an
  aftertouch note rasps harder — the opt-in cuivré rides the existing growl seam.
- **Per-program `brassiness` B**: trumpet/trombone 1.0, muted-tpt 0.9, section 0.7,
  horn 0.5, tuba 0.35; synth 62/63 = 0 (and the synth path never runs the cascade).
- **High-f0 derate** (quartic, floor 0.06): full rasp ≤ ~A4, shedding fast above so a
  high note's dense upper harmonics stay under the 2× alias floor. Top-register ff
  rasp is explicitly Fork-B territory.
- **Radiated-output opening**: the cascade also opens `out_lp` (`1 + RADIATE·
  cascade_amt`) so the extra harmonics leave the bell instead of being rolled off.
  Gated by `cascade_amt` ⇒ pre-BR12 / flat-twin render is bit-identical.

Runs inside the 2× oversampled sub-step, before decimation, so the extra harmonics
meet the same 13.5 kHz anti-alias cliff as BR1.

## Tuning (what the numbers said)

- First attempt (stack, not split): BR-O12 on/off = **1.04** — barely any rasp.
  Diagnosed: at forte stage 1 is already near hard-clip. → switched to split-drive +
  out_lp opening.
- Pushed the levers (MAX 1.0, SPLIT 0.6, K2 2.6, RADIATE 1.2) to the Fork-A ceiling.
  Diagnostic (trumpet, cascade on vs cascade-off twin, hp-band ratios):
  - C4 vel120: 3k **1.16** · 5k **1.24** · 7k 1.34 · 9k **1.43**; vel80 ≈ 1.02 (clean).
  - C3 vel120 up to 9k **1.47**; C5 vel120 up to 9k 1.24 (derate biting).
  The effect concentrates in the top and **blooms** with loudness — exactly right.
- **BR-O11 alias floor (A5 vel127) is the wall.** It sat at the 0.030 limit; the
  cubic derate left the floor non-binding at A5. → quartic derate + floor 0.06 gives
  A5 = **0.0289 dry / 0.0285 growl** (≈4% margin) with C4-and-below rasp untouched.

## Oracle

New **BR-O12 `brass_rasp_blooms`** — a differential (trumpet vs a `brassiness=0`
twin, same seed), fixed pitch C4, high band ≥ 5 kHz (clear of the 2.9 kHz bore
formant): forte on/off ≥ 1.18, mp ≤ 1.06, and forte ≥ mp + 0.12 (the bloom).
Follows the 2026.07.08 lesson (fix pitch, keep the band clear of static structure).
