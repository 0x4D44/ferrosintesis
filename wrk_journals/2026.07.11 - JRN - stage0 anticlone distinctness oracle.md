## Goal / acceptance criteria (HLD §9.1, §10 Stage 0)

A synth-wide anti-clone distinctness matrix: for every GM family, assert no two of
its 8 programs render as the same instrument, EXCEPT an explicit allowlist that
distinguishes GM-legitimate near-identical pairs (`Legit`) from known current
collapses tagged with the HLD stage that fixes them (`Collapse(stage)`). Must be
GREEN on current HEAD (so the current collapses are allowlisted), and must FAIL on
a new accidental collapse. ε calibrated so the exemplar good families (reed 64-71,
brass 56-63) clear it with margin (HLD §12 Q1).

## Design decisions

- **Home:** `testutil.rs`, new `#[cfg(test)] mod distinctness` — a synth-wide guard
  belongs beside oracle 34/35/36, not among per-family oracles in voices.rs.
- **Probe:** model-only (`samples=false`) — the layer the preset tables control and
  where the collapses are worst. Sample-layer clones remain covered by the
  render-diff inventory at integration (HLD §9.3). Two probe keys spanning
  registers, fixed vel/seed.
- **Feature vector (5, timbre not pitch):** centroid, flatness(500–9000),
  odd/even harmonic ratio (pitch-relative), HF fraction (hp3k/rms), h2/h1.
- **Distinctness score:** max over features of relative difference
  `|a−b|/(|a|+|b|+floor)`. Byte-identical clones → 0; distinct → far higher.
  `EPS` separates them; calibration test pins the good families above it.
- **Allowlist:** seeded from the coverage map, then corrected to the *measured*
  sub-EPS pairs via an `#[ignore]` matrix printer.

## Review + simplify + gate

- **Independent review** (one fresh-context reviewer, per Arthur's lean brief — no
  wider fan-out): no must-fix findings. Correct, deterministic, panic-safe,
  lint-clean. Three nice-to-haves; my calls:
  - **Applied #1:** compare features per probe key and take the max, instead of
    averaging the two keys' features before differencing. Averaging could cancel a
    bright-low / dark-high difference of opposite sign; per-key comparison keeps
    byte-identical collapses at 0 and gives genuine pairs more headroom. A genuine
    robustness gain to the central metric, no added complexity.
  - **Skipped #2** (silent-render legibility guard) — guards a hypothetical future
    silent voice; an energy assertion risks false failures on legitimately-quiet
    probes. Don't predict the future.
  - **Skipped #3** (Legit-entry self-pruning) — harmless by construction; a
    self-pruning mechanism would be overengineering.
- **Simplify/altitude:** covered by the review (clean); the per-key change is itself
  a simplification. No dead code / abstraction to strip. Not re-run as a separate
