## Surprises from the audit

- **The showcase's 12 missing programs are not filler.** 112–119 are eight *individually
  modelled* voices (tinkle bell, agogo, steel drum, woodblock, taiko, melodic tom, synth
  drum, and ReverseCymbal — its own voice family with its own unit tests). GM 15 is a real
  `DULCIMER` preset with a bespoke double-course mechanism. None has ever been heard in
  anything committed.
- **GM 1/2/3 are NOT holes.** `voices.rs:6687` dispatches `0..=3` to `acoustic_piano()`
  *without passing the program byte*, and the seed isn't program-derived — they render
  **bit-identically to GM 0**. My first read called them "low value"; they are zero value.
- **GM 31 guitar harmonics: the sounding pitch is not the written key.** The KS loop rings at
  **2f** below key 64 and **3f** at/above (`voices.rs:2083-2091`) — *non-monotonic across the
  boundary*: key 63 sounds higher than key 64. This is undocumented anywhere outside a terse
  `// G7 flageolet` marker. A naive chromatic audition of GM 31 renders a broken line. This
  is the single most audition-hostile behaviour in the melodic set.
- **A whole second instrument surface is undemoed**: the CC0 alt bank (`altbank.rs:997-1061`)
  — legacy church organ 19, tam-tam 14, sustaining DRIVE_LEAD 29/30, frozen v0.9 bowed 40–45
  and strings 48–54, and a complete second melodic-percussion set 112–119.
- **Drums are ~10% covered repo-wide.** The showcase triggers 5 keys against 48 implemented
  voices. Across all 108 committed MIDIs, **13 drum voices are triggered by nothing at all**
  (vibraslap, ride 2, both timbales, both agogos, both whistles, both guiros, both cuicas,
  mute triangle).
- **The engine reads 25 CCs, not the 8 the README documents.** Seven shipped features are
  undocumented *and* unheard: CC2 breath, CC66 sostenuto, CC67 una corda, CC71 resonance,
  poly key aftertouch, a guitar sympathetic resonator (GM 24/25 only), and a drum-room reverb
  (ch10 only, and it *ignores CC91* — a dry-drums bar is impossible from MIDI).

## Design

`wrk_docs/2026.07.12 - HLD - ferrosintesis reference audition.md`.

Key calls:
- **Separate artifact**, not a showcase rewrite — the showcase's musical shape is legitimate
  and signed off.
- Walk **0..127 in index order**, aliases included but *labelled* — a reference is looked up,
  so holes at 1/2/3 would be a lookup bug.
- **Three** gesture classes (SUSTAIN / STRUCK / ONESHOT), not per-family phrases. A bespoke
  phrase per family is precisely the showcase's mistake — it makes A/B impossible.
- Registers from the **voice's own constants**, not GM convention. Also keeps the LA sample
  layer engaged (`sampler.rs:817` silently drops the sampled attack outside ±1 octave).
- **Drop** the showcase's `check_arc` / `check_stereo` and analyze.py's arc + mono-loss
  checks. They demand a dynamic arc and stereo movement that a flat, dry reference must not
  have. Adapting them would force the reference to be musical.

## Review — the design was wrong in four load-bearing ways

Internal adversarial review (3 reviewers + cross-refutation): **30 of 31 findings survived**,
4 CRITICAL, several *measured against the real binary* rather than argued. My pre-review doubt
(above) was right, and there were three more I had not seen:

1. **Nothing in Rev 1 actually stopped a ringing voice.** CC121 only note-offs *held* notes
   (`engine.rs:1468-1471`); CC123 is a release. **Only CC120 (All Sound Off) chokes**
   (`engine.rs:1386-1398`) — and CC120 appeared *nowhere* in Rev 1. Plucks clamp at 14 s decay.
2. **"Dry" was CC91-only, which is not dry.** A program change re-derives a **non-zero**
   chorus/echo send from `fx_profile` — for **~76 of 128 programs**. The echo bus (0.34
   feedback) would have bled straight into the next slot's gap.
3. **`check_isolation` — the "defining oracle" — was vacuous.** A channel-level predicate that
   cannot fail once T1–T4 sit on one channel. Deleted. Isolation is *temporal*, not channel-based.
4. **`reset_controls` sends CC71=0, which *instantiates* the wah filter** — after which CC74=127
   parks it at 12 kHz instead of bypassing. So Rev 1's "raw voice" would not have been raw.
   Needed a forked `slot_reset` that omits CC71 and CC74 entirely.

Plus: default `jt`/`jv` humanisation would have silently destroyed the A/B premise — the one
thing the suite exists for.

**D2 reversed.** Rev 1 auditioned all 128 programs "for lookup integrity". A reviewer argued
the other side and won: a *labelled cross-reference* (`GM 002 — alias of GM 000, see 0:12`) is
strictly more informative than an unlabelled duplicate render, and costs zero seconds of audio.

## External review (Codex) — and the measurement that settled it

Codex called Rev 2 **BLOCKING**: "CC120 does not silence the renderer" — bus tanks, sympathetic
combs and reverb keep ringing under zero input.

So I measured it instead of arguing (`demos/ferrosintesis_reference/probe_cc120.py`),
RMS dBFS after the choke:

| case | pre-choke | +0.05–0.30 s | +0.30–1.0 s | +1.0–2.0 s |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| piano, dry (sympathetic comb, 0.85 fb, **not CC-gated**) | −30.5 | **−96.3** | −96.3 | −96.4 |
| koto, dry (**t60 = 7.0 s**) | −64.5 | **−96.3** | −96.3 | −96.4 |
| lead, **not** dry (echo bus) | −17.6 | −39.3 | −47.6 | **−67.1** |

**Codex's mechanism is right; its conclusion is wrong for this design.** CC120 genuinely does
not flush the tanks — but the design never *fills* them, because the sends are zeroed. CC120 +
dry hits the dither floor within **50 ms**, sympathetic comb and 7-second koto included. The
third row is the counterfactual that proves the dry discipline is load-bearing: without
CC93=0/CC94=0 the echo rings ~2 s past the choke.

**But two Codex findings stand, and one of them is the best finding of the whole review:**

- **The CLI peak-normalises the entire render** (`normalize_to_i16`: `scale = target / peak`,
  `engine.rs:2045`). So every "absolute floor" in Rev 2 was **globally coupled** — make GM 30
  2 dB louder and unrelated quiet slots fail. *My internal review missed this completely.*
  → Every threshold is now a **ratio**, which also immunises against the cross-machine render
  nondeterminism already in `scratchpad.md`.
- **Choking every slot hides the decay — and the decay *is* the instrument** (the alt-bank gong
  rings 6–15 s; piano sympathetic resonance is the piano). Rev 2 optimised for the oracle at the
  artifact's expense. → **Ring, then choke.**
- Also: an "every drum key sounded" oracle is **vacuous** — unmapped keys emit a generic tick
  (`drums.rs:2023`). → Drum coverage asserted structurally.

Codex's sandbox failed, so it never read the local HLD — it reviewed from source + my prompt.
Weighted accordingly: its source-grounded findings are gold, its doc-grounded ones part strawman.

## Implementation (2026.07.13)

Built `demos/ferrosintesis_reference/` data-driven: `programs.py` holds the
register/gesture/alias/alt-bank tables (16 GM-family rows + documented trap overrides),
and one slot emitter (`tracks/audition.py`) loops over it. My own `build.py` — NOT the
showcase's — so `build_score` skips the trailing `reset_controls` (which sends CC74=127
and would instantiate the wah filter). Six tracks, ~13 min. Byte-reproducible rebuild.

Both oracle suites green on the real render:
- Structural (`build.py --verify`): coverage (109 distinct voices + 24 alt + 48 drum keys
  + 10 effect CCs), flat (jt=0/jv=0), dry (CC91/93/94=0 after every PC), gap (CC120), registers.
- Audio (`analyze.py`): every slot `audible`, every slot `unmasked` — **the payoff, proven
  on the real render**. Both ratios (peak-normalisation and cross-machine drift cancel).

### As-built deltas from the signed-off HLD (all noted, none silent)

1. **`check_la_band` folded into `check_registers`.** The oracle as designed checked
   register *width*, but the phrase only ever plays a 7-semitone figure near the register
   root — so a note can't stray the >1 octave that drops the LA layer, and for LA voices
   the register IS the sampled range. A separate width oracle would just mirror sampler.rs
   zone tables (R8 warned that rots). `check_registers` already does the job.
2. **`audible` floor calibrated to −50 dB rel global peak.** The design left it TBD "against
   a real render". Measured: the quietest legitimate voice is GM 125 Helicopter at −40.8 dB
   (SFX 120–127 are intentionally quiet). −50 dB clears it by 9 dB yet sits 46 dB above the
   dither floor — cleanly separating "sounds" from a silent regression.
3. **~13 min, not the design's ~16.** The alias/T7-inline cuts landed lighter than estimated.

### Code review (found one real bug)

`/code-review` (8 angles, self-run) surfaced a **T6 filter-state bleed**: `effects.py`
`_section` used the melodic `slot_reset`, which omits CC71/CC74 to keep the melodic channel
virgin. But the resonance section *instantiates* the wah filter (CC74=45, CC71=127), and
nothing reset it — so the 8 sections after it (sustain, vowel morph, breath, bend,
aftertouch, portamento, the A/Bs) all rendered through a resonant lowpass at ~990 Hz.
Confirmed by dumping the CC stream (no CC121 anywhere). Fixed: `_section` now emits **CC121**
(the only hard filter reset, engine.rs:1458) before each demo. Plus a `dry_sends` helper
(the CC91/93/94=0 triple was repeated ~19×) and two trivial cleanups. Re-rendered, re-analyzed
— green.
