# 2026.07.13 - JRN - raw render loudness measurement

**Task:** measure what ferrosintesis outputs *before* peak normalization, to design
the loudness-normalization fix against real numbers (Arthur's step 1).

## What I did
- Wrote `crates/ferrosintesis-cli/examples/raw_dump.rs` (dev-only): calls
  `offline::render()` and dumps the raw f32 buffer to a 32-bit float WAV, bypassing
  `normalize_to_i16`. Chose this over arithmetic back-out because the CLI only prints
  `peak` to 2 decimals (too coarse for quiet tracks); the example gives full precision.
- `measure_raw.py` + `analyze_raw.py`: render all 104 album MIDIs raw, ffmpeg-measure,
  compute per-album inter-track spread, album-gain simulation, LUFS-target limiting
  feasibility, and note-energy correlation. Evidence in `raw_loudness.csv`.
- Validated the tool on Five Fables: raw-derived normalized loudness −20.9 vs committed
  −20.7 (0.2 LU). Good.

## Key findings
- Synth runs hot: 56/104 tracks clip raw (peak >0 dBFS, max +6.1). "Quiet renders" are
  mostly the normalizer *attenuating* clipping mixes; loudness = −1 − crest.
- Native inter-track spread is extreme (Through Lines 25.7 LU, VIGIL 17.5). **Album-
  relative gain ships sparse tracks inaudible** (Landing Lights −44.3 LUFS). Refutes
  both my earlier "go album-relative" and the review's "album-peak first step".
- Native staging IS faithful to composed dynamics (ρ +0.5..+1.0) — quiet tracks are
  composed sparse; the degree is just unlistenable and −38 LUFS tracks lean entirely on
  the normalizer.
- Per-track LUFS to −18 + −1 dBTP limiter: median 2 dB limiting, only 3 tracks >6 dB.
  Feasible and gentle. −16 too aggressive (93/104 limited). Never-limit leaves 66/104
  short of target → reintroduces the variance.

## Recommendation
Per-track LUFS-norm to −18 + true-peak limit, replacing peak-norm at main.rs:128.
Preserves within-track arcs (scalar), guarantees audibility, minimal limiting.
Follow-ups need ears: +20 dB makeup on −38 LUFS tracks may surface noise floor; the
hot/clipping raw synth is a deeper voices.rs/engine.rs gain-staging question.

## Surprises / dead ends
- Expected "quiet = synth too quiet"; actual = "synth too HOT, normalizer pulls it down,
  crest sets where it lands". Inverted my mental model.
- Bright Matter ρ −0.60 (only album with negative note-energy correlation) — a 5-track
  build-drop album; worth a look if it's ever redesigned.
