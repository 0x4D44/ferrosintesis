## The one new DSP: `fenv` (a one-shot filter envelope)

`SawStack`'s only cutoff modulator was a free-running, random-start-phase LFO — a
periodic wobble. Pad identity needs a NOTE-SYNCHRONOUS MONOTONE cutoff trajectory
(bright onset → warm bed, or slow swell → open) which no LFO/ADSR/detune can make.
The amplitude envelope's decay makes a note *quieter*, not *darker* — a different
sound. `fenv` is a one-shot multiplier on the cutoff, relaxing from `start_mult` to
1.0 over t60, composed multiplicatively with any sweep. `start>1` = falling (88, 90,
93); `start<1` = rising swell (92). One mechanism, both directions, four identities.

**Byte-identity by construction:** `fenv_k == 0.0` leaves `fenv` at exactly 1.0;
the control_tick retune runs `cut * fenv` = `cut * 1.0`; and a caller with no sweep
and no fenv skips the retune entirely (as before). Verified: sawstack_family_
signatures, determinism_bit_identical, golden_mix all green. **The fenv coefficient
is built at `sr / CTRL`** (control rate) — the MM-BUG-KILN-00003 trap, avoided.
