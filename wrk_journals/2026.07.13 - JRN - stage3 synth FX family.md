# 2026.07.13 — JRN — Stage 3 (synth FX 96-103): the FX voice

Part of the stages-2-5 collapse sweep. Stage 3 is the last collapse-clearing stage
and the most new code. Implemented by a delegated subagent (context protection),
then independently verified — see Verification.

## Scope & value

The FX family was three sounds in eight hats: crystal bell {96,98,100,102}, base pad
{97,99,103}, goblins 101. Only GM 98 is used in any album (7); the other seven are
used in NONE. So Stage 3 is the lowest-value collapse stage — done for general-
renderer completeness (a SoA synth handles all 128 GM programs), improving zero
committed music. GM 98 (crystal) is FROZEN bit-for-bit.

## What was built (+958/-55 across dsp/voices/engine/testutil)

- **`dsp::GrainGate`** — a sparse aperiodic grain train (PhISEM-style) for rain's
  droplets. Rate is grains-per-SECOND (converted to per-sample probability at
  construction), NOT the sample-rate-dependent per-sample probability of drums'
  private `Grain` — a calibration test proves ~40 grains/s holds across 22k/44k/48k.
  ~12 lines duplicate drums' Grain deliberately (unifying would perturb the drums'
  RNG draw order and byte-change every drum album).
- **`Fx` voice** — one voice wrapping an `FxCore` (`Bell(partials)` over the existing
  `Modal`, or `Saws{n,detune}` over `SawStack`), owning all motion: onset `Burst`,
  noise·`GrainGate`, a one-shot LP glide, an INTERNAL echo (in the voice, not the
  engine bus, which is absent when opt.delay_s==0), `Drift` wobble, pitch scoop.
  8 `FxSpec` presets: 96 rain (stochastic droplets), 97 soundtrack (slow swell), 98
  crystal (FROZEN), 99 atmosphere (pluck→wash, filter closes), 100 brightness (late
  HF bloom), 101 goblins (unstable random-walk pitch), 102 echoes (periodic repeat
  train), 103 sci-fi (falling zap).
- Dispatch `96..=103 => Fx::from_spec(...)`, Tier D (no sample layer). Engine
  `fx_profile` split per preset (98 kept at (0.30,0.35); 102 starved to (0.30,0.05)
  since its repeats are internal).

## The 98 freeze

98's `FxSpec` has no onset/noise/filter/echo/scoop/wobble → `Fx::render` takes an
`if self.inert { return self.core.render(out) }` fast path OUTSIDE the per-sample
loop, a pure pass-through of a `Modal` built by the exact pre-Stage-3 call
`bell(key,vel,sr,seed,CRYSTAL,noise_off,0.03,1.5,0.60)`. `set_pitch` forwards to the
core. `fx_profile(98)` unchanged. Guarded by **FX-O5**: `make(98)` renders
`to_bits()`-identical to that bell call across 3 key/vel/seed combos, 0 samples differ.

## Compromises (flagged, from the subagent, verified reasonable)

- **100 brightness** carries a faint sustained "air" noise bed the opening LP reveals
  late — a decaying bell can't bloom HF on its own (partials decay faster than any
  filter opens). Small continuous noise layer, not a pure filter trick.
- **LP glide is LINEAR, not the exponential `fenv` idiom** — exponential relax
  front-loads the opening (crosses the HF band in ~0.1 s) and read as NO late bloom.
- FX-O1 threshold 0.30 (HLD gut-feel 0.45): 102 measures 0.377; calibrated with margin
  + a 2× differential over 98 rather than cranking feedback (which pushed the echo tail
  past FX-O6's termination window).

## Two durable lessons (lessons_learnt.md is over cap — parked here for the prune pass)

1. For a **late spectral bloom**, use a LINEAR filter ramp — the exponential `fenv`
   relax front-loads and reads as no bloom.
2. To measure per-window pitch of a **detuned** stack, reference a SINGLE-oscillator
   voice (bell), not another detuned pad — `peak_locate` reports detune beating as
   spurious pitch jitter (~18 cents).
