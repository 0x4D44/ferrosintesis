# 2026.07.14 — JRN — GM 109 bagpipe (drone latch + Highland voicing) and GM 96 rain (event-density fusion)

Task: two audition-only voices flagged in the reference audition — bagpipe
"sounds terrible, nothing like a bagpipe"; rain "sounds like stones falling
rather than rain". Both are faithful-GM-player surface (no album uses 96 or
109), so the fix bar is realism plus a zero-contamination blast radius.

## Bagpipe — three root causes, three layers

1. **Engine drone lifecycle (the big one).** A chanter NoteOff called
   `release_bagpipe_drone_if_idle` unconditionally, so with a plain melody
   (no low drone-control note — the shape of every real-world GM 109 file)
   the channel drone was RELEASED at every note boundary and a FRESH drone
   spawned at the next NoteOn — at the NEW note's pitch. The "continuous"
   drone pumped an amplitude notch at every note and lurched pitch with the
   melody. Fix in `engine.rs`: the drone now LATCHES. NoteOff releases it
   only on an authored stop (the last drone-control hold ending); otherwise a
   block-rate `tick_bagpipe_drone_latch` releases it after
   `BAGPIPE_DRONE_HANG_S = 1.0 s` of chanter silence (the bag emptying). The
   tick is gated on a per-strip `bagpipe_drone_live` flag, so a channel that
   never spawned a drone — every non-GM109 channel, hence every album — does
   no work and sees no change.
2. **Drone voicing.** The old drone was root + a FIFTH at duty 0.22 (an
   all-harmonic buzz), velocity-scaled and quiet. Real Highland drones are
   two tenors + one bass, all octaves, cylindrical single reeds (odd-harmonic
   square — the clarinet's width law), constant level, never quite locked.
   Rewrote `BagpipeDrone`: tenor pair at the drone pitch detuned ±0.2%
   (→ a deterministic ~0.9 Hz beat on an A drone; UNEQUAL tenor gains
   0.42/0.30 so the beat never nulls), bass an octave below, duty 0.50,
   flat amp 0.075 (velocity ignored), sustain 1.0.
3. **Chanter mannerisms.** The chanter ran through the generic sax-shaped
   `reed()` dynamics: velocity amplitude, velocity-opened brightness and tanh
   drive, tongue chiff, onset scoop, vibrato. A chanter has none of those —
   constant bag pressure, articulation by grace-note flicks. Fix: post-
   construct overrides in `reed()` gated on `program == 109` ONLY (flat amp,
   vn/drive pinned at 0.82 pressure, flat env, chiff 0, scoop 1.0, vib 0), so
   `from_preset` and the sax/oboe/clarinet path — including RNG draw order —
   is untouched.

Gotcha worth remembering: after flattening amp/env/vn, the chanter STILL had
velocity dynamics (vel40/vel120 RMS = 1.120, inverted!) — the RD4 tanh
`drive = 1 + drive_vn·vn` compresses harder at high velocity, so soft notes
came out LOUDER. Velocity hides in the shaper index, not just the amp.

Second gotcha: the "no fifth drone" oracle first failed at 0.414 of root
after the rewrite — the bass drone's own 3rd harmonic (odd square at root/2)
lands exactly on the twelfth. That residual is physically real on any pipe;
the bound is set at 0.55 to reject only a dedicated fifth oscillator (0.73).

## Rain — an event-density problem

`FX_RAIN` gated its droplet band at ~15 grains/s — a countable drip train —
with the crystal bell at full level. 15 events/s sits below the ~20-30/s
auditory fusion edge, so each droplet reads as a discrete pitched OBJECT
(= stones); real rain is hundreds of events/s fused into a wash. Fix, scoped
to the 96 preset constants plus one new `FxSpec` field:

- `grain_hz` 15 → 380 (fusion regime), `grain_t60` 0.045 → 0.035,
  `noise_amp` 0.55 → 0.20 (the dense gate carries ~4× the RMS),
  `noise_fc/q` 5200/1.6 → 4800/0.9 (broadband hiss, not a tuned plink).
- New `core_gain` field (1.0 everywhere; 0.35 for rain) trims the bell to a
  faint pitched sparkle under the wash. `x * 1.0` is exact in IEEE 754, so
  every other preset's sample path is bit-identical; GM 98's inert fast path
  never touches it at all (FX-O5 freeze still green).

FX-O2 was REWRITTEN, not just retuned: the old oracle asserted leptokurtic
sparseness — it encoded the bug. The new `fx_o2_rain_96_is_a_fused_aperiodic_wash`
asserts fusion (gap fraction ≤ 0.02, envelope crest ≤ 1.8), keeps the v1
aperiodicity anti-cheat (env autocorr ≤ 0.30), and pins wash-dominance with a
faint bell hint (band ratios).
