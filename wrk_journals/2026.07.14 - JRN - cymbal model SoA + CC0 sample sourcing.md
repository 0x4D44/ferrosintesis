# 2026.07.14 — JRN — cymbal: SoA design + CC0 sample sourcing

Arthur's cymbal feedback drove two research streams. Both are captured in
`wrk_docs/2026.07.14 - HLD - MetalPlate V4 dense fixed-plate cymbal model.md`; this logs the
process and the sourcing detail.

## The four listener clues (all confirmed against code/measurement)
1. "Grain real cymbals don't have" → ~1 detuned mode-pair per ERB beating at 5–20 Hz (near-max
   audible flutter) + a globally coherent shimmer AM.
2. "Weak, especially 01:40+" → the ride-driven section; ride is pure model (no sample), 40+
   hits/10 s, its "ping" is highpassed noise, and every hit re-rolls the plate.
3. "SSHHH not a cymbal" at 2:47 (densest section) → the wash is ~90% white noise through a
   static highpass; N washes sum to whiter noise. Model flatness 0.83 vs real 0.58.
4. Raising the crash sample gain only slightly helped → the sample is on the attack; every
   defect is in the body.

## Design (Fable consult, verified by understanding)
SoA landscape: FDTD von Kármán (reject — research trap); reduced nonlinear modal (park, 2–6
wks); 808 (instructive not adopted); sampling (keep crash hybrid = commuted synthesis); SMS/
neural (offline calibration only). **Recommendation: MetalPlate V4 — a dense (~620) FIXED
per-key modal bank that BECOMES the wash, with t60(f) damping, a ride ping-lane, the wash
demoted to a contact click, engineered beats + shimmer deleted. All runtime-linear/feed-forward
→ BIBO-stable, no nonlinearity risk. ~1 week.** 8 ratio/differential oracles incl. a grain
oracle (4–25 Hz AM autocorr + cross-band coherence). I verified the ERB-density and
polyphony-flatness arguments and the compute arithmetic (~1.2 Gflop/crash hit, ~13× today,
viable with SoA layout + mode culling).

## Sourcing (parallel web research) — the assumption was WRONG
CC0 drum-kit cymbals DO exist, licenses verified from repo LICENSE files:
- **Virtuosity Drums** (`sfzinstruments/virtuosity_drums`, CC0 1.0) — stick jazz kit, ride
  (bow+bell), crash, hi-hat, ~4 RR. The recommended sample source.
- **Big Rusty Drums** (`sfzinstruments/karoryfer.big-rusty-drums`, CC0 1.0) — ride, china,
  crash, hi-hat.
- **VCSL** (`sgossner/VCSL`, CC0, same author as our VSCO bank) — hi-hat.
- Excluded (attribution/SA/Sampling-Plus): ltibbits Zildjian ride, quartertone hats, FreePats
  Muldjord (CC-BY), Philharmonia (CC-BY-SA). "Hat With The Phat" ride is GPL-vs-CC0 ambiguous.
- VSCO 2 CE confirmed to have ONLY orchestral crash + suspended (no kit ride/hats), matching
  our current usage.

## The decision for Arthur
Both paths are open. Engineering points MODEL-first for the ride (a 4-RR sample set
machine-guns on a 40-hits/10 s pattern; a fixed-plate model gives consistent-yet-varied hits),
SAMPLE for the exposed crash. Recommendation: build MetalPlate V4 (fixes all cymbals, keeps the
from-scratch identity, no repo growth); keep the crash sample hybrid; Virtuosity Drums is the
proven CC0 fallback for tripwire 3. The full sample path is viable but identity-shifting.
