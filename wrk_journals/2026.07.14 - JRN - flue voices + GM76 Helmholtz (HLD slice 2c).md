# 2026.07.14 — JRN — flue voices + GM 76 Helmholtz (HLD §2.8.5, slice 2c)

Implements the flue family (Wind, GM 72–79) on top of slices 2a/2b (commits
0678f0a, eb996dd), per the SIGNED-OFF voice-quality HLD §2.8.5, with the
§2.8.2 honesty scoping. Scope held: `Wind`/`WindPreset`/flue presets/flue
tests + the flue O-PRESSURE legs + the engine flue `set_breath` arms. Reed
code untouched.

## What landed (voices.rs WD9/WD10/WD11, engine.rs two flue arms)

- **WD9 pressure law** — the RD9 law verbatim (the `RD_P_*` constants are
  SHARED, not copied, so reed and flue pressure cannot drift):
  p = clamp(vel_floor + 0.11·env + authored CC2/CC11), smoothed ~15 ms at
  CTRL. `bright` (per-partial `amps[i]`, spawn-fixed before) and `vel_air`
  (now `air(p) = 0.35 + 1.1526·p²`) retune from p at CTRL rate. Anchored at
  `WD_P_ANCHOR = 0.7515` (= the law at vel 100, sustain 0.92) with
  `WD_P_GAIN = (1−WD_VN0)/0.2485` so the vel-127 ladder end and the vel-100
  table-read point land exactly where the accepted spawn law put them —
  WD-O2/O3/O9 (flute canary) pass untouched. Alias gating became a one-way
  `dead[]` mask so a pressure retune can never resurrect a gated partial.
- **WD10 chiff split (§2.8.5.1)** — the onset chiff no longer shares the
  f0-tracked bed bandpass: it gets its own broadband highpassed burst
  (max(1800 Hz, 1.2·f0), Q 0.7, own unconditional RNG draw — WD-O5/O8
  differential seams stay aligned), `WD_CHIFF_NORM = 0.36` matching the old
  narrowband level at the WD-O8 probe. Breath bed = narrow f0-tracked halo;
  chiff = broadband "tuh".
- **Air raise (§2.8.5.2)** — recorder breath 0.03→0.055 (−46 dB → −40 dB
  under the tone, still the family's purest; WD-O5 ordering rec < flute
  preserved), whistle 0.16→0.30 (−35 dB → −29 dB). WD-O5 bands re-pinned.
- **WD11 GM 76 Helmholtz (§2.8.5.4)** — dedicated path (`WindPreset.
  helmholtz`): a Q-32 constant-peak-gain bandpass at the sounding f0 driven
  by one-pole-shaped breath noise as the PRIMARY source (~75 % of steady
  energy), sine bank scaled to a 0.52 whisper (pitch anchor), broadband jet
  spill. Drive carries √(Q·sr/f0) — the gain normalisation across keys.
  Retuned at CTRL with bend/scoop/vib. Excitation dies with the envelope;
  the resonator state rings on, tracked by a peak follower that keeps the
  voice alive past env death (`helm_out` is summed OUTSIDE the envelope
  product). Pressure leg: louder (air(p)), airier (spill law 0.25 + p),
  slightly wider (excitation LP cutoff f0·(1.5 + 2.5·p)). ~95 shipped lines.
- **Engine seams** — two flue arms (spawn seed + CC pass), authored-gated
  exactly like the reed arms: `matches!(program, 72..=79) && (expr_authored
  || breath_authored || at_authored)`. `Wind::set_breath` mirrors
  `Reed::set_breath` (√(expr·breath), carve-down, growl unused).

## Estimator gotchas (both cost a red run to find)

- A tail-4096 Hann-DFT centroid over a LIVE breath bed wobbles ±8 %
  window-to-window — it cannot see a 10 % within-note ramp. P2 split into
  P2a (dry, deterministic, ladder pipes) + P2b (breath-on, full-window
  `spectral_band_rms` fraction — thousands of bins average the noise away).
- P2b's band floor must sit ABOVE every tone lattice line the voice owns:
  at 2.5·f0 the whistle's constant h3 diluted the ramp to 1.36x; at 3.5·f0
  the pure air ramp reads 1.97x.

## Tripwire found (out of scope, needs the lead's call)

`LaVoice` (sampler.rs, `impl Voice for LaVoice`) does NOT forward
`set_breath` to its wrapped model — only `SampleOverlay` does. So on the
samples-on engine path, authored CC2/CC11 timbre-tracking silently no-ops
for every LA-wrapped wind: brass 56–61 (BR9), reeds 68–71 (RD9, slice 2a),
and now flues 72/73 (WD9). The audible flue targets (74/76/78) are
unwrapped and unaffected. A 3-line forward in `LaVoice` would fix all three
families at once, but it changes committed 2a behaviour too — flagged
rather than fixed.

Every album with GM 72–79 channels will diff (default-on timbre change +

album with NO flue channels is a bug; the reed/sax families must be

`RD_P_*` constants are read-only from the Wind side).
