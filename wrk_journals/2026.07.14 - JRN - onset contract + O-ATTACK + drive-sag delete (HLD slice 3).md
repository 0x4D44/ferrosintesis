## §2.7 — the onset-ownership contract (LaVoice, one change for all wraps)

`LaVoice::render` now: `[0, fade_start)` sample-only (the wrapped model renders every block
so its state ages, output discarded); `[fade_start, fade_end)` ONE sum-to-one complementary
crossfade (smoothstep-shaped for C1 seam edges); `[fade_end, ∞)` model-only (the sample
branch also stops advancing at fade_end, so a spent transient no longer holds voices alive).
Crossfade law: sum-to-one (constant-voltage), NOT equal-power — sample and model at the same
pitch are correlated, equal-power boosts/cancels at the midpoint; verified by the
la_level_continuity probe across all 20 rows. Applies to voices.rs and altbank.rs wraps alike.

Consequences measured with a per-row window probe (wrapped vs model-only, 100 ms windows):

- **GM 24**: `LA_GUITAR` 0.25 → 0.42. At 0.25 under the contract the pick inverts (nylon
  key 45: late 0.069 > attack 0.059 — verified RED against the rewritten oracle, then
  restored). At 0.42 attack dominates at every probed key (ratios 0.16–0.56 voice-level).
- **GM 0–3**: `LA_PIANO` 0.42 → 0.90. The sample now REPLACES the model onset instead of
  stacking on it; at 0.42 it spoke 9 dB below the model's own attack (0.11 vs 0.32) and the
  model body overtook it (ratio 1.82 at key 48). At 0.90 the wrapped attack sits at the
  model-onset scale and every key is attack-dominant at the sample's internal ratio (0.85).
  **Largest blast radius, intended.**
- **GM 56–60**: `LA_BRASS` 0.35 → 0.45 (+ `LA_TUBA_GAIN_MUL` 1.8 for 58): with the model
  onset no longer underneath, the sampled attacks stepped 2.5×/3.8× (trumpet/tuba) under
  the model at the seam; re-matched to 1.98/2.02 worst-window.
- **GM 61**: sample layer dropped (model-only; `LA_BRASS_SECTION_GAIN` and the
  `program == 61` branch removed; `brass_bank` no longer claims 61). `BR_SECTION.players`
  3 → 5, `onset_scatter_s` 0.028 → 0.06, and the scatter is now STRATIFIED (each player
  draws inside its own slice of the window) so no seed can bunch the section into one
  instant. New pin `brass_section_61_skips_sample_layer` (bit-exact on/off).
  **BR-O7 metric rewrite**: the old `rise_10_90`-to-global-peak leg was beat-fragile (the
  envelope peak lands wherever the detuned players' random phases align — the 3-player
  section passed by luck; 5 players exposed it, and the rise stayed 0.05 s regardless of
  env-attack changes). Replaced with the front-loading energy fraction E(0–30 ms)/E(0–150 ms):
  solo 0.075 vs section 0.007–0.033 across seeds, pinned section ≤ 0.6× solo.
- **GM 48/49 split: NOT done** (HLD ranks it optional). Probe: `nearest` does alternate
  cello/violin zones with rising key (roots 196c→219v… interleaved), but the onset-centroid
  jumps at zone boundaries (1.12–1.78×) do NOT stand out above within-zone variation
  (up to 1.92×) — no boundary-specific discontinuity, so the split isn't warranted on this
  evidence. Revisit only if the slice-5 render-diff or the listen flags it.

## la_level_continuity rewrite (§3.2)

- The no-step leg is now DIFFERENTIAL: each adjacent-window ratio of the wrapped render is
  compared with the model-only render's ratio, and the wrap may not introduce > 2.4× beyond
  the model's own envelope shape. Reason (measured): an absolute cap cannot serve both
  families — a high plucked string legitimately decays 5.5×/100 ms once the pick is honest,
  while a slow-speaking tuba legitimately rises; only the wrap-INTRODUCED step is a seam
  defect. This is the §2.7 "measured, per-key seam limit".
- Added struck leg: for piano/nylon rows, the attack (first 150 ms, 50 ms windows from t=0)
  is the global peak — the leg that makes the GM-24 quiet-pick hack impossible (verified
  red at 0.25). Rows extended: piano keys 48/69, nylon keys 45/52/64; GM 61 row dropped.
