# 2026.07.14 — JRN — Sampled cymbal voice, Stage B+C (routing + oracles)

Follow-on from Stage A (the `ferrosintesis-samples-drumkit` CC0 asset crate,
cherry-picked here as the base). This stage builds the `SampledCymbal` voice,
routes the GM cymbal keys to it, and gates it with oracles.

## What was built

- **`SampledCymbal` (sampler.rs)** — pure sample playback, no model
  underneath. Velocity picks the bank's dynamic layer
  (`Bank::layer_for_velocity`), the engine's per-key hit counter picks the
  round-robin take, linear-interp resampling at a per-hit playback rate.
  `note_off` is a no-op (percussion rings; house rule, same as
  `Drum`/`MetalPlate`), `released()` = true, `choke()` fades −60 dB in
  ~15 ms (hat-grab/CC120 semantics).
- **Anti-machine-gun, two mechanisms** (both required; each proven
  necessary by a fail-first run):
  - (a) clean per-(key) round-robin cycling: `EngineCore.drum_rr[128]`, a
    per-key wrapping hit counter incremented on every ch-10 note-on and
    threaded through `drums::make(.., rr)`. Consecutive hits of the same key
    step takes 0→1→2→3→0… regardless of interleaved other drums.
  - (b) per-hit micro-variation from the note seed: playback rate
    ×(1 + U(−0.025, +0.025)) (±~43 cents — decorrelates waveform and decay
    length, inaudible as pitch on an unpitched cymbal) and gain
    ×(1 + U(−0.06, +0.06)).
- **Routing (drums.rs `make`)**: with samples on and kit V3/Brush,
  49/57→CRASH, 51/59→RIDE, 53→RIDE_BELL, 52→CHINA, 55→SPLASH return the
  sampled voice before the model arms; the old 49/57 crash `sample_overlay`
  arm was removed (unreachable — the intercept fires under exactly the same
  condition). `--no-samples`, V1, V2, and hats 42/44/46 are untouched.
- **Levels** calibrated against the model at vel 100 (RMS over 3 s):
  49→0.65, 57→0.68, 51/59→0.45, 53→0.45, 52→0.68, 55→0.34 — all landed
  within ±0.05 dB of the model (`sampled_cymbal_level_parity`, tol ±3 dB).
- Cargo: `ferrosintesis-samples-drumkit` added as an optional dependency
  behind the existing `embedded-samples` feature (mirrors the other two
  asset crates); `sampler::prewarm` warms the kit cache (one `pcm()` call
  decodes all 109 files). Version 0.18.0 → 0.18.1 in lockstep.

## The measurement surprise (worth remembering)

Plain NCC **cannot** catch a seed-modulo round-robin repeat once
micro-variation exists: ±2.5% rate jitter alone decorrelates a 30 ms cymbal
window to NCC 0.07–0.19 *for the same take*. The headline oracle therefore
uses a **rate-warp-searching NCC**: warp one hit over ratio candidates
±5.5% (anchored at the detected onset), correlate **first differences**
(tilts toward the take-specific HF sizzle, away from the shared plate modes)
over a 40 ms window starting 30 ms in (past the stick transient all takes of
one cymbal share). Measured separation on the ride bank: distinct takes
0.12–0.48, same take under micro-variation 0.70–0.86, clone 1.0 — threshold
0.60, ≥0.10 clear of both populations. Without the differencing the
rr1↔rr2 take pair read 0.71 and the margin vanished; without the late
window it was worse still (0.68 on the raw 0–30 ms window).

## Oracles (Stage C) and fail-first evidence

- `engine::sampled_ride_hits_are_decorrelated` — 8 same-key ride hits via
  the full engine. Clause 1: consecutive-pair warp-NCC < 0.60. Clause 2: no
  pair plain-NCC ≥ 0.98, no pair bit-identical.
  - fail-first (a): rr from seed-modulo → a consecutive repeated take
    warp-correlated **0.924** → red. Reverted.
  - fail-first (b): jitters zeroed → same-take pairs plain-NCC **1.000** →
    red. Reverted.
- `sampler::sampled_cymbal_round_robin_cycles` — hit indices 0..5 give takes
  [0,1,2,3,0]; single layer; no immediate repeat.
- `sampler::sampled_cymbal_velocity_selects_layer` — SFZ boundaries 42/85
  honored end-to-end; soft/hard play different PCM; gain stays continuous.
- `sampler::sampled_cymbal_choke_reaches_silence_within_20_ms` — post-choke
  peak < 2e-3 of pre-choke within 20 ms; voice terminates.
- `sampler::sampled_cymbal_has_no_boundary_click` — every routed take starts
  <5% and ends <2% of peak (the generator's 2 ms fade-in / squared
  fade-out), and the rendered stream enters and dies near zero.
- `drums::sampled_cymbals_route_only_on_v3_brush_with_samples` — routing
  seam: `--no-samples`/V1/V2 stay `kind() == "drum"`, samples+V3/Brush give
  `"sampledcymbal"`, hats never sampled.
- `drums::sampled_cymbal_level_parity` — ±3 dB vs the replaced model.
- Existing `v3_crash_tail_is_broadband_in_samples_off_and_on_modes`: the
  sampled-path clause `f3s >= 0.90*f3` encoded the overlay-era
  implementation (model + 5.5% sample). A real recorded crash is naturally
  less spectrally flat than the MetalPlate's filtered-noise wash and darkens
  in the tail (measured 0.605/0.464 vs the model's 0.742/0.820); the clause
  now holds the sampled path to the absolute broadband floor (0.18), the
  actual "cymbal, not tonal bell" guard. Model clauses untouched.

## Render proof (CLI, release)

- `--no-samples` canary: a 7-cymbal MIDI rendered by the trunk binary and
  this branch's binary is **byte-identical** (SHA-256 equal); with samples
  on they differ (the feature engaged). Model path untouched, proven.
- crash49.mid renders 6.1 s, RMS 0.034, peak 0.42 (non-silent).
- ride16.mid — key 51 struck 16× at 8 hits/s: 0 bit-identical hit pairs,
  max pairwise |NCC| over 120 pairs **0.129** (mean 0.033) on 100 ms onset
  windows — no machine-gun in a real dense ride.

## Dead ends / notes

- First warp-NCC design (raw waveform, 0–30 ms window) had no usable margin
  (distinct takes up to 0.71 vs same-take 0.90) — the takes share their
  stick transient. Onset-anchored, differenced, late-window fixed it.
- The brief said "hits 3-4 vel×3-4 rr" for hats; they ship in the asset
  crate but stay un-routed by design (bigger blast radius, out of scope).
- CRASH_SIZZLE is also un-routed for now (no GM key assigned; candidate for
  a future alt-bank or key-57 differentiation).
