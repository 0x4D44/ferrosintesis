# 2026.07.14 — JRN — sax rasp shaper (HLD §2.8.4, slice 2b)

Implements the sax rasp stage on top of slice 2a (commit 0678f0a), per the
SIGNED-OFF voice-quality HLD §2.8.4. Saxes GM 64–67 only; all other reeds ship
filter-only with the stage bypassed at spawn (`rasp: None` — no hot-path cost,
unchanged rng draw order, byte-identical render).

## What landed (voices.rs RD10, dsp.rs `Biquad::retune_highpass`)

- `ReedRasp`: ReedPulse → leaky one-pole integrator (18 Hz) → tent normalised
  to ±1 → `tanh(g·(tent + turbulence + b)) − dc_ref`, at 2× internal
  oversampling, two-biquad 15 kHz decimation cliff (BR1 idiom), then an
  f0-tracked 0.55·f0 highpass (T-N2 DC guard + kills the k=0 baseband
  turbulence copy).
- Bias `b = 2w−1` is exact for the ideal tent (each linear segment spans the
  full level range, so time-above-threshold is (1+b)/2 on both segments); the
  g→∞ limit is the duty-w bore rectangle — evens preserved by construction.
  `reed_rasp_bias_lands_duty` verifies the landing (±0.02);
  `reed_rasp_bias_is_load_bearing` pins the unbiased collapse (even fraction
  0.79/0.85 biased vs 0.01/0.02 unbiased at ff).
- dc_ref and the 0.5-RMS output normaliser are closed forms over the tent's
  uniform level distribution (lncosh/tanh at CTRL rate) — level does not ride g.
- Turbulence enters PRE-shaper (per-preset fraction of the register-scaled
  breath, so the RD-O8a breath=0 seam kills it), lowpassed at 0.30·f0 —
  full-band white through a memoryless shaper stays spectrally flat and makes
  hiss, not skirts (lesson added). Input rms scales with p² (flow-tracked) and
  authored growl (`set_breath` arg 2, aftertouch; default inert).
- Drive `g = (g0 + g_press·p)·derate(f0)`, per-preset (0.5, 15.5); derate
  smoothsteps to 0.35 over 800–1600 Hz (BR12 precedent, kept as margin).

## Oracle changes

- P5 stationarity generalised for rasp presets: the source-ladder slope is
  now pressure-law, so the F1-band argmax may walk UP the lattice; new legs:
  on-lattice at every step (±2 %), monotone upward, settles over the top two
  steps. Filter-only presets keep the strict pin.
- `reed_ab_gate_probe` now prints dry AND wet skirts.

## Surprises / dead ends

- First drive law (g_ff ≈ 4) collapsed G2 to ~1.7×: the tanh edge in the
  LEVEL domain maps to edge TIME via the tent slope — harmonics die above
  n ≈ g/2w. Restoring pulse-bright ff needs g_ff ≈ 16 (edge n ≈ 30 ≈ N_hi).
- First T-N2 read 2.9e-2 "sub-audio" — a single 12 Hz one-pole leaks the
  349 Hz carrier at −6 dB/oct (3.4 %). Four cascaded poles read the truth.
- The HLD's "h51 at −34 dB" fold arithmetic assumed a full 1/n ladder
  (near-sign drive); the shipped g_max caps the ladder at n ≈ 30, so 2× is
  clean even underated.
