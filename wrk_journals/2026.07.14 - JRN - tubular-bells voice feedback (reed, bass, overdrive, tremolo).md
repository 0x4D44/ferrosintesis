## Diagnosis (5-agent Workflow fan-out) → outcomes
- **Tremolo (p25) — SHIPPED.** The one objective, ear-independent win. First hypothesis
  (soften the pick-click) was PROVEN WRONG — killing the click changes the 4–20 kHz band
  by nothing; the "rain" is the per-note KS RE-ATTACK, not the click. Escalated to Fable,
  which found three stacked layers (fresh-spawn restart → inter-stroke release chop →
  treble damper killing the fundamental in ~130 ms) and built+gated a full tremolo-restrike
  fix (voice reuse + release-stretch + carrier-hold sustainer + pick-catch + h1-floor).
  See `wrk_docs/2026.07.14 - HLD - tremolo restrike ...`. Verified: real ch4 key-76 carrier
  0.10→0.36–0.47 (4–9×); 436 tests green; byte-identical off the tremolo path.
- **Reed fifth (p20) — REVERTED, now a judgment call.** The p20 additive table's
  half-integer partials (1.5/2.5/3.5/4.5f) paint a parallel fifth ("organum ghost"). Removing
  them (integer harmonics) killed the fifth — BUT broke `reed_organ_..._free_reed_character`,
  which pins those off-harmonic partials as intentional free-reed character (off_harmonic_residual
  ≥ 1.5× church organ). The quints are simultaneously a parallel-fifth AND test-pinned character;
  which reading wins is an ear call. Reverted; A/B clip handed to Arthur. Proper fix if he wants
  the fifth gone: re-realize the character as reed-NOISE/beating, not clean quints.
- **Bass (p33) — judgment call.** Not a broken voice (fundamental already dominant); it sits
  ~8.6 dB under a triple-doubled melody with no kick until 4:08. Boosting the widely-used BASS
  preset (+3 dB) or trimming the melody doublers both have album-wide blast radius. Arthur's call.
- **Overdrive (p29) — judgment call.** The e-bow warble the diagnosis first suspected does NOT
  fire on the short riff notes (proven: no 2.8 Hz throb, clean decaying plucks). Faithfully
  rendered overdrive; "odd" is likely the transcription's heavy-overdrive choice or the doubling.

## Round 2 — Arthur's decisions (2026.07.15)

  broadband reed BUZZ (the physically-correct realization of the reed's inharmonic
  character). The free-reed-character test's residual clause measured energy at
  1.5/2.5/3.5/4.5f — i.e. it measured the quint FIFTH itself and could only pass WITH the
  bug; a harmonic reed is mellower than the bright legacy church organ (GM20 upper-harm 0.04
  vs GM19 0.15), so the GM20>GM19 comparison can't be honoured. Corrected the clause to an
  honest absolute broadband-buzz floor; added `reed_organ_gm20_has_no_parallel_fifth`.
- **Bass (0:48): "Yamaha-XG driving bass."** Re-voiced GM33 from the dark flatwound/McCartney

  Blast radius: every GM33 album bassline (widely used) — approved re-voicing.
- **Overdrive (3:38): parked** at Arthur's request.

## Round 3 — bass low-end kick, sub-octave (shelved), then muffled flatwound (2026.07.15)
The driving-XG bass (round 2) still lacked "deep bassy kick"; Arthur then reversed course
entirely to a MUFFLED flatwound. The arc:

- **Low-end KICK.** Grounded in a REAL reference: rendered the actual Tubular Bells bassline
  through a real **Yamaha MU-80 (XG hardware)** via the mdsc55 emulator (ROMs on disk). The
  XG bass onset is LOW-dominated (spectral centroid ~100-135 Hz); ours was mid-forward (~248).
  Added a `kick` field: a deep lowpassed-noise onset THUMP (KICK_LP_HZ 55, KICK_T60_S 75 ms),
  in parallel like the stop thump, gated on `kick>0` so every other preset stays bit-identical.
  A tonal-sub-octave-punch first attempt was ABANDONED (2·fs=f0 makes shaping beat on the
  fundamental; and a noise thump matches the XG's broadband low attack better). Iterated the
  low weight up to Arthur's "+2-3 dB" (sub 0.24→0.72, low body 50/100 Hz +9/+6.2, kick 3.9).
- **Sub-octave — Fable consult, then SHELVED.** Fable designed a windowed f0/2 sub-octave
  (W(fs) level law = anti-rumble fade below 30 Hz + rise to a capped top, pure sine, per-note
  scalar not filter, 12-assertion oracle) — see the HLD. But implementing it revealed the
  design's load-bearing premise is FALSE: the KS bass ALREADY emits a strong pitch-tracking
  f0/2 SUBHARMONIC (dry isolated A2 measures f0/2 +5.7 dB OVER the fundamental; E2 +0.3, F#3
  +11.7; tracks f0/2, not the fixed body peak). It's a LONE f0/2 line (no 1.5·f0), so it reads
  as sub-reinforcement/"bigness" — which is *why v5 already sounds big*. A dedicated
  oscillator is redundant (the band is saturated) AND octave-flip-risky. Reverted the
  oscillator; HLD marked SHELVED with Fable's reasoning preserved. (Independently verified
  Fable's "XG sub-octave is an artifact, not a design law" verdict with a 7-point XG scan:
  the level jumps non-monotonically with pitch = multisample-zone boundaries.)
- **MUFFLED FLATWOUND (final).** Arthur: "twangy — I like it more muffled, McCartney with old
  flat strings." Kept the v5 low weight + kick, but rolled the top off hard: bright 1550→1100,
  pick_lp 1250→850, out_lp 2100→1150, pos 0.30→0.37 (neck-ward), REMOVED the electric bite
  (pickup_rlc → 0), cut the roundwound zing (attack_noise 0.30→0.12), dropped the 760 Hz
  presence bump. Soloed centroid 148→99 (twang band 640-1280 down ~8 dB); low still leads.
