## Surprises / gotchas

- The GM6 velocity-spread oracle's two probe renders use different seeds, so
  round-robin excitation luck alone measured 1.47× of the 1.5× budget — the
  velocity law was almost irrelevant. Fixed at the model layer (see above),
  not by touching the oracle.
- `off_harmonic_residual` cannot measure a Reed's breath: the pulse's own
  off-lattice floor dominates (doubling breath moved the residual 0.5 %).
  The same-seed differential isolates the air exactly (breath is a pure
  additive level; the twin's rng draw order is identical).
- The Reed's shared RD_SCOOP_K (τ ≈ 8 ms) is a sax articulation — invisible
  to the free-reed scoop oracle and inaudible as a bend. Hence per-preset
  `scoop_k`.
- Timpani release semantics: Modal's release env multiplies the sounding
  decay, so lengthening the sounding T60s *shortens* the relative post-
  note-off ring unless the release T60 moves with it.
