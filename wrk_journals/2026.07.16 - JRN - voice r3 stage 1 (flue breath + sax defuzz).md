## Intent (one line)

Kill the flue pipes' "shaking sshsshssh" (a deep pitch-vibrato-locked breath AM at the
~4 Hz fluctuation-strength peak) and the saxes' "fuzz on top" (p⁴ rasp turbulence ~200×
the real module), by model tuning only — no new samples, no other families.

## STEP 1 — UNDERSTAND (code read, worktree)

- Flue breath AM: `voices.rs:6778` `breath_mod = 1.0 + 0.5*self.vib_val`, applied to all
  three noise components (helm jet 6788, bed 6794, muraiki 6799). `vib_val` is the pitch
  vibrato LFO (control-rate, preset rate 4.5-5.5 Hz) → the AM is coherent with pitch and
  deep (±50%). Presets carry the breath level: PAN_FLUTE 0.24 (6405), SHAKUHACHI 0.30
  (6455), BLOWN_BOTTLE 0.80 (helm), WHISTLE 0.30, RECORDER 0.055, FLUTE 0.09.
- RNG alignment: the render loop keeps a FIXED `rng.white()` draw count per sample so the
  WD-O5 differential seam (`wd_o5_breath_fraction_bands_and_ordering`, 18035, renders
  full vs breath-zeroed with the same seed and subtracts) stays aligned. `control_lfo`
  (voices.rs:103) draws one `rng.white()` for its rate jitter → a flutter LFO must use a
  SEPARATE throwaway `Rng`, not `self.rng`.
- Sax rasp: `ReedPreset.rasp = (g_base, g_span, noise_base)` = `(0.5, 15.5, X)`; X per sax
  SOP 0.12 (7767) / ALTO 0.085 (7785) / TENOR 0.058 (7803) / BARI 0.040 (7821). Turbulence
  rms = `noise_base·p⁴·(1+span·growl)` (`ReedRasp::set_drive`, 8152). Only `noise_base`
  (X) needs cutting; the tanh harmonic drive `g = 0.5+15.5·p` stays (that IS the reedy
  edge). Oracle `reed_sax_rasp_blooms_with_pressure` (17313) asserts `ff_db >= -40` — will
  drop below with a smaller rasp; re-pin the floor (bloom-with-pressure intent survives).

See the HLD for the design + acceptance criteria.
