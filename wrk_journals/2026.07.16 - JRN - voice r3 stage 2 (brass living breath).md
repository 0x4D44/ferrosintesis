## The complaint (round 3)
Natural brass 56–061: "attacks good, holds synthetic." Round-3 root cause (roadmap PLN,
integrated to trunk): the sustain is **frozen** — with no authored CC/aftertouch (all
committed album MIDIs author none) every timbre driver is constant during the hold
(bloom=1.0, pressure=1.0, growl=0, bite decayed). Measured centroid p5–p95 wander
**2–13 Hz** vs SC-55 **88–191 Hz** (7–75× less motion); RMS ripple **0.1–0.4 dB** vs
SC-55 **0.5–4 dB**. Solo naturals also have **no vibrato** (`BR_DEFAULTS.vib=(0,0,0)`).
A perfectly periodic waveform is the textbook synth tell — exactly why the LA+rasp attacks
sound good and the holds don't.

## The fix (mechanism)
Fold a shallow, zero-mean, decorrelated **living-breath** into the `l` loudness scalar in
`Brass::control_tick()` (voices.rs:9215). `l` is the single scalar that already drives the
whole timbre — lip cutoff, shaper index `kws`, the BR12 rasp cascade, and the radiated
`out_lp` corner. Breathing `l` therefore makes the whole spectrum wander together (a
physical brass note: as breath pressure micro-varies, brightness+drive+level all move
together). Reuses the Stage-1 `control_lfo` primitive.

## STEP 1 — UNDERSTAND (done)
- Confirmed the frozen mechanism by reading `control_tick`/`render`: at sustain `l` is
  constant; the only motion is broadband breath NOISE (line 9376) + per-player Drift
  (pitch random-walk). Naturals have zero vibrato.
- Sensitivity: `out_lp` corner = `out_base * 2^(out_oct*bright)` with out_oct=4.2 — very
  sensitive to `bright`(≈l). So a SHALLOW l-breath yields a healthy centroid wander; depth
  can stay small (guard against overshoot → wobble).
- Synth brass 62/63: `l` has **no downstream effect** (k span 0, no out_lp, brassiness 0,
  not oversample) → breathing `l` cannot color synth. Gate anyway (depth=0 for synth) so
  the natural-only guarantee is by construction, and guard it with an oracle.

## STEP 3+4 — REVIEWS (2 adversarial + Codex gpt-5.6-sol xhigh)
Strong three-way convergence. Load-bearing changes adopted:
- **O-LIVE measured the wrong variable (CRITICAL, unanimous).** The fix modulates *timbre*
  (`l→bright→fc/kws/cascade/out_lp`), not amplitude (`sum·amp·e`, fixed `e`). An envelope-AM
  oracle false-passes a tremolo / false-fails a good wander. → Rewrote as a **centroid-wander
  p5–p95 differential** (reuse `spectral_centroid`), floor+ceiling; envelope-AM → tremolo ceiling.
- **Section 61 excluded (MAJOR).** 5 players share one `l` → coherent ensemble pump; already
  alive; scope narrowed to **solo naturals 56–60** (`oversample && players==1`, API-free).
- **Value noise, not sines (MED).** Two sines leave a 0.8 Hz beat → 0.37 Hz slow-swell tell
  under jitter. Value noise (smooth interpolated random targets) is aperiodic — kills the
  wobble at the root. Codex literally recommended this.
- **Attack ramp (HIGH).** Breath fades in [0.15,0.40]s so the praised onset stays bit-identical.
- **`render_brass` frozen** to isolate the ~16 static-timbre oracles (zero-mean does NOT protect
  ratios under the nonlinear l→timbre map; BR-O3 highest risk). Test synth 62 AND 63. Depth
  0.06→0.035 first-cut (out_lp very sensitive; likely overshoot).

Full findings + resolutions in the HLD §9. Design → **SELF-APPROVED**, proceeding to implement.

## STEP 6 — IMPLEMENT (tests-first)
- `BreathNoise` value-noise generator (after `control_lfo`): smoothstep-interpolated random
  targets in [-1,1] every `hold_s`, own rng. Bounded/zero-mean/aperiodic — unit-tested.
- `Brass.breath_gen`/`breath_depth` fields (note: `breath` was taken — it's the BR6 noise LEVEL;
  renamed to `breath_gen`). Gate `oversample && players==1` → solo naturals 56-60 only.
- Fold into `l` in `control_tick`: `l = (base_l * (1 + depth*ramp*breath.next())).min(1.3)`.
  `ramp = smoothstep(0.15,0.40,t/sr)` → attack bit-exact (ramp=0 ⇒ ×1.0 exact).
