## The complaint / root cause (roadmap PLN)
Harp "octave-hollow": h2 measured **+5.7 dB OVER f0** at C4, where real SC-55 has h2 at
**−26.6 dB** (f0-dominant); MU-80 −19.4. The octave partial dominates instead of the
fundamental. Fix: a **1/n excitation tilt** so f0 dominates, WITHOUT killing the harp's
shimmer. Plus recalibrate the **defect-encoding** `harp_46` oracle (currently rewards the
octave-hollow balance) against SC-55.

### Fix (design basis)
Harp-only `exc_tilt`: an f0-tracking roll-off of the KS excitation (filter a STEADY-STATE
periodic signal — concat copies, keep last period — NOT a single period). Calibrate steepness
for f0-dominance (h2 clearly below h1, toward SC-55) while keeping shimmer. Recal harp_46_shimmers
to f0-dominance + a decoupled bounded-shimmer floor. Body peak + shimmer (bright/pick_lp) left
(minimize scope); re-trim HARP.amp if the level oracles move.

## STEP 2 — DESIGN + STEP 3/4 REVIEWS
See `wrk_docs/2026.07.16 - HLD - voice r3 stage 2 (harp excitation tilt).md`.
Initial design = harp-only excitation lowpass tilt (`exc_tilt` field). **Reviews reworked it twice:**
- **[CRITICAL, all 3] A monotonic lowpass can't hit the octave target without killing shimmer**
  (max f0→2f0 correction 12 dB; the −8 dB floor needs cutoff ~0.6·f0 → craters h4-h8 = the reverted
  K2 "buries the pluck"). Codex: the octave is EVEN-harmonic → use the even-selective PICK COMB, not
  a tilt. The comb lever already exists: `HARP.pos`. → pivoted to **`pos`→~0.5** (centre pluck nulls
  evens, keeps odd shimmer). No new field, no filter, no gate — harp-only by construction.
- **[CRITICAL, DSP review — BLOCKER] The +3.5 dB octave may be a MEASUREMENT ARTIFACT.** The modeled
  filters (comb −0.8, body EQ FAVORS f0 by −1.57, loop decay favors f0) predict h2 ≈ −4 dB
  (f0-DOMINANT), contradicting the measured +3.5 by ~8 dB. Cause suspect: two detuned KS loops
  (f0·1.0013, mix 0.74/0.26) BEAT at 1.47 s → a single-bin Goertzel at 2f0 swings ±6 dB with beat
  phase. → **STOP before implementing: re-measure detune-robustly** (band-integrated, beat-averaged,
  ours vs SC-55). If it's an artifact and the harp is ~SC-55-close, ABORT the slice (don't "fix" a
  non-defect). If a real octave excess survives, proceed with the `pos` fix recalibrated to the truth.
- (Also: oracle must band-Goertzel around 2f0 for detune, shimmer = ODD uppers, two temporal windows.)

## STEP 1b — RE-MEASURE (detune-robust) → DEFECT REAL, but MECHANISM UNRESOLVED → PARKED

**The defect is REAL** (artifact hypothesis refuted): beat-robust, band-integrated, `--no-samples`-
identical → it lives in the modeled voice. And it's far WORSE + more localized than "+3.5 at C4":

| note | ferro h2/h1 | SC-55 | excess |
|------|--------:|--------:|--------:|
| G3 | +16.0 | −9.7 | **+25.8** |
| A3 | +16.5 | −9.9 | **+26.3** (worst) |
| B3 | +15.7 | −9.9 | +25.6 |
| C4 | +6.1 | −9.8 | +15.9 |
| C5 | −15.7 | −5.8 | −10.0 (deficit) |

A **pitch-localized octave excess centered on G3–C4** (the octave partial ~16 dB LOUDER than the
fundamental — those notes sound thin / an-octave-up), tracking a FIXED ~400–520 Hz band. SC-55 is a
clean f0-dominant reference (−6..−10) everywhere.

**But the MECHANISM is unresolved — all obvious levers ruled out:**
- **Pick comb (`pos`)** — RULED OUT. The comb is pitch-INDEPENDENT in harmonic space (h2/h1 ≈ −0.8 dB
  at every note); the defect swings 32 dB across the register (+16→−16). A `pos`→0.5 even-null would
  wreck C5 and still leave G3–B3 hot. (Killed the Codex/pos pivot.)
- **Body EQ** `[(90,·,3.5),(180,·,2.8),(400,·,1.8)]` — RULED OUT. Peak gain is dB (`10^(g/40)`), so
  ±1.8 dB max; ~14 dB too weak to cause +16 dB, and at C4 it net-FAVORS f0. (Killed the measurement
  subagent's attribution.)
- **Membrane/soundboard resonators** — RULED OUT. HARP inherits `membrane: &[]` from DEFAULTS (empty);
  the resonator stage (voices.rs:3886) is skipped for harp. No `cab` either.
- Comb + body + loop-decay together predict h2/h1 ≈ −4 dB (f0-dominant). Measured +16 at A3. **An
  ~20 dB gap with no identified source** — it must be in the KS loop signal path (the two detuned
  loops horiz@f0 + vert@f0·1.0013 mixed 0.74/0.26, the loaded-excitation projection, or an unmodeled
  interaction) but a decomposition to localize it is beyond a lean-slice budget.

**DECISION: PARK the harp slice.** The defect is real and high-value, but I pivoted the fix 3× and
cannot make a CORRECT (right-layer) fix without the mechanism — and a body-EQ patch is provably too
weak. This is a research-level blocker warranting a dedicated decomposition session (instrument the
KS signal path: excitation spectrum → per-loop output → mix → body) and/or Arthur's knowledge of the
harp's design, not an autonomous guess. NO code changed; NO integration. Findings preserved here.
