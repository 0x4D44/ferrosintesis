### Fix (design basis)

of the rasp chain). Target: h3 ≈ −6 dB, centroid ≈ 1800-1900 Hz. cone ~0.20-0.30 per sax.

## STEP 2 — DESIGN + STEP 3/4 REVIEWS

- A 1/n saw adds h1/h2 (worsens ferro's already-hot h2, risks f0 phase-suppression) AND is
