### Findings (measured)
- ALTO h3 **−17.0 dB** vs SC-55 **−7.2** (10 dB gap); centroid 1411 vs 1905 (490 Hz).
- TENOR h3 **−19.2** vs SC-55 **−5.8** (13 dB gap); centroid 1067 vs 1819 (750 Hz).
- The rasp (RD10) DELIBERATELY re-lands the duty-w rectangle (bias=2w−1 → g→∞ limit = same
  rectangle) → it PRESERVES the null. So h3 must be filled by a PARALLEL source, not by
  touching the rasp/duty.
- Even-harmonic fraction ferro 0.89/0.92 vs SC-55 0.47/0.57 — ferro far ABOVE the oracle
  floor 0.35, so adding odds (a saw) moves toward SC-55 with margin.
- **No existing oracle encodes the nulled h3 as correct** → NOT a defect-encoding recal.

### Fix (design basis)
Per-preset `cone` blend: a parallel `BlepSaw` (complete 1/n series — the cone-vs-cylinder
correction; BlepSaw already exists in dsp.rs) summed into the pre-formant signal, sax-gated
(cone=0 → Option None → non-sax byte-identical). Preserves bias=2w−1 (parallel, downstream
of the rasp chain). Target: h3 ≈ −6 dB, centroid ≈ 1800-1900 Hz. cone ~0.20-0.30 per sax.

## STEP 2 — DESIGN + STEP 3/4 REVIEWS
See `wrk_docs/2026.07.16 - HLD - voice r3 stage 2 (sax body cone-fill).md`.
Initial design = parallel BlepSaw. **Reviews (2 adversarial + Codex) killed the saw:**
- A 1/n saw adds h1/h2 (worsens ferro's already-hot h2, risks f0 phase-suppression) AND is
  low-harmonic-dominated (its centroid ~2.5·f0) → it would LOWER the composite centroid, WIDENING
  the SC-55 gap. Fills the notch but fails the "brighten" half of the goal; an h3-only oracle
  ships it green. Both external reviewers independently prescribed an upper-harmonic residual.
- → **Pivoted to `ConeResidual`**: additive sine bank of partials h3–h8 (NO h1/h2), per-note
  retuned (follows vibrato), alias-free (sub-Nyquist sines, no polyBLEP) + DC-free by construction.
- Oracle strengthened: assert centroid≥off + f0 stable + h2 unchanged (not just h3/f0 ratio).
- O-SCOPE: enumerate all 11 presets incl. HARMONICA (missing from the reed arrays). render-diff
  FULL-catalog (not --program subset) to prove non-sax byte-identity. Semver rationale corrected
  (ReedPreset is pub(crate)-internal). reed_o13 DC + reed_o6 span + rasp-differential added to verify.
Design → **SELF-APPROVED**, implementing.
