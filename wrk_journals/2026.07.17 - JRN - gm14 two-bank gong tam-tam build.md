## The decision (Arthur, baked into the HLD)

GM14 becomes a **two-bank** program:
- **CC0=0** — Tubular Bells (GM-correct, **unchanged, byte-identical** — guarded by G5).
- **CC0=1** — near-pitchless **orchestral tam-tam** (NEW sampled one-shot; modeled dense-cloud = the `--no-samples` fallback).
- **CC0=2** — pitched **gong ageng** (the album use). The two *Through Lines* movements move CC0=1 → CC0=2 so they keep the pitched gong.

Goal (Arthur): the default GM set as realistic as possible; compatibility with the wider MIDI world matters more than our local set.

### Gong pitch analysis (Goertzel scan of the sustain, done 2026.07.17)
The Gong Ageng is genuinely low + pitched, with two strong partials (the "ombak" beat):
- soft (261890): **~80 Hz (D#2)** dominant, ~101 Hz (G2) second.
- loud (261893): **~101 Hz (G2)** dominant, ~80 Hz (D#2) second.

→ Treat **root ≈ 80 Hz (D#2)** as the repitch anchor (the partial common to both, and closest to the album's C2=65 Hz colotomy anchor → mild downshift, factor ~0.81). The gong's fold target and register are a **design output of the sample's measured root** — write the fold oracle FROM the measured root, don't inherit the modeled tam_tam's 98 Hz.

## Decisions made this build

1. **One new crate `ferrosintesis-samples-gong`** holds BOTH one-shot banks (CC0 tam-tam + CC BY 3.0 gong). Rationale: they are one coherent subsystem, both **full-ring one-shots** (a different asset kind from the orchestral *attack-transients*, so they do NOT belong in `-orchestral`), one publish-chain addition. `license = "CC-BY-3.0 AND CC0-1.0"` (crates.io-valid SPDX). Ship a **NOTICE** (CC BY 3.0 attribution for the gong) + a `PROVENANCE.md` (mirror the drumkit crate) + license texts. *(Reversible; if the tam-tam is deferred, the crate is gong-only and `license = "CC-BY-3.0"`.)*
2. **Per HLD tier policy, CC BY 3.0 = Tier 2 = part of default `embedded-samples`** (not a separate opt-in). So the published `ferrosintesis` embeds the gong and must carry the NOTICE. Not a fork — settled by policy.
3. **Reproducibility:** commit BOTH the raw `gong-src/` WAVs (irreplaceable — Freesound needs a login, cannot be auto-fetched) AND the processed crate `samples/`. +~6 MB raw is worth offline-reproducibility for un-re-fetchable source. Requires two new `.gitignore` exceptions.
4. **Sequence gong (CC0=2) FIRST** — it is album-critical and the sample is in hand; the tam-tam (CC0=1) follows (its modeled fallback already exists, and the albums are moving OFF CC0=1). If the loop must stop early, the gong is the shippable win.
