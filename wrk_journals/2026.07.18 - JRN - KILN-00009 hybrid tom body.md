# 2026.07.18 — JRN — KILN-00009: hybrid tom body (the high-tom fix)

Task: fix MM-BUG-KILN-00009 — the sampled kit repitches one rack tom (~181 Hz)
across the high tom keys, thinning/chipmunking the top of fills.

## The plan we started with didn't survive contact with the data
Arthur first chose "source a new mid/high tom." I hunted the CC0 sample libraries
this repo already trusts and MEASURED their toms (calibrating the pitch method on
the known Virtuosity htom=181 → read 176, ltom=113.5 → read 111, so the method is
sound):

| Source (CC0) | toms measured | highest |
|---|---|---|
| Virtuosity Drums | htom/ltom | **176 Hz** |
| Big Rusty Drums | tom_14/15/18/22 | 116 Hz |
| VCSL | Tom1/Tom2, tenor hi/lo | 111 Hz |

Every curated CC0 tom sits ≤176 Hz; GM keys 48/50 want 293/352 Hz. Real toms just
aren't tuned that high — the instruments that are (rototoms/octobans) aren't in the
curated libraries, and Freesound's need OAuth (lossless) or are lossy previews. So
"add a higher tom" is unbuildable at this crate's quality bar. Reported that; Arthur
chose the **hybrid body** approach.

## The fix: model-under-sample, applied to drums
`HybridTom` (drums.rs) sums a modeled tom body (at the key's target pitch) under the
sampled attack on keys 47/48/50. The sample keeps the transient/character; the model
puts back the shell/low-mid the repitch thinned. Same idea as the melodic LA layer,
which drums didn't have (until now the recorded hit WAS the whole drum voice).
`HYBRID_TOM_MIX = [0.55, 0.70, 0.78]` scales the body with the repitch; it's a named
constant precisely because the exact blend is an ear call and this box has none.

## Tuning notes (for whoever revisits the mix)
- First oracle attempt used LATE-window RMS — wrong: the repitched sample isn't that
  *short*, so late RMS barely moved (+6%). The real complaint is TIMBRE (energy
  shifted up = chipmunk). Switched to spectral centroid + low-mid band: the body
  pulls the centroid DOWN and adds low-mid. Keys 48/50: centroid −10/−12%, low-mid
  +17/+21%. That's the meaningful, measurable signal.
- Level budget bites: the hybrid renders for keys 47/48/50 in
  `sampled_drum_level_parity`. At mix 1.2 key 50 hit +4.27 dB (over the ±3 dB guard).
  A hybrid should be a FULLER drum, not a LOUDER one — retuned the mix to land key 50
  at +2.46 dB (within budget) rather than weaken the guard. Raising the mix further
  needs the parity tolerance revisited.
- Two routing tests (`sampled_kit_routes_only_on_v3_brush_with_samples`) asserted
  `kind()=="sampleddrum"` for every kit key; keys 47/48/50 are now `"hybridtom"`.
  Updated both the V3 and Brush loops (the hybrid IS the sampled path, wrapped).
