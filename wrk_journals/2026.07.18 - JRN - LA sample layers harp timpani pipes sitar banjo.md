## Surprises / dead ends
- **`.gitignore` silently drops new sample-crate WAVs.** `*.wav` is ignored except an
  explicit per-crate `!crates/…/samples/*.wav` whitelist. A new crate's samples are
  ignored until whitelisted, so `git add <crate>` commits the crate WITHOUT its WAVs and
  the build breaks from a clean checkout. Fixed for `-orchestral2` (whitelist added in
  211289d, covers all its future families). ALWAYS `git ls-files <crate>/samples` after
  committing a sample crate to confirm the WAVs are tracked.
- **SF3 pipe presets are THIN + mixed sample-rate** (probed 2026.07.18): GM75 pan flute
  = 8 zones F#3–C7 but sample rates vary (18000/24000/28000/30000/44100) → the SF3 bake
  MUST resample each zone to 44.1k (`prepare.py::resample`, using `z[5]`); the clavinet
  bake got away without it because all its zones are 44100. GM76 blown bottle = **1 zone
  (C6)**; GM77 shakuhachi = **1 zone (C5, 38000)**. Single-zone presets mean the LA onset
  is only credible within the `LaVoice::wrap` `step` clamp (~±1 octave of the sample root);
  outside that the wrap falls back to bare model — graceful, but 76/77 onsets cover only a
  narrow register. Acceptable (Arthur pre-approved soundfont sourcing); flagged for the
  final report. Sitar (104) = 8 zones E3–G6, all 44100 — clean.
