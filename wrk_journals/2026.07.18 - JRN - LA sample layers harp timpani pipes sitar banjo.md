## Surprises / dead ends
- **ganjo (banjo) source had two traps.** (1) IEEE-float WAV (fmt tag 3) — stdlib `wave`
  raises `unknown format: 3`; `ensure_banjo_sources` transcodes to PCM via ffmpeg. (2) file
  labels sit ONE OCTAVE ABOVE sounding pitch (probed: label A#4 sounds A#3) — dest named by the
  MEASURED sounding pitch, not the label. Plus harmonic-rich → added to TWO_F_STRONG. Always
  probe an external source's actual sounding pitch AND its WAV format before trusting names.
- **Near-sine winds (ocarina) are 2f-STRONG → measure_f0 grabs the 2nd harmonic** if the
  F0 ceiling admits 2f, and the octave-snap then "confirms" the wrong octave (it can't tell a
  true fundamental from a 2f that lands on a label×2 candidate). Recording labels were sounding-
  accurate; the misread was purely the ceiling. Fix: keep the zone span UNDER one octave and set
  the single ceiling above every fundamental but below the lowest 2f. ALWAYS probe roots at a
  tight per-note ceiling (`measure_f0(x,sr,150,label*1.5)`) before trusting the default-ceiling
  print. Applies to the SF3 pipes next (pan flute etc. may be 2f-strong too). GENERALIZED in
  Unit 4: `prepare.py::TWO_F_STRONG` families get a per-note ceiling cap (label×1.5) in main(),
  so a >1-octave 2f-dominant instrument (recorder) works without narrowing the span. Add a
  family to that set rather than hand-narrowing when you hit this again.
- **`.gitignore` silently drops new sample-crate WAVs.** `*.wav` is ignored except an
  explicit per-crate `!crates/…/samples/*.wav` whitelist. A new crate's samples are
  ignored until whitelisted, so `git add <crate>` commits the crate WITHOUT its WAVs and
  the build breaks from a clean checkout. Fixed for `-orchestral2` (whitelist added in
  211289d, covers all its future families). ALWAYS `git ls-files <crate>/samples` after
  committing a sample crate to confirm the WAVs are tracked.
- **SF3 pipe presets are THIN + mixed sample-rate** (probed 2026.07.18): GM75 pan flute
  = 8 zones F#3–C7 but sample rates vary (18000/24000/28000/30000/44100) → the SF3 bake
  MUST resample each zone to 44.1k (`prepare.py::resample`, using `z[5]`); the clavinet
  bake got away without it because all its zones are 44100. GM76 blown bottle = **1 zone
  (C6)**; GM77 shakuhachi = **1 zone (C5, 38000)**. Single-zone presets mean the LA onset
  is only credible within the `LaVoice::wrap` `step` clamp (~±1 octave of the sample root);
  outside that the wrap falls back to bare model — graceful, but 76/77 onsets cover only a
  narrow register. Acceptable (Arthur pre-approved soundfont sourcing); flagged for the
  final report. Sitar (104) = 8 zones E3–G6, all 44100 — clean.
