# 2026.07.18 — JRN — GM42/43 vibrato, brightness, chorus (synthesis follow-ups)

**Task:** the three synthesis-side follow-ups surfaced (but deliberately deferred)
during the solo-cello / double-bass sample swap. All three are default-on timbre
changes to the shipped `BowedString` voices, scoped to GM42/43 only.

## Method

Ran a design + measurement + verify workflow against the current trunk render (this
box has no ears): three design agents re-located the code and refined values, a
measurement agent quantified the current behaviour on a fresh build, and an
adversarial verify pass checked each against its oracles. All three confirmed.

**Measurement grounding (current trunk):**
- Vibrato depth cello ~3.3c / bass ~2.7c one-sided (real ~15-40c / 10-30c) — 5-10x
  too shallow. Rates cello 4.46 / bass 3.90 Hz (nominal 4.8/4.2 ± the ±10% LFO jitter).
- Short notes (<0.4 s) get NO vibrato: `vib_delay` 0.16-0.40 s + 0.25 s ramp → full
  depth only at 0.41-0.65 s.
- Spectral centroid at A2=110 Hz: contrabass **1289 Hz vs cello 831 Hz — bass +55%
  BRIGHTER** than the cello at the same pitch (backwards; a bass is the darkest of
  the family). Cause: both share `refl_sustain` 2600 but only the cello has an
  `out_lp` (2100 Hz), so the bass is unfiltered.

## Changes (voices.rs BowedString::new + engine.rs fx_profile)

1. **Vibrato** — per-program (was one shared formula): cello `0.0055+0.0055*u`
   (~9.5-19c peak) at 5.2 Hz (was 4.8, a slow wobble); contrabass `0.0050+0.0050*u`
   (~8.6-17c peak) at 4.2 Hz. Bass owns 100% of its vibrato (VSCO SusNV onset is
   non-vibrato). Onset floors cello 0.12 s / bass 0.10 s (was 0.16 s) so short notes
   breathe; cello floor timed against the Bigcat sample's 0.13→0.40 s fade so the two
   vibratos never peak together at the seam.
2. **Brightness** — cello `out_lp` 2100→2600 (radiate full harmonics); contrabass
   `refl_sustain` 2600→2200 (heavier, more-damped string) + a new `out_lp` 1800
   (below the cello's) so the bass is darkest. Bass `loop_comp` re-derived 4.03→4.52
   to hold pitch-independent tuning under the darker reflection filter (one-pole
   group-delay Δ +0.49 samp, confirmed analytically and by the loop-latency harness).
3. **Chorus** — split GM42/43 out of the `40..=45|110` fx_profile arm to `(0.0, 0.06)`:
   no ensemble chorus to smear a solo voice, only a trace of slap. 40/41/44/45/110
   keep the old (0.10,0.10); 40==110 preserved for the gm110 oracle.
