## Findings

- **CR-8 (Low, CR-report only):** numeric CLI args aren't range/finiteness-validated —
  `--wet nan`, `--tail inf`, `--rate 0`, `--tail -5` etc. pass unchecked into the
  render and produce silent garbage/empty output instead of a `usage()` error. Rust's
  `f32` `FromStr` accepts `nan`/`inf`, and `usage()` only fires on a parse *failure*.
  Low, offline, user-error → CR report, not a bug.
- **No bugs, no reqs.** The CLI is small, well-guarded, clean.

- **Did NOT file CR-8 as a bug:** low severity, offline single-user tool, user-error
  failure mode. Recorded in the CR report per "nits stay in the CR report".
- **examples/ are DEV-ONLY** (marked): their `.expect()` is fine; `measure_wav`'s WAV
