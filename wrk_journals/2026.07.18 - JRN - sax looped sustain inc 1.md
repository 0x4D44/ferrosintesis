# 2026.07.18 — JRN — sax looped sustain, Increment 1

Approved rework (see the PLN): replace the sax's onset-only sample layer with a whole-
voice real recording — attack played through into a pitch-synchronous loop of the
recorded sustain, model demoted to a modulator (inc 2).

## Inc 1 — the model→loop handoff (committed `5e2c310`, green)

`SaxLoopVoice` (sampler.rs): reads the zone `data` 0→loop_end then wraps
loop_start↔loop_end (wrapped-neighbour interp). Loop found at construction by
`find_sax_loop` from the measured zone root. `LA_SAX` removed; modeled reed is the
`--no-samples` voice + fallback.

## Two things bring-up taught me

1. **A seam-clean loop is NOT a flat loop.** My first `find_sax_loop` biased to the
   LONGEST seam-clean loop. It picked a ~320 ms loop spanning the note's natural decay,
   so the hold pulsed loud→silence→loud at ~3 Hz (measured frame-RMS CoV 0.63; the
   envelope literally ramped to near-zero and jumped back). Fix: cost now combines the
   wrap seam with the loop's amplitude IMBALANCE (first-half vs second-half RMS), and the
   loop is bounded to a short window (0.05–0.13 s) inside the steady body (< 0.50 s, clear
   of the release). After: flat hold, CoV 0.033.

2. **One gain constant can't level-match the four saxes.** The samples are uniformly
   peak-normalised (0.9) but the modeled reeds differ per sax, so the loop/model ratio
   spans ~7 dB (alto +6.6, baritone −0.1 at a shared gain). Went to a per-program
   `SAX_LOOP_GAIN[4]` = [0.390, 0.289, 0.511, 0.623], each calibrated so the looped hold
   sits within ~1 dB of the model it replaces (level parity → album balance preserved).

Runtime loop from the committed WAV + root — no re-bake, no network, no asset change.

## Caveat — inc 1 is deliberately STATIC, not shippable alone

The raw loop is flat by design → hold liveness **0.020**, actually a touch MORE static than
the model (0.024) and well under the SC-55's 0.052. Real timbre, no movement. It is also not
yet CC-responsive (the model used to answer CC11/CC1 via `set_breath`; `SaxLoopVoice` no-ops
them for now). So the 4 alto tracks currently render a real-but-static sustain. **Do not
integrate inc 1 alone** — inc 2 (model as modulator: gentle intrinsic vibrato + breath drift,
then CC1/CC11 response, targeting the SC-55's ~0.05 liveness) is what makes it sound alive, and
its vibrato/breath character wants Arthur's ears.
