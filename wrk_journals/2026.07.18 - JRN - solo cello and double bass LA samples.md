Two structural defects, both in the SAMPLE layer, not the physics:

1. **GM 43 was a cello *section* repitched an octave down** — `contrabass_bank` borrowed

## The octave-offset trap (the load-bearing gotcha)

Both libraries — like the existing `celens`/`vlnens` sections and the VCSL keyboards —
**label one octave BELOW sounding pitch**. Autocorr alone is ambiguous here: the
strongest peak sits at 2×label whether the true root is an octave up (offset label) or
the label with a weak/2f-dominant fundamental. **A spectral-line check disambiguated:**
Bigcat "A1" is a clean 110 Hz A2 with *no* 55 Hz and *no* 165 Hz energy → root = 110 Hz.
VSCO "C1" → a 65.9 Hz C2 line. So every zone is named by its MEASURED sounding pitch,
never the filename. Both `cellosolo` and `dbass` went into `TWO_F_STRONG` (per-note
ceiling = nominal×1.5) so the bake's autocorr can't grab 2f at the low end. Bake roots
came out within a few cents of nominal, no octave errors.
