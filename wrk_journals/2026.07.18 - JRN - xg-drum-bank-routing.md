## Diagnosis (pre-worktree, on main clone)
- Symptom: `04-incantations-part-iv-xg.mid` degrades at ~2:40.
- Parsed the MIDI: at **t=162.4s** ch14 (idx 13) sends `CC0=127` + `program=16` (XG
  Rock drum kit) — a 2nd percussion part (congas/bongos/shaker, keys 58–64/82).
- ferrosintesis routes drums by `ch==9` only, and treats any `CC0!=0` as alt bank →
  program 16 falls through to GM Drawbar Organ. Congas render as a sustained
  D4/Eb4/E4 organ cluster.
- Render confirmed: ch13-solo sustained pitched energy at Eb4/D4/D3; full mix +6 dB
  at the break. Root cause solid.

## Design
- Wrote HLD `wrk_docs/2026.07.18 - HLD - XG drum-kit bank routing (CC0=127).md`.
- Enumerated all `ch==9`/`ch!=9` sites in engine.rs; classified drum-routing gates
  (must change) vs inert melodic gates (leave).
- Key architecture fact: ch9 drum gain is baked into bus master
  `g9 = strips[9].gain·DRUM_FORWARD` + optional drum wah — entangled, doesn't
  generalize.
- ch13 authors **435 CC11 expression events** → losing its dynamics would be audible,
  so chose **A2**: XG-drum voices take a small parallel path (own channel gain/pan →
  master mix, DRUM_FORWARD + own CC91 reverb + shared drum room), leaving ch9's bus
  path literally untouched (byte-identical albums by construction).
- Rejected A (share ch9 bus — loses ch13 dynamics) and B (refactor ch9 gain path —
  album-render risk).
