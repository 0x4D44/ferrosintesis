# 2026.07.19 - JRN - Bright Matter onto the ORIGINAL drum kit (ch-10 PC 25)

## Decision change
The earlier journal (`2026.07.19 - JRN - original kit for Three-Sixty and Slipstream.md`)
recorded Bright Matter as **deliberately NOT switched**. **Arthur has since decided it should
switch too** — so all five tracks now author ch-10 PC 25 (`Kit::V1`, the original kit),
matching Three-Sixty-One, Three-Sixty and Slipstream. This note supersedes that hold.

For the record, the two reservations I had raised (both still true as facts, judged not to
outweigh the decision):
1. Its lineage is to "Three-Sixty" (T10) and is explicitly "production grammar only"
   (`ALBUM.md:87`) — no melodic borrowing.
2. Its backbeat differs from Three-Sixty-One's: a real snare (key 38, 204-312 hits/track)
   and ZERO hand claps, where T16 uses claps (39 x272) and few snares. The sampled kit's
   snare is one of its stronger voices, so this album gives up more than the others do.
   Worth a listen on the regenerated renders; easy to revert per-track if the snare suffers.

## The blocker that had to be fixed
Unlike Through Lines and Slipstream — whose `check_programs` skip the drum channel
(`if ch == DRUM_CH: continue`) — Bright Matter's `curated_programs_only`
(`verify.py:47-54`) checks **every** program change including channel 10, against
`PROGRAM_WHITELIST`. 25 was not in it, so the switch would have failed the album's own
oracle. Added 25, and corrected the now-false comment that said "Program 1 on channel 10 is
kit metadata" — on ch-10 the program is a live KIT SELECTOR, not inert metadata.

## Changes
- `tracks/t01..t05`: `sc.program(CH_KIT, 1, 0.0)` -> `sc.program(CH_KIT, 25, 0.0)` + comment.
- `verify.py`: 25 added to `PROGRAM_WHITELIST`; the stale ch-10 "metadata" comment rewritten
  to explain that ch-10's program selects the kit and therefore must be whitelisted here.
- `midi/*.mid` rebuilt.
