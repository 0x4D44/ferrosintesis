  the fetch loop, and the bake union.

- **orchestral2 `lib.rs`:** 10 `vibes_*` entries added (hand-maintained sorted slice; parity test sorts, so
  order/count self-adjust). Crate now ~5.7 MB (well under the 10 MiB cap).
- **sampler.rs:** `vibes()` + `vibraphone_bank()` with the measured roots.
- **voices.rs:** GM 11 arm wraps the `bell()+motor` model in `LaVoice::wrap` under `if samples` (byte-identical

- **altbank.rs:** `11 => voices::make(…, false)` arm — the pure model as CC0≠0 (brass/guitar freeze idiom).
