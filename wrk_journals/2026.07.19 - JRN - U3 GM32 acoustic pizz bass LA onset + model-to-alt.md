## What landed
GM 32 acoustic (upright/double) bass gains a real VSCO pizzicato pluck onset as the **default**, with the
pre-sampling `Pluck(&UPRIGHT)` model preserved as the **CC0≠0 alt** (GM32's alt slot was free — a plain
`voices::make(.., false)` arm, unlike GM14).

- **Source (already-pinned, zero new download):** VSCO-2-CE "Solo Contrabass" **Pizz** (`BKCtbss_Pizz_*_v1_rr1`,
  same VSCO_REV, CC0) — sibling of the GM43 arco (`dbass`) → the CC0 **-strings** crate.
- **prepare.py:** `_PIZZBASS_ZONES` (sounding→source-label, +1 octave like dbass), `PIZZBASS_URLS`, + `pizzbass` in
  F0_RANGE (38–520), TWO_F_STRONG, KEEP_FAM (0.9/0.30 pluck), FAMILY_PACKAGE (-strings), fetch loop, bake union.
- **Measured roots:** E1 41.09, G1 48.60, A#1 58.25, C2 65.04, E2 82.41, G#2 102.12, A2 112.51, G#3 205.95.
  **C#3/E3 DROPPED** — autocorr grabbed subharmonics (C#3 read octave-low 69 Hz/conf 0.59; E3 garbage 55 Hz), the
  weak low-string-fundamental trap again. G#2→G#3 confirms the +1-octave convention. 8 zones sound E1–G#3; LaVoice
  ±1oct covers the register.
- **sampler.rs:** `pizzbass()` + `pizzbass_bank()`. **voices.rs:** GM32 wraps the Pluck model in `LaVoice::wrap`
  under `if samples` (byte-identical `--no-samples`); `LA_PIZZBASS = (0.40,(0.05,0.35))` (pluck onset; ear-tunable) +
  registry. **altbank.rs:** `11 | 32 => voices::make(.., false)`.
- **Oracle:** appended `(32, 40, 1)` to `altbank_sampled_programs_preserve_pure_model_and_default_layers`.
