# 2026.07.19 - JRN - the ORIGINAL drum kit for Three-Sixty + Slipstream

## What and why
After Three-Sixty-One (Through Lines T16) moved to the ORIGINAL drum kit (ch-10 PC 25,
`Kit::V1`), Arthur asked which other Three-Sixty-One-derived albums should follow.
Investigated (parallel read-only sweep of the whole corpus), A/B-rendered, and switched
**11 tracks**: Through Lines T10 + all ten Slipstream tracks.

## Findings
- **Through Lines T10 "Three-Sixty"** — the biggest find, and it is not another album.
  T16 is a remix of T10, and their ch-10 drum lanes are **identical in ticks and keys**
  (2557 note-ons each, zero differences; only velocities differ). T10 still authored PC 1,
  so the same drum performance was playing through two different kits on one album.
  Verified by parsing both committed MIDIs directly.
- **Slipstream** (10 tracks) — a *commissioned sibling* of Three-Sixty-One, not a loose
  homage: six of its eight drum-fill shapes are T16's data note-for-note, and it quotes
  T16's orbit riff + A6 scream verbatim under oracles that fail the build on drift. Same
  idiom: four-on-the-floor kick, sixteenth closed hats at 43-58% of all ch-10 hits (so the
  hi-hat Arthur objected to is the dominant voice), offbeat open hats.
- **Bright Matter (gpt5-6) — deliberately NOT switched.** Its lineage is to "Three-Sixty"
  (T10) and is explicitly "production grammar only". Two real reasons to hold: it uses a
  real snare (38, 204-312/track) and ZERO claps where T16 uses claps — the sampled snare is
  where V3 earns its keep — and its `verify.py` `PROGRAM_WHITELIST` lacks 25 AND (unlike
  Through Lines / Slipstream) does **not** skip ch-9, so PC 25 would fail
  `curated_programs_only`. Needs its own listen + a whitelist change.
- No third derivative exists in the corpus. Stylistic-only candidates (no lineage) exist —
  Sub Rosa is *more* arena-idiom than T16 itself (66% vs 33% of windows), plus Heliopause
  I/II and Seven Kinds of Sunlight — left alone as out of scope.

## Safety check done BEFORE editing
Both Through Lines and Slipstream `check_programs` skip the drum channel
(`if ch == DRUM_CH: continue`), so PC 25 cannot trip their program whitelists. Bright
Matter's does not — which is what disqualified it.

## Verification
- A/B rendered "The Flypast" and "Three-Sixty" both ways from ONE binary (render-time
  `FERRO_DRUM_KIT` override in a throwaway worktree — no tracked file touched); Arthur
  confirmed the original kit by ear.
- Rebuilt MIDIs are clean: e.g. Flypast rebuild vs HEAD is **identical in every note and
  CC** (7460 notes, 2909 CCs) and differs ONLY in the ch-9 program byte 1 -> 25.
- Post-change render of **Three-Sixty is byte-identical** to the approved A/B original.
- **Flypast differs — and it is NOT this change.** Trunk advanced mid-task with other
  agents' "driven-lead soft onset" + "pluck redesign Phase 1"; Flypast's whitelist includes
  GM29 (the driven lead ship) so it moves, while Three-Sixty has no GM29 and so matched
  exactly. Three-Sixty's exact match across the two trunk states is itself proof the drum
  path is unchanged.
- Both albums' full oracle suites: green.

## Corpus note
PC 24 (`Kit::Synth`, the modelled synth kit shipped as bank 1) is authored by **nothing**
in the album corpus — it exists only in the synth's tests. Worth deciding later whether it
earns its keep.
