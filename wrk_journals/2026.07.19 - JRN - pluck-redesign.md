### Unit 0a — metric primitives + Oracle-0 calibration  ✅ (commit pending)
Added to `crates/ferrosintesis/src/testutil.rs` (all `#[cfg(test)]`, zero shipped-binary impact):
- `fft_inplace` — hand-rolled iterative radix-2 Cooley–Tukey FFT (f64 twiddles, bit-reversal).
- `fft_mag_hann(seg, n)` — Hann-windowed magnitude spectrum, normalised so an on-bin unit sine ≈ 1.0.
- `spectral_tilt_db_oct(seg, sr, lo, hi)` — least-squares dB/oct slope over log-spaced FFT probes.
- `crest(seg)` — peak/RMS (HLD G2).
- `att_sus_ratio(seg, sr, f0)` — the PRIMARY metric: RMS[0,max(15ms,1.5/f0)] ÷ RMS[100,250ms].
- `inter_harmonic_floor_db(seg, sr, f0)` — median inter-harmonic vs harmonic-peak magnitude, dB (G6).
- `max_slew_norm(seg)` (G5), `dc_offset(seg)` (G10).

Oracle-0 calibration tests (all green): FFT recovers a tone (peak bin + ≥40 dB octave leakage); tilt
orders white≈0 / one-pole≈−6 / two-pole≈−12 dB/oct (mean over 8 noise seeds to tame single-window
variance); crest √2 vs impulse ≫; att/sus reads 2.0 for a 2× front-loaded tone, 1.0 flat; inter-harmonic
floor deeply negative for a pure harmonic tone, rises with noise; slew ≈ 2πf/sr, DC recovered exactly.

Gate: `cargo test -p ferrosintesis calibration::` 20/20 green; `cargo fmt --check` clean; `clippy
-p ferrosintesis --all-targets -D warnings` clean.
