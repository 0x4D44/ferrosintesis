## Phase 1 A/B follow-up — driven-lead soft onset (Arthur: "much better")

Arthur judged the ch14 driven-lead solo "still quite plucky" after the click cut. Diagnosis: the
click is the MINOR lever; the driven pluckiness is the excitation front-load, which the amp-sim then
accentuates (loud pick attack clips hardest). Rendered a 3-way ch14 solo A/B; Arthur approved the
soft-onset take ("much better").

DRIVE (prog 29/30) change: `attack_s: 0.0 -> 0.015` (a ~15 ms soft onset eases the string front-load
into the amp-sim so it clips the transient far less — the lead sings in rather than snapping) +
`click 1.0 -> 0.7`. Kept above DRIVE_LEAD's 0.6 so the main still has a firmer pick than the alt lead.

Design consequence + resolution: softening the DEFAULT bank's attack collapsed the attack-swell gap
with the CC0 DRIVE_LEAD (a_main 20 ms vs a_lead 29 ms = 9 ms, < the oracle's 25 ms). DRIVE_LEAD
CANNOT swell further to restore it — its attack_s 0.03 is sized to complete inside the sustainer's
80 ms capture window (a longer swell deflates the held reference; documented in the preset). So:
- driven_main_and_alt_banks_diverge clause (2) RE-BASELINED 25 ms -> 5 ms (announced): the attack
  contrast is now the MINOR axis; clause (1) — the >=6 dB SUSTAIN gap (main decays, lead holds),
  UNCHANGED — carries the bank distinction. Bar 5 ms still RED if the main softens to swell as much
  as the lead.
- phase1_click_cut_reduces_onset_hf aggregate 10% -> 5% (announced): DRIVE's soft onset ramps its
  string front-load in, trimming the click's onset-HF share, so the aggregate click-reduction eased
  ~10% -> ~9%. 5% robustly GREEN, still RED if clicks restored.
All driven/attack oracles green (o_attack_struck_attack_dominates, driven_sustain_stays_distorted,
sustained_lead_stays_harmonic_through_drive). This globally softens the driven-guitar attack across
the 53 driven albums (a deliberate, Arthur-approved voice change).
