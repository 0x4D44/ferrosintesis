# 2026.07.19 — JRN — trim the sax synthetic breath ("wind") layer

## Trigger

Arthur: "The wind effect on the saxes is too strong — go investigate."

## Diagnosis

Saxes (GM 64–67) default to a **sampled** voice, `sampler::SaxLoopVoice` (a real
MTG.SoloSax recording, looped for sustain). Its `render()` sums in a high-passed
white-noise "breath/air" term on top of the recorded tone (`sampler.rs:2872–2876`):

```rust
let w = self.rng.white();
let breath = (w - self.breath_hp.process(w)) * self.breath_amt; // HP white noise = air
*o += (tone * amp + breath) * self.gain * e;
```

`breath_amt = SAX_BREATH_FLOOR (0.004)`, lifted to `FLOOR + SAX_BREATH_CC (0.05) · p`
on channels that author CC11/CC2/aftertouch (the RD9 gate, `engine.rs:2633–2643`).
At full CC the lift is **12.5× the floor**.

The layer is added **on top of a recording that already breathes** — a double breath.
That is the "too strong" wind.

### Which tracks hit it

The two — and only two — album tracks that sound a sax both author CC11 on the sax:

- **Through Lines / "Night Train to Tirana"** (#11): alto sax ch 6, CC11 expr 66–78
  (`t11_night_train.py:508`) → `breath_amt ≈ 0.041`.
- **Atlas of Becoming / "Every Door Opens at Once"** (#14): "corridor sax" ch 6,
  expression arc **peaking 114/127** (`…14_every_door_opens_at_once.py:226`) →
  `breath_amt ≈ 0.049` (near the ceiling).

### Measured (solo-sax stems, stock minus a breath-zeroed build)

Difference signal = the pure synthetic breath layer (RNG call left intact so the tone
is bit-identical → clean subtraction). Breath-to-tone level, tone-active region:

| Track | mean | peak 0.5 s | breath HF frac (>1800 Hz) | tone HF frac |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Night Train | −30.0 dB | −25.7 dB | 0.70 | 0.09 |
| Atlas | **−24.1 dB** | **−20.9 dB** | 0.75 | 0.27 |

The difference is 70–75 % HF air (tone is 9–27 %) — genuinely the breath layer, not
tone residual. Night Train is a subtle floor; Atlas is an audible hiss riding the swells.

## Fix

The CC lift is the offender (it makes Atlas 6 dB hotter). Cut it hard, trim the floor,
so even the worst case lands at a subtle ~−32 dB floor under the recording's own breath
(`sampler.rs:2761–2763`):

- `SAX_BREATH_CC`    0.05  → **0.012**  (−12 dB on the CC-driven air)
- `SAX_BREATH_FLOOR` 0.004 → **0.0025** (−4 dB floor)

Projected: Night Train ≈ −41 dB mean, Atlas ≈ −35 dB mean / −32 dB peak. Degree is
Arthur's ear (this box has none) — these are the "don't double the breath" values;
easy one-line retune either way.

Minimal, right-layer change: two constants, no new mechanism.
