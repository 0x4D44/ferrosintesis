## What the prior session got right and wrong
- RIGHT: root cause of the hang (denormal churn in always-running buses),

- WRONG (understandable): the held reasoning said "no available mechanism —

  DSP feedback loop — it is the **rounding function itself**.

## Diagnosis (measured, not asserted)
1. Dumped the pre-normalize RAW f32 from both binaries (`raw_dump` example).
   The signal path GENUINELY diverges: max 6.476e-5 (~2 LSB), **abrupt onset at
   t=123.654 s, exactly 0.0 before**. So it is NOT a normalizer/limiter ghost —
   it is a real signal-path difference. (Refutes the "normalizer-ulp" framing.)
2. Structure: RIGHT channel 33× > LEFT; smooth ramp from 2^-27 (= exactly 1 ULP)
   at onset; a brief −24 dBFS piano dip at 123.4 s right before onset; re-locks
   to bit-exact by ~128.4 s and stays identical for 62 s.
3. Flush-site bisect (revert one site, rebuild, re-dump, diff vs baseline):
   - PingPong (echo) flush reverted → NO change. Exonerated.
   - **Sympathetic per-block flush reverted → divergence collapses 6.5e-5 →
     7e-15.** The seed. (`--wet 0` keeps the diff → not reverb; `--no-samples`
     kills it → the sampled piano feeds the sympathetic bus.)
4. Mechanism (Fable + Codex independently, correcting my "resonator pumps it"
   guess): `flush_denormal` is bit-exact above the floor, but **every f32 add's
   round-to-nearest step is a discontinuity with one threshold per ULP.** The
   Sympathetic per-block flush of `hp`/`damp` states seeds a sub-floor δ≈1e-19
   in each ~330 ms staccato gap of ch0's piano. δ rounds away against the loud
   mix (123 s of bit-identical output) until one exact sum straddles a rounding
   boundary within δ → δ **teleports** to a full 1-ULP jump. That 1-ULP enters
   `BusGlue` (shelf_r memory + env); its transient-displaced `if level>env`
   atk/rel branch injects Δenv≈(atk−rel)(level−env) ~ 1e-5..1e-4 → gain error →
   the 4.8 s, ≤6.5e-5 burst. Self-heals when transients relax.

## Fix chosen: lower the floor (F2), not remove the site (F1)
Two validated options:
- **F1 — remove the Sympathetic per-block flush.** Byte-identical to baseline on
  Wire and Wake (default + peak-norm, 0 diff). But leaves the other buses at the
  1e-20 floor (tie hazard ~1e-12/add) and drops denormal protection on the
  sympathetic bus.
- **F2 — lower the flush floor 1e-20 → 1e-34 (SHIPPED).** 1e-20 is a NORMAL f32
  (subnormals start ~1.18e-38); flushing there was never byte-transparent. 1e-34
  still snaps ~4 orders above subnormal (perf intact: `--solo 8` 46.7 s vs the
  456 s crawl) while shrinking δ so the per-add tie hazard drops ~1e-12 → ~1e-26.
  Keeps protection on ALL buses; one constant + the guard-test floor. Byte-
  identical to baseline on Wire and Wake (default + peak-norm, 0 diff).

F2 is the root-cause fix and Fable's recommendation; F1 only patches the worst
site. Chose F2.
