## Outcome: 0 landed fixes, 6 Blocked, 1 parked-Open
The queue turned out to be dominated by ears/design/external-asset work. Two bugs whose
"obvious" fix I actually prototyped had to be reverted because the fix was unsafe:

- **00028** (ch-10 CC91 → drum-room send). Prototyped the sketch (a `reverb_authored` gate
  scaling `ROOM_SEND` by CC91) with green unit + isolated-render regression tests. Then a
  stdlib MIDI scan of the catalog killed it: **86 of 124 tracks author ch-10 CC91** (values
  20–110), so the coupling re-mixes their drum-room ambience by **0.5×–2.9×**. That fails the
  controller-feature render-diff rule ("any diff at all is a bug" — must be inert on existing
  content). CC91 is the documented, broadly-authored reverb controller, so no
  inert-on-existing-content fix exists → **Blocked** on a design/ears call (new dedicated
  controller vs accept catalog re-mix). Reverted the two commits I had already pushed.

- **00030** (harpsichord LA onset). Prototyped the exit condition literally (LA `vel_gain`
  squares the same `vel_sense`-compressed velocity the model does, `vn²`; new `LaFx.vel_sense`
  field; deleted the self-retiring `harpsichord-low` exception). The bloom **was** fixed
  (attack owns the peak; bloom 1.12→0.74) — but it **inverts** the harpsichord's aggregate
  velocity response: momentary LUFS goes from monotonic-rising to monotonic-falling (v72 −21.7
  louder than v110 −22.7), failing `exempt_voices_keep_their_documented_velocity_behaviour`.
  The loud sample onset, boosted ~18 dB at low velocity, exposes the model's own
  inverted-in-LUFS behaviour (entangled with 00029) that the quiet onset had masked. The right
  onset law is a tuning/ears extension → **Blocked**. Reverted.

- **00029** (voice velocity turnover) → **Blocked**. The bug's own analysis is definitive: a
  scalar exponent can't fix a curve that turns over; it needs the GM42/43/GM4 **models**
  re-voiced, "a different risk class [that] needs ears plus the render-diff inventory." No
  ears here. Contained by self-retiring oracle exclusions.

- **00024** (GM48/49 EarPending) → **Blocked**. Resolution *is* recording a human A/B ear
  verdict; an unattended box can't produce it without fabricating it.

- **00019** (per-program loudness damping) → **Blocked**. The bug's own words: "not a defect
  so much as a deliberate stopping point awaiting a listening pass"; both halves are
  ear-in-the-loop.

- **00016** (slap-bass 36/37 onset) → **Blocked**. GM32–35 already landed; the remaining slap
  sub-item needs a CC0/CC-BY real-slap multisample the sourcing HLD says doesn't exist — an
  external asset hunt, not code.

- **00027** (`--solo 8` >200× slowdown) → **parked Open**. Does **not** reproduce on current
  HEAD: instrumented the whole offline render — active voices peak at **3**, no
  stuck/never-reaping voice, no O(n²). Solo renders fewer voices than the full mix (ch-8's
  music ends ~145 s, then a cheap silent tail to song end ~500 s), so it's *faster* than the
  full mix, opposite to the report. The one "solo slower" reading during triage was CPU
  contention from running several test renders at once on a 4-core box. Parked with evidence
  (per method: non-repro → park, don't guess); asked the reporter to confirm on current HEAD.
