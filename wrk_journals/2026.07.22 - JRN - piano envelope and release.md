# 2026.07.22 — JRN — piano envelope and release (MM-BUG-KILN-00042)

## Intent

Arthur reported that the default Ferro piano in the Ferrosinthesis Reference
Audition sounds thumpy on the second and third notes, while SC-55 and S-YXG50
carry those notes into one another. The agreed sequence is to normalize the
source-bank macro-envelopes first, then calibrate a piano-only release from that
stable base.

Task success is a coherent default GM0 upright across round robins and adjacent
zones, followed by enough key-up damping to bridge the FRA phrase's short gaps
without pedal-like smear. MIDI authorship, other programs, spectral character,
and unrelated sample banks are out of scope.

## Reproduction and diagnosis

The opening GM0 figure in demos/ferrosintesis_reference/tracks/audition.py is
D4, F#4, A4 at velocity 104 and 96 BPM. Each note lasts 0.5 beats (312.5 ms)
and starts 0.6 beats (375 ms) after the previous one. The key-up gap is therefore
62.5 ms. No CC64 or CC68 participates.

The voice seed alternates the default upright round robin. Those three keys read
the C4 and G4 forte zones, so the phrase deliberately crosses both a zone and a
round-robin boundary.

Measured from the tracked 44.1 kHz source assets:

- peak normalization is already uniform, but the 8–38 ms attack versus
  100–180 ms body ratio varies by more than 20 dB across the forte bank;
- forte same-zone round robins differ by roughly 4.9 dB at G2, 5.5 dB at C3,
  4.3 dB at G3, 4.3 dB at C4, and 8.7 dB at C6;
- C4 forte takes are body-heavy (-5.2/-9.5 dB in that ratio), while G4 is
  attack-heavy (+2.4/+0.3 dB), matching Arthur's note-order observation.

The delivered FRA render loses 16–23 dB during the three short gaps. A retained,
matched SC-55 dry piano probe loses about 6 dB over the same 62.5 ms. A low-pass
comparison did not show a material extra sub-200 Hz transient in the selected
source takes, so the reported thump is primarily time shape, not a separate
bass-resonance defect.

Code confirms the two layers:

- prepare.py trim_to_onset conditions each file independently and only
  peak-normalizes it;
- LaVoice applies a generic 60 ms sample-release T60;
- acoustic_piano applies a 100 ms model-release T60;
- at FRA note-off, the LA crossfade is still about 90 percent sample-owned.

## Design direction

The draft HLD uses a deliberately small bank-level transform:

- one slope-bounded minimax attack/body trend across the whole 54-take bank;
- scalar body gain, smoothed in after the first 40 ms;
- no filtering, compression, resynthesis, dependency, or runtime flag;
- a hard 13 dB correction bound and tracked-asset oracle;
- a release decision only after the conditioned FRA render is measured.

The design is in:
wrk_docs/2026.07.22 - HLD - GM0 upright envelope and release.md.

## Review and implementation log

### Design review

Two independent reviewers checked the draft from the sample-conditioning and
release/routing sides, then each tried to refute the other's findings against
the code. Seven findings survived and were folded into the HLD: full bank
seam/attack coverage, a no-secondary-swell guard, explicit dry release windows,
two-bake determinism, runtime zone-edge ownership, alternate-bank isolation, and
numeric reap/render-diff rules.

The one external Claude Opus pass completed read-only after 762 seconds. It
confirmed the diagnosis and found a critical ambiguity: using the non-bright
GM0 path as a release selector would also select every GM0 alternate bank.
The revised design now threads an explicit optional override and authors it only
from the default program-0 arm. Claude also exposed a gap in ratio-only
conditioning: equal attack/body ratios can retain absolute RMS steps due to
different crest factors. The conditioner therefore fits one whole-bank shape
trend and one shared absolute body-level trend.

One targeted re-review checked only the critical routing correction. It returned
RESOLVED against the default GM0, GM1, alternate, and undefined-CC0 fallback call
sites. The design is now SELF-APPROVED. No architectural fork remains.

### Implementation

The first Python regression failed before implementation because the bank-level
conditioner and metrics did not exist. A synthetic 54-take fixture now proves
deterministic shaping, preserved early-hammer scaling, round-robin shape parity,
absolute body-level parity, and headroom.

The draft's robust straight register trend failed its own real-bank safety gate:
piano_C6_f.wav needed +13.65 dB, above the 12 dB emergency stop. A quadratic fit
then passed that source-only check, but the new runtime sweep exposed a 6.14 dB
attack jump at a zone boundary. That was the wrong trend: it fitted the source's
abrupt curvature instead of normalizing it.

The final fit is one minimax line through all layers, roots, and round robins,
with its slope capped at 0.26 dB per semitone. The first per-layer fit passed its
local shape checks but caused a 3.42 dB perceived-loudness jump at the v95/v96
forte boundary. The whole-bank fit removes that envelope discontinuity. Its
measured maximum correction is 12.55 dB, so the emergency stop is 13 dB rather
than the draft's unrepresentable 12 dB. Onset detection anchors its 3-percent
threshold to the untouched first 40 ms, so a raised body cannot move the
measurement origin.

After 16-bit quantization, the final bank's maximum same-root RR shape mismatch
is 0.000501 dB, body mismatch is 0.000209 dB, and fitted-trend residual is
0.001453 dB. Peaks span 0.144226–0.899963 after one shared headroom shift. A
second identical piano bake changed zero of 54 hashes. All 18 prepare.py tests
pass, and the repitched runtime body sweep passes at every root and both sides of
every zone boundary.

### Conditioned-bank FRA measurement

The release binary embedded the conditioned assets with the existing 60 ms
sample and 100 ms model releases. On the exact dry FRA key track, equal 20 ms
windows immediately before note-off and over the final 20 ms of each 62.5 ms
gap measured:

| note | conditioned-only gap loss |
|---|---:|
| D4 | 23.1 dB |
| F#4 | 26.4 dB |
| A4 | 19.3 dB |

These measurements came from the first safe conditioned-bank candidate. The final
gradual-line conditioner changes exact levels but not the conclusion; a dedicated
legacy-release negative control is retained in the Rust suite. Normalization alone
is not being presented as the phrase fix. On that final bank, the direct legacy
release loses 31.37 dB at D4, 28.25 dB at F#4, and 28.92 dB at A4 over the same
62.5 ms window.

### Release calibration

The direct release regression failed red at 30.43 dB on the first modeled-only
FRA key before implementation. The engine-level conditioned phrase failed at
23.01, 26.38, and 18.98 dB. A single explicit release override now leaves every
existing LA caller and every alternate piano on the legacy path.

The draft started at 0.60 s. The final 0.45 s exponential, combined with a
conditioned-bank handoff of gain 4.0 and a 180–450 ms fade, measures 4.13–6.77 dB
over the FRA gaps on sampled fixtures and 7.32–8.12 dB on modeled fixtures.
Every path is at least 28.53 dB down after 250 ms and dead after the 2 s reap
probe. The forte recordings are 4.9 dB louder in the K-weighted velocity oracle,
so both owners receive the same 0.569 trim above the v95/v96 layer boundary. The
full dry engine phrase stays below the 10 dB ceiling without changing the global
square velocity law.

### Code review and simplicity pass

One independent code-reviewer pass found two Issues and two Nits. Both Issues were
valid test weaknesses, not shipped-path defects. The runtime bank oracle had used
the production LA crossfade while its sustain model was intentionally silent, so
it now delays the test-only fade beyond the probe and measures the sample bank
alone. Alternate-bank isolation now compares GM0 and GM1 routes with explicit
legacy constructors and narrowly pins GM3 and the non-piano LA canary. The stale
60 ms release comment and quadratic decision-log entry were corrected.

The quality-only simplicity pass kept the explicit optional T60 route and one
small LaVoice entry point. It found no dead code or useful abstraction to add;
the three explicit legacy controls remain duplicated on purpose because each
proves a different routing call site. Workspace lint then exposed an eight-argument
factory. Removing the redundant sample-presence argument and deriving that fact
from the bank kept the interface below the lint limit without adding an abstraction.
