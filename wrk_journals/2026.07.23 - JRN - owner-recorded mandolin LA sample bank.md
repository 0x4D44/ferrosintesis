# 2026.07.23 — JRN — owner-recorded mandolin LA sample bank

Arthur recorded his own mandolin and asked for it to become a ferrosintesis voice. This is the
build log and the handoff for the remaining work.

## Where the mandolin belongs (settled by the code, not by preference)

General MIDI Level 1 has **no mandolin program**. GM2, Roland GS and Yamaha XG all place one as
a **bank-select variation of the Steel Guitar**, and two of the three put the variation in the
**LSB**, which is what `voices::make_variation` already implements.

- **CC0 (bank MSB) on GM 24–25 is already taken.** `altbank.rs:1128` maps `24..=25` to
  `voices::make(.., samples = false)` — CC0 ≠ 0 on the guitars already *means* "pure-model, no
  sample layer", pinned by `altbank_guitars_skip_sample_layer`. The mandolin cannot go there.
- **CC32 (bank LSB) is the correct seam.** Target cell: **`(25, 96)`**. That is the XG Mandolin
  slot, and it sits alongside the Ukulele the repo already has at `(24, 96)` — Yamaha uses LSB
  96 for the "related but different plucked instrument" on the guitars. The existing `(24, 96)`
  cell being a real XG voice is the in-repo corroboration for 96.
- Dispatch order at `engine.rs:2462` is `altbank::make` (CC0≠0) XOR
  `make_variation(CC32)` XOR `make`. A non-zero CC0 suppresses the CC32 path entirely.

### The one contract change

`make_variation` accepts `samples` and discards it (`let _ = samples;`, `voices.rs:11777`) with
the comment *"Variations are modeled-only (no dedicated sample banks)"*. That is a statement of
**current inventory, not a design invariant** — on any real GM module every voice is sampled,
variations included. The mandolin is the first sampled variation.

The invariant that *is* load-bearing in that function is RNG-purity: every arm draws only from
the passed `seed`, so the `None` path stays bit-identical to `make`. Plain `LaVoice::wrap` takes
no seed, so the minimal change preserves it. Keep all eight existing arms untouched.

## The recording

`DR0000_0198.wav`, 24-bit/48 kHz stereo, 2:45. Three passes over ten positions (open + 5th fret
on all four courses, plus 10th and 12th on the E course), at normal / soft / hard. Ten pitches:
G3 C4 D4 G4 A4 D5 E5 A5 D6 E6 = MIDI 55 60 62 67 69 74 76 81 86 88.

Positions were chosen so no two coincide: courses are 7 semitones apart, so the 7th fret would
have duplicated the next open string — open+5th and 10th+12th never collide.

## Findings worth keeping

- **Octave detection is the whole game on this instrument.** Autocorrelation reported D6 as D5
  and G3 as G4. A mandolin's low fundamentals are weak (small body radiates them poorly), so the
  lower note's harmonics contain the upper note's entire comb and a bare harmonic comb scores
  the octave *above* higher. Fixed with a **sub-harmonic veto**: a candidate with real energy one
  octave below itself is not the fundamental. Five of the ten zones are octave pairs, so this is
  not an edge case. `prepare.py` has the same hazard covered by `TWO_F_STRONG` — **the mandolin
  family must be listed there**, or the measured roots for the low zones will come out an octave
  high.
- **A 0.28 s onset refractory hides re-plucks.** Two soft takes (G3, G4) contained a fluff
  followed by the real note 0.17–0.23 s later — invisible to the detector that produced the cut,
  so the zone would have double-struck on every note. Caught by an independent oracle (envelope
  rise inside the kept window), *and* independently flagged by the timbre measurement as the only
  two notes whose soft take was brighter than its loud take. Two unrelated oracles converging is
  what promoted it from noise to a bug.
- **"Last take wins" needs a credibility guard.** Applied naively it re-anchored A5_p onto
  −49 dBFS finger noise 0.88 s later, turning the cut into near-silence. A real retake is played
  at a comparable dynamic; anything >12 dB below the original attack is handling noise.
- **The third dynamic layer was measured and dropped.** Normal vs hard: 6/10 sign consistency,
  median +0.09 harmonics — a coin flip. Soft vs loud: 8/10, median +0.56, sign test p≈0.055 —
  weak but consistent and in the physically expected direction. Shipping `_p` and `_f` only.
- **Intonation:** open strings −12 to +1 cents; fretted notes sharp, up to **+29 cents at the
  5th fret on the thickest course**. Harmless — roots are stored as measured — but it is why
  nominal pitches must never be used as zone roots here.

  reproduces real tremolo alternation, which random round-robin selection would destroy.
  A new session **replaces** this bank rather than augmenting it: round robins must share one
  setup, so takes from different days cannot be mixed.
