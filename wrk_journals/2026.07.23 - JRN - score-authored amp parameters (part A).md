# 2026.07.23 — JRN — score-authored amp parameters, Part A

Round 1 gave the CC0 alt bank its own lead amp+cab (`e2a78c1`). Arthur listened, then reframed the
whole request:

> "Maybe what I'm really asking for is some more knobs which we can use to control the distortion &
> overdrive... Has to be author — that's where the real magic will happen."

So the deliverable stops being *my* voicing choice and becomes a **control surface**. I argued for a
design first and Arthur agreed. This is that work.

## Why a design, and what it actually had to settle

Not ceremony. Exposing knobs is easy; three things are not:

1. `Drive` is **destroyed and rebuilt** on Program Change, CC0 and CC121 — a rebuild throws away nine
   biquad states, the `MicroCab` line, and heap-allocates. Any parameter feeding construction is
   either lost at the next rebuild or becomes a click generator.
2. This area has **already shipped two bugs**: KILN-00033 (authored CC93/94 discarded at Program
   Change — cost three Big Weather tracks minutes of lead echo) and KILN-00034 (an NRPN select
   corrupting the bend range to 24 semitones). This change edits the very guard added for 00034.
3. The surface is **permanent** — once an album authors it into a committed `.mid`, a wrong
   parameter number is baked into album source.

The design's load-bearing choice is that values are **offsets from the shipped voicing, 64 =
unchanged**. That makes the inertness invariant structural rather than a special case, and it means
the oracle-tuned 29/30 × main/alt matrix from `e2a78c1` composes with authoring instead of being
discarded by it. It also lets the change ship with a **0-of-124 render-diff**, which is the strongest
safety evidence available here.

## Reconnaissance findings that changed the design

**`retune_peak` and `retune_highpass` already exist** (`dsp.rs:544`, `:516`), alongside
`retune_lowpass` and `retune_bandpass` — added for the reed voices, which move formant gain with
blowing pressure at control rate. My draft had proposed adding them. So the DSP crate needs **no
change at all**, and the click-free in-place retune is a proven in-repo pattern rather than a new
mechanism. Good reminder to check before designing a helper.

**`Biquad`'s constructors clamp to `.min(0.49)` of the sample rate**, so an out-of-range parameter
cannot push a corner past Nyquist — the stability question for the Tightness knob's upper end is
already answered by construction at `dsp.rs:478`.

**The base voicing values live only as inline literals inside `Drive::new`.** `from_stages` takes
already-constructed `Biquad`s, so an offset applied later has nothing to offset *from*. Fix: `Drive`
stores its own base voicing, set once at construction, and `apply_params` becomes a pure function of
(base, offsets). One source of truth, no duplication between `new` and the apply path.

## Review — what the two passes changed

**Three-lens adversarial pass** (state / DSP / scope, then mutual refutation): 23 findings, 16
survived, 7 refuted. The load-bearing ones:

- **`post` was hand-waved.** All three lenses independently found that "level-compensated like
  `amp_sim`" is not a spec — `amp_sim`'s formula is a function of an absolute drive the program path
  doesn't have, and the four shipped `post` constants are hand-measured. Rewrote it as a trim anchored
  at the knob's neutral point so 64 is bit-identical, with the residual *measured*, not asserted.
- **Drive at 127 breaks the alias budget.** Measured the draft's `g1 ×4` pushing the worst fold bin
  to ≈−36 dB against the −40 dB floor the repo already asserts. So the alias oracle now *sets* the
  knob's top end — the whole reason to design before freezing it into album source.
- **Cut Bite.** `g1` and `bias` are one expression in the shaper, so Drive moves Bite's own measure
  further than Bite does — it fails separability by construction. And round 1 had already measured
  `bias` and refused to vary it. Spent the slot on **Cab Tone** (the HF cliff corner, downward-only),
  which is the real "which cab" axis and the only level-independent tone control.
- **`Biquad` can't be read back**, so an offset needs a stored base or it duplicates a table Part B is
  chartered to change. `Drive` now carries `DriveBase`.
- **The click detector can't see the zipper** the design names as its hazard. Scalars now slew
  per-sample; the oracle looks for a spectral line at `sr/BLOCK` = 689 Hz.

**Cross-family pass** (Codex, GPT-5.6-sol) caught four things the Claude panel missed:

- A **real arithmetic error**: my `post` ratio form divides by zero at `k ≈ 1.585` and inverts sign
  at `k = 2`, because I'd extended `amp_sim`'s non-negative-domain formula to negative `n`. Replaced
  with a strictly-positive exponential trim.
- **NRPN `0x30` is not collision-free** — no manufacturer namespace, and Casio's XW uses those exact
  numbers. Downgraded the claim to "contained" and recorded that the collision-free alternative
  (SysEx + manufacturer ID) is *unavailable* to a published crate, so NRPN is the best we can use.
- The `data_msb` fix **still leaked from the initial null/null state**; a Data Entry with no live
  selector must be dropped.
- **No priming for a same-tick rig** — a single primed bool would ramp five of six parameters up from
  64. Made per-parameter, and a rebuilt `Drive` seeds from persisted `amp_cur`.

Design marked **SELF-APPROVED** (not human-signed-off; the human gate is integration). No
architectural fork remained: the one either/or (NRPN vs SysEx) is settled because SysEx needs a
manufacturer ID we don't hold. Proceeding to implement Part A. Baseline binary for the render-diff
captured at `852a19d` (ferrosintesis v0.21.56).
