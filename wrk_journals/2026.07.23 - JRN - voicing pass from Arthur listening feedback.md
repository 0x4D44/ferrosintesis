## Why this is a COORDINATED build, not isolated tweaks

The plucked presets are defined **relative to each other** by oracles. Fixing the banjo
alone (t60 0.75→3.0, done + oracle updated) immediately broke
`sitar_shamisen_koto_have_distinct_pluck_presets`: with the banjo ringing, its sustained
upper partials caught up to the sitar's jawari buzz, and the sitar/shamisen are *also*
mis-fit (sitar too fast, shamisen dead), so the whole set has to move together to stay
mutually distinct. The banjo change is correct in isolation but can't land alone.

## Why it needs Arthur, not a unilateral apply

Matching the references means **large** t60 increases across the family — nylon 3.8→~8,
sitar 2.1→~10, shamisen 0.55→~2.4. That materially changes how every album's guitars and
ethnic plucks *ring*. The albums were composed and mixed against the current (shorter)
sound; "more correct vs the references" may or may not be the album aesthetic Arthur
wants. This is a genuine fork.

### The honest caveat for Arthur

Matching the references makes the family ring beautifully but **narrows their mutual
contrast** — the KILN-00042 hold, now engaged in the playing register by the longer t60,
homogenises the plucked family's brightness (KILN-00050, amplified). Each instrument sounds
good in isolation (Arthur approved), but they are more alike than before. The fix is
per-instrument voicing or a bright-dependent held corner — KILN-00050, whose value just rose.
