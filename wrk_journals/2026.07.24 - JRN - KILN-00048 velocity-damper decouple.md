# 2026.07.24 — JRN — KILN-00048: decoupling velocity from the KS loop-damper decay

Keystone of the plucked-family calibration. The KS loop damper's corner scaled with
velocity, so a plucked string's decay RATE depended on how hard you plucked it (physically
wrong — damping is a property of the string, not the pluck). It also blocked the KILN-00042
hold on the dark basses (GM33/35 were `Off` purely for it). Designed with Fable via HLD.

## What landed
- **The anchor.** The loop damper corner is pinned at its vel-100 value per preset
  (`KS_ANCHOR_VN`), so decay rate is a function of pitch and t60 alone. The
  velocity→brightness the damper used to supply is transferred ONCE onto the excitation.
  Anchoring at vel 100 (not 1.0) makes every vel-100 render bit-identical — the cheapest
  canary, and it kept `v2_untouched_pluck_signatures` and every vel-100 golden green.
- **VEL_LEVEL_EXP re-derived** for the plucked family (the decouple makes low-vel notes ring
  longer → windowed level rises → the compensation rises to hold the square law at 2.0). A
  machine-checked `[1.5, 2.35]` anti-papering bound replaces Fable's informal 2.2 red line.
- **Koto pitch-relative pp floor** (`KS_PICK_F0_FLOOR`, c=2.2) — an absolute 300 Hz floor
  let a soft koto collapse to a near-sine; the pitch floor + a bound-capped e=2.35 hold it
  in the square law (documented Pareto-frontier residual, k60 1.79).
- **`ks_decay_rate_is_velocity_invariant`** — the rendered end-to-end decay-rate oracle.

## The two Fable bug-catches (why the escalations mattered)
Fable's escalation budget earned its keep. The first HLD's §2.2 transfer had a real bug it
only found on re-escalation:
1. **Double-application.** Applying the velocity factor to `pick_lp` AND `out_lp` in series
   is t², not t — correct for single-filter presets (STEEL/NYLON) and the floor-pinned wound
   basses, but it over-darkened the dual-filter koto/shamisen. Mechanism correction: `out_lp`
   is a post-loop OUTPUT filter on an amplitude-normalized signal, so it's a LEVEL term; the
   double-application's out-swing was accidentally feeding the windowed metric in the passing
   direction, which is why removing it (correct) made the honest law flatter, not steeper —
   the opposite of the prediction. Fix: single-application, spill only the floor-truncated
   share to `out_lp`.
2. **Shortfall denominator.** My first single-application cut over-routed for the dark wound
   basses (a pre-decouple floor clip got double-counted onto `out_lp`, moving do-not-touch
   GM33). Fix: floor the denominator at 300 too.

Both falsified a Fable prediction on the census — and each time, following the "re-escalate,
don't paper against a guessed invariant" rule surfaced a genuine defect rather than a fudge.

## The deferred bass flip (a scope call)
KILN-00048's stated acceptance was lifting the GM33/35 hold-exclusion. But flipping
BASS/FRETLESS to `Derived` (tried, reverted) makes them ring long enough to expose a
velocity-KEY-dependent sub/mwah LEVEL bend a scalar VEL_LEVEL_EXP cannot fit (FRETLESS spread
0.58 — no exponent lands both keys in ±0.25). That bend is the §3 bass-family reconciliation
(KILN-00045), and Fable's final sequence groups the flip WITH §3. So 00048 delivers the
DECOUPLE (proven by the rendered oracle, which covers BASS/FRETLESS and is green with them
`Off`); the flip lands in KILN-00045, WITH the key-dependence fix. The decay-rate coupling —
the actual defect — is gone regardless of the hold.

## Test fallout (all expected, all at non-127 velocities)
- Square law re-derived (24/26/27/28/29/30/31/36/37/105/106; koto capped).
- `v2` NYLON signature rms −0.25 dB (VEL_LEVEL_EXP pivots at vel 127, not the vel-100 anchor).
- `shaped_g7` NYLON canary (Legacy, per-cell ±0.05) re-baselined; PIZZ HEAD re-captured as
  post-decouple Legacy (forced `exc_model: Legacy`, then reverted — the decouple is upstream
  of the Shaped/Legacy split, so its shift belongs in both).
- `guitar_low_velocity_seam`: the decouple amplified KILN-00051 at low velocity (soft high
  note rings more than its soft sampled onset) — bounded exception ≤1.4.

## Lessons
- A vel-100 (or vel-127) anchor choice IS the bit-identity contract — pick the pivot that
  makes the frozen tests bit-stable, and the rest of the blast radius is exactly the intended
  set.
- `out_lp` is a level term, not a timbre term — opening a post-loop filter through occupied
  spectrum raises transmitted loudness (× the BS.1770 HF shelf). A velocity swing on it is an
  in-family level slope, which is why the compensation table exists at all.
- Frozen-Legacy baseline tests (`shaped_g7`) break on ANY upstream model change; re-capturing
  the Legacy side requires forcing `exc_model` — the canary is designed to fail and force
  that conscious re-baseline.
