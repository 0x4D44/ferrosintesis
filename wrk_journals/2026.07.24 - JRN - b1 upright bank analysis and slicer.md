# 2026.07.24 — B1 upright sample bank: analysis, gain recovery, slicer

Arthur recorded his own Yamaha B1 upright (Tascam DR-05) and asked to turn it into a
sampled piano model, auditionable against the existing pianos, raw takes archived as
`.opus` under a repo-root `samples/` if we keep it.

## What happened
- **Forensic characterisation** of the raw takes (parallel workflow + adversarial
  verification). Full reports at `D:/tmp_b1/strands/{segment,gain,clip,room,tuning,
  timbre,pipeline}.md`. Headlines: strong Railsback stretch + F3/F#3 bridge break;
  sustain pedal up (layerable); 2–5 Hz rumble needs a 20 Hz HPF; take channel L.
- **The first hard take (0197) was clipped** on 10 notes (E3-up riding the rail). Arthur
  re-recorded it ~9 dB lower as **0200** — verified clean, complete A0→C8.
- **Gain recovery.** No calibration notes in 0200 (Arthur forgot), so recovered the
  offset hard-vs-hard against the unclipped 0197 notes, separating gain from playing
  force via gain-invariant spectral brightness: **−9.4 dB ± 0.6 dB**, and Arthur played
  the same dynamic (force −0.1 dB). A ~2 dB bass→treble tilt + stereo-fingerprint change
  say the recorder was **re-seated** between takes — same instrument/room/distance/tune.
- A stray **0198.wav** exists (abandoned 2nd hard attempt, clipped + fast). Ignore.
- **Slicer built + tested:** `tools/b1-slice/slice.py` (+ `test_slice.py`, 49 tests
  green). Segments a ladder take on channel L, fits the inharmonic partial series for
  pitch, aligns to the expected ladder (Needleman-Wunsch), writes per-note 24-bit slices
  + a manifest carrying measured f1/f0/B (f1 is the bake's zone root). Ran clean on 0200
  (27/27); 0195's messy normal/soft passes need a few `--assign`/`--drop` overrides
  (missed A0/C8 in the quiet takes) — do that during the bake.

## Design agreed — see `wrk_docs/2026.07.24 - HLD - b1 upright sample bank.md`
root = measured f1 → exact ET, inharmonicity kept. Three timbre layers (soft/normal ←
0195, hard ← 0200), loudness from the engine's shared `vel_amp` law. GM0 CC0=**5**
(free). DSP: 20 Hz HPF → band-limited 48→44.1k → trim ~1.6 s + fade → per-layer peak
norm → 16-bit dither. First-party provenance. No version bump (release-only repo).
