### Predicate lesson for the payload oracle (A6)

The first two drafts of `no_document_states_a_stale_asset_crate_count` both false-positived,
and each failure was informative:

- draft 1 flagged the NOTICE's "The **ten** below are not [CC0]" — a correct statement about
  a different quantity;
- draft 2 flagged "The remaining **eleven** banks are CC0" — which WAS genuinely stale
  (should be fourteen), but revealed that a subset count is not a total claim.

The predicate now binds a number to its noun (walking back over filler only) and skips
subset markers (`remaining`, `other`, ...). Per `CLAUDE.md`'s warning that a derived oracle
is only as good as its enumeration predicate,
`the_oracles_reject_the_prose_they_were_written_to_catch` runs the REAL extractor over the
verbatim historical prose and asserts it trips — and over benign prose and asserts it does
not.
