# Review journal — dark-Salamander sample crate

Reviewed `crates/ferrosintesis-samples-dark-salamander/` statically under the
overnight code-review contract. No code, build, test, render, application,
generator or exploratory harness was run.

The multi-lens review and adversarial verification confirmed:

- the published CC0 selector and A/B baseline route users to the wrong
  instruments (MM-BUG-KILN-00122);
- the documented dark-grand rebake retains obsolete generated WAVs
  (MM-BUG-KILN-00123);
- the defining high-shelf derivation lacks a durable semantic/output oracle
  (Draft MM-REQ-KILN-00033).

Static asset inspection found 54 current WAVs, all complete PCM mono 44.1 kHz
16-bit files with unique payload hashes. The current source-derived and
destination filename sets match. These facts refuted present asset corruption;
they do not replace a future regression oracle.
