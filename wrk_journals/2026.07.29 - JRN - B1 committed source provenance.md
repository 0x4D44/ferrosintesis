# B1 committed source provenance

MM-BUG-KILN-00168 found that the committed B1 Opus source archives lived outside
the provenance oracle’s automatic `tools/ferrosintesis-samples/*-src` discovery.
Their names appeared in packaged provenance, but their exact bytes were unbound.

The oracle now retains automatic tool-source discovery and adds an explicit
registry for repo-root first-party source archives. `samples/b1-upright` is the
first registered root. Display paths remain repository-relative, and the B1
provenance table now ships both independently verified SHA-256 digests.

The adversarial test proves the registry enumerates both Opus files and that a
document naming them without hashes still reports both as unpinned. Fail-before:
the B1 source root was absent. Pass-after: all 5 provenance tests, formatting,
and focused clippy pass.
