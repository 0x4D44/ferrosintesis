## GM32/33 dry distinctness

Rendered the current default-bank acoustic bass and fingered bass through the
shipping profile with an identical 12-note phrase and CC91/93/94 set to zero.
No alternate sample bank was selected.

Confirmed:

- GM32 native level: -20.95 LUFS, -4.26 dBTP;
- GM33 native level: -18.21 LUFS, -2.74 dBTP;
- the 24-bit listening files both verify at -20.0 LUFS;
- over the 0.45–10.20 second phrase, GM32 carries 2.9% of spectral energy at
  400–2000 Hz versus GM33's 1.5%;
- GM32's held-note body drops 25.01 dB versus GM33's 23.15 dB over the same
  1.4-second interval;
- aligned, loudness-matched signals correlate at 0.762 and leave a -3.16 dB
  null relative to their mean RMS;
- their median 30–2000 Hz spectral-magnitude difference is 4.61 dB.

The measurements confirm two different renders, but do not prove that the
musical identities are far enough apart. Added a factory-level render oracle
so GM32 and GM33 cannot silently route to effectively identical signals while
their preset tables remain different. `_cal/listening/bass/` contains the blind
pair and answer key. No bass preset changed pending Arthur's verdict.
