## What the fix does

`SaxLoopVoice` gains multi-slice "grain motion": as the current recorded slice nears its
end it crossfades (25 ms smoothstep) to another phase-aligned slice elsewhere in the
steady body, RMS-matched and gain-clamped to [0.5, 2.0]. Gated at the construction site
on `program == 67 && key >= 68`, so everything else keeps the single-slice reader.

## Two process notes worth keeping

**A refutation can be right about the code and wrong about the world.** The adversarial
reviewer's key-58 finding was refuted on the grounds that the residual "is tracked by an
in-flight item" — 00176 itself, still `Fixed`. True at the time, and false the instant I
closed it. When a verdict's premise is a state your own next action changes, the verdict
does not hold. I kept the refutation's *verified facts* (key 58 renders bit-identically;
the RNG stream is untouched) and discarded its conclusion.

**The adversarial pass earned its keep on the boundary question.** A reviewer flagged that
key 67 keeps the buggy reader on the same loop key 68 was fixed for. That finding was
CAP-dropped before verification, so I measured it myself — and it was the single most
consequential fact in the whole pass. Worth remembering that a dropped finding is not a
refuted one.
