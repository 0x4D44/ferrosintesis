## The render-diff inventory for 00171 (previously unrecorded)

`MM-BUG-KILN-00171`'s own Fix note asked for the render-diff inventory before Fixed, and the
fixer left no evidence of running it. Rather than close on inference, I did the event census
first and then confirmed it with a bounded two-piece render diff against a `503e9ee^`
baseline binary (both binaries mtime-checked as freshly built, per CLAUDE.md's stale-baseline
trap).

**Census.** The `renorm_ticks` seed is masked to `0x1fff`, so the first correction lands
between tick 57,345 and 65,536 — i.e. ~1.30–1.49 s at 44.1 kHz. Reach is therefore confined
to `Sine` instances that tick more than ~57k times: audio-rate oscillators on long-held
notes, and control-rate LFOs held beyond ~21 s. Notably the organ wavetable builders
(`voices.rs:6396`, `:6426`) fill only `ORGAN_TABLE_LEN` = 1024 ticks, so every organ table is
provably bit-identical.

**Confirmation.**

- `albums/fable5/Winter Guests/midi/01 - Winter Guests, Part One.mid` (9.5 min, sustained):
  **differs** — 5.69% of samples, max |delta| 4 LSB against a 26,029 peak (−78.3 dBFS),
  delta RMS −101.6 dBFS. Dither-floor territory; a bounded correction, not a structural change.
- `demos/orchestral_demo.mid`: **bit-identical** — the NON-diff, and the one that pins the
  mechanism: nothing in it holds a phasor past the first-correction threshold.

That is exactly the predicted pattern, with no unexpected reach. Per CLAUDE.md the `.opus`
renders are git-ignored build output, so no asset refresh is owed.
