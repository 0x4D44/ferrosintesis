## Worth remembering

**A two-line revert can reproduce a static-review finding that no one had measured.**
00013 was raised from source reading: >128 queued messages *should* realloc
`RealtimeSynth.pending` on the audio thread. Restoring the old ring size and the old
incremental knob path turned that inference into a number — one counted `realloc`
inside `rtalloc::measure`. When a bug is raised statically, the fails-before revert
is the cheapest way to find out whether the reported mechanism was actually real.
