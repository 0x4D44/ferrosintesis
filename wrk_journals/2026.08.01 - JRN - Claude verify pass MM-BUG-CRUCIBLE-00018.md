## Worth remembering

**The regression test proves the fix; a scratch fixture proves the BUG.** The shipped
test failed on the pre-fix path at its first assertion (`44 != 1` discarded events)
and stopped there — it never reached the interesting question. The report's actual
claim was "a dropped NoteOff can leave a voice sounding", which its own fixture
cannot show: it interleaves on/off pairs, so overflow drops complete pairs and
strands nothing.

Reordering the fixture — all 150 note-ons, then all 150 note-offs, so overflow eats
only note-offs — and printing the state gave:

    after overflow: xruns=44 playing=true pos=0 live_voices=128

That is the stuck-note consequence, measured. Worth doing whenever a bug asserts a
consequence its regression test only implies: the test is written to pin the fixed
behaviour, not to demonstrate the defect, and those are different fixtures.
