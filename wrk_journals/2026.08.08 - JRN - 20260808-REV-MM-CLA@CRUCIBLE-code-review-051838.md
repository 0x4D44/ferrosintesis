## Decisions and surprises

**The most useful result was a refutation, not a finding.** Two of four reviewers
independently concluded that the bottle's `PROVENANCE.md` SHA-256 pin is verified only by the
Python suite and never by `cargo test`. Both were wrong, and wrong the same way: neither found
`crates/ferrosintesis/src/provenance.rs`. Its
`every_committed_source_is_pinned_by_a_packaged_document` (`:426`) hand-rolls SHA-256, hashes
everything under `tools/ferrosintesis-samples/*-src`, and requires the digest to appear in a
packaged `PROVENANCE.md`. One reviewer also asserted the Python bake suite "isn't gated by
anything automatic" — `.deltic-integrate.toml:59,71` runs it on every integration touching
`crates/` or `tools/`.

Two agents agreeing is not evidence. Both searched `licensing.rs` (which the brief named) and
neither looked for a sibling oracle the brief did not name. **Naming files in a review brief
bounds where reviewers look, including away from the answer.** A future brief for this repo
should name `provenance.rs` alongside `licensing.rs`, or say explicitly "find every oracle that
touches this crate" rather than listing them.

**The pitch label question was settled by controls inside the same file.** The shipped WAV is
`bottleloop_G3.wav` but its root is 205.0 Hz — 77.7 cents sharp of G3. The question was which
half is wrong, the label or the measurement. Two zones in the same `sampler.rs` settle it:
`violin_G3_f.wav => 196.00` (`:368`) shows the repo labels 196 Hz as G3, and
`pizzbass_G#3.wav => 205.95` (`:922`) shows it labels ~205 Hz as G#3. Then
`bottle_loop_pitch_integrity` (`:8304`) asserts rendered pitch within ±45 cents while
repitching by the declared 205.0 root — a true fundamental of 196.0 would land 77.7 cents flat
and fail it. So the measurement is sound and the filename is the wrong artifact. That last step
is inference: I did not execute the suite, and the report says so.

**A cost claim checked out exactly.** Recomputing `find_bottle_loop` from its own constants
gives 30,560 square roots and 67,384,800 multiply-accumulates, matching the doc comment at
`sampler.rs:44-46` to the digit. Worth recording because comments carrying performance numbers
usually rot; this one did not, and the MM-BUG-KILN-00064 prewarm fix around it holds up.

**Severity discipline mattered more than usual here.** The headline candidate — "the crate's
three tests cannot detect a substituted WAV" — is true as stated but was offered at
Medium-High. It ignored the Python re-bake's exact-length and ≤24-LSB assertions and
`parse_wav`'s format assertion, both of which run in the gate. Downgraded to a requirement
about the missing `EXPECTED_BYTES` pin rather than filed as a bug. Per CLAUDE.md's
"enumerate all of L" rule I counted all 25 sample crates before writing it: exactly four lack
the pin — bottle, musescore, orchestral2, strings.

## Blocked

**Nothing was filed to `bugs/` or `reqs/`.** `deltic` is denied in this session's permission
mode, so no ID could be minted, and hand-deriving one is both forbidden by the task brief and
demonstrably unsafe here: `bugs/` runs `MM-BUG-CRUCIBLE-00001`…`00022` but skips `00007`,
which `reqs/MM-REQ-CRUCIBLE-00007.md` holds. `max+1` over `bugs/` alone would reissue a live
number, and git merges the two halves without a conflict.

Complete drafts for all four bugs and all three requirements are embedded in §5 and §6 of the
CR report, each with observation, evidence, file:line and fix. A follow-up with `deltic`
available needs only to mint the IDs and paste them across. Until that happens **these defects
are not in the Open queue and the fix loop will not drain them** — that is the one loose end
from this pass.

`PowerShell` and several `Bash` forms were also denied, so no binary inspection of the WAV was
possible. Its geometry was established arithmetically instead: 145,574 bytes − 44-byte
canonical header = 145,530 ÷ 2 = 72,765 mono 16-bit frames = exactly 1.6500 s at 44.1 kHz,
matching `PROVENANCE.md`'s stated trim of source frames 4411…77176. That agrees with the
72,765-frame figure independently quoted in `sampler.rs`.
