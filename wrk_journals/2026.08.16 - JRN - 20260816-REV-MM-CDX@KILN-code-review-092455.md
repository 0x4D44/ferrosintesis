# Review journal — YDP Grand sample crate

Static review covered `crates/ferrosintesis-samples-ydp-grand/` plus the narrow
generator and consumer paths needed to verify its contracts. Seven independent
lenses and a lead re-check found the current nine WAVs structurally sound,
distinct, consistently routed, and consistently documented.

Two maintenance-path defects survived adversarial review. YDP generation writes
zones to final paths before it proves the complete bank, so a late failure can
leave mixed generations (`MM-BUG-KILN-00205`). The shared Rust-table generator
opens final `src/lib.rs` before writing and formatting succeed
(`MM-BUG-KILN-00206`).

The pass did not duplicate the existing YDP source-pin requirement
`MM-REQ-KILN-00144` or the payload/root-binding bug `MM-BUG-KILN-00201`. The
latter remains awaiting independent verification; its recorded equal-sized PCM
swap is not exercised by the current filename/root source scan.

exploratory harness ran. Deltic owns commit and integration for this pass.
