# Review journal — dependency-free FLAC decoder

Reviewed
`D:\worktrees\ferrosintesis\20260816-REV-MM-CDX@KILN-code-review-214226\crates\ferrosintesis-flac\`
at Deltic-supplied baseline `59509f7b02f2d6cbd85b4bdad49294b8c4600f2e`.
This was a static pass: no app, build, test, decoder, generator, render, package
command, or exploratory harness ran.

Six independent finder lenses covered correctness, security, performance/scale,
reliability, maintainability, and test coverage. A separate devil's advocate
challenged the promoted findings. The lead re-read the relevant source and RFC 9639
rules and checked both ledgers for overlap.

Five defects survived: unbounded allocation from `total_samples`, unchecked predictor
overflow, discarded CRC-8/CRC-16, accepted forbidden LPC precision, and rejection of
valid explicit 44.1 kHz encodings. They are Open as `MM-BUG-KILN-00232` through
`MM-BUG-KILN-00236`. Draft `MM-REQ-KILN-00237` records the missing explicit codec-path
and strict-prefix oracles while preserving caller tests' indirect decode of current banks.

The skeptic refuted an alleged byte-alignment panic, and the primary specification
refuted a claim that negative LPC shifts are valid. Both remain out of the ledger.
