# 2026.11.22 — JRN — Lantern Year album (ten parallel composers)

Claude Fable 5.2 lead, ten Fable 5.2 (high effort) composer agents, one per track, in
parallel — the model, the effort and the parallel fan-out were all Arthur's instruction.

## What was done

- New album `albums/fable5/Lantern Year`: ten tracks, 50:00, each 4:52–5:05. Ten nights at
  a lighthouse across a year, each in its own idiom, bound by a five-note BEAM cell
  (degrees 1-5-6-5-8), a 16-bar keeper's hymn and a tubular-bell LAMP stroke.
- The lead wrote the framework first (`conductor.py`, `material.py` with the shared
  detectors and three album-wide oracles, `COMPOSER-NOTES.md` with the contract and ten
  briefs) and landed it. Engine, builder, verifier and analyzer are the Slipstream copies.
- Each composer wrote one `movements/tNN_*.py` in a shared worktree, oracle-first, and
  reported build → `--verify` PASS → render → `analyze.py` PASS.
- "Integrate as you go" (Arthur): each finished track was copied into its own fresh
  worktree, rebuilt there, `cmp`'d byte-identical against the composer's MIDI, verified,
  committed and integrated — twelve landings in all (framework, ten tracks, this one).
- Final landing: `album_manifest.json`, `ALBUM.md`, `README.md`, the `ALBUMS` entry in
  `render-catalog`, the root README entry and counts, and the corrections below folded
  into the composer notes.

## Evidence (observed by the lead, in the final worktree)

- `python3 build.py` then `python3 build.py --verify`: exit 0, 215 checks, none failing.
- All ten rendered with the release synth; `python3 analyze.py`: `AUDIO: PASS - 10
  track(s) analyzed`. Mono loss 0.36–1.54 dB (cap 2.0); max sample step ≤ 17794 (cap 22000).
- `cargo fmt --all -- --check`, `cargo clippy -p render-catalog --all-targets -- -D
  warnings`, `cargo test -p render-catalog` (includes the committed-MIDI overlap audit): green.

## What the composers found

- `ferrosintesis --solo` is **0-based** (the CLI README says so; the repo CLAUDE.md's
  `--solo 11` example does not). The lead's notes said 1–16; corrected mid-flight by
  message and in the committed notes.
- Every render is loudness-normalised on its own, stems included, so a stem is not
  comparable with the mix. Working method: solo the voice together with a reference voice
  that plays alone somewhere, and calibrate on that passage.
- Basses (32/33/35) render hot — every composer who used one cut it to volume 72–78.
  Harp and fingerstyle steel guitar render quiet.
- In dense orchestral scores even a mild pan cost 1.5–5 dB of a stem's level in mono;
  T8 and T10 centred their tune-carrying voices to stay under the 2 dB cap.
- Unverified by the lead: T9 reports the brush kit's hi-hats (keys 42/44, ch-10 PC 40)
  tripping the click scan at 28495 in a quiet mix, and T7 the synth kit (PC 24) kick and
  hats at 38846. Both composers worked around it in the score (hats removed; kit volume
  72). A peak-normalised solo-hat repro by the lead was inconclusive — bright noise at
  full scale has large sample steps by nature — so no bug was minted.

## Notes for next time

- The repo CLAUDE.md advises running album composers serially. This parallel run landed
  all ten; what made it work was a contract small enough to read in one sitting, lazy
  per-track loading (`build.py --track N`), and one file per composer.
- The first launch hit the account's usage limit within seconds; all ten were resumed with
  SendMessage rather than respawned.
- One composer's cleanup deleted other composers' scratch scripts from the shared session
  scratchpad. Brief each agent to work in its own scratch subdirectory.
